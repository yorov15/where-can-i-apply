Eligibility ( Nationality / Education / Language Proficiency ) A. Nationality Common Requirements (Freshman/ Transfer) Must be a non-Korean national, and both parents must be non-Korean nationals Parents and applicant holding foreign nationality(if they hold foreign nationality before starting an educational program equivalent to the high school curriculum) Dual Nationality holders are not eligible B. Education Common Requirements (Freshman/ Transfer) Applicants who have completed homeschooling, cyber learning, adult education, or domestic/international qualification exams (such as GED) during their entire education are not recognized as eligible students. ※ Based on Article 98, Paragraph 1, Clause 9 of the Enforcement Decree of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act (Recognition of Equivalent Academic Qualifications to High School Graduation). Freshman Applicants must be foreign nationals whose both parents are foreign nationals and must have completed the entire educational curriculum equivalent to Korea's primary and secondary education at domestic or overseas educational institutions. High school graduates (including those expected to graduate before the start of the semester) are eligible to apply. Transfer (3rd year) Applicants must be foreign nationals whose both parents are foreign nationals and must have either graduated from a 2-year or 3-year junior college (domestically or internationally), or Completed at least 2 years (4 semesters) at a 4-year university with a minimum of 65 credits from their previous institution. Applicants can only apply to the same or a similar major as their previous field of study. Those who have only completed 2 years at a 3-year university are not eligible to apply. Applicants from universities without a credit system are not eligible to apply. Applicants who are currently enrolled or on a leave of absence from Kyungpook National University as of the application start date are not eligible to apply. C. Language Proficiency Freshman 1) Applicants must meet at least one of the following language proficiency requirements (①-⑤): ① TOPIK Requirement: Achieve at least Level 3 on the Test of Proficiency in Korean (TOPIK or TOPIK iBT). ② University-Affiliated Korean Language Program: Complete at least Level 3 of a Korean language course at a university-affiliated Korean language education institution. ③ English proficiency test scores: TOEFL iBT 59, IELTS (Academic) 5.5, CEFR B2, NEW TEPS 202 or higher ※ Applicants whose native language is English may substitute language proficiency with proof of completion of higher education (e.g., graduation certificate) (English-speaking countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, South Africa, New Zealand, Australia, Ireland) ④ Korean Immigration & Integration Program (KIIP): Complete at least Level 3 of the Ministry of Justice's Social Integration Program (KIIP) or obtain at least 61 points on the preliminary evaluation. ⑤ Overseas Sejong Institute Certification: Submit a certificate of completion for the Basic Education Program issued by an overseas Sejong Institute (confirming completion of at least the Korean Intermediate 1 level). (※ Certificates from the online Sejong Institute are not accepted.) 2) Certain departments require applicants to meet specific language proficiency criteria to be eligible for admission. ※ Example: Department of Media and Communication - TOPIK Level 5 or higher, Department of Computer Science and Engineering - TOPIK Level 4 or higher, etc. ※ For further details, please refer to the Admissions Guide announced in early March / early September.
