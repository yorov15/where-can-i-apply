Tuition Scholarships Application Application Procedure Admission Guide Download Documents should be submitted via post after completing the online application. Step 1 Visit Application Website Visit JBNU IOA website ( https://ioffice.jbnu.ac.kr ) and check the notice board. Sign up and log in to the application portal ( https://www.uwayapply.com ). Step 2 Fill Out Application Form Review the Admission Guidelines and fill out your personal information. Upload the required documents (transcripts, ID photo, etc.). Step 3 Pay Application Fee Your application will be processed only after payment is completed. Step 4 Submit Required Documents Send all required documents by post to JBNU. Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances. Step 5 Interview Interview schedule will be announced in advance on the website. Step 6 Admission Results Results will be announced via the JBNU website and email. Step 7 Tuition Payment Pay your tuition fee within the designated period. Step 8 Visa Application Overseas residents: Apply at the Korean Embassy. Domestic residents: Apply through JBNU Office of International Affairs. Step 9 Arrival and Semester Start Arrive in Korea and begin classes at the start of the semester.
