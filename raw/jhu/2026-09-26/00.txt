International Applicants You are not a U.S. citizen, permanent resident (a person who holds a Green Card), eligible noncitizen, or have DACA or undocumented status. THERE IS NO SEPARATE APPLICATION FOR INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS. As part of the admissions application, all international citizens must document the available funding for their education by submitting the Certification of Finances (COF) form. International citizens who are applying for financial aid must submit a CSS Profile in addition to the COF. Hopkins accepts both the Common Application and Coalition on Scoir. We share key dates and break down the process to make sure you and your family have all the information needed to submit your best application. We meet 100% of demonstrated need for all admitted students. This means we work with you and your family to understand your unique circumstances to determine what you can pay toward college costs. Then, we cover the rest through need-based scholarships that don't need to be paid back, and work-study opportunities. We are need-aware for international students. If you anticipate needing any financial assistance throughout your four years at Hopkins, you must apply for financial aid as a first-year applicant. If your family demonstrates that you can only pay part of college costs, or none at all, we cover the rest. Follow our step-by-step instructions to apply for need-based scholarships.
