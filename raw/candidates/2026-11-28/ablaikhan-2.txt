MASTER'S DEGREE PROGRAM Application Periods Summer Admission: August 1 - August 25 Winter Admission: December 10 - January 5 Foreign citizens who have completed higher education programs (Bachelor's degree or equivalent) are eligible to apply for admission to the Master's Degree Program. All foreign applicants must undergo an interview conducted by the University Admissions Committee. Based on the interview results, the Committee makes the final decision on enrollment. Tuition Fees: https://www.ablaikhan.kz/en/for-applicants/for-applicants/tution-and-fees.html Required Documents Original Bachelor's degree diploma and transcript (academic record), together with notarized copies certified in the Republic of Kazakhstan; Two (2) photographs, size 3 × 4 cm; Medical Certificate Form 075/U issued by any licensed medical center in Kazakhstan; Notarized copy of the passport certified in the Republic of Kazakhstan - 2 copies. Important Note: The translation of educational documents into Kazakh or Russian must be certified by a notary in the Republic of Kazakhstan or by the diplomatic service of the Republic of Kazakhstan in the country where the document was issued. DOCTORAL DEGREE PROGRAM (PhD) Application Periods Summer Admission: August 1 - August 25 Winter Admission: December 10 - January 5 Foreign citizens who have completed postgraduate education programs (Master's degree or equivalent) are eligible to apply for admission to the Doctoral Degree (PhD) Program. All foreign applicants must undergo an interview conducted by the University Admissions Committee. Based on the interview results, the Committee makes the final decision on enrollment. Tuition Fees: https://https://www.ablaikhan.kz/en/for-applicants/for-applicants/tution-and-fees.html Required Documents Original Master's degree diploma and transcript (academic record); Two (2) notarized copies of the Master's degree diploma and transcript; Notarized copy of the passport - 2 copies; Two (2) photographs, size 3 × 4 cm; Medical Certificate Form 075/U issued by any licensed medical center in Kazakhstan; Curriculum Vitae (CV); List of published scientific articles, conference papers, and other research publications (if available). Important Note: The translation of educational documents into Kazakh or Russian must be certified by a notary in the Republic of Kazakhstan or by the diplomatic service of the Republic of Kazakhstan in the country where the document was issued. RECOGNITION OF FOREIGN EDUCATIONAL DOCUMENTS IN THE REPUBLIC OF KAZAKHSTAN Applicants who have obtained their education outside the Republic of Kazakhstan are required to undergo the procedure for recognition of their educational documents in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan. After submitting documents to the University Admissions Committee, applicants must apply for the recognition procedure through the State Corporation "Government for Citizens" (Public Service Center). The recognition procedure is carried out by the authorized state bodies of the Republic of Kazakhstan in accordance with the established regulations and requirements.
