Repeating a Course - All Students (Including Tuition Fee-Exempt Students) are charged Tuition Fees for each occurrence a course with a Final Grade is repeated. Program change- After enrollment, Qatari Students can transfer an academic program only once before being assessed Tuition Fees upon the second transfer Tuition Fee-Exempt Students Tuition exemption applies to Undergraduate and Foundation Program Students only. The Tuition Fee-Exempt Students and percentage of Tuition Fee waiver are categorized as follows, for students applying for admission for the first time, applying for re-admission, or continuing from the previous semester. Qatari Students (Self-sponsored): 100% Tuition fee waiver. Qatari Students ( sponsored by a third party): 100% Tuition fee waiver. Children of Qatari mothers: 100% Tuition fee waiver. Children of UDST - sponsored non-Qatari University employees: 50% Tuition fee waiver. Payment Schedule Payment Schedule Payments may be made in installments throughout the semester or in one lump sum payment. All outstanding fees for the semester must be paid by end of the same semester. Fees & Charges Application and Testing Fee Total application fee is 300 QAR (non-refundable). This includes Placement Assessment Testing. Applicants wishing to retake the placement tests are required to pay 200 QAR. Regulations Governing Payment of Fees & Charges Students are able to register without paying at the time of registration. Students who have an outstanding balance from a previous semester cannot register for the following semester. Should the University cancel a program, all fees will be refunded. Refunds Textbooks Refunds will be given for returned textbooks under the following conditions: Books are unmarked and in saleable condition. Books are returned within the first three weeks after the commencement of classes. Original receipts must be presented before a refund is issued Tuition fees Students are responsible for initiating their own refund requests and must complete the Student Revenue Refund Form. The form is available at the Finance Helpdesk in the Admissions and Registration Department. Any approved refund will first be applied against any outstanding balances on the student's account before the remaining amount, if any, is refunded to the student.
