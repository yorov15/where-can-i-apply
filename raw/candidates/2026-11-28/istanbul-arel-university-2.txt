These include: Sibling Tuition Discount Granted to students who have a sibling concurrently enrolled at the university. This discount applies only to associate and undergraduate degree programs and is not applicable to graduate programs. Scholarship for Students with Disabilities Awarded to students with officially documented disabilities in order to promote equal opportunities in education. Honor Scholarship Awarded to students who demonstrate outstanding academic achievement.
