Tri-City SLW Hackathon: Designing the Future of Cities Tri-City SLW Hackathon: Designing the Future of Cities Most of us can see what isn't working in our cities-from a lack of greenery to traffic congestion. The Tri-City SLW Hackathon - "Crafting Future Cities" is your chance to stop just thinking about it and start designing concrete changes. Over the course of two days, we will tackle the theme of public space as "living infrastructure." This event is unique because it takes place simultaneously in three European cities: Stockholm, Lisbon, and Wrocław. You can join us in person or participate online. WHAT: Tri-City SLW Hackathon WHEN: May 21-22, running parallel in three cities LANGUAGE: English WHO: Students, researchers, local authorities, and entrepreneurs REGISTRATION: Open until May 9 via Unite website! Why Should You Participate? Solve Real-World Problems: You will collaborate with experts (Problem Framers) who are seeking specific solutions to be implemented in urban spaces. International Networking: This is an opportunity to test your skills working with people from different countries and disciplines-ranging from architecture to programming and data analysis. Practical Skills: You will learn how to rapidly prototype and pitch your ideas to a jury.
