Fees are to be paid for a remote study course (distance learning) (100.00 EUR/semester), EUR 350 per semester for a second degree in accordance with § 13 para. 4 of the Saxon Higher Education Act (SächsHSG), provided that a degree has already been successfully completed and the total duration of the previous degree exceeds the standard period of study for the first degreeof study by 6 semesters. This does not apply to students who are enrolled in a Master's program as a prerequisite for a Bachelor's degree. long-term studying for students exceeding the regular study period in their current course by at least 4 semesters (500 EUR/Semester). special courses of studies , if mentioned in the University Fees Regulation. The fees may vary per course. (look in SINS ) Tuition fees are due in addition to the semester fee. Students liable to pay fees will receive a fee notification from the Enrolment Office/International Office before the start of the re-registration period. The fee is not due until then.
