Postgraduate Apprenticeship Scheme International Applicants APU Scholarships & Merit Awards for international students are available to new international students on first come first serve basis. As part of our continuous efforts to provide better opportunities to pursue higher education at Malaysia's Award-Winning University, we are offering deserving & talented school leavers a wide range of fee Awards leading to rebates/discounts. Applicants with Sports Excellence - Participation at International sporting events (Olympics, SEA Games, Youth Olympics, Asian Games, World Cups and Commonwealth Games) can be considered for the Merit Awards on a case to case basis. International Merit Awards Scholarships & Merit Awards This Scholarship & Merit awards is applicable to all countries / nationalities. The Merit Awards are fee Awards leading to rebates/discounts as follows: Qualifications Minimum Score in 2027 Final Results APU Merit Awards A-Levels AAA 30% Discount A-Levels AAB 20% Discount A-Levels ABB/AAC 10% Discount O-Levels/IGCSE 8 - 9 As 30% Discount O-Levels/IGCSE 6 - 7 As 20% Discount O-Levels/IGCSE 4 - 5 As 10% Discount International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma 34 Points & Above 30% Discount International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma 30 - 33 Points 20% Discount International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma 26 - 29 Points 10% Discount International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (IB MYP) 48 Points & Above 30% Discount International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (IB MYP) 44-47 Points 20% Discount International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (IB MYP) 35-43 Points 10% Discount * Merit Awards are awarded on a yearly basis and are renewable upon meeting minimum CGPA 2.50 at the end of each academic year. Conditions of the Awards All Awards leading to fees rebate/discount are on a first come first serve basis and you may secure by paying Application & Processing Fees of RM 3,600 (approx. USD 950). All applicants must meet the minimum entry requirements & maintain the CGPA of 2.50 in order to enjoy the Fee Awards in the subsequent years (where relevant). Failure to meet such academic requirements will result in the award being terminated/ suspended. All Awards offered by APU/APIIT to any student are at the absolute discretion of the Scholarships & Merit Awards Committee. Awards are limited to course/tuition fees only. All other fees & expenses including Registration Fees, Accommodation Fees, Deposits, Partners University Fees, Resit or Retake fees, living and travel expenses; as determined by the Award shall be borne by the Award holder. Awards are not transferable or exchangeable for cash either in part or in full. All the Award Applications must fulfil the Award Academic Entry Requirements with actual results. The University reserves the right to decide on the number of Awards to be awarded for each programme. Recipients who fail to comply with all the terms and conditions of the Awards will be required to pay back in full the total value awarded to him / her by APU. Award holders must practice exemplary behaviour and read, understand and comply with all rules, regulations and requirements as stipulated by the Rules & Regulations and Professional Code of Conduct of APU/APIIT. Award holders should attend regularly, diligently and without fail all classes, lectures, tutorials and lessons required by the course or programme of study. Award holders must meet all requirements pertaining to completion of assignments, projects and examinations as required by the course of study. All Awards are applicable for studies at APU in Malaysia only and are not applicable for study at overseas partner universities. Application Deadline The deadline for this International Scholarship & Merit Awards is 30 November 2026 . We must receive the full set of documents for scholarship application on or before this date. All Scholarships & Merit Awards are on first come first serve basis. Please email us at scholarship@apu.edu.my for more information. Applications will not be considered if the required information and/or documents are incomplete or not submitted.
