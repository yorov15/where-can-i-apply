Engagement Industry Community International Students UTHM offers a unique opportunity to further your studies in a university with a worldwide academic reputation, while enjoying the cultural, social and recreational facilities at a serene countryside location. Graduate students play a valued and important role in the School's contribution to scholarship and research excellence. These pages should provide you with the information you need to apply for your chosen programme. Undergraduate Postgraduate UTHM receives all applications from international students. However , all applicants are strongly advised to submit their applications online (by the require d date ). Please note - it is rare that our programmes are able to stay open (after the require d date ) and therefore, applicants are strongly advised to comply with the deadline. Entry Requirements Undergraduate Students Read more Master's Degree Bachelor Degree with honours from Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia or any other Higher Learning Institutions recognised by the Senate; Other qualifications equivalent to Bachelor Degree with related experience deemed acceptable by the Senate; * Minimum CPA : Coursework : 2.50 Mixed-mode : 2.75 and Research : 2.75 Other Qualifications Will Be Considered Working experiences in related field for those who do not meet the minimum CPA ENGLISH LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS FOR INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS: Minimum score of TOEFL 500 , IELTS 5.0 , or MUET Band 3 . Exemption shall be granted to a candidate(s) or international students who graduated in Malaysia. Doctor of Philosophy Master Degree from Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia or any other institution of higher learning recognised by the Senate; Other qualifications equivalent to Master Degree with related experience deemed acceptable by the Senate; A Bachelor Degree with CPA 3.67 and above from Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia or any other institution of higher learning recognised by the Senate. Note: If the Bachelor Degree is of the related field, other Master Degrees can also be considered. For online application, first you need to create your account through http://wapps.uthm.edu.my/intake 2. Fill in your online application. Then, print the form when completed. 3. The following documents need to be uploaded: a. Identification Card (MyKad) / Passport; b. Academic transcripts and certificates (Bachelor Degree for Master's Application; Bachelor Degree and Master for PhD's application) c. For final semester applicants, please enclose: i. Examination results for each semester ii. Verification Letter as a Final Semester applicant d. For research programmes, please enclose a Research Proposal; e. If you are currently employed, please enclose Proof of Work Experience; f. If you are an applicant with scholarship, please enclose Sponsorship Letter. 4. With effective from 1st January 2017, all applications will be processed via an online system, there is no need to submit any hardcopy. PROCESSING FEE 1.
