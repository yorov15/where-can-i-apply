Osmonov, we are committed to making high-quality medical education accessible to all deserving students. In collaboration with the Doing Good Charity Foundation, we conduct an annual scholarship program aimed at supporting our students financially and academically. This partnership underscores our dedication to fostering academic excellence and ensuring that financial barriers do not impede the educational aspirations of our students. Since the inception of the scholarship program, more than $100,000 in scholarships have been awarded, directly benefiting over 2,000 students. This substantial financial aid helps ease the burden of tuition fees, allowing students to focus more on their studies and less on financial concerns. The scholarships are awarded based on academic merit, financial need, and a commitment to the values of Jalal-Abad State University, ensuring that the most dedicated and promising students receive the support they need. Our scholarship program is designed to recognize and reward academic achievement, encourage leadership, and support those who are committed to making a positive impact in their communities. By providing this crucial financial support, we empower our students to pursue their studies with greater determination and confidence. The program also aims to inspire students to excel in their coursework and to engage actively in extracurricular activities that enhance their personal and professional development. Each year, the scholarship application process is meticulously planned and executed to ensure fairness and transparency. Students are invited to apply, and their applications are carefully reviewed by a dedicated committee. The selection process involves a comprehensive evaluation of each applicant's academic performance, personal statement, and demonstrated financial need. Successful applicants are awarded scholarships that help cover tuition fees, books, and other educational expenses. The impact of the Doing Good Charity Foundation Scholarship extends beyond financial support. It also fosters a sense of community and belonging among recipients. Scholarship students are encouraged to participate in various university activities, including leadership workshops, community service projects, and academic conferences. This holistic approach ensures that scholarship recipients are not only financially supported but also integrated into the vibrant academic and social life of Jalal-Abad State University. We are proud of the achievements of our scholarship recipients, many of whom have gone on to make significant contributions in the field of medicine and healthcare. Their success stories are a testament to the value and effectiveness of our scholarship program.
