Application and Registration Steps: 1. You can create a new account and apply through abuapply.antalya.edu.tr. 2. After your application get pre approval, an Offer Letter will be sent to you. 3. As mentioned in the Offer Letter, pay 1,000 USD deposit to the university's bank account, t he receipt is to be uploaded to abuapply.antalya.edu.tr. 4. Once the university recieve your payment, you will get an official Acceptance Letter. 5. Students who need a visa to enter Turkey can apply for the visa with the Official Acceptance Letter. 6. Complete the official registration by providing the original documents and the payment receipt of the first installment. ( https://antalya.edu.tr/en/departments/international-student-office/content/information-document-4/how-to-complete-registration ) 7. Students who complete their official registration can apply for a student residence permit with the Student Certificate. ( https://antalya.edu.tr/en/departments/international-student-office/content/information-document-4/student-residence-permit ) Note 1: Offer Letter is not an Official Acceptance Letter and cannot be used for visa application. Note 2: The deposit made to the university is refundable only in case of visa rejection. Çıplaklı Mah.
