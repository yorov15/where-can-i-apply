1:30 Step 1 Choose your study programme Undergraduate programmes Graduate Programmes Doctoral programmes Double Degree Programmes English Foundation Programme Step 2 Learn about admission requirements, intake, services, fees, and the refund policy of the University of Miskolc. Step 3 Register on our DreamApply platform, create your account, and start your online application. During the application procedure, pay the application fee (150 EUR) easily and securely online via Flywire, the official payment partner of the University of Miskolc. You can do that directly through the DreamApply system by clicking the 'Pay Online with Flywire' button on the invoice generated during your application. Applications are processed and evaluated after the payment is credited and confirmed by the financial department of the University of Miskolc. The application fee is non-refundable. The €150 application fee includes the following services: Administrative processing of the application Providing necessary feedback and managing the document completion process Entrance interview Registration procedure in case of a successful entrance exam Shuttle service for accepted students from Budapest airport to Miskolc on pre-scheduled dates set by the university Payment options More information Step 4 Mandatory Medical Certificate Template 2024. November 4. pdf pdf Click on the Apply Now button by the selected study programme, fill in the application form on our DreamApply online application platform and upload the application documents and the completed mandatory medical certificate template . Application documents Curriculum Vitae (CV) (Europass format) Motivation letter (written in 3000-3500 characters) Certified and translated copy of bachelor's /master 's degree and transcripts Official copy of the language examination certificate (minimum level: B2) OR an official document, issued by the previous university, confirming that the language of instruction was English Medical certificate (it can be uploaded after submitting the application) Copy of valid passport / national ID Proof of payment of 150 EUR application fee If you are applying for a doctoral programme, you will also need to upload additional documents. Additional documents Two Letters of Recommendation from former or current supervisors, recognized experts or professors of the field Research plan (minimum 3 pages) List of publications (if available) DreamApply Important All documents must be submitted in English or with an official translation. The original hard copies of bachelor's /master 's degree(s), of transcripts and of language examination certificate must be shown after your arrival. It is not possible to enrol at the university without presenting the original documents! No provisional degree is accepted! Step 5 Submit your application before deadline: Autumn Term of the 2026/2027 academic year Opening: 1 November 2025 Deadline: CLOSED Term starts: 1st week of September 2026 Please note that all university dormitory places have now been fully allocated. We are currently only able to accept new applications from applicants who are willing to arrange their own accommodation during their studies in Miskolc. If you wish to apply under these conditions, please indicate your intention by sending an email to apply@uni-miskolc.hu . Important: Please note that you must have confirmed accommodation before applying for your student visa, as proof of accommodation is a required part of the visa application process. Due to the high volume of applications and the anticipated extended duration of visa processing procedures, the University of Miskolc will close the applications for applicants from the following countries as of 30 April 2026: Bangladesh Pakistan Syria Afghanistan Yemen Applicants from the above-mentioned countries are strongly advised to submit their applications before the deadline. Please note that applications submitted after the stated closing date will no longer be considered or accepted. Step 6 As soon as the application fee is credited and confirmed by the financial department of the University, the formal and content evaluation of the application documents starts. Step 7 In case of eligibility, applicants are invited to take an online oral entrance examination where their professional knowledge, skills and command of English are tested by the examination board. The date of the oral examination is not bound to the date of the application submission. For details of the entrance examination, please visit the website of the specific study programme. Entrance exam is free of charge. Step 8 In a week, following the successful entrance examination, applicants are issued a conditional letter of admission indicating their tuition and accommodation (dormitory) fee paying obligation. Tuition fee The amount of tuition fee varies by study programme . V isit the website of the specific programme to check the exact details . The first semester tuition fee sh ould be transferred as stated in the Conditional Letter of Acceptance . The tuition fees of the forthcoming years sh ould b e transferred prior to each semester . Students will be notified of their fee-paying obligation in due time . Accommodation fee The University provides accommodation for first-year students either in the on-campus dormitories or through university partner accommodation providers to support and facilitate applicants' visa process. The first semester accommodation fee (850 EUR - a pprox . 300.000 HUF for 5 months ) is to be transferred together with the tuition fee . Step 9 Once the first semester tuition and accommodation fee are credited and confirmed by the financial department of the university, applicants are issued a F inal Letter of Acceptance confirming that the tuition fee is paid, and that accommodation is provided for the applicant either in the dormitory of the University of Miskolc or in external housing near the campus . The Final Letter of Acceptance serves both as proof of admission and accommodation, and must be presented during the visa procedure. Step 10 Applicants must submit their Visa application to the relevant Embassy/Consulate as soon as they receive the Final Letter of Acceptance.
