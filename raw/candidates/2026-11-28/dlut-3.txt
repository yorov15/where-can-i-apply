Admission Qualifications 1. Non-Chinese Citizen; 2. Age between 18 to 25 years old and in good health; 3. High School Diploma and Transcript (If you have not graduated at the time of application, you must provide a certificate of pre-graduation or expected graduation certificate.And than,obtain a high school diploma at the latest before enrolment); 4. Satisfy the language proficiency requirements for the Major speciality; 5. Starting from the 2027 academic year, applicants must participate in the "the China Scholastic Competency Assessment(CSCA)" (hereinafter referred to as the CSCA Test) before submitting the application. The test results will be one of the essential materials for our university's review and admission; 6. Comply with the regulations of the Ministry of Education's document. [2020] No.12 and the relevant regulations of our university. ※High school graduates under 18 years of age, with a guardian in China (Dalian) can also apply. Ⅱ . Required Documents 1. 《Dalian University of Technology Application Form For International Student》 （Finish the Online Application, the form will be auto-forming）. 2. Notarized High School Diploma ： If the applicant is a student, he/she must submit a certificate of enrolment issued by the school he/she is attending. Documents in languages other than Chinese or English must be attached with notarized translations in Chinese or English. 3. Transcripts ： Transcripts should be including all courses taken in high school up to the most recent semester. The transcripts should be issued and stamped by the Registrar's Office of the school that you are attending or the relevant student management office. Transcripts should be provided in Chinese or English. For languages other than Chinese or English, an English translation and notarization are required. 4. Language Certificate ： English-Taught Program, requirement of English proficiency certificate as following ： (1) To submit a TOEFL or IELTS or Duolingo transcript, in principle, the TOEFL score must be 80 or higher, and the IELTS score must be 5.5 or higher, and the Duolingo score must be 100 or higher. ; (2) the applicants that use English as the language of instruction at high school, may use the language certificate issued by the high school; (3) Applicants from countries where English is the official language are exempt from proof of English language proficiency. If the applicant cannot provide the above proof,the final tutor and department could evaluate your language level according to the interview or written test. 5. China Scholastic Competency Assessment(CSCA) transcript, scanned document. 6. Self Statement/Study Plan ： The language should be written in English or Chinese，not less than 600 words，please following " Attach 2 Self Statement Study Plan" for more details. 7. Recommendation Letter ： A letter of recommendation from your high school principal or teacher(Signature of referee with email address and contact number). Please following " Attch 3 Recommendation Letter" for more details. 8. Foreigner Physical Examination Form ： The applicant should strictly follow the items required in the "Foreigner Physical Examination Form", which must be completed in Chinese or English. The Foreigner Physical Examination Form will be invalid if the following items are missing: pages,self-photo, a cross-page stamp over the photo and the form or a signature and a stamp of physician and hospital.Please following " Attach 4 Foreigner Physical Examination Form" for more details. 9. Blood Test Report ： The Blood Test Report should include results of HIV, Syphilis/V.D.R.L, HbsAg and HCV. 10. Passport Photo Page and Blank Page ： Scan copy of Passport Photo Page and Blank Page. The passport expire day should later than September 2027. 11. Certificate of No Criminal Conviction: It needs to be issued by the relevant department of the applicant's country, and the validity period is six months. If it is not possible to provide it at the application stage, the Undertaking for a Clean Criminal Record in attachment 5 can be signed as a temporary substitute document. Please following " Attach 5 Undertaking for a Clean Criminal Record" for more details. 12. Others ： （1）Financial Certification（The deposit certificate issued by the bank in the applicant's name of not less than RMB 50,000 or equivalent in foreign currency.This is limited to the applicant at his/her own expense）; （2）If applicants have already been to China before, please attach the entry and exit visa page as well; （3）one photo（3cm*4cm）; （4）As of September 1, 2027, applicant under 18 should provide relevant materials for notarization of guardians. Notes ： a.Documents fail to meet the above requirements will be rejected; b.Please provide documents 2-11 in scanned copy and upload to the online application system of DUT; c.Documents other than Chinese or English are required to be translated and notarized ; d.During the review process, the school may require students to submit other materials if needed. Ⅶ . Application for admission 1.Take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment(CSCA) Test 2 . Log in online application system of School of International Education to upload the scanned copy of all the required documents Application system: http://iso.dlut.edu.cn; 3. Upon receiving the application documents submitted by the applicants, the school will undertake an examination.Applicants once admitted, the Admission Notice and the Application for Entry Form (JW202) will be sent to the enrolled applicants. ※ Important Note： 1.Applicants must take the "CSCA Test" organized by the China Scholarship Council on behalf of the Ministry of Education of China and have a valid "CSCA " test transcript before applying undergraduate studies. To take the test, please visit official CSCA website (www.csca.cn) for specific test dates and content. 2.To ensure a smooth application process, it is advisable to take the CSCA test at an early date and submit your results promptly, allowing sufficient time for subsequent review procedures. For specific test subjects, please refer to the "2027 Dalian University of Technology CSCA Test Subjects for International Undergraduate Students". 3.Applicants who apply to our university before November 30, 2026, may first submit the DUT online application and then supplement it with a valid transcript after completing the CSCA test.
