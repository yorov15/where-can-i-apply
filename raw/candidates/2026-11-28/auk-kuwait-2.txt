A competitive, merit-based scholarship awarded to top-performing students. Eligibility: First-Time College Students: Minimum 3.75 high school GPA Continuing Students: Minimum cumulative AUK GPA 3.70 with 60+ AUK credits Application Process: Complete admissions application Submit AUK Scholarship Application Form Adhere to the deadline announced each year before the fall semester To Keep Your Scholarship: Maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.50 or higher Enroll in at least 12 credits per semester (Fall and Spring) What is it? Partial tuition support for currently enrolled, non-scholarship students facing financial hardship. Eligibility: Must have earned 45+ AUK credits Must have a minimum cumulative AUK GPA of 2.00 Cannot be on an AUK or Government Scholarship Coverage: Tuition only (not fees) Up to 12 credits per semester for maximum two semesters per academic year (excludes summer) Application Process: Submit Financial Aid Application Form Provide required financial documentation Deadlines are announced via AUK email PUC Government Scholarships AUK is a recognized and approved institution under the Private Universities Council (PUC) of the Ministry of Higher Education in Kuwait. Each academic year, PUC allocates a limited number of internal government scholarships to eligible students enrolled at AUK. Who qualifies? Kuwaiti nationals Applicants must meet GPA and eligibility criteria set by the Private Universities Council (PUC) Covers: Tuition and book allowance Important Notes: Scholarship seats are limited and subject to PUC allocation and approval. All applicants must meet the eligibility criteria and guidelines set by PUC. Final decisions on awards and renewals are made by the PUC. What's offered? Private organizations occasionally fund scholarships for AUK students based on academic merit or financial need. How it works: Opportunities are shared with eligible students via AUK email Criteria, timelines, and amounts vary by sponsor Stay tuned to your AUK email for scholarship announcements or contact the Office of Scholarship and Financial Aid for inquiries .
