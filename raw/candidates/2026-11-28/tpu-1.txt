Send a copy of the international passport and a copy of the educational document of appropriate level (see B. Section of the Policy) in English or in Russian to an admission officer by e-mail: omrs@tpu.ru Receive the official reply about your admission to the University within 2 weeks. Send your contact information (postal address and phone number) to the admission officer and receive the Visa Support Letter and the Official Letter of Admission within 2 months after approval. Apply for a student visa and legalize your educational documents, translate educational documents into Russian and notarize. Buy a ticket and send information about your arrival to the admission officer not later than a week before your arrival in order to use a free pick-up service. Upon arrival to the University campus submit all the necessary educational documents legalized in a proper way (see B. Section of the Policy), an international passport, 12 recent color photos (3 x 4), an original medical certificate that proves HIV-negative result taken within the last 30 days, a certificate of health. Sign the contract and pay the tuition fee for the first academic year to the university bank account. An application will not be considered by the Admission Office until it is deemed complete (i.e. receipt of all the necessary transcripts and supporting documents) Educational documents for admission and requirements for the submitted documents Pre-University Course/ Pre-Master's Course/ Postgraduate Preparation Course (1-1,5 academic years) To enter a university, an applicant should submit the following documents by fax or e-mail: Pre-University Course: A copy of the Education Certificate that verifies the Secondary School level (including academic transcript: the list of studied subjects, grades and marks) in English or Russian.
