Nationality (Freshmen/Transfer) ✔ Applicant with non-Korean nationality whose parents also having non-Korean nationality as of the date of application. * Both parents and applicant(student) are recognized as foreigners only if both parents and applicant(student) acquire foreign nationality before the applicant(student) begins the curriculum corresponding to Korean high school curriculum. * Above eligibility requirements apply to those who acquired foreign nationality through emigration or adoption (Refer to [4. Required Documents - Additional Family Relationship Documents]). * Applicants are not eligible if they or their parents hold South Korean nationality, additional nationalities including Korean citizenship, or are stateless. Educational Background Freshmen Completed (or expected to complete) high school education at equivalent level in Korea Transfer Completed high school education at equivalent level in Korea, and meet below conditions while enrolled in 4-year university. OR Graduated (or expected to graduate) from college or university. * Graduated from a university without credit system are not eligible. - Transfer to Junior : Completed more than two years (4 semesters) and acquired more than 66 credits * Essential to verify whether the applicant has completed at least the second year (4 semesters, excluding summer/winter sessions) at their previous university. * For 3-year bachelor's programs (e.g., in the UK), at least 2/3 of the program must be completed; for 6-year programs, at least 1/3 must be completed. - Transfer to Sophomore : Completed more than one year(2 semesters) and acquired more than 36 credits * Essential to verify whether the applicant has completed at least the first year (2 semesters, excluding summer/winter sessions) at their previous university. Notes ✔ Formal education equivalent to Korea high school ※ Enforcement decree in the Law of Elementary & Middle School Education No. 98-1-9 - Applicants should complete all formal education in ONE country. - Applicant who studied in more than two countries should complete 12 years of education. - Applicant who have completed less than 11 years should apply for the high school as a freshman or transfer in Korea or overseas. - In the case of 11 years (above) of foreign system applicants should complete the last 3 years of education in ONE country to acquire qualification of high school. - For students with unfinished years due to a gap in the education system, the period of years at an overseas university will be counted as equivalent to korean high school years. ✔ Those who are enrolled in our university cannot apply for transfer. ✔ Applicants who attained their level of education by qualification examinations, home-schooling and cyber learning systems are not eligible. 9 Language Proficiency (Freshmen/Transfer) Must meet more than one of the following requirements Korean TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) Level 3 or CNU Language Center Korean Language Course Level 3 * Yeosu campus Culture and Tourism Management: TOPIK Level 2 or CNU Language Center Korean Language Course Level 2 English TOEFL iBT* 4.0, IELTS 5.5, New TEPS 290, CEFR B2, or TOEIC 700 or higher ※ TOEFL iBT: For tests taken on or before January 20, 2026, a score of 80 or higher under the previous scoring system will apply, and such scores will be accepted for up to two years from the test date, until no later than January 20, 2028.
