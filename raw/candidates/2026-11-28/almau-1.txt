Search Button Admission conditions Admissions Committee Admission to Bachelor's programmes is based on the applicants' application, on a competitive basis in accordance with the admission rules: For school leavers, graduates of previous years - based on the results of the Unified National Test (UNT); For college graduates - according to the results of the UNT on the basis of TIPO (college) or internal examinations. Application period - 18 June - 25 August Admission requirements: You will need to score over 50 points Exception: Law - 75 points Business Administration - 80 points The pass mark for paid admission is 60 points and a minimum of 5 points in each subject (except for mathematics and reading literacy - 3 points). A link to register for entrance exams for applicants who have not scored a threshold score: https://almau4u.kz/admission List of documents required for admission: 1. Identity document (original and copy) 2. Original document on general secondary, technical and vocational education (certificate/diploma with attachment); 3. 6 photos (size 3×4); 4. Medical certificate of the form 075-U (with a fluorography photo); 5. Health passport (or vaccination card of 063 form); 6. Certificate of Unified National Testing - UNT; 7. Enrolment certificate (for young men); 8. Copy of the child's birth certificate when signing the agreement on educational services by one of the parents for a child under 18 years of age; 9. EDS. You will also need to scan all of the above documents to expedite the application process. Creative exam requirements: Application Periods: From June 20 to August 10, 2025 - for applicants applying on a paid (self-funded) basis From June 20 to July 7, 2025 - for applicants applying for a state grant Creative Exam Dates: First session: July 8 and 10, 2025 Admission conditions for foreign citizens: During the summer admission period (18 June - 25 August). 1. It is necessary to pass an online interview via Zoom, MS Teams platform with representatives of the Schools you plan to apply to; 2. After you will need to submit a full list of original documents to us in the Admissions Committee in person. List of required documents: Application to the Rector (to be filled in at the Admissions Committee); Document on general secondary, technical and vocational, post-secondary or higher education (original) with a notarized translation into Kazakh (state) and/or Russian; A foreign passport and its copy with a notarized translation into Kazakh and/or Russian ; 6 photo cards of 3 x 4 centimeters in size; Medical certificate in the form 075-U (with a fluorography photo); A certificate on the presence or absence of a criminal record. Note: Documents submitted in a foreign language must have a notarized translation into the Kazakh, English or Russian language. Documents on education, issued by foreign educational organizations, undergo the procedure of nostrification in the order established by the legislation after enrolment of persons, during the first semester of study. Please note! Foreign citizens are admitted without entrance examinations or UNT on the basis of an interview! After a successful interview you will have to leave the country during the period of stay in the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan without a residence permit, and when we make an official invitation for you, you, having received it by mail, come back to the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan already in the status of a student. Link to register for an interview for international students: https://almau4u.kz/internationalstudents Admission requirements for the English Department: Minimum threshold score for admission to the English Department: IELTS - 5.5 TOEFL ITP - 460 TOEFL IBT - 87 TOEFL - 560 In case these certificates are not available, an internal language examination is taken at the University . Exam format : offline/online The admission process after graduating from college: Admission in a major: If the chosen major is related to one that you graduated from college, admission is carried out through an internal exam (essay, interview or testing). Length of schooling - 2-3 years depending on the major. Internal exams are held from June 20 to August 25. Changing the major: If you plan to change your major, admission is carried out in the same way as for school graduates who have passed the Unified National Testing (UNT) in five subjects. Length of schooling - 3 years. More information about this can be found on the website testcenter.kz. Submission of documents: From June 20 to August 25 in offline format at the address: 227 Rozybakiev Str., Almaty. Stages of registration: Conclusion of the contract; Payment for tuition (the first tranche until August 25); Start of studying. Admissions Committee In order to enter the Master's programme, you need to take the Comprehensive Test (CT) in the summer period by pre-registering on the website testcenter.kz. Comprehensive Testing Dates Application period: from June 1 to July 8, 2026. Registration is carried out on the official website of the National Testing Center: https://testcenter.kz Testing period: from July 20 to August 10, 2026. CT entrance examinations for the RTM: For Research and Teaching Masters (RTM) 25 points in a foreign language; 7 points in other subjects and/or creative exams. Will need to pass: 1. Foreign language test includes: a. listening; b. vocabulary-grammar; c. reading. 2. Test to determine readiness for learning: a. Critical thinking; b. Analytical thinking. 3. Test on the profile of the group of educational programs: 2 profile disciplines. Number of test tasks - 130 Testing time - 235 minutes (3 hours 55 minutes) Passing score - 75 points out of 150 CT entrance examinations for PM: Profile Masters (PM). Passing score - 30 points out of 70. The CT consists of: Test on the profile of the group of educational programs: 2 profile disciplines. Number of test tasks - 70 Testing time - 160 min (2 hours 40 min) List of educational disciplines at the Master's program: Management Project Management Urban Planning Finance Marketing Jurisprudence Supply Chain Management Please note! The study format is hybrid (online + offline). In-person classes are held during the 1st, 8th, and 15th weeks of study. If your first higher education is in a different field, you will need to additionally complete and pass the prerequisite courses during your studies. Training for the prerequisites is provided free of charge. Minimum points for the Master's grant for 2025: Management and Administration - 36 Management and Administration - 104 Finance - 30 Finance - 108 Marketing - 32 Marketing - 90 Law - 30 Law - 108 Supply Chain Management - 96 List of documents: Higher education diploma with transcript (original); Copy of an identity document; 3×4 photo (2 pcs); Medical certificate in form 075-U (with a fluorography image); Certificate of passing a foreign language test (if available); Certificate of passing the Complex Testing (KT); Military registration certificate / certificate from eGov (for male applicants). Admissions Committee For whom? 1.Masters of scientific and pedagogical direction; 2.Masters of profile education, who have mastered the OP of pedagogical profile. Stages of admission: 1.Registration for the entrance exam on the NCT website and submission of documents to the university; 2.Passsing the entrance exams; 3.Enrolment. List of required documents: 1.Copy of the identity document 2.Education documents 3.International certificate confirming foreign language proficiency: English: IELTS (Academic) - 5,0; TOEFL IBT - 35; TOEFL ITP - 417; TOEIC - 550; Duolingo English Test - 80. Germany: DSH - B1; TestDaF - B1. France: TFI - B1 ; DELF/DALF B2, TCF - B1. 4.Medical certificate in form 075-U 5.Photo 3*4 6.Personal sheet on personnel records 7.List of research papers for the last 3 calendar years (scientific publications, imprints, research plan, essays) 8.Official KAZTEST certificate Stages of entrance exam: 1.Online interview; 2.Readiness to learning test; 3.Exam questions on profile; 4.ESSAY. Main topics for exam questions: Essay topics for applicants to the PhD doctoral program in the group of educational programs «D072 Management and management» - 2025 Essay topics for applicants to the PhD program in the group of educational programs «D074 Finance» - 2025 Essay topics for applicants to the PhD program in the group of educational programs «D075 Marketing and Advertising» -2025 Key topics for entrance exam for PhD programme applicants on the group of educational programs «D072 - Management and Administration» Key topics for entrance exam for PhD programme applicants on the group of educational programs «D075 Marketing and Advertising» -2025 Admissions Committee The process for applying for a state grant is as follows: Preparation for UNT, taking mock tests (January-April) Registration and passing the UNT for the grant (May - June) - Submission of the application for the grant through the University Admissions Office / egov.kz in the period from 13 to 20 July - Receipt of the results of the drawing of state grants (August). During participation in the state grants competition, the following parameters are taken into account: 1. The UNT score for the grant 2. The average score of the certificate 3. Presence of prizes / awards for participation in subject international / republican Olympiads 4.
