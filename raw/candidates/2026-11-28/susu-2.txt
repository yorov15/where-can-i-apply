Each year 15 000 international students receive state scholarships to study in Russia. South Ural State University is among the university destinations you can choose. You can study for a Bachelor's, Specialist, Master's or PhD level degree, and also learn Russian. The government scholarship includes: free tuition for the entire duration of the chosen programme; maintenance allowance (for the entire duration of study); dormitory accommodation (if available). This is YOUR chance to get some truly amazing experience and discover the unique culture and nature of Russia. Applying for Russian Government Scholarship To apply please register on the website education-in-russia.com When submitting your application, choose South Ural State University as your first priority. Follow the 5 step plan to get your Russian Government Scholarship: https://education-in-russia.com/ NOTE! Rossotrudnichestvo (Russian Federal Agency) or the Russian Embassy hold the competitive selection for scholarships in every country. For any enquiries concerning the selection process and its date, please contact Rossotrudnichestvo or the Russian representation in your country.
