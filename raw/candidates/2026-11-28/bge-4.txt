The Budapest University of Economics and Business offers one of Hungary's most widely recognised and respected degrees, one you can be proud of for a lifetime. BGE has a long-standing tradition of practice-oriented education, delivering up-to-date, career-focused programmes that equip students with the skills and knowledge needed for successful employment. Our programmes combine hands-on learning with a solid theoretical foundation, preparing graduates to understand and navigate the rapidly evolving global business environment. All major fields of economics and business studies are available, complemented by interdisciplinary programmes such as business informatics, communication and media studies, and international studies. University life at BGE goes beyond academics, offering a life-long experience shaped by professional networks, friendships and personal growth. With around 20,000 students, BGE is a large, diverse and inclusive academic community where students from Hungary and around the world can connect, grow and find their place. 1857 year of establishment 1,500+ international students from 100 countries 78% of our students get a job offer before graduation
