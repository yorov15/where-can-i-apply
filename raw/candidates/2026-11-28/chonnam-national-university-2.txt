Global Scholarship Benefits: Full tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: You do not need to submit a special application form to apply for the Global Scholarship. All applicants will be considered for this scholarship. Strategic Researchers Scholarship(SRS) Benefits: Full tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: You do not need to submit a special application form to apply for the SRS. However, you must have a prospective advisor at CNU to be considered as a candidate. Test of Proficiency in Korean(TOPIK) Scholarship Benefits: Full tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: Please submit a valid TOPIK certificate when you apply for admission. Kindly keep in mind that we do NOT accept expired certificates. Academic Excellence Scholarship(AES) Benefits: Partial tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: You do not need to submit a special application form to apply for the AES. All applicants will be considered for this scholarship. Please refer to the admission guidelines for information since scholarships are subject to change. Undergraduate(new students) TOPIK scholarship Benefits: Full or Partial tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: All applicants will be considered for this scholarship. CNU-BRIDGE Scholarship Benefits: Partial tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply:Applicants who have registered at least *two regular Korean courses at language education center of CNU. Academic Excellence Scholarship(AES) Benefits: Partial tuition waiver for the first semester How to apply: You do not need to submit a special application form to apply for AES. All applicants will be considered for this scholarship.
