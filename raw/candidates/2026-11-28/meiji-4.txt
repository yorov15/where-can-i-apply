Scholarships by Meiji University (Determined before enrollment) Meiji University Special Grant for Privately Financed International Students 〈No application required〉 This program aims to support promising students from overseas who can contribute to the advancement and globalization of Meiji University's education and research. It seeks to nurture individuals who will succeed in the international arena. 【Eligible Students】 ●Students enrolling in the 2026 academic year ※Eligible students will be determined by each faculty based on the results in the following entrance examinations: ・Entrance examination for international students（Type Ⅰ and Type Ⅱ） ・Entrance examination for English Track of School of Global Japanese Studies ・Entrance examination for overseas designated schools 【Amount of Grant】 Tuition fee（4 years） ※Continuing review every fiscal year 2. (3) Meiji University Parents' Association Union Scholarship for International Students 〈No application required〉 This scholarship aims to support first-year international s tudents, ensuring a smooth start to their study life at Meiji University . Recipients are nominated by International Education Center. Stipend Number of recipients Selection period 200,000 JPY 10 Undergraduate students (One from each school) Spring semester (4) Meiji University Emergency Scholarship for International Students This emergency scholarship is available to international students enrolled at Meiji University who are experiencing financial difficulties due to a sudden change in their living environment. Financial support Example of sudden change in living environment Acceptance of application One-fourth of the annual tuition fee 【Important Note】All of the following circumstances must occur within ONE YEAR after enrollment. (1) Householder's death, disappearance, missing (2) Unemployment of the householder (only involuntary loss of employment, excluding voluntary unemployment, pay cuts, poor performance, etc) (3) Injuries/disease of the householder (only in cases of absence from work due to hospitalization for more than three months) (4) Extensive damage of the householder's house from natural disasters or fires As needed Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship / JASSO Scholarship ■ MEXT Scholarship Program (Embassy Recommendation) Check here for the outline of MEXT Scholarship for 2027 Embassy Recommendation.
