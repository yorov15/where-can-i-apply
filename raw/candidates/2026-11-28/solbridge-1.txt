Applications are assessed on an individual basis. The screening process takes into account the applicant's academic records, English proficiency, financial status, recommendation letter(s) as well as extracurricular activities. Admission is selective and students are encouraged to apply as early as possible to ensure sufficient time for application review, visa processing, and moving preparations. These agreements help fuel our diversity and allow us to focus on our mission; Educating the Next Generation of Asian Thought Leaders. If your university does not have a partnership agreement with SolBridge, we do offer a limited number of applicants to apply as visiting students. Visiting students are treated as part of the SolBridge family. Scholarships are available to visiting students. Transcripts will be issued after you complete your semester at SolBridge. As a visiting student, it is your responsibility to ensure that SolBridge's credits (we are an AACSB accredited school) can be transferred back to your home university. Our Academic Affairs team is prepared to assist you, but the final decision on the transfer of credits is with your home university. We do advise you to talk to your home university before applying as a visiting students.
