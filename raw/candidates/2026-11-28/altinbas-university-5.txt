Before You Apply Admission requirements depend on the applicant's degree level, citizenship status, educational background, selected program and medium of instruction. Meeting the minimum requirements makes an application eligible for evaluation but does not constitute automatic admission. Applications are evaluated according to the current regulations and decisions of the Turkish Council of Higher Education (YÖK), Altınbaş University legislation and the requirements of the relevant academic program. Eligibility Confirm that you are eligible to apply through the international-student admission route. Academic Qualification Your previous education must satisfy the academic requirements of your selected degree. Language Proficiency English, Turkish or German requirements depend on the program and degree level. ELIGIBILITY Who Can Apply? Undergraduate & Associate Degree Applicants must be in the final year of high school or have graduated from high school and must satisfy the current YÖK principles governing admission of students from abroad. Citizenship and secondary-education history are assessed together. Turkish, dual-national and TRNC applicants may therefore be subject to additional eligibility conditions. Graduate Programs International applicants may apply for graduate programs subject to the citizenship, previous-education and academic requirements stated in the University's current International Student Application, Admission and Registration Directive. Additional program-specific conditions may apply. Eligibility rules may change. The YÖK principles and University legislation in force for the relevant admission cycle always prevail over summaries published on this page. Current YÖK Principles International Admissions Directive ACADEMIC REQUIREMENTS Academic Qualifications Undergraduate & Associate Degree Applicants must satisfy the country-based diploma, certificate or examination criteria approved by Altınbaş University. Requirements for Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy may be higher than those applied to other programs. High-school completion examinations such as Abitur, IB, GCE and Tawjihi have no University-defined validity limit, while university entrance examinations such as SAT are valid for two years. View Country-Based Minimum Criteria Master's Degree Applicants must hold an eligible bachelor's qualification from a higher education institution accepted under the University's institutional-recognition procedures. MINIMUM BACHELOR'S GPA Science Programs: 2.00 / 4.00 Social Sciences Programs: 2.00 / 4.00 Health Sciences Programs: 2.50 / 4.00 Doctoral Degree Doctoral admission is subject to the current Graduate Education and Teaching Regulation and the specific requirements of the relevant doctoral program. Under the current International Student Admission Directive, applicants applying on the basis of a master's degree must have a master's GPA above 3.00 / 4.00 . Additional degree, thesis, examination and language requirements may apply. Scientific Preparation Where the applicant's previous field of study differs from the admitted graduate program, the student may be assigned a scientific preparation program in accordance with the applicable academic regulations. DOCUMENTS & RECOGNITION Documents and Previous Education Application Documents Depending on the level of study, applicants normally submit the relevant diploma or final-year document, transcript, passport/identity document, photograph and any examination or language documents applicable to the selected program. Documents issued in languages other than Turkish or English must also be submitted with an approved Turkish or English translation. High-School Equivalency For undergraduate and associate-degree registration, the University may require an official Equivalency Certificate confirming that the foreign high-school qualification is equivalent to a Turkish high-school diploma. Equivalency Information LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS Language Proficiency and Preparatory Exemption Current School of Foreign Languages Directive Language proficiency and preparatory-program exemptions are governed by the current Altınbaş University School of Foreign Languages Exemption Directive for Associate, Undergraduate and Graduate Programs . International students applying for document-based preparatory exemption must submit their application within 30 days of registration. View Current SFL Exemption Directive Official document in Turkish. English-Medium Master's Programs For admission to an English-medium master's program, the current YDYO Directive requires completion of the A2 level through one of the accepted results below, obtained within the last two years. AU YDY: 60% completion of the A2 section in the Placement Exam TOEFL iBT: 60 PTE Academic: 30 CAE: C CPE: C YDS / YÖKDİL: 50 Oxford Test of English Advanced: 51 Oxford Test of English: 51 Cambridge Linguaskill General: 91 English-Medium Doctoral Programs Turkish and international students beginning an English-medium doctoral program must satisfy the current YDYO exemption requirement using a result obtained within the last two years. The current Directive lists the relevant Altınbaş YDY/DİLMER examinations and Yunus Emre Institute Turkish Proficiency Examination (TYS), subject to the applicable level and validity requirements. Partially foreign-language programs: international students enrolled in programs conducted 70% in Turkish and 30% in English must satisfy the applicable Turkish and English exemption requirements separately. German-Medium Programs Special German Preparatory Program exemption rules apply to programs conducted fully or partially in German. Accepted qualifications include the certificates and examinations specified in the current YDYO Directive, including applicable University of Cologne, DSH, TestDaF, DSD II, Goethe and telc qualifications.
