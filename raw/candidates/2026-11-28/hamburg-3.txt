Organizational matters Student contribution Tuition fees and semester contribution Semester contribution The semester contribution forms part of Universität Hamburg's financing scheme for AStA (student council) and Studierendenwerk (student services). The semester contribution is due at the beginning of each semester. Further information at semester contribution . Tuition fees In accordance with Hamburg's Student Financial Assistance Act (Hamburg Government Gazette, HmbGVBl. no. 32 p. 376), Universität Hamburg also implemented tuition fees in 2007. As of winter semester 2012/13, the University abolished these tuition fees. However, semester contribution payments are still mandatory. For details, please go to Studiengebühren . How to finance your studies Apart from applying for BAföG (federal student loan scheme), students may consider alternative financing options such as student loans, scholarships, etc. Find additional information on financing options at financing your studies . Late semester re-enrollment For information on late semester re-enrollment, please go to Semester reenrollment .
