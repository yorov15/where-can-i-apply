Exceeding the study period Fee for studies according to § 58 (3) of the Act. It is levied when the standard period of study is exceeded by more than one year. The amount of the fee is: CZK 18 000 for each additional six months of study. The fee shall be charged to the student by the Rector or the Dean. It is payable within 90 days from the date of delivery of the decision on the assessment of the fee.
