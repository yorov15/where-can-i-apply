studies in English Degree Studies in English Introduction Study information Study programmes International Students Ambassadors Contact E-application Brno University of Technology offers a wide selection of English programmes on all three levels of education - Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral. Studies in English are paid. The length of study is usually 6 semesters for Bachelor's programmes, 4 semesters for Master's programmes and 8 semesters for Doctoral programmes. The programmes are open to applicants from all countries, however you might need a visa to stay in the Czech Republic.
