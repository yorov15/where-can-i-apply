Scholarship Opportunities International prospective students may qualify for tuition fee exemptions ranging from 25% to 75% based on their diploma grades and performance in SAT or ACT exams. Details 100% English Medium of Instruction The medium of instruction at TED University is entirely in English . Students at TED University complete their courses, assignments, and exams in English during their academic tenure. Social Activities and Students Societies Student organizations such as Communities, International Student Associations, Science Project Teams, and Sports Teams are managed by students under the supervision of an academic advisor and operate under the Dean of Students. These organizations have their own management, budgets, and inspection mechanisms. Details Dormitories TED University Dormitories aim to provide a home-like and safe environment for students. The dorms offer common areas for studying, resting, and socializing, along with a professional staff and a student-centred satisfaction policy. Details Study in the Heart of Ankara TEDU Campus is located in the city centre and is equidistant from everywhere.
