Scholarship The American University of Ras Al Khaimah values the importance of access to quality education. We also realize that enrolling at a university often has an enormous impact on personal finances. AURAK exists to dismantle those obstacles between you and your future career and therefore offers various academic scholarships and financial aid programs to fit your specific needs. AURAK's merit scholarships are offered to eligible full-time students of all nationalities. The academic merit scholarships aim to recognize high-achieving students who go above and beyond and to encourage students from Ras Al Khaimah, the region, and abroad to join its academic programs. Continuing students may also qualify for up to a 10% increase (conditions apply) on their current scholarship based on their academic performance from the previous year and meeting the qualifying criteria. For many AURAK students sports activities are an essential component of their student experience. Students participating in these sports activities can develop leadership skills, teamwork, discipline, as well as a competitive spirit! Participation in sports positively contributes to the physical and mental health of student athletes. The following guidelines are expected to be followed by applicants in preparing the portfolio for being considered for the Athletic Scholarship: 1. Athletic Scholarships providing a 30% reduction in tuition are limited to 1 to 2 scholarships per budget year. 2. Athletic Scholarships are awarded to entering undergraduate students who are highly talented and skilled athletes. In the application portfolio, you must: Provide the name(s) of the sport or sports in which you excel (for example, Soccer, Basketball, Volleyball, Cricket, Indoor games and Athletics, etc.). Provide a summary of your achievements in sports, including documentation which may include videos, pictures, certificates, and dates and locations of your sports accomplishments. Describe any leadership roles or responsibilities held during your secondary school experience in athletics. Share a written reference letter from a coach/PE teacher to validate your participation in athletics. Provide the contact information for references should we require additional information from them. How can I Apply for AURAK Athletic Scholarship? Fill the AURAK Athletic Scholarship from link : https://forms.office.com/r/md1wF5Kdsm The deadline for the athletic scholarship application is August 11, 2025. The final assessments of the applications will be conducted by the athletics staff in the Department of Student Life. The Office of Admissions will inform applicants of the status of their athletic scholarship application by August 18, 2025. Criteria for maintaining the Athletic scholarship 1. Athletics scholarships are for a period of one year and will be renewed if: the CGPA is 2.75 and above. the student participated during the year as a member of a university team there are no disciplinary actions against the scholarship recipient 2. If the student is injured playing for a university team, the student can still retain the scholarship for the following year. AURAK has partnered with renowned organizations to offer scholarships for aspiring individuals like you. TDRA Scholarship One of our esteemed scholarship partners is TDRA (Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority). They provide scholarships under their education funding category, supporting students in their educational journey For more information or to apply for a scholarship, visit: https://www.tdra.gov.ae/ictfund/en/funding-categories/education/scholarships.aspx Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi Foundation for Policy Research Scholarship Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi Foundation for Policy Research Foundation offers another exceptional scholarship opportunity. They are dedicated to empowering students and making higher education accessible. For more information or to apply for a scholarship, visit: http://www.alqasimifoundation.com/scholarships#How-to-Apply We wish you the best of luck in your scholarship applications and look forward to welcoming you to AURAK AURAK is committed to providing incentives in the form of scholarships and financial grants to attract and retain high academic achievers. In addition to meeting the expectations set for each scholarship/grant, students must meet other conditions set including, at a minimum, meeting all requirements for admission to the program of interest. Sibling Scholarships AURAK provides a 20% tuition discount for the second, or more sibling of an existing fully enrolled student in any academic program at the university (continuing and new). This scholarship is valid with the condition that all siblings are enrolled at AURAK at the same time. Partnership Scholarships For students admitted who are employees of entities that have been designated as "AURAK Scholarship Partners" will be granted a 20% tuition discount. Faculty / Staff Scholarships Faculty: 100% tuition waiver for self, spouse, children. Staff: 30% tuition discount for self, spouse, children. Alumni Scholarships For AURAK alumni admitted to pursue an additional graduate degree will be granted 20% tuition discount.
