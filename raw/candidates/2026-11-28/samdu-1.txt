RASHIDOV Admission to university is an important step, which has significant impacton person's future life and destiny. For this reason, it is so important to determine the desired field of study and university, in which you will go to study. If you desire to get a higher education at Samarkand State University, this will undoubtedly be the right choice! Samarkand State University (SamSU) is one of the most ancient and largest universities in Uzbekistan. There are more than 27 000 students' study from various regions of Central Asia in 4 Institutes and 8 faculties of the University. Sport complex, medical center, computer classes, creative and other centers also distinguish our university. We are happy to call university students those, who set a goal to get quality education, based on modern technology. Always aspire to novelty, be inquisitive and challenge the difficulties. All this will help you to realize your potential and based on this find your career in science, education and society. Always glad to meet you at our university!
