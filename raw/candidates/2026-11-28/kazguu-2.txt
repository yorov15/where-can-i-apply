Contact us today to learn more about the admission process and study opportunities at MNU Contact us Apply for a grant Frequently asked questions 1 . When does the academic year start? The academic year begins in the first week of September. 2 . When is the application period? Applications are accepted from June 1 to August 25. 3 . What documents are required for admission? • Copy of passport; • Original education document (certificate/diploma with transcript); • нотариально заверенный перевод документов на казахский или русский язык; • IELTS / TOEFL iBT / Duolingo / PET / CAE / FCE certificate; • Copy of birth certificate (if applicant is under 18); • Criminal record clearance certificate; • Math Test results (for ISE programs); • Motivation and recommendation letters; • Admission fee payment receipt. 4 . Do documents need to be translated and notarized? Yes, all documents issued in a foreign language must be officially translated into Kazakh or Russian and notarized. 5 . Is diploma recognition required? Recognition (nostrification) is mandatory for foreign education documents. Required: ID, application (submitted at Public Service Center), and apostilled document with translation. More info: https://egov.kz/cms/en/services/185pass_mon 6 . Is a visa required to study in Kazakhstan? Yes, international students must obtain a student visa (category C9). More details: https://egov.kz/cms/en/articles/visa_regime_for_foreigners 7 . Are entrance exams required? For programs of the International School of Economics (except International Relations), passing the MathTest is mandatory. 8 . Can I apply online? Yes, preliminary application can be submitted online, but original documents must be provided before the academic year starts. 9 . What is the tuition fee? The cost per credit is 55,000 ₸. The average workload is 60 credits per year. Applicable to all programs except International Relations. 10 . Are there scholarships or grants? Yes, international applicants can apply for internal grants via www.intl.mnu.kz. Deadline: April 15. 11 . Is accommodation provided? Yes, international students can apply for dormitory accommodation. Allocation is based on university policies.
