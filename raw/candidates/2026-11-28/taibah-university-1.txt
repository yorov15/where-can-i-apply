The university combines quality education, diverse majors, and study opportunities in an environment that helps international students adapt and succeed. Why Taibah University? Taibah University provides a comprehensive educational experience that combines the academic aspect with the student experience, enhancing the student's readiness for their scientific and professional future. Academic programs in various specializations An organized and modern learning environment A multicultural and multinational student community A distinguished location in Medina The university also focuses on academically supporting the student and enabling them to take advantage of educational opportunities and related activities during their study period. For more information, you can refer to International Students Guide for Studying at Taibah University External Scholarships External Scholarships General conditions for nomination for the external scholarships program The applicant must hold a high school diploma or its equivalent from outside the Kingdom. Documents must be uploaded during electronic application with certified translation, and certificates and official documents must meet authentication requirements according to the applicable regulations. Final verification and validation requirements must be completed before issuing the university ID number. The high school diploma must not be older than five years The applicant must not have received another scholarship from any educational institution inside the Kingdom. The applicant must not reside inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The applicant's age must be no less than 17 years and no more than 24 years for undergraduate studies. The applicant must not have a previous record at Taibah University for the same academic level. The applicant must not have been expelled from another university for disciplinary reasons. Pass the approved medical examination and be medically fit for study. Additional Conditions for Paid External Scholarships: In addition to the general conditions, the following are required for paid external scholarships: Health and Medical Specializations. a. Obtaining a high school certificate or its equivalent with a grade not less than. (90%) b. Achieving a score of not less than (1350) in the SAT 1 test. c. Achieving a score of not less than (6) in the IELTS test or its equivalent. Demonstrating the financial ability to cover tuition and living expenses by providing a financial guarantee. Providing a valid medical insurance document for the visa holder and their dependents. Committing to pay tuition fees before the start of each semester. Paying the administrative fees before issuing the university number, which amounts to (250) riyals, excluding VAT. For more about the terms and details External Grants Guide Internal Scholarships Internal Scholarships The university provides study opportunities for students residing within the Kingdom according to the approved regulations. Admission Requirements for the (Internal) Scholarship Program (Paid): The applicant must have obtained a high school certificate from within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia or its equivalent. The applicant must have a valid legal residency in the Kingdom. The applicant's age must not be less than 17 years and not exceed 24 years for the bachelor's level. The applicant must not have received another scholarship from any educational institution in the Kingdom. Passing the medical examination determined by the university. Obtaining the employer's approval for studying, provided it is certified by the Chamber of Commerce, according to the type of approved residency. The applicant must not have been expelled from any educational institution in the Kingdom. Payment of the prescribed administrative fees before completing the final admission procedures. The programs include: Health and medical specialties Scientific and engineering specialties Administrative and applied specialties Humanitarian specialties Review the academic programs Steps to Apply The university has been keen to simplify the application procedures to be clear and easy for international students: Choosing the appropriate academic program Reviewing the admission requirements for the chosen track Applying through the Edus Platform in Saudi Arabia Submitting an application on the platform Uploading the required documents Reviewing the application and issuing the decision Completing the procedures after acceptance Study in Saudi Arabia The Study in Saudi Arabia platform is a unified electronic portal affiliated with the Ministry of Education in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, designed to facilitate the application process for international students. The platform allows students to easily explore and apply to universities and available majors. It also enables applicants to track their application status electronically until final acceptance. Furthermore, it offers diverse opportunities for enrollment in academic programs and scholarships.
