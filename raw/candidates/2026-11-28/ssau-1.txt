Для получения квоты: Register in your personal account ; Prepare the necessary documents; Select your study program and universities in order of priority. Note: to study at Samara University, select it as your first choice in the list of priority universities; Submit your application online; Participate in the competitive selection. This may include document verification, interviews, and/or testing organized by the Rossotrudnichestvo representation; Wait for the selection results. If your candidacy is approved, the application will be sent to universities according to your chosen priority; Once the university approves your candidacy, you will receive your admission letter; Obtain a student visa if necessary. All visa support is provided by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation. Wait for the visa invitation and contact the Russian Federation Consulate / Embassy in your country to issue your visa; Start your studies in Russia! Tuition-free education for Open Doors Olympiad winners "The Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project of the "Global Universities" Association" , supported by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation, has been held since 2017. Winning the Olympiad provides the opportunity to study bachelor's/specialist, master's, doctoral, or postdoctoral programs at Samara University. To participate: Register on the website od.globaluni.ru ; Take the required tests according to your chosen track: bachelor's/specialist, master's, doctoral, or postdoctoral; Wait for the publication of the winners' lists; Do not miss the stage where universities upload winners' documents on the site russia-edu.minobrnauki.gov.ru to obtain the education quota. Respond promptly to requests from the Open Doors Organizing Committee and the university coordinator to provide the necessary documents; Receive your admission letter, obtain a visa if necessary, and begin your studies at Samara University. How can I study at Samara University? ▼ You can enroll: Under the quota of the Government of the Russian Federation; Through a scholarship, by winning the International Olympiad of the "Global Universities" Association; On a state-funded basis (free of charge as a compatriot with Russian roots) by passing entrance exams; On a paid basis by passing entrance exams. 2. What programs are available at Samara University? ▼ Samara University offers: Main programs: bachelor's, master's, doctoral; Short-term programs: international schools, pre-university preparatory programs. Study programs International schools Preparatory course 3. Are there any English-taught programs? ▼ Yes, Samara University offers one bachelor's programs in English. In 2025, the available program is: 24.03.05 Design and Maintenance of Aircraft Engines All the postgraduate programs are Russian-taught. 4. When should I submit my application? ▼ Every year, the admission campaign starts on June 20. Follow updates on the official website or Telegram . 5. What documents are required to apply? ▼ An educational document, legalized according to the rules of your country (certificate, diploma, transcript/supplement to the diploma); An international passport with at least 18 months validity from the start of the program; Notarized translations of all documents. 6. How can a foreign citizen study for free? ▼ Through a scholarship, by becoming a winner of the International Olympiad of the "Global Universities" Association; Under the quota of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation; On a state-funded basis through the general competition: citizens of Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and compatriots living abroad (people historically or culturally connected to Russia, e.g., former USSR citizens) can apply for state-funded places in Russian universities under the same conditions as Russian applicants. 7. How can I get a recommendation letter from Samara University? ▼ If you want a recommendation letter to apply for a scholarship, you can contact the Mobility and Recruiting Department: Address: 32a Moskovskoye Shosse, Office 2 E-mail: admission@ssau.ru . 8. Can I change my program if I am a scholarship holder? ▼ Yes. According to Clause 2.3 of Section IV of the Protocol of the Interdepartmental Commission on the Admission of Foreign Citizens under the Government Quota of the Russian Federation dated 31.07.2021 № АК-130/05, changing the program (specialty) is possible within the same enlarged group of professions, fields of study, and specialties, with the approval of the relevant institute of Samara University. 9. Will I have a dormitory room? ▼ Yes, if you are a foreign applicant and you are admitted to a full-time program. Information about accommodation costs and dormitory addresses is available on the university website . For questions regarding check-in, you can contact the Student Dormitories Office at uso@ssau.ru ) or write on VK . 10. How much does dormitory accommodation cost? ▼ The cost of living in the dormitory ranges from 770 to 5100 RUB per month (10-65 USD). The price depends on the comfort level of the dormitory. 11. I arrive late in the evening / on the weekend. Will I be able to move into the dormitory? ▼ Unfortunately, no. Check-in is available only during working hours: Mon-Fri from 10:00 to 17:00. Please plan your travel and arrival time in advance, taking this schedule into account. 12. What documents do I need to enter Russia? ▼ An international passport with a student visa (for citizens of visa countries); Educational documents, legalized if required. 13. How can I get a visa invitation? ▼ Admission on a paid basis A visa invitation is issued after you successfully pass the entrance examinations and your educational documents are verified (for Bachelor's, Specialist, Master's, and Postgraduate programs), or after your application and educational documents are checked (for preparatory programs). The processing time for a visa invitation is 45 days. Visa invitations are not issued in an expedited mode. Admission on a state-funded basis (quota) The Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation will prepare your personal visa invitation number after your application is approved. You can find this number in your personal account. With this number, you will need to apply for a visa directly at the Embassy of the Russian Federation in your country. 14. Do I need to come to Samara to take the entrance exams? ▼ No, it is not required. Entrance exams are conducted both offline and online. 15. Do I need a VHI (voluntary health insurance) policy? ▼ Yes, international students must obtain a VHI (Voluntary Health Insurance) policy upon arrival in Russia. For this matter, you can contact the International Protocol Department at dip@ssau.ru. 16. What procedures do I need to complete when I arrive in Russia? ▼ Please see the details here .
