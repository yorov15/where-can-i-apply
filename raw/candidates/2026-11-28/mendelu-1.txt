Here are six reasons you'll love MENDELU A large foreign student community: 20 % of our students come from abroad. Brno is a top student city; it´s safe and more affordable than capital cities. Europe in the palm of your hand. Germany, Prague, Vienna and many other popular destinations are only few hours away. You won't get lost. Everything important is on the campus or nearby. We are a green university. Our research focuses on the climate and sustainability. We provide unique facilities where you can further develop your skills and knowledge.
