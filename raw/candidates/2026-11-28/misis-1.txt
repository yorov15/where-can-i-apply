Admission based on the intergovernmental quota NUST MISIS welcomes foreign citizens and compatriots living abroad for training each year, within the quota set by the Government of the Russian Federation . State funding within the quota covers the full cost of education for the entire period, including the cost of training in the preparatory department for foreign citizens who do not speak Russian. Scholarship holders are provided with accommodation at prices set for students studying on budget places (currently, the cost ranges from 1,900 to 3,500 rubles per month, depending on the type of accommodation and living conditions), and a monthly academic scholarship is paid as well. All other expenses, such as travel, registration and translation of documents, obtaining and extending a study visa, insurance, meals, etc., are covered by the scholars at their own expense. Within the framework of the government scholarship, admission to the following programs is available: Taught in Russian: Bachelor's degree, specialty, basic higher education Master's degree, specialized higher education Postgraduate studies Taught in English: Master's degree, specialized higher education . It is also possible for winners and prize winners of the Open Doors Olympiad to enroll in free bachelor's, master's, and postgraduate programs. For more detailed information about the Olympiad and its conditions, please visit the website . When filling out the form, please select the NUST MISIS study program as your priority. We also recommend adding up to 5 additional universities as backup options. If you are not sure about your Russian level, sign up for the preparatory course (for this, be sure to indicate a special field in the application.) Additionally, NUST MISIS offers programs where you can study in English. After filling out all the required fields in the form, upload a color photo of yourself and print, sign, and scan the completed application. ➁ Upload the necessary documents to your personal account: a completed application form, a copy of your passport, education documents, and documents on individual achievements such as diplomas, certificates, and a list of scientific publications. Also, provide a letter of recommendation from NUST MISIS if applicable. All documents in a foreign language must be translated into Russian and notarized. You can find more information on the official website of NUST MISIS. ➂ Apply for verification before the deadline and expect an invitation to selection tests (or an online interview) from the Russian Center for Science and Culture (RCSC or Russian House) or the Embassy of the Russian Federation in your country.
