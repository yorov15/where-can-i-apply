and guided process designed to accommodate both local and international applicants. Whether you choose to apply online, by email, or via a physical submission, the steps are clear and well-supported by our Admissions Office. To begin, select your preferred application method and ensure all required supporting documents are prepared. These include academic transcripts, identification documents, and proof of payment for the application and registration fees. Once your application is submitted, our team will review the information provided and may contact you if any clarification or additional documentation is needed.
