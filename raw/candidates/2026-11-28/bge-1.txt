Programmes > Portfolio > Bachelor's Budapest University of Economics and Business BGE Search Menu Search Menu Bachelor's programme Bachelor's programme If you continue your studies after graduating from High School, you can choose from plenty of programmes and institutions of higher education. BGE is Hungary's biggest university in economics and business, with almost 20,000 students studying with us to become a business professional! You can choose from six undergraduate programmes across our four faculties. The programmes last six to eight semesters and are available on a full-time or part-time basis. Depending on your programme, you'll earn a Bachelor of Arts (BA) or Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree. During your studies, you'll gain practical skills and build the knowledge you need to start your career in your chosen field. You can apply for the following BA/BSc programmes at BGE: Business Administration and Management (Hungarian, English) Commerce and Marketing (Hungarian, English) Communication and Media Studies (Hungarian, English) International Business Economics (Hungarian, English) Finance and Accounting (Hungarian, English) Tourism and Catering (Hungarian, English) 2026 - Budapest University of Economics and Business - All rights reserved v1.14.3 Useful links Data of public interest Regulatory Documents IT guides Campus locations Conditions of Use Cookie settings Privacy Policy Requirement System for Students Developments Follow us made by
