International International Welcome to Sunway University Greetings and a warm welcome to our vibrant and inclusive Sunway community. As an integral part of Sunway Education, a legacy that began in 1987, Sunway University has dedicated itself to delivering exceptional education to both local Malaysians and students hailing from various corners of the globe. Immersing yourself in our campus means becoming a member of a diverse tapestry that spans over 80 countries and embraces over 2,000 international students. This multicultural environment will undoubtedly enrich your academic journey, fostering interactions that transcend borders and enhancing your cultural and learning encounters. Our International Office (IO) offers comprehensive support that covers Admissions, Student Welfare, Visa Assistance, and University Placement. Our dedicated team is ready to provide guidance on immigration-related queries and walk alongside you on your path to academic excellence. Your adventure with Sunway is about to unfold, and we're here to make it truly unforgettable.
