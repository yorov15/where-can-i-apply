Family (Sibling) Discount Eligible students may apply for the family discount by completing the official application form and submitting the required supporting documents. Applicants must be enrolled in an undergraduate degree program in the same academic year as their sibling or siblings. The discount applies only to tuition fees and must be renewed for each academic year. Applications must be completed before the applicable registration deadline. Official proof of relationship must be submitted, such as birth certificates or passport copies showing the parents' names. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval; eligibility is assessed by the University. Open the Family Discount Application Form Prepayment and Refund Policy Please read these conditions carefully before making any payment. Making a payment confirms that you have reviewed and accepted the applicable payment and refund terms. 1. Refund eligibility The tuition prepayment is refundable only when the applicant's Turkish student visa is officially refused by the relevant Turkish Embassy or Consulate. The applicant must provide the official visa refusal document. Altınbaş University may verify the document with the relevant diplomatic mission. Failure to register for personal, financial, travel, accommodation, academic or other reasons does not create refund eligibility. 2. Refund amount and processing time An administrative deduction of USD 250 applies to an approved refund. Accordingly, up to USD 750 of the USD 1,000 prepayment may be refunded before bank charges. Bank transfer fees, correspondent bank charges and other intermediary costs are deducted from the refundable amount. Approved refunds are normally processed within two months after the visa refusal is verified and the required refund information is completed. 3. Applicants who are not eligible for a visa-refusal refund Applicants whose citizenship does not require a Turkish student visa cannot claim a refund on the basis of visa refusal. For applicants from Afghanistan, the tuition prepayment is strictly non-refundable. The University reserves the right to reject incomplete, unverifiable or late refund submissions. 4. Prepayment validity and deferral If registration is not completed, the tuition prepayment may remain valid for up to one academic year . Program availability, admission requirements and the applicable tuition fee are reassessed for the intake in which the applicant later registers. A deferral does not guarantee that the originally offered program will remain available. 5. Refund application process Refund requests must be submitted through the official refund procedure communicated by the University. After the request is received, the applicant may be sent a secure link to provide bank account information. Refund correspondence and secure links are sent to the email address registered in the applicant's admission record. 6. Full tuition payment for visa and registration purposes Applicants may pay the full applicable tuition fee and complete online registration before travelling to Türkiye. After registration, the student may use the issued student certificate and registration documents in support of the visa application. Completing registration ends eligibility for a prepayment refund. Tuition refunds after registration are governed by the applicable University rules. Graduate Program Fee Coverage Non-thesis Master's Programs 3 semesters The published program fee covers the standard three-semester program period. Thesis Master's Programs 4 semesters The published program fee covers the standard four-semester program period. Students are expected to complete their program within the stated fee-coverage period. Additional registration or study periods may be subject to additional fees under the applicable University rules. Tuition Payment Rules Undergraduate and associate degree programs Tuition is charged for each academic year. The annual tuition may be paid in full at the beginning of the academic year or in two installments, subject to the payment schedule stated by the University. The first installment is payable before the Fall semester and the second installment before the Spring semester. Graduate programs The full program fee is payable during the first academic year. Payment may be made in full or in two installments, with one installment payable before each semester. Graduate tuition arrangements stated in the official offer letter take precedence where a program-specific payment plan applies. Late payment Payments made after the applicable enrollment or payment deadline may be subject to a 5% late-payment charge . Outstanding balances may prevent course registration, document issuance or access to student services until the payment obligation is completed. English or Turkish Preparatory School Undergraduate and associate degree students: where preparatory education is required, the Preparatory School tuition fee is the same as the tuition fee of the degree program to which the student has been admitted. Master's degree students: the Preparatory School tuition fee is USD 1,000 per semester . The applicable preparatory requirement and payment amount are confirmed in the student's offer and registration records. Important Notice Tuition fees, payment schedules, program availability and policy conditions may be updated by the University. For new admissions, the conditions stated in the official offer letter and registration record apply. For continuing students, re-registration charges and the individual payable balance are determined according to the student's initial enrollment conditions and the University's financial records.
