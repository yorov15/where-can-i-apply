admission to FEFU it is necessary to submit a full set of documents before the end of the application process. List of documents: Document on secondary general education or secondary vocational education + appendix with grades + consular legalization/apostille (if required) + notarized translations into Russian; Copy of passport + copy of Russian visa (if available) + notarized translation of passport (if passport data is not duplicated in Russian) ; Certificate of completion of Russian language courses or preparatory faculty (if available) ; Documents confirming the status of a compatriot (in case of applying on the compatriot's line) : birth certificate of the father/mother confirming birth in the USSR territory; passport of the father/mother; birth certificate of the applicant confirming kinship; document confirming the applicant's current residence abroad + notarized translations of the documents into Russian (if the information is not duplicated in Russian) ; Consent for enrollment in FEFU (for those applying for the budget on the compatriot's line). The app can be downloaded from App Store , Google Play , RuStore or AppGallery . Through the unified portal of public services Госуслуги (for applicants who have an authorized account ). Documents should be submitted in a full set, including notarized translations into Russian. Each document is provided in PDF format ! PDF instruction: How to apply documents through RuID app. Video instructions: Part 1 How to register in RuID app , Part 2 How to apply documents through RuID app . After checking the correctness of the information and submitted documents, you will receive further instructions on the admission process (check notifications in the app and email).
