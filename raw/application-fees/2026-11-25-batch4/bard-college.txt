Why apply early ? Every student admitted in an early round has their full demonstrated need met. At Bard, the ability to pay should never decide who gets a world-class education. For students admitted in an early round, we meet full demonstrated need, covering tuition, food and housing, required fees, and first-year academic programming (if applicable). We work closely with families to make Bard affordable, without compromising the academic and creative freedom that defines us. Bard College has been test-optional for over 50 years You do not need to take the SAT or ACT to apply to Bard Submit SAT or ACT scores only if you feel they accurately reflect your academic abilities. They inform our review in only a very small fraction of applications that include them, and not submitting scores is never held against you. Bard helps cover tuition and fees beyond what Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits pay. About Yellow Ribbon × Yellow Ribbon Program For veterans. Bard helps cover tuition and fees beyond what Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits pay. For veterans and those eligible for veterans benefits. Through the Yellow Ribbon Program, colleges and universities enter a voluntary agreement with the Veterans Administration to cover tuition and fees in excess of the amounts payable under the Post-9/11 Veterans Educational Assistance Act of 2008. The program financially aids veterans, and those eligible for veterans benefits, so a Bard education is accessible regardless of a student's socio-economic circumstances. Learn More + Instructions × For Parents & Families Submitting materials After the 24-48 hour confirmation, your student receives a portal login and link. The Application tab shows an Application Checklist to review from time to time. Students upload everything there, including optional supplemental materials. Transcripts and recommendations show a green checkmark once received. If one doesn't appear, your student should follow up with their counselor, teachers or school office. We email you and your student if anything is missing. Recommendation letters can never be uploaded by the applicant. Recommenders must send them directly to Bard. Please don't mail or email materials to the Admission Office unless we ask. Students upload everything through the portal. Submitted on the deadline, with materials still to come? Students can upload them after the deadline as long as the application is already submitted. Do it as soon as possible, because the committee can't read incomplete applications. Bard Entrance Exam applicants receive a confirmation email with portal access within 48 hours of submitting their essays. Learn about the Bard Entrance Exam. Fire and Safety Report Notice of Availability of the Annual Security and Fire Safety Report Bard College is committed to assisting all members of the community in providing for their own safety and security. The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report is available on the Office of Safety and Security website. If you would like to receive a hard copy of the Annual Security and Fire Safety Report which contains this information, you can stop by the Office of Safety and Security Office in Memorial Hall, 30 Campus Road, Annandale-on-Hudson, NY 12504, or you can request that a copy be mailed to you by calling 845-758-7460. The report contains information regarding campus security and personal safety including topics such as: crime prevention, public safety authority, crime reporting policies, fire safety, disciplinary procedures and other matters of importance related to security on campus. The report also contains information about fire statistics in Bard residential facilities and crime statistics for the three previous calendar years concerning reported crimes that occurred on campus; in certain off-campus buildings or property owned or controlled by the College; and on public property within, or immediately adjacent to and accessible from the campus.
