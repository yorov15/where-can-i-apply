Early Decision Tips Frequently Asked Questions Expand All Collapse All Is Bryn Mawr test-optional? Yes, we are standardized test-optional for all applicants. If you're an international applicant, you'll still need to submit English proficiency results. Be sure to review our testing policy for all the details. Are interviews required? No.* While we do not require interviews, we do strongly recommend them as they can be a great opportunity to share more about yourself beyond your application materials and learn more about Bryn Mawr. Browse our interview options . *We reserve the right to require an interview for students who attend homeschool or online school, or are graduating early. Is Early Decision right for me? We recommend that prospective students think carefully about Early Decision. Early Decision at Bryn Mawr is binding, which means that when you submit an Early Decision application, you are making a commitment to enroll at the College if admitted. Students applying Early Decision should consider Bryn Mawr their first and only college choice and, if admitted under the Early Decision plan, are required to withdraw applications from other colleges and universities. We recommend running our MyinTuition and Net Price Calculator cost calculator tools and discussing Early Decision with your family and counselor prior to applying. If I attend a community college, am I required to complete my associate's degree in order to apply as a transfer student? No. We can consider your application with a minimum of 1 full-time term (or 4 individual courses) of completed college-level work. Does Bryn Mawr have a policy for AI use in applications? Yes, review our statement on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Your Application to learn more. Can nontraditional-aged students enroll part-time? Yes, nontraditional-aged students may enroll part-time and will participate in the same classes as first-year students. Do transfer and nontraditional-aged students have access to financial aid? Yes, transfer and nontraditional-aged students have access to need-based financial aid. Does Bryn Mawr have any articulation agreements for transfer students? Yes, we have articulation agreements with the Community College of Philadelphia and Northampton Community College. I'm a nontraditional-aged student, how should I apply? Nontraditional-aged students (24 and older in August the year of matriculation) who have completed at least one semester at a college or university should apply as transfer students. If you have not completed any college courses, you should apply as a first-year student. Can transfer and nontraditional-aged students live on campus? Yes, transfer and nontraditional-aged students enrolled full-time can live on campus. Campus housing is traditional with limited access to kitchens. Applicants should indicate their interest in living on campus on their application. Part-time students may not live on campus.
