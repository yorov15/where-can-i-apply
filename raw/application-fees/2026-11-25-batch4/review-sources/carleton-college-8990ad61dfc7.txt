Carleton is more affordable than you might think. Yes, you can go to a top-ranked school like Carleton, get an exceptional education, graduate without debt, and make a profound impact on the world. We meet the full demonstrated need of every student, without loans. When you apply, we determine what your family can reasonably pay, and we use a combination of grants, scholarships, and student employment to cover what's left. No-Loan for All Our no-loan financial aid policy means every Carleton student can get an exceptional education without debt. We meet 100% of your demonstrated financial need without loans - and we're proud to be one of a small number of colleges nationwide with the resources to make this promise. Learn More About No-Loan Our Middle-Income Investment Carleton's reputation as a national leader for enrolling and supporting middle-income students speaks for itself: for more than a decade, one-third of every incoming class has come from middle-income households. At Carleton, the middle isn't being squeezed out of an elite education - with financial aid that's made up of grants instead of loans, we make sure college is entirely within reach. Get an Instant Cost Estimate $100M to Support Low-Income Families, Forever At Carleton, excellence and access are the same commitment - that's why we've always provided generous financial aid to low-income families.
