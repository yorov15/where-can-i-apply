Full Scholarship: Tuition waiver, comprehensive medical insurance, monthly living stipends, and on-campus dormitories (or off-campus housing subsidy). Partial Scholarship: Tuition waiver. Scholarship applicants must submit their scholarship applications through the USTC Online Application System . Based on a comprehensive evaluation of each applicant, USTC will determine whether to award a scholarship and, if so, the level of the award. Eligibility 1. Applicants must be under the age of 25 as of September 1, 2027. 2. For all other eligibility requirements, please refer to the 2027 USTC Admission for International Undergraduate Programs . ※ Applicants who have been granted for other Chinese scholarship or funds (2027-2028）cannot apply for this scholarship. Application Time : October 25, 2026 - March 31, 2027 Application Guideline 1. Create an account in the USTC Online Application System and select the appropriate bachelor's program. 2. Fill in all the information and upload the documents as required. * After submission of your application, please keep checking your email box used for login in the application system for important notices, including requests for supplementary application materials, notifications regarding online interviews and so on.
