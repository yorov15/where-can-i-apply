Application Fee Waiver We understand that the application fee can present a financial hardship for some applicants. If you are applying for financial aid and the application fee presents a hardship for you or your family, please complete the Caltech Active Transport Pass form to determine if you are eligible for an application fee waiver. For students applying through QuestBridge , you are never charged an application fee when applying to Caltech with your QuestBridge application. Questions? Contact your regional counselor or email us at [email protected] . Caltech Active Transport Pass (ATP) In biology, substances normally move through cell membranes according to the concentration gradient, from high concentration to low concentration. In order for a substance to move against the concentration gradient, the cell needs to use energy, usually in the form of ATP (Adenosine triphosphate). This is called active transport. There are many reasons why students may face a concentration gradient when applying to college, and the application fee is one of them. As a result, we are introducing the Caltech Active Transport Pass (ATP!) to allow eligible students to get through this initial barrier and submit an application without worrying about the cost. If you are applying for financial aid and the fee presents a financial hardship, you may submit the form to determine if you are eligible for an application fee waiver.
