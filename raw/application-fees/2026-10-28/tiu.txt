Applying to TIU is simple and straightforward Whether you're applying from Kazakhstan, South Korea, Turkey, or anywhere else in the world - the process is the same. Everything can be completed online, there's no application fee, and our International Office is available to assist you at every stage. Most applicants receive a decision within two weeks. No Application Fee Apply at zero cost International students pay no application fee. Submit your application and documents online completely free of charge. Start your application Fast Decision Get a response within 2 weeks Our admissions committee reviews applications promptly. Most international applicants receive their admission decision within 10-14 working days. Admissions FAQs Two Intakes September and February entry Missed the autumn deadline? You can still apply for the spring intake. TIU offers two entry points per academic year for international students. How to apply in 4 steps From choosing your programme to arriving in Tashkent 1 Choose your programme Browse our bachelor's and master's degrees across Business, IT, Education, and Languages - all taught in English 2 Submit your application Complete the online form and upload your documents: passport, transcripts, English certificate, and motivation letter 3 Receive your offer Get your admission decision within 2 weeks. Accept the offer and pay the tuition deposit to confirm your place 4 Visa & arrival Receive your invitation letter, apply for your student visa, and prepare for your journey to Tashkent What you'll need Documents & requirements Prepare the following before starting your application. All documents should be in English, or accompanied by a certified translation. 01 Valid passport With at least 12 months validity remaining from your intended start date 02 Academic certificates & transcripts Secondary school certificate (Bachelor's) or Bachelor's degree (Master's) with full transcript 03 English language certificate IELTS 5.5+ / TOEFL 65+ for Bachelor's, IELTS 6.0+ / TOEFL 78+ for Master's 04 Motivation letter A personal statement explaining why you want to study at TIU and your career goals 05 Passport-size photos 2 recent photos (3.5 x 4.5 cm) on white background 06 Medical certificate Health clearance certificate (required for visa application) Arrival & Orientation We'll be there when you land From airport pickup and housing arrangements to campus orientation and city tours - our International Office makes sure you feel at home from the moment you arrive in Tashkent. Accommodation & arrival support International application deadline: 1 June 2026 Don't leave it too late. International applicants have an earlier deadline to allow time for visa processing, document verification, and travel arrangements. Apply early to secure your place and accommodation. International Applicant FAQs Do I need a visa to study in Uzbekistan? Most international students require a student visa (type "O'T-2") to study in Uzbekistan. Citizens of some CIS countries may be exempt. TIU's International Office will issue an official invitation letter to support your visa application once you've been accepted. Can I apply without an IELTS or TOEFL score? If you completed your secondary or university education in English, you may be exempt from the language test requirement. Contact our admissions team with your academic documents and we'll confirm your eligibility. How long does the visa process take? Once you receive your invitation letter from TIU, the student visa is typically processed within 5-10 working days at your local Uzbek embassy or consulate. We recommend applying for your visa at least 4-6 weeks before the semester starts. Does TIU provide accommodation for international students? Yes. TIU offers student housing options near campus. International students are given priority for university accommodation. We can also help you find private rental options in Tashkent at affordable rates starting from $200/month. Are scholarships available for international students? Yes. TIU offers merit-based scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition for high-achieving international applicants. All applicants are automatically considered - no separate scholarship application is needed. Can I transfer credits from another university? Credit transfer is evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Submit your previous university transcripts along with course descriptions, and our academic office will assess which credits can be transferred to your TIU programme.
