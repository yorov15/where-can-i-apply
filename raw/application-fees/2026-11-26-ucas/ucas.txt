Extra information
There are some key points to note:
You must complete all mandatory questions and you can’t skip any sections. These must all be complete before your application can be sent. But you don’t have to do it all at once – sign in and save your progress at any time.
Once you've completed every section and have a reference, your application can be paid and sent to UCAS. For 2027 entry, the application fee is £34.50 for up to five choices.
Uploading supportive documents is optional - it does not replace the need to complete and answer questions in the UCAS application. It is up to you to decide if you would like to upload documents to your application; if you decide not to, it won't disadvantage your application.
Make sure your email address is always up-to-date – so you don’t miss important updates on your application. You can change your email address at any time, but only your primary email address will receive email communications from UCAS and your chosen universities and colleges.
We’ll ask for your residency status – if you’re from outside the UK and need clarification on this, visit the UKCISA website for detailed information.
