Eligibility Freshmen Admissions International applicants whose parents are both non-Korean nationals must meet all of the following requirements: He or she must have completed his or her entire elementary and junior high school education and He or she must have completed (or be about to complete) high school education at a comparable and equivalent level to that which is provided in Korea. Applicants of Korean origin must have completed his or her entire elementary and junior high school education and must have completed (or be about to complete) his or her high school education outside Korea at a comparable and equivalent level to that which is provided in Korea. NOTE International applicants graduating from vocational schools are not eligible for enrollment. However, such applicants can be selected through additional screening if they are from vocational schools which have signed an agreement with KNU. Eligible Nationality status must be established before the due date of application. Multi-nationals are not eligible. Transfer Admissions (third grade) International applicants whose parents are both non-Korean nationals must meet either of the following requirements: Graduated or about to graduate from a 4-year university, or; Graduated or about to graduate from a 2 or 3-year accredited college or completed at least two years of a 4-year university program (with over 70 credit hours completed) Applicants of Korean origin who have completed their entire elementary school, junior high and high school education outside Korea must meet either of the following requirements: Graduated or about to graduate from a 4-year university outside Korea, or; Graduated or about to graduate from a 2 or 3-year accredited college or completed at least two years of a 4-year university program outside Korea (with over 70 credit hours completed) NOTE Applicants who have completed two years at a 3-year university are not eligible. Applicants who graduated from a university which does not have a Credit system are not eligible. Eligible Nationality status must be established before the due date of application. Multi-nationals are not eligible. Important Notes for Applicants All documents should be in Korean or English. Documents in another language should be accompanied by a notarized Korean or English translation. If any required documents are found to be false or counterfeit, your application will be canceled. Any applicant submitting a certificate of expected graduation must submit the original copy of the graduation certificate before beginning their first semester at KNU. The Office of International Affairs is not responsible for application cancellation due to incorrect contact information (address and phone number etc.) If an applicant's visa is not issued or is rejected, his or her application will be canceled. Applicants receiving a scholarship more than US$18,000 from another institution are not required to submit a certificate of financial guarantee; however, they must submit a certificate/letter of confirmation from the institution that will pay their tuition during their period of study at KNU. No submitted documents or application fees will be returned to the applicant. (Original copies of certificates of graduation will be returned after the screening process is completed.) Please complete your application in advance to avoid a system overload on the last day of the application period, Foreign students who are admitted to the university or enrolled at KNU must have Korean health insurance or health insurance from an insurance provider in their own country.
