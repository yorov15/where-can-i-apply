Studies are offered in Uzbek, Russian, and English. Applicants can choose from Bachelor's programs, evening studies, and a second degree (second specialization). The university provides scholarships/grants, hosts international students, and offers courses delivered by international faculty members. In partnership with leading universities in South Korea and Europe, the university runs joint programs ( 2+2, 3+1 ) as well as exchange programs. Students gain study experience across Europe and Asia. Graduates benefit from internship and employment opportunities. " Kimyo International University in Tashkent " maintains partnerships with leading universities in South Korea, Belgium, Malaysia, the United Kingdom, the USA, Latvia, Finland, Bulgaria, Poland, Italy, Japan, and Kazakhstan. Applications can be submitted offline or online ( admission.kiut.uz ). Registration requirements Applications for the 2026/2027 academic year are accepted until the official deadline, both offline and online. Required documents (electronic copies): Passport (color copy); Lyceum/college diploma or school certificate (color copy); Photo 3.5 × 4.5 cm (taken within the last 3 months); If available, language certificates: IELTS 5.5, CEFR B1, Duolingo 85, TOEFL 58, TOPIK 3, TOMER and above.
