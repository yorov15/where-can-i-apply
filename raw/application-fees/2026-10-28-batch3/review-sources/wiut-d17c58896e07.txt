VACANCIES HOW TO APPLY CERTIFICATE IN INTERNATIONAL FOUNDATION STUDIES APPLICATION DEADLINES Application Deadline for CIFS: 15 July, 2026 Deadline for bringing requested documents (hard copy): 12 August, 2026 *We recommend giving yourself plenty of time to complete your application before the application deadline. STEPS TO APPLY STEP 1 Choose a course Explore our Foundation course and ensure it is the right pathway for your academic goals. STEP 2 Check our Entry Requirements Review the academic and English language requirements before starting your application. STEP 3 Set Up Your Account and Submit Your Application Create your account on admission.wiut.uz in just a few minutes and complete your online application. IMPORTANT: The WIUT Admission System is the only official channel for managing your application. All formal communication regarding your application will be sent to the personal email address provided during registration. Any formal enquiries should be sent to admission@wiut.uz. STEP 4 Upload Required Documents Upload all required documents, including a passport-sized photograph, passport copy, and document confirming your secondary education. On admission.wiut.uz you will also find our Admissions Policy, Terms and Conditions, and detailed guidance on how to complete each section of the application. STEP 5 Book and Pay for the Mathematics Entrance Examination Select your preferred date for the computer-based Mathematics Entrance Examination and pay the examination fee of UZS 300,000 . STEP 6 Take the Entrance Examination Attend the Mathematics Entrance Examination on your selected date. A score of 50 or above is considered a passing score. Applicants who do not hold an IELTS certificate at the time of the examination, or those wishing to apply using an SAT certificate, may submit the relevant documents by 15 July 2026 . Please note that SAT scores will not be considered for scholarship eligibility . STEP 7 Complete Your Application Ensure all required documents have been uploaded by 15 July 2026 so that your application can be reviewed by the Admissions Committee. For assistance or additional information, please please visit our FAQs section. STEP 8 Wait for a Decision Once you have successfully passed the Mathematics Entrance Examination and submitted all required documents, the Admissions Committee will review your application. Successful applicants will receive an Acceptance Letter and further enrolment instructions. Typically, complete applications are reviewed within 14-20 days . Please note that applications that remain incomplete after the deadline will not be rejected immediately. They will be considered after all complete applications have been reviewed. The University may, at its sole discretion, request additional evidence of authenticity - including, but not limited to, an apostille, official attestation, or direct verification from the issuing institution or authority. Failure to provide such verification upon request may result in the rejection or cancellation of an application, withdrawal of an offer of admission, or other administrative actions deemed appropriate by the University. All applicants are responsible for ensuring that the documents they submit are genuine, valid, and lawfully obtained. Submission of forged, altered, or misleading documents constitutes a serious violation of university policy and may be subject to legal action in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
