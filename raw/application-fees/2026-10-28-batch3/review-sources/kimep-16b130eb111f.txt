Digital photo Uploading your digital photo into the "Photo" field is very important, because all KIMEP units will use this picture of yours for your personal identification. FILL IN THE "INTERVIEW" SECTION AT THE APPLICANTS' PORTAL After your online application will be received by our Student Recruitment and Admissions Office staff a personal Student ID number will be assigned to you and the block of information titled "Interview" which is mandatory to be filled in will be opened. In this section several questions will be offered which you will have to answer in writing in English. Each answer should contain no more than 300 words. Criminal record (according to the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Migration) STEP 4.
