Step 1: Fill out the Application Form Follow the announcements of the International Office and complete the online application form fully. Step 2: Check the Acceptance Letter If your application is accepted, your acceptance letter will be sent to your email address. Step 3: Prepare the Required Documents You can access the list of documents in the International Undergraduate Registration Guide on the website. Step 4: Complete your final registration in person Come to the Başakşehir Campus on the specified dates and complete your final registration process. Step 5: Check your university email regularly.
