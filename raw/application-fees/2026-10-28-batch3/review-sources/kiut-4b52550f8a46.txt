Admission to Fee-Paying Programs Admission to fee-paying programs at Kimyo International University in Tashkent is available to applicants who meet the minimum admission requirements established by the University. Each academic year, the University determines admission quotas and tuition fees for programs offered on a fee-paying basis. Admission Scholarships for International Students Up to a 50% tuition fee waiver for the first academic year. Based on the results of document screening and an admission interview, successful candidates may receive an admission scholarship. Students who receive the scholarship may maintain it in the following academic years by demonstrating strong academic performance and meeting the University's scholarship renewal requirements. The scholarship aims to support talented and motivated international students and to encourage academic excellence throughout their studies at KIUT. The Application Process for International Students 1 Online application Submit your application through the official admission page of the relevant KIUT campus: Tashkent campus Samarkand campus Namangan campus 2 Document screening The Admissions Committee reviews the submitted documents and checks whether the applicant meets the minimum admission requirements for the selected program. 3 Interview For international students, admission is based on an interview only. Eligible applicants are invited to an online or face-to-face interview with the Admissions Committee. 4 Scholarship consideration Applicants may be considered for an admission scholarship of up to a 50% tuition fee waiver for the first academic year, based on document screening and interview results. The scholarship may be maintained in the following years given strong academic performance. 5 Final results Successful applicants are notified by the Admissions Office and receive further instructions regarding enrollment, tuition payment, visa support, and the start of studies at the selected KIUT campus. Supporting Documents International applicants are required to submit the following supporting documents: Passport copy Copy of high school, lyceum, college, undergraduate, or graduate diploma, depending on the level of study Recent electronic photo Foreign language proficiency certificate, if available Documents issued in a foreign language should be submitted with a notarized translation into English or Russian.
