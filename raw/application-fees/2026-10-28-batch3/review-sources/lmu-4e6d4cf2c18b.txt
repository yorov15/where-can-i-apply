I have two nationalities. Which should I use when I apply / register? If you have German nationality and a passport from another country as well, please always apply with your German nationality. The same applies if you are in possession of an EU/EEA passport and a non-EU/EEA passport; here you should always apply with your EU/EEA passport. For more information on the question "What role does my nationality play when I apply for a place at university?", please refer to the information here . 2. Do I have to make an appointment if I need support from International Admissions? No. Please refer to the International Admissions opening hours . 3. What sort of a resume / CV do I have to submit when I apply to the International Office? Your resume (curriculum vitae) should be written in tabular form. It should include your personal details, your education to date (all schools / universities / degree programs you have attended, with the respective dates), university entrance examinations, qualification and language skills / and language examinations. Please make sure you enter your details in full and in chronological order, accounting for all the time up to when you submit your application. Sample resume (PDF, 135 KB) 4. Does LMU offer German language courses for which I can register once I am a student? We don't, no. However, in our information leaflets, we list a number of language courses for you to refer to. To remain impartial, we do not make any specific recommendations. The following service providers are recommended for German courses by the Beirat Qualitätssicherung für studienvorbereitende und studienbegleitende Deutschausbildung an der LMU - the council repsonsible for quality control. I have completed a degree in German Studies at a university abroad. Do I still have to sit the German language test? Yes. Even if you have completed a degree in German Studies at a university abroad, you still have to sit the German language test . 6. I have received an official notification from the Zeugnisanerkennungsstelle (accreditation office), do I still have to get my documents and certificates translated? Yes. You must always submit your certificates in the form required in your admission application. 7. Does the University offer an MBA? Not at present. Please refer to the Business Studies Faculty website to see which master's degree programs are currently being offered in the field of Business Studies . 8. If I want to do a bachelor's degree program at LMU such as English Literature / English Linguistics (both of which are taught in English), do I still need to prove that I am proficient in German when I apply? No matter which bachelor's degree program you wish to pursue, you will always need to furnish evidence of proficiency in German when you apply. 9. There is a registration date written on my letter of admission. Unfortunately, I am unable to register during the prescribed period. What should I do? Please contact International Admissions . If you wish to register outside of the prescribed period, you will need special permission. 10. I have registered at LMU, and I have paid my semester fee on time. Should I have received the validation of my LMUCard for the upcoming semester? Please contact the Office of the University Registrar, Sub-Unit 2 . For the opening hours and telephone number, please see the Office of the University Registrar . 11. I have received a notification of rejection, and would like my documents / certificates returned to me. What should I do? No problem. Three months after the deadline for applications, you can collect your documents. Please contact us via email (room G 018, Ludwigstraße 27, Munich, ground floor) and provide us with your postal address: zulassung.international@lmu.de . In the event of rejection, and in accordance with data protection regulations, we will destroy all documents pertaining to the application after 12 month. We do allow you to authorize another person to collect your documents: Refer to authorization (PDF, 81 KB) . 12. Is it possible to apply for more than one restricted admission program? Unfortunately, not. International applicants can apply for two programs, but only one restricted admission program . There is an exception: If you apply for a major and a minor in a bachelor's degree program, it is possible to choose a restricted major and a restricted minor. 13. Is it possible to apply for more than one non-restricted admission program? If you are applying for a non-restricted admission program (without numerus clausus) at the International Office, please send us only one application and wait until you have received a letter of admission for this application. If you would like to choose a different non-restricted admission program, the change can be checked during the registration process. 14. Is it possible to receive a letter of confirmation known as a "Hochschulbefähigung" for the Foreigners Office? Unfortunately, it is not possible to send you such a letter. You have to apply for a study program at LMU first. If you receive a letter of admission, you can then contact the Foreigners Office for your residence permit. FAQs: Application status 1. I submitted an application to study at LMU, but have not yet received a letter of admission. When will I hear back to know whether or not I have been accepted? First, please check which address you listed on your application form for admission, and whether this is still valid. We send the letter of admission via email or the rejection notification via post to the address you provide on the application form. For non-restricted degree programs, you can submit documents (leaving certificates etc.) up until 21 August 2026. For restricted admission degree programs, and for Studienkolleg (preparatory courses), the deadline is 15 July 2026. If you have not heard back from us by 31 August 2026, either with a letter of admission or notification of rejection, please send us an email. In the subject line, write "No letter received - please check", include your name, date of birth and desired degree program, and send to zulassung.international@lmu.de . 2. The deadline for the winter semester 2026/27 has ended. Can exceptions be made for submitting an application after the deadline? Yes. If any of the following apply to you, you may submit your completed application form up until 21 August 2026: You have successfully completed the pre-registration process or academic advising and orientation for your desired degree program. You have successfully completed the aptitude assessment for your desired degree program. You have been accepted for a master's degree program. Please note : Applications for doctoral degree programs can be submitted until 21 October 2026. 3. I have received a letter of admission. When and how do I register? You must register at the International Office during the prescribed registration period. The documents must be sent for registration via post to the International Office within the following deadlines: Restricted admission programs: 2 - 10 September 2026 Non-restricted admission programs: 2 September - 1 October 2026 Master's and doctoral degree programs: 2 September - 28 October 2026 4. When I apply for a student visa, how can I prove that I have been offered a place at LMU? With the letter of admission issued by the International Office. This letter is accepted by all embassies as proof when you apply for a student visa. 5. I cannot attend in person during the stated registration period. What should I do? Can I authorize someone to register on my behalf? No. You don't have to come in person. You can send the documents to us. In certain exceptional cases you may be allowed to register at a later date. However, this applies only to bachelor's degree programs which are not subject to admission restrictions. To register later, please send us an email - briefly stating your reasons for late registration - to zulassung.international@lmu.de . Please use the following subject line for your email: "Application for late registration." In your email, state your name, date of birth, registration number, and the degree program for which you have been accepted (these details are included in your letter of admission). Please note that the registration period for master's degree programs and doctoral degree programs is longer (see FAQ 3). 6. I have received a notification of rejection. However, I would definitely like to study at LMU in the winter semester 2026/27. What can I do? The reason for the rejection of your application is stated in the notification you received. If you were rejected because you did not submit all the required documents, you can submit certificates up until 21 August 2026 for non-restricted degree programs. For restricted admission programs and for Studienkolleg (preparatory course), the deadline is 15 July 2026. Once you have submitted all the necessary documents which entitle you to study at LMU, we will immediately send you a letter of admission. 7. I have received a final notification of rejection. What happens now to the documents and certificates I submitted? Do I get them back? Yes. Three months after the deadline for applications, you can collect your documents. Please contact us via email and provide us your postal address here: zulassung.international@lmu.de . In the event of your being rejected and in accordance with data protection regulations, we will destroy all documents pertaining to your application after 12 months. It is possible to authorize another person to collect your documents: authorization (PDF, 81 KB) 8. How and where can I find out more about studying at LMU? Do you host information sessions? Yes. When you register you will be given more information on introductory courses (Online Pre-Arrival Session and Welcome Day, held in German and in English). Here you can view the Welcome Information of the International Office . 9. I still have questions. Who can assist me? If you have further questions, please send an email with your question(s) to zulassung.international@lmu.de and confirm that you have read the FAQs above. If you wish to contact us by telephone, the contact and opening hours for International Admissions in the International Office are available via the link. 10. What is the application period for the next semester? The International Office application period is as follows: for the summer semester it is from November until 15 January; for the winter semester it is from May until 15 July - please do not send any applications outside of this period because your application will not be processed before and this will render it out of date.
