YEDİTEPE ÜNİVERSITY INTERNATIONAL STUDENT ADMISSION GUIDELINES SECTION ONE Purpose, Scope, Basis, and Definitions Purpose and Scope ARTICLE (1) - The purpose and scope of this guideline are to determine the procedures and principles related to the admission of international students to the associate and undergraduate programs of Yeditepe University. Basis ARTICLE (2) - This guideline is prepared based on Article 14 of the Higher Education Law No. 2547 and the "Principles Regarding the Admission of Students from Abroad" as determined by the Council of Higher Education. Definitions ARTICLE (3) - In this guideline, the following terms shall mean: (a) Board of Trustees: The Board of Trustees of Yeditepe University, (b) Rectorate: The Rectorate of Yeditepe University, (c) Senate: The Senate of Yeditepe University, (d) University: Yeditepe University, (e) International Full-Time Enrollment Office: The International Admissions Office of Yeditepe University, (f) Directorate of Student Registration Affairs: The Directorate of Student Registration Affairs of Yeditepe University, (g) Preparatory School: The Coordinations of Preparatory Schools of Yeditepe University. SECTION TWO Quotas ARTICLE (4) - The quotas for the admission of international students to Yeditepe University are determined annually by the University Senate and reported to the Council of Higher Education. If there are no applications for the allocated quotas or the quotas are not filled, the remaining quotas for programs other than Law, Medicine, Dentistry, and Pharmacy can be transferred to other program quotas by Senate decision, followi ng the "Principles Regarding the Admission of Students from Abroad" determined by the Council of Higher Education. For Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, and Law programs, quota transfers can be made by the decision of the Executive Board of Higher Education. Required Exam Types, Minimum Scores, and Diplomas for Application ARTICLE (5) - Candidates who possess the exam types or diploma grades specified in Appendix (1), either national or international, can apply. New exams and admission criteria can be added by a Senate decision. There is no validity period limitation for exams recognized as high school completion exams (e.g., Abitur, International Baccalaureate, GCE, Tawjihi). The validity period for university entrance exams (e.g., TR -YÖS, SAT) is two years. Candidates wishing to apply to programs that require a Special Talent Exam must t ake the Special Talent Exam on the dates specified in the academic calendar. Required Documents for Application Article (6) - (1) Applications are made electronically through the application page during the announced application dates, along with the required documents listed below. (2) If the application documents are in languages other than Turkish or English, certified translations in Turkish or English must also be provided in addition to copies of the originals. (a) International student application form (b) High School Diploma (c) Transcript showing all courses and grades obtained during high school (d) English language proficiency document (TOEFL IBT) or the exam result document accepted by Yeditepe University's English Preparatory School (e) Photocopy of the passport page with the photo and identity information (f) Letter of intent. Evaluation and Announcement of Applications ARTICLE (7) - The evaluations and admissions of international student applications occur within the periods specified in the academic calendar. The evaluations of candidates by the International Student Admission Committee consider exam scores, overall grade averages in the transcripts, references, and letters of intent, within the framework of the quotas. Candidates deemed suitable for admission are determined (Appendix 1). The positive evaluation results are pres ented for approval to the responsible Vice Rector. Conditionally accepted candidates are notified via email with a Conditional Acceptance Letter. Tuition Fee ARTICLE (8) - The tuition fees and scholarships to be provided to international students admitted to higher education programs at Yeditepe University are determined annually based on the recommendations of the Rectorate by the Board of Trustees. Final Registration Conditions and Required Documents ARTICLE (9) - Accepted candidates must personally apply for registration at the Directorate of Student Registration Affairs within the specified dates and with the documents announced by the University. During registration, candidates must present the originals of the documents they submitted during the application, the originals of the translated documents, and the additional documents listed below: (a) Original high school diploma or, if not yet issued, the original temporary graduation certificate, (b) Original "Equivalence Certificate" from the Turkish Ministry of National Education, proving that the high school diploma is equivalent to those issued by Turkish high schools, (c) Original acceptance letter, (d) Two biometric photographs, (e) A copy of the official ID or the identity information page of the passport, (f) Online application form, (g) Entry and exit document of the country, (h) Learning agreement located in Appendix 2, (i) Registrations cannot be made with incomplete documents or by mail. Application Process, Guidance and Assistance ARTICLE (10) - The Full-time International Student Registration Office is responsible for promoting the University's academic programs to prospective international students; assisting applicants with program selection and the admissions process; providing guidance on visa applications, credential evaluation, and related matters; developing and distributing informational materials; representing the University at international education fairs and events and facilitating the residence permit application process for enrolled students. The Office oversees these activities to support international student recruitment and retention. SECTION THREE Miscellaneous and Final Provisions Cases Not Covered by the Guidelines ARTICLE (11) - In cases not covered by these Guidelines, the principles contained in the Higher Education Council and Yeditepe University legislation shall apply. Effectiveness ARTICLE (12) - This guideline shall enter into force on the date it is approved by the Senate of Yeditepe University. Execution ARTICLE (2) - This guideline shall be executed by the Rector of Yeditepe University. Appendix 2: International Student Learning Agreement Educational Principles Subject: The subject of these principles is the regulation of the terms related to the education to be provided to the student by the University and the service fee to be paid to the university in return. 1) These principles are valid for the entire duration of the study. Each academic year, students must renew their registration within the dates announced by the University. 2) Registration at the University, except for scholarship students and those who pay upfront, is contingent upon the payment of the tuition fee deposit and the signing of the payment plan arranged by the University for the remainder. 3) Students wishing to transfer to another university must pay the fee for the Fall semester if the transfer occurs after the beginning of the Fall semester and the entire annual fee if the transfer occurs after the Spring semester starts. There are no r efunds or cancellations of debt. 4) Students whose study (registration) suspension is accepted by the Academic Units are charged the full tuition fe e specified in these principles . The fee collected for the semester/year of the suspension is not refunded, but is offset against the fee for the next semester/year. 5) Changes in class/department/scholarship status are deemed accepted by the student and reflected in the fees/installments. 6) Students who do not complete their normal study period on time pay the course fee for up to two courses; if taking three or more courses, they pay the fee for the relevant term. 7) All scholarships continue throughout the study period provided the student commits no disciplinary offenses. A student who commits a disciplinary offense loses their scholarship rights and is obligated to pay the fee for the program they are enrolled in from the date of the offense. 8) Scholarships granted to students are merit -based. Students lose their scholarship rights if their GPA falls below 2.00 . The scholarship is reinstated at the end of the academic year, in which the student raises their GPA to 2.00 or above. 9) If a student fails to pay their debt to the University or does not comply with the terms written in the Credit Deposit Account Protocol (CDAP -TR:KMHP) with the relevant bank, their course registration will not be made. 10) Tuition fees are set by the University, and students are responsible for staying informed about any changes. It is essential for students to actively monitor for updates regarding tuition fees, as these are subject to adjustments by the University based on financial policies and other institutional considerations. 10. Madde bire bir çeviri: Tuition fees are determined by the University, and changes should be followed by the student. . 11) Tuition fees must be paid into the Deposit Account of a bank contracted with the University.
