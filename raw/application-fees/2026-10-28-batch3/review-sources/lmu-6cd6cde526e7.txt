Who is responsible for my application? Read more If you fulfill all the prerequisites for studying at LMU Munich, you can start the application process. International applicants (without German nationality) who wish to study at LMU will generally need to submit their application to the International Office. However, in certain cases they will need to apply to the Office of the University Registrar ( Studentenkanzlei ) or the Foundation for University Admission ( Stiftung für Hochschulzulassung ). So, please first find out where you need to apply ! If you want to study a subject for which you have to go through an aptitude assessment, you will be required to apply additionally in time via the respective institute . You can find out the registration dates at the respective institute. Please check the list of subjects to see if you want to choose such a subject. Preliminary examination documentation (VPD) Would you like to apply for an undergraduate degree programme (Bachelor's/State Examination) ? From the 2027 summer semester onwards, we will be working with uni-assist. You can register with uni-assist regarding the preliminary examination documentation (VPD). Please apply for the VPD well in advance, as processing times at uni-assist may be lengthy (8 weeks). Once issued, the VPD is valid for one year.You can then use your VPD to apply directly to LMU for your chosen degree programme. More information: Who does need a VPD? Applications to the International Office If the International Office is responsible for processing your application, you have to apply for admission. To do so, complete the form and submit it before the stipulated deadline together with all the documents required. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us: International Admissions opening hours Deadlines for submission 15 January for the summer semester 15 July for the winter semester (Deadline - receipt by LMU) Please note LMU is redesigning the application and enrolment process from the summer semester of 2027 onwards. From December 2026, you will find further information on applying on this website. Until then, unfortunately, you cannot submit applications for the summer semester 2027.
