The semester fee (and if applicable tuition fee) is due as part of initial enrollment and in the course of your studies to re-register for each semester. Please make sure to review the exact amount applicable to you as well as your individual payment reference in your heiCO account under "Semester/tuition fees" or in the next steps section of your application under the link "Tuition fee". In addition to regular tuition fees, there are additional fees for international students (excluding EU or EEA citizens) as well as for students pursuing a second degree or a professional master's program. To ensure that financial barriers do not prevent you from completing your studies, there are many options for financing your education such as the BAföG, scholarships, and part-time jobs.
