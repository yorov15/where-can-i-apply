Reference Only) IELTS An overall band score of 6.5 or better in IELTS Academic Module, achieved in the same attempt, is required. The test must be taken within two years prior to September 1, 2026. Note: The IELTS Indicator (home edition) / IELTS Online is not accepted to fulfill the English language requirement. TOEFL A score of 93 or better in the TOEFL internet-based test (TOEFL iBT) is required, achieved in the same attempt. The test must be taken within 2 years prior to September 1, 2026. Note: The TOEFL ITP, TOEFL ITP Plus and TOEFL Home Edition tests are not accepted to fulfill the English language requirement. (d) Grade 4 in SL English - Literature and Performance (A1 syllabus) (e) Grade 4 in HL English Language (B Syllabus); (f) Grade 5 in SL English Language (B Syllabus); or (g) Grade 4 in HL / SL English Language (A1 or A2 Syllabus) Note: "Higher Level" is referred to as "HL", and "Standard Level" as "SL" GCE (a) Grade C or better in English Language at GCSE/ GCEO;
