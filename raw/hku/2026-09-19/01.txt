IELTS An overall band score of 6.5 or above in the IELTS Academic Module, with no less than 6.0 in both the Writing and Speaking components, achieved in the same attempt. The test must be taken within 2 years prior to start of your programme (September 1). Note: The IELTS Indicator (home edition) / IELTS Online is not accepted to fulfill the English language requirement. TOEFL For tests taken on or after January 21, 2026: An overall score of 5.0 or above in the TOEFL internet-based test (TOEFL iBT), with no less than 5.0 in both the Writing and Speaking components, achieved in the same attempt. For tests taken before January 21, 2026: An overall score of 95 or above in the TOEFL internet-based test (TOEFL iBT), with no less than 5.0 in both the Writing and Speaking components, achieved in the same attempt, under the legacy TOEFL iBT scoring scale. The test must be taken within 2 years prior to start of your programme (September 1). Note: The TOEFL ITP, TOEFL ITP Plus and TOEFL Home Edition tests are not accepted to fulfill the English language requirement. International Baccalaureate (a) Grade 5 in English Language A (HL/SL); (b) Grade 6 in English Language B (HL/SL) Note: "Higher Level" is abbreviated as "HL", and "Standard Level" as "SL." GCE (a) Grade B or Grade 6 or above in English Language at GCSE or GCE O Level; (b) Grade B or Grade 6 or above in English First Language with Speaking and Listening component at IGCSE; or (c) Grade C or above in English Language or English Literature at GCE AS Level or A Level Note: General Paper is not accepted. SAT Section score of 610 or above in the Reading and Writing section of the SAT, achieved in the same attempt.
