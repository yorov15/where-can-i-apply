Application Dates English Proficiency Required Documents Application Requirements Undergraduate Programs for International Students Share Tweet Last Updated: 26/02/2026 - 08:04 English Türkçe Listen Application Period for the 2026-2027 Academic Year: June 1-July 12, 2026 International student applications for undergraduate programs offered at METU Ankara campus for the 2026-2027 academic year will be received between June 1 and July 12, 2026. (Application program will open at 16:00 on the 1st of June and it will close at 23:59 on the 12th of July 2026, Turkish time) Please see our 5-step application guide for the details of the application: https://iso.metu.edu.tr/en/5-step-application-undergraduate-programs-met... * Attention to the international A-level applicants: Due to the incompatibility between the METU application calendar and the announcement date of the 2026 A-level results, students who will use their A-level results in the application will first receive "Admission deferral" status for their applications. Upon the submission of the final A-level scores in August 2026, the evaluation of your application will be finalized. The important point here is to submit and finalize the online application between June 1 and July 12, 2026, even if you do not yet have your final A-level results.
