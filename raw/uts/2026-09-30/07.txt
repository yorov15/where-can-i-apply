You're a few simple steps away from joining a global top 100 university and your new home away from home. In the heart of Sydney, creativity meets technology and we can't wait for you to join our diverse community of students from over 140 nationalities. This page will guide you through how to accept your offer as an international student and what to expect next. Your future awaits, are you ready? Before you accept The information on this page is for international students who have received an unconditional offer from UTS or the Universities Admissions Centre (UAC). If you are a domestic student (Australian citizen), please visit the Domestic students page to accept your offer If you applied through a UTS-approved education agent, your agent will support you with accepting your offer. How to accept Use the buttons to expand or collapse the section. Step 1: Review and sign your acceptance form To accept your offer, you will need to log in to the UTS Application Portal with your application credentials. If you've received an offer, your application status will show 'Offer'. Your offer letter includes: Your course details and conditions Estimated tuition fees and living costs Your acceptance form Take your time to carefully read your offer letter - it includes important information about your course, fees and estimated living costs. When ready, sign the acceptance form. Your signature must match the one on your passport. When you accept your offer online, you'll need to pay a tuition deposit to guarantee your place and receive an Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (eCoE). The eCoE is required to apply your visa. You must accept your offer by the deadline stated in your offer details, or you will lose your place in the program. If you need to update your start date, you can defer your offer - more information on this can be found further down the page. If you applied via a UTS representative, contact your agent to manage acceptance on your behalf. If you are under 18, special conditions apply to ensure your welfare. If you don't want to accept your offer online, you can drop off your acceptance documents at the International Student Centre on campus or send the documents to our mailing address . Step 2: Accept your offer and submit documents If you applied directly to UTS (without an agent), select "Accept Offer" on the Respond to Offer page in the application portal. Then, you will need to upload the following documents: Completed and signed acceptance form Proof of payment (ie Flywire payment ID, copy of bank draft or bank cheque) Copy of your passport ID page (if not provided already) Scholarship Acceptance Form, if applicable Financial Guarantee Letter, if you are sponsored by your government or employer Under 18 Welfare Arrangement form (if applicable) Once all documents are uploaded, you can proceed to payment. If you applied via a UTS education agent, send your acceptance documents to your agent, who will submit them on your behalf. Step 3: Pay your tuition fees To secure your place at UTS, we ask you to pay a deposit using one of the approved methods: Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) via the third party provider Flywire . Bank draft or bank cheque drawn on a bank in Australia and made payable to the 'University of Technology Sydney'. If you make a payment using this method, please post the original bank draft/cheque with your full name and student number clearly written on the back to: Manager, International Admissions Future Learner Recruitment and Admissions University of Technology Sydney PO Box 123 Broadway, NSW 2007 AUSTRALIA BPAY - only available to students previously enrolled at UTS. If you're a sponsored student (by your government or employer), payment of a deposit is not required. However, your Sponsor must provide a valid financial guarantee that covers the sponsorship/scholarship period along with the permission to release information form. Your sponsor must issue a valid financial guarantee on official letterhead that includes: Full name as per your passport Date of birth as per your passport Institution name (University of Technology Sydney) Course name and course CRICOS code Deferring your offer We understand life is busy and plans can change. If you are no longer able to start your degree for the intake specified on your offer letter, you can request to update your commencement date at UTS for up to 12 months. If you wish to defer, you must apply in writing before the deadline in your letter of offer. If you applied through an agent, please contact your agent to arrange deferment. If you applied directly, contact UTS. Update your intake arrow_forward Contact us If you have questions about your offer or enrolment, please call UTS at +61 2 9514 1531, email reps@uts.edu.au , or visit us on campus. What happens next? Once your acceptance and payment are received, UTS will prepare you for the next steps in your journey - including your visa application and information about enrolment (ie how to choose your classes). In the meantime, find out more about moving to Sydney, how to find a place to live, transport options, and the best ways to make new friends.
