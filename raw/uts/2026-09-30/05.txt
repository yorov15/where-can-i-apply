Did you know that the majority of scholarships don't require an application? At UTS, you will be automatically assessed and offered a scholarship if you meet the eligibility criteria and unless otherwise stated. Read transcript close Close transcript Studying a Master of IT in Australia on a scholarship | advice for international students transcript My name is Tlaitirang Sekgobela Rathete. I'm studying Masters of Information Technology. I received the Postgraduate Academic Excellence Scholarship. What drew me towards UTS was that I really loved the weather back home from South Africa and Australia has the best weather comparison. And then mixing it with it being the best technology university in Australia was just a straightforward choice. Back at home when I did my bachelor's in computer science, a lot of the curriculum was quite outdated while over here I can see that they're a bit ahead of the curve. They even have a course I'm taking currently, which is RS development, which is Apple coded with SwiftUI-something that I don't even know if way back home they're going to start implementing such techniques. My scholarship supported me immensely because it really reduced the financial burden from back home with my family. It allowed me to be able to come in and experience this amazing opportunity and be able to actually study here and survive the cost of living. My biggest advice I'd give to international students, is to write down all the academic achievements, whether it be volunteer work, high school marks or previous achievements that they got through their career. By actually portraying your best self and showing everything you've done, all of these contribute towards you actually having that chance to get a scholarship. Read transcript close Close transcript Studying computer science in Australia on a scholarship | advice for international students transcript My name is Phuong Ngan, I'm studying Bachelor of Computing Science Honours. And I received the UTS GCE A level and IB Scholarship. I've been so curious about how computers and programs work. I want to immerse myself in the technology. So that's why I chose UTS. In my home country everything was quite theory-based, but here I'm getting engaged with the industry stuff day by day. My favourite spot on campus would be the Alumni Green where I can just put myself down the sun and get some fresh air. And after class, my friends and I usually grab lunch and just have a chat on the grass. UTS offers a number of scholarships, and if you're interested in applying for the A level and IB scholarship, make sure to be prepared first and also make sure to mark the deadline. And thirdly, my advice would be just put all of yourself in the application. So any volunteering or any work experience, just put yourself in there.
