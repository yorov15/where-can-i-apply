Scholarships at UTS Scholarships Search UTS Academic Excellence International Scholarship UTS Academic Excellence International Scholarship UTS Academic Excellence International Scholarship VALUE 30% of UTS Tuition Fees Application Open 01/01/2026 DURATION The standard number of credit points required for the course. STATUS Open Overview UTS Academic Excellence International Scholarship was established to attract high performing commencing international students who wish to undertake an undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program at UTS in Sydney. Who is eligible To be eligible for the Scholarship, an applicant must meet all of the following criteria: Remain an international student (not an Australian citizen, New Zealand citizen or permanent resident of Australia); and Meet all admission requirements for the selected UTS undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program; and For undergraduate coursework admission, completed an Australian Year 12 or UTS recognised high school studies comparable to an Australian Year 12 or an Australian Foundation Studies program, and be admitted to UTS based on this qualification; or For Honours course admission, completed a UTS recognised bachelor degree and be admitted to UTS based on this qualification; or For postgraduate coursework admission, the most recently completed and UTS recognised tertiary qualification at an Australian Bachelor degree level or higher or the Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT Exam) or the Graduate Record Examination (GRE General Test) (both tests accepted for admission into UTS Business School programs only), and be admitted to UTS based on this qualification; and Commence full-time and on campus (Sydney) [1] ; and Must not: Be studying a UTS online degree or distance degree; or Complete an approved external articulation or pathway program from an institution outside of Australia; or Be a recipient of other UTS scholarships or grants or the UTS Alumni Advantage Program for the duration of this Scholarship.. [1] On campus studies include face-to-face and/or online (remote) learning in all degrees except online degrees and distance mode degrees. Selection process For undergraduate coursework admission, academic merit in the Australian Year 12 or UTS recognised high school studies comparable to an Australian Year 12 or an Australian Foundation Studies for entry into the selected UTS Bachelor degree program, or a UTS recognised bachelor's degree for entry into the selected UTS Honours program. For postgraduate coursework admission, academic merit in the most recently completed tertiary qualification at an Australian Bachelor degree level or higher or the Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT Exam) or Graduate Record Examination (GRE General Test) (both tests accepted for admission into UTS Business School programs only) recognised by UTS for entry into the selected UTS postgraduate coursework program. Other information Applicable to international students applying to commence in intakes from 2025 - 2028. How to apply UTS Future Learner Recruitment and Admissions will automatically consider all commencing international students applying for an undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program at UTS in Sydney for this Scholarship. There is no separate scholarship application or documentation in addition to what is required for the course application. Offers will be authorised by UTS Future Learner Recruitment and Admissions.
