Why UTS? Study in the heart of Sydney's technology precinct at Australia's #1 ranked young university. Our courses are highly-regarded, developed in partnership with industry leaders using practice-based learning approaches. No matter what path you choose, you'll learn the skills to become an innovative and creative thinker, driving positive change in society. trophy TOP-RANKING #7 in Australia Recognised in Australia for excellence in teaching, research impact, industry engagement and international outlook. Times Higher Education University Rankings 2026 workspace_premium INTERNATIONALLY RECOGNISED Top 100 worldwide Ranked #87 globally, UTS stands among the world's leading universities, recognised for academic excellence and industry impact. QS World University Rankings 2027 payments GRADUATE OUTCOMES 93.3% employed Measured three years post-graduation, reflecting strong career readiness and employer demand for UTS graduates. 2024 Graduate Outcomes Survey - Longitudinal play_arrow Read transcript close Close transcript At the University of Technology Sydney, we live and breathe technology, innovation and creativity - and you will too! It's all around us. Business. Engineering. Health. Science. Design. Education. Expand your horizons with students from around the world. Study in the heart of Tech Central, Sydney's creative and cultural precinct - surrounded by over 3000 creative industries and close to the CBD, Darling Harbour, nightlife and beaches. Meet new friends, mentors, industry partners, and experience hands-on learning among people who will support you throughout your studies. When you become a UTS graduate, you'll be ready to deliver real solutions to the challenges of now and the future. At UTS, you come first. No matter what you choose to learn, we'll be with you every step of the way. Be creative. Dream big. UTS. Life in Sydney Ranked one of the world's most liveable cities, Sydney is a vibrant, safe and multicultural metropolis with plenty of jobs on offer. UTS is located near Central Station, in the heart of Sydney's creative and innovation precinct home to tech giants, frontier start-ups and a diverse UTS community from over 180 different nationalities. Learn more arrow_forward Contact us arrow_forward Hear from our graduates Jose Rodriguez had already made his mark in Colombia, working with big multinational brands in his early 20s. But when he wanted to push his career further, he realised he needed something more. Encouraged by a friend, he decided to take a leap and apply to UTS Business School. While completing his Master's Degree in Digital Marketing, he found his role as an International Marketing Advisor right here at UTS, thanks to the support and guidance of UTS Careers. Now working at the forefront of innovation, he's using the skills and confidence he built during his time on campus to help global brands innovate and grow. "I love what I'm doing and every day is different," Jose shares. play_arrow In this video, we revisit the UTS campus with Jose as he takes us through his journey from Colombia to Sydney and how UTS helped him achieve his dreams. Read transcript close Close transcript I started my marketing career in Colombia when I was 21, feeling pretty settled in my career. However, it was time for a change, and I wanted to learn more about marketing. So my friend who studied at UTS said, "Hey, Sydney is a great city. Why don't you just apply and see what happens?" So I did, and I got accepted at UTS Business School. My name is Jose Rodriguez, and I've been living and working in Sydney ever since doing my Master's degree in Marketing at UTS. After my first month here, I was like, okay, I love this place - this is where I want to be. So I'm going to do my best to stay. The people around me were the most important thing in helping me to settle into Sydney, and I was lucky enough that I had a very good support system here. I also learned a lot about networking from UTS Careers. I learned so much from the people around me - their different cultures, and their ways of problem-solving. Their passion and their dedication to their work was really inspiring. Fast forward 5 years, and I'm now the associate director at a market research agency. Now working with the innovation team, using solutions based on concept testing, AI, and neural marketing. I love what I'm doing, and every day is different, and I'm able to use the skills I had learned from my time on campus.
