International fees Financial aid Financial aid explore arrow_forward_ios International fees International fees open icon arrow_forward_ios Financial aid Financial aid open icon arrow_forward_ios arrow_forward US Financial aid arrow_forward Payment of international student tuition fees arrow_forward Protocol on Refund of Fees for International Students arrow_forward Refund Protocol for Transnational Education Courses at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) arrow_forward Tuition fee refunds due to overpayment Are you an international student looking for financial aid options? Some countries, including Denmark, Germany and the US, may financially support their citizens to support their studies in Australia. Find out if you're eligible and who to contact for assistance. If you are from one of the following countries you may be eligible for some form of financial aid to support your studies at the University of Technology Sydney. Canada: Canadian Provincial / Federal Loans (opens an external site) UTS Code is VUSL Denmark: SU (opens an external site) Germany: Bafög (opens an external site) Norway: Lånekassen (opens an external site) Sweden: CSN (opens an external site) United States of America (USA): US Financial Aid There may also be other loan systems that are not listed above. It is advisable to check in your home country. Need some assistance? Within UTS International there are dedicated staff to assist students with the provision of required documentation, advice about eligibility and information about conditions relating to these financial aid programs. Various other countries, not listed above, also provide financial aid to their citizens. International Sponsored Students team The team provides support and assistance to sponsored students from the time of application to arrival in Sydney and throughout their studies at UTS.
