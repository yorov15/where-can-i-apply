For students International students Find an agent in your country / region Find an agent in your country / region explore arrow_forward_ios Find an agent in your country / region UTS works with a global network of approved education agents to support international students through the application and enrolment process. Education agents are trusted advisers in your local country who can help you navigate UTS degrees, check entry requirements, prepare your application, and guide you through the offer acceptance process, visas and next steps. All UTS agents receive regular training and support from UTS, so they have up-to-date knowledge about our courses and processes. To find an agent near you, select your country below. If there isn't an agent in your country, an adviser in a neighbouring country may be able to assist. Alternatively, you can contact UTS directly for support.
