Already studying elsewhere and planning to transfer? Or looking to begin your UTS journey through one of our international partner campuses? No matter your starting point, we offer a range of international pathways designed to help you get there. From UTS College programs to approved offshore articulations and transnational education (TNE) opportunities, you'll find flexible options tailored to your goals. Explore our international pathway programs and take the next step toward UTS today. Find the right fit The right entry option depends on your goals, background, and circumstances. Explore the main pathways below and choose the one that fits your journey. UTS College UTS College, owned by UTS, provides various pathway programs to support a smooth transition into university life. Whether you're looking to strengthen your academic skills, improve your English, or fast-track your studies, UTS College is your way into UTS. Learn more arrow_forward Articulation pathways External articulations allow you to complete part of your degree at one institution and transfer those credits to UTS to complete your degree. By leveraging an articulation pathway, you can reduce both the duration and expense of your course, enabling an earlier start to your career. Learn more arrow_forward Transnational education Dreaming of a world-class Australian education without leaving your home country? UTS brings its globally recognised degrees to your doorstep through our offshore programs in Vietnam and China, delivered in partnership with Asian universities.
