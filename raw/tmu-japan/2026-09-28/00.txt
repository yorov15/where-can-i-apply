International House The International House, located on Minami-Osawa Campus, provides accommodation for researchers and graduate students from overseas. for more information International Student House TMU's International Student House is designed to facilitate mutual and international exchange among residents through shared everyday life by accepting international students and regular students of TMU on the premises. In the House, Resident Assistants (mainly Japanese students) support international students in daily dorm life. for more information International Student Dormitory International Student Dormitory, our off-campus accommodation, provides fulfilling support.The room is furnished and offers a nutritious breakfast and dinner every day. There are resident assistants and live-in dorm directors to guide you to a brand new life in Japan.Sharing facilities are also well-equipped and open to every resident.(This dorm is scheduled to release in October 2026.More details will be available at a later date.)
