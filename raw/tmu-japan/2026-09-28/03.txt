Download Scholarship Application Guidelines 2027 Global Futures Scholarship Brochure2027 ＜SCHOLARSHIP OBJECTIVE＞ This scholarship program is established to attract and support outstanding international students who demonstrate exceptional academic potential. Eligibility is dedicated to applicants of designated undergraduate English-taught degree programs. By providing financial support, we aim to empower these future leaders to focus fully on their academic pursuits, envisioning and creating their own paths at our university. ＜SELECTION POLICY＞ Scholarship recipients are selected based on at least one of the following criteria during the entrance examination process: ・Academic Excellence: Recognizes high-ranking applicants who demonstrate outstanding academic performance in the entrance examination, regardless of nationality. ・Global Diversity: Recognizes talented applicants from university-designated priority regions, aimed at fostering a diverse campus environment. BENEFITS ・Stipend: 100,000 JPY per month (1,200,000 JPY per year) ・Tuition Support: Half-Tuition Reduction ※Based on academic performance each year after enrollment, students may be eligible for an additional stipend of JPY 20,000 per month and may also qualify for full tuition exemption. SCHOLARSHIP PERIOD Standard Duration: 4 years (from April 2027 to March 2031). NUMBER OF RECIPIENTS Up to 10 ELIGIBILITY Applicants must satisfy all of the following requirements： Academic Program and Admission ・Apply for and be determined as a successful applicant to one of the designated undergraduate English-taught degree programs at TMU through the specified entrance examinations ( Click here for details ) Residency and Visa Status ・Be a non-Japanese national residing outside of Japan at the time of application. ・Be able to newly obtain a "Student" visa to enter and reside in Japan upon admission. Regional Priority Consideration (Optional) Refer to the " List of Priority Regions " SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION TIMING At the same time as applying for the designated entrance exam Study Abroad at TMU
