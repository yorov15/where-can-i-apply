Current Students Alumni Admissions TOP Admissions READ MORE Admissions Information NUCB Undergraduate School receives full-time students who can complete a Bachelor's degree program in 4 years. Enrollment is held twice a year in Spring(April) and Autumn(September). There are two tracks available, open to all nationalities: English Track: Global BBA Japanese Track: BBA, BSc, BA Admissions Information READ MORE FAQ READ MORE Student Voice READ MORE STEP 1 Online Application Confirm your eligibility in the application guide, then complete the online application. STEP 2 Payment Please pay the application fee via credit card. STEP 3 Upload your Documents Upload your documents digitally on the online submission portal. STEP 4 Issuing Interview Information Those that are accepted past the document review stage will be invited to interview. STEP 5 Admission Result Notification will be sent via email to all applicants.
