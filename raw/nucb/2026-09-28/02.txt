below. Spring (April) 2027 Entrants: All fees are in Japanese Yen ¥ 4-Year Total Tuition: 4,874,000 JPY (Excludes 270,000 JPY One-Time Entrance Fee) Tuition includes a brand new MacBook Air for non-scholarship recipients as well as your textbooks and club fees. Tuition is paid in two installments per year (Spring/Fall). Students accepted to the program are required to pay the first-semester fee as well as the entrance fee to secure their place. Spring Entrants: 270,000 JPY + 633,500 JPY = 903,500 JPY Fall Entrants: 270,000 JPY + 625,000 JPY = 895,000 JPY Amount correct as of July 2026. The tuition amount may change at the point of application or while you are enrolled due to extenuating circumstances. Payment Period After enrollment, the payment is to be paid twice a year by the following dates. If the delivery date is a holiday, the previous day will be the deadline. March 15 August 24 Installments Fees can be paid in three installments in both the first and second semesters. If you would like to apply, please contact the General Affairs Department within the application period. *Fees Apply *Installments are not possible for enrolment fees.
