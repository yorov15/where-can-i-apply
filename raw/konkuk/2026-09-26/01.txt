The Global Frontier The First Step 2026 Konkuk University is the university that has demonstrated the most prominent growth over the last decade. With projects such as KU SMART Factory, K-CUBE, and X-Space, we spare no resources when it comes to investing in cutting-edge education facilities. We also strive to improve our educational system through various measures, such as flexible school affairs operations and support for employment and startups. While most colleges in Korea transition departments to regions outside of the capital area, Konkuk University's Seoul Campus has created an optimal hub for interdisciplinary and convergent education, gathering various academic fields such as humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, engineering, athletics, and arts all in one place. In the 2024 JoongAng Ilbo University Rankings, Konkuk University won 2nd place in "Universities With the Highest Potential for Progress" and 4th in "High School Students' Most Preferred Universities." These results demonstrate the high interest and preference for our school in the field of education. Nestled in a transportation hub and the cultural center of Millennials and Gen Z, Konkuk University's campus boasts a flatland terrain and a pleasant vista. The school grounds are directly connected to the intersecting subway stations of Lines 2 and 7, offering easy access from all areas across Seoul. For international students aiming to obtain a high TOPIK score, we offer special lectures even after admission, covering intermediate and advanced vocabulary and grammar usage, along with topic-based writing practice and one-on-one feedback to support systematic improvement in writing skills. How is this department different from Media & Communication and Digital Culture & Contents?
