(U) Undergraduate program: Korean Language (up to 1 year) + Degree Program (4 years) (G) Graduate program Master's program: Korean Language (up to 1 year) + Degree Program (2 years) Doctoral program: Korean Language (up to 1 year) + Degree Program (3 years) ELIGIBILITY Citizenship: Applicant and his or her parents must not hold Korean citizenship Age (U) Applicant must be under 25 years of age (G) Applicant must be under 40 years of age Health: Applicant must be in good health, both physically and mentally Level of Education (U) Applicant must have graduated or be expected to graduate from a high school (G) Applicant must hold or be expected to hold a Bachelor's (applying for Master's program) or Master's(applying for Doctoral program) degree Grades: Applicant must have maintained the overall grade point average (CGPA) equivalent to or higher than 2.64 on a 4.0 scale SCHOLARSHIP AND BENEFITS NIIED Airfare Entry to Korea: economy class flight ticket from the scholar's home country Program) 14,100,000 KRW / (Degree) 15,700,000 KRW yearly 1-Year Korean Language Training Fee: fully covered Degree Program Tuition: maximum 5,000,000 KRW per semester (amount exceeding 5,000,000 KRW covered by Konkuk University) Korean Proficiency Grants: notice will provided (scholars with valid TOPIK level 5 or 6) Konkuk University Admissions Fee Degree Program Tuition: amount exceeding 5,000,000 KRW Dormitory On-campus dormitory guaranteed Partial financial support of up to 200,000 KRW per month (about 250,000 KRW per month will be deducted from the monthly allowance) 1:1 Student
