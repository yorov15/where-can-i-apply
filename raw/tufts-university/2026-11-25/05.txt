Applicants that have enrolled in college or university courses for credit should also submit official transcripts from those institutions. Senior Year Grades Counselor/School Official Recommendation Teacher Recommendation from a core academic teacher in English, mathematics, natural or physical science, history or social science, or world language. Applicants to the BFA program may submit a recommendation from a visual art teacher to satisfy this requirement. English Proficiency Test Scores Visual Art Portfolio : Required for applicants to the BFA or BFA+BA/BS Combined Degree programs at SMFA at Tufts. This portfolio is submitted via the Digital Portfolio tab in the online Applicant Status Page, available after an application has been received/processed. $75 USD Application Fee or Fee Waiver: Applicants automatically receive an application fee waiver based on responses to prompts in the application, or may choose from a select set of fee waiver criteria specific to Tufts University in the application. Optional Application Components: Standardized Testing Please also note that applying for financial aid is a separate process, with its own required documents. Applicants can use their Applicant Status Page to check on the completeness of their admissions file. We reserve the right to evaluate an application and make a final decision, even if all parts of the application have not been received.
