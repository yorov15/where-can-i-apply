The Admissions Committee is looking for students with strong academic profiles who will enrich our campus community in myriad ways. At Tufts, we accept the Common Application , the Coalition Application powered by Scoir , and we are a QuestBridge college partner. We have no preference among application options. First-year applicants may apply to Tufts through any of our three admissions rounds: Early Decision I, Early Decision II, and Regular Decision. The Early Decision Program is a binding option for students who have decided that Tufts is their first choice and that they will enroll if accepted. Early Decision deadlines allow the student to apply and be notified well in advance of the Regular Decision notification dates. Application Deadlines Application Type Application Deadline Notification Date Early Decision I November 2, 2026 Mid-December Early Decision II January 4, 2027 Early February Regular Decision January 4, 2027 By April 1 Applicants apply to either the School of Arts and Sciences, the School of Engineering, or the School of the Museum of Fine Arts at Tufts. Alternatively, students may apply to the 5-year BFA + BA/BS Combined Degree program between the School of the Museum of Fine Arts at Tufts and the School of Arts and Sciences. Students are only considered for admission to the School to which they apply; enrolled students may take classes across all three Schools. Submitting the Application All application materials are submitted online via the Common Application, Scoir, or QuestBridge, including high school transcripts and letters of recommendation. Materials that cannot be sent in these methods may be emailed to undergraduate.admissions@tufts.edu or mailed to: Office of Undergraduate Admissions Tufts University Bendetson Hall 2 The Green Medford, MA 02155-7057 Once an application is received and processed, applicants receive access to an online applicant status portal to monitor receipt of each required application credential.
