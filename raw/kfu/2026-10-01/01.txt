Annually, Russian Government allocates 15 000 scholarships for foreign nationals. More information about the application process can be found here. Further information on grants and scholarships is available here: Future in Russia , Rossotrudnichestvo , Russian diplomatic missions or at the Ministry of Education of your country of residence.
