VWO-equivalent qualifications Contact My University Student Portal Nederlands English Contrast modus Bachelor's language requirements and exemptions In order to be eligible for admission you will have to meet the language proficiency requirements applicable to the programme of your choice. Accepted tests We accept the following language tests: Cambridge C1 Advanced and Cambridge C2 Proficiency IELTS Academic LanguageCert Academic Pearson PTE Academic TOEFL iBT We do not accept IELTS Online and IELTS One Skill Retake certificates. TOEFL MyBest Scores are also not taken into consideration. Validity of certificates Keep in mind that IELTS, LanguageCert, Pearson PTE, and TOEFL certificates are only valid for 2 years. Cambridge certificates do not have limited validity. This means we also accept older CAE (now C1 Advanced) and CPE (Now C2 Proficiency) certificates. Please note that certain exemptions* from the above-mentioned requirements may apply in the following cases: Applicants with a Dutch VWO diploma and a 7 or higher in English; Applicants with a German Zeugnis der Allgemeinen Hochschulreife, including English PF, eA or LK with a minimum grade of 10 Exemption options NB: If you have been diagnosed with dyslexia or another learning disability, please get in touch with the relevant test providers directly. Overview exemption options You are exempted from proving your proficiency in English if you apply on the basis of a (to be obtained): American High School Diploma with at least 3 content-based AP courses externally examined by the College Board (grades 3-5) and a high school unweighted GPA of 3.0/4.0; European Baccalaureate diploma from a European School or one of the Accredited European Schools and have (completed) English as an L1 or L2 subject; French Diplôme du Baccalauréat Général, Baccalauréat Francais Internationale (BFI) - British version; GCE A2-level certificates (British Examination Board), minimum 3 with grades A* to C from an accredited British examination board; International Baccalaureate diploma, with English as the medium of instruction; Maltese Advanced levels, at least three, from MTSEC Board, or Matriculation Certificate (when studied at the University of Malta) Singapore/Cambridge GCE/ A-levels in at least three H2, and one H1 content courses (of which at least 1 course in a contrasting discipline), together with either the course General Paper, or Knowledge and Inquiry (5 courses in total); South Africa National Senior Certificate (NSC) in combination with an IEB Advanced Programme Certificate in English (with minimum result 55% in case this is the only IEB AP certificate; otherwise with a passing grade), or in combination with an IEB Further Studies (Certificate) in English (with minimum result 55% in case this is the only IEB certificate; otherwise with a passing grade); In addition, the following may be exempted: A (to be obtained) upper secondary qualification, or a (to be) completed Bachelor's programme obtained in one of the following countries: USA, UK, Ireland, New Zealand, Australia, Canada (English speaking provinces/territories), Antigua and Barbuda, The Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, St Kitts and Nevis, St Lucia, and St Vincent and the Grenadines. Providing the applicant also meets our general end-level requirements for that specific qualification. Additional exemptions Additional exemptions may apply for the programme of your choice; however, this is at the discretion of the Admissions Board and will be assessed when your application has been received by the Admissions Office. The following faculties offer additional options for an exemption to the English requirement: the Faculty of Arts the Faculty of Behavioural and Social Sciences the Faculty of Economics and Business the Faculty of Science and Engineering the Faculty of Spatial Sciences If you are applying to a programme at one of these faculties, make sure to check the list below to see if an exemption applies.
