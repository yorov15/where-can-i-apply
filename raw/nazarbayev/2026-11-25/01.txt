Admission Requirements Important Dates FAQ Financial support Step 1. Receiving an admission offer in the Personal Account To complete the financial support application, the applicant must click the "Submit Financial Support Application" button. After submitting an application, changes to the application form and uploaded documents can not be made. The Admissions Office will review the application and send a notification to the applicant's email informing the applicant of the documents that need to be corrected and the deadline for their correction, if necessary. Incomplete or incorrectly submitted applications (uncorrected ones) will not be considered. Important: Applicants are recommended to check the Spam folder, as a notification from the University may end up there. Step 2. Fill out the application form Complete the financial support application form at admissions.nu.edu.kz in the Admission Results section. Applicant must select one of the following categories under which the financial support application will be considered: NUET Applicants* SAT/ACT Applicants Winners of International and/or Republican Subject Olympiads IB Diploma Holders A-Level/NIS Grade 12 Certificate Holders NUFYP Students Application for financial support is considered if the applicant meets the minimum requirements for the selected category. If the established requirements are not met, the application will not be considered. *When selecting the NUET category, the applicant must choose a test city and pay the NUET fee . Important: Applications without NUET fee payment will not be processed, and the applicant will not be allowed to take the test. Step 3. Submit the required documents Depending on the category selected, the applicant must upload documents confirming the results. For the NUET category, uploading documents is not required. Step 4. Submit the application To complete the financial support application, the applicant must click the "Submit Financial Support Application" button. After submitting an application, changes to the application form and uploaded documents can not be made. The Admissions Office will review the application and send a notification to the applicant's email informing the applicant of the documents that need to be corrected and the deadline for their correction, if necessary. Incomplete or incorrectly submitted applications (uncorrected ones) will not be considered. Important: Applicants are recommended to check the Spam folder, as a notification from the University may end up there. Further steps For NUET Applicants: Five days before the NUET exam, the information about the upcoming exam date, time and venue along with exam rules will be sent to the applicant's email address. Important: Applicants are recommended to check the Spam folder, as a notification from the University may end up there. IELTS/TOEFL tests must be taken in person at the test center (not remotely) IELTS Online, TOEFL iBT Home Edition, and TOEFL PDT results are not considered. Results of IELTS One Skill Retake (OSR) are accepted. The date of the original certificate, meaning the date of the first IELTS test, is taken into consideration. Official IELTS/TOEFL electronic results must be sent through the Testing Center, and a scanned copy of the certificate must be uploaded to the Personal Account. Code for IELTS: NU, Admissions Office Code for TOEFL iBT: 6762 The certificates' expiration period is two years. IELTS/TOEFL test results are considered valid if they do not expire before August 1, 2027. Academic Results (one of the results) NUET: 120 total score (with a minimum score of 50 in each subject: Mathematics, Critical Thinking and Problem Solving NUET (NU Entrance Test) - test that evaluates academic training for studying at the University. NUET is held in two subject sections - Mathematics and Critical Thinking & Problem Solving. Total number of questions - 60. NUET Fee - 7,000 KZT. Test content - multiple-choice questions. Test duration - 120 minutes. Minimum passing score to be eligible for a state grant - 120 in total for two subjects, while the minimum passing score for each of the subjects is 50. For example, 50 + 70 = 120 - is a minimum passing score in total. To take the entry exam, an applicant must have an original of a passport or a national identity card that was uploaded to the Personal account; The number of test questions, the procedure, and the duration of the entrance examination may be subject to change at the discretion of the Test Administrator. Information about access to the entry examination is placed in the applicant's Personal account. Applicant will be informed about the examination date and venue, information on format, as well as required documents and test regulations during the examination via email approximately five days prior to the exams. The examination schedule is published in the "Important dates" section. In the event of a violation of the rules or conditions of the test, the applicant's test results will be cancelled. In this case, the applicant's financial support application will not be considered. Disqualified applicants will not be able to enroll at NU until a certain deadline. Test results are available in approximately six weeks in a Personal account. There is no appeal procedure. NUFYP (Nazarbayev University Foundation Year Program) is a one-year intensive program at the Center for Preparatory Studies of Nazarbayev University. The program provides intensive personal development, study skills, mathematics, and academic-language proficiency preparation for the undergraduate programs of Nazarbayev University. Education in this program is available under the state grant or other forms of funding, as well as on a fee-paying basis. Following the results of study in the Foundation Year Program, students are accepted to Nazarbayev University undergraduate programs. Can a 10th-grade student apply to NUFYP? No, he/she cannot. Only final-year students and graduates of secondary/post-secondary educational organizations can take part in the competition for admission to NUFYP. In other words, to participate in the competition for admission to NUFYP, an applicant must be a student of a final grade/course or have graduated from a school or postsecondary educational organization. In order to confirm their status, applicants provide a verification letter from the school ( spravka ) or school diploma. Is it possible to combine studies in high school and NUFYP at Nazarbayev University? Combining studies is not possible. At the time of enrollment and study at NU, a student of NUFYP must be a graduate of a secondary/post-secondary educational organization. NUFYP is a full-time program. What is the language of instruction at Nazarbayev University? The language of instruction at Nazarbayev University is English. Is it compulsory to enroll in NUFYP? Admission to NUFYP after high school/college is optional. Applicants can enroll directly in the undergraduate program. more questions Карта сайта × Admissions (не клик) Why NU?
