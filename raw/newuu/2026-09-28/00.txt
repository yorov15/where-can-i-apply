Undergraduate Programs School Program name Duration School of Engineering Chemical and Materials Engineering 4 years Mechanical Engineering 4 years School of Computing Software Engineering 4 years Cyber Security 4 years AI and Robotics 4 years School of Humanities, Natural and Social Sciences Applied Mathematics 4 years Economics and Data Science 4 years Pedagogy (STEM specialization) 4 years School of Management Industrial Management 4 years Undergraduate entry requirements Educational background: Applicants must hold a high school diploma (secondary school, academic lyceum, or professional college), with a Notarized English translation of the diploma and academic transcripts. Minimum English proficiency: Applicants must meet one of the following: IELTS 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 46 (only TOEFL iBT taken at approved test centers is accepted; TOEFL iBT Home Edition is not accepted). Interview Requirement All international applicants are required to complete an interview with the Admissions Committee as part of the application process. Document checklist for Bachelor's applicants International Passport English proficiency test certificate (IELTS, TOEFL iBT, CEFR, or other equivalents) Secondary school / academic lyceum / professional college diploma with academic transcripts, Notarized English translation International Olympiad certificate, if available Graduate Programs School Program name Duration School of Engineering Sustainable Chemical Engineering and Materials 2 years Mechanical Engineering 2 years Wireless Technologies 2 years School of Computing Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning 1 year Computer Science 2 years School of Humanities, Natural and Social Sciences Economics and Data Science 2 years Applied Linguistics and TESOL 1 year Modern Physics 2 years School of Management Industrial Management, Innovation and Business Expansion (double-degree option) 2 years Project Management 1 year Graduate entry requirements Educational background: Applicants must have a Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution (minimum 180 ECTS credits), completed in a full-time or part-time format, with a Notarized English translation of the diploma and academic transcripts. English proficiency: Applicants must meet the minimum IELTS/TOEFL iBT score for their program (see table below). A Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificate, confirming that a previous degree was taught fully in English, is also accepted in place of an IELTS/TOEFL score. Entrance Exam Requirement Admission requirements vary depending on the program specialization, each program has its own selection criteria. Please refer to the Master's Programs page for detailed information regarding the specific requirements of each program. Notes CEFR certificates are not accepted for double-degree programs. Only TOEFL iBT tests taken at approved test centers are accepted. The TOEFL iBT Home Edition is not accepted.
