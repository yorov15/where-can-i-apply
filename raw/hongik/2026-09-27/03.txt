Excellent GPA scholarship Subjects : Students in school (Payment after 2nd semester in first grade) Amount of payment and method of selection : Different payment based on GPA of the students who obtained 12 or more credits in previous semester Excellent GPA scholarship GPA in previous semester Amount of payment 4.0 or more All tuition ≥ 3.5, < 4.0 80% of tuition ≥ 3.0, < 3.5 60% of tuition ≥ 2.5, < 3.0 40% of tuition ≥ 2.0, < 2.5 KRW 1,200,000 GPA in previous semester : 4.0 or more Amount of payment : All tuition GPA in previous semester : ≥ 3.5, < 4.0 Amount of payment : 80% of tuition GPA in previous semester : ≥ 3.0, < 3.5 Amount of payment : 60% of tuition GPA in previous semester : ≥ 2.5, < 3.0 Amount of payment : 40% of tuition GPA in previous semester : ≥ 2.0, < 2.5 Amount of payment : KRW 1,200,000 In case of College of Architecture and College of Fine Arts, above-sown scholarship is allowed to foreign students of each college having within 3% of good GPA in previous semester. The students of good GPA scholarship are selected by Scholarship Team, corresponding department offices notify them and receive foreigner registration certificates and photocopies of passports, etc., and scholarship is paid to personal accounts. If the study starts with Korean course in the first semester and if a student completes all subjects (15 credits), the process as same as described above and KRW 900,000 is paid (excluding the students of College of Architecture or College of Fine Arts). International Scholarship Qualifications : Freshmen with TOPIK II level 5 and above Method of selection : The Admission Committee select in total 10 students among the incoming students max. 2 students from each country March admissions: 4 students / September admissions: 6 students Benefits : tuition fee including admission fee dormitory fee during regular semesters (double room) prior consideration for work-study scholarship Payment period : 4 years [5 years for Architecture(5-year program)]; from the 2nd semester, students who completed 12 or more course credits in the previous semester and has a GPA of 3.0 or higher are awarded Korean Language Scholarship Qualifications : All freshmen with TOPIK II level 4 or higher TOPIK II level 5 or higher in case of Architecture majors and Fine Art & Design majors Scholarship Scholarship Campus Category TOPIK II level 4 TOPIK II level 5 TOPIK II level 6 Seoul Scholarship 50% of tuition and accommodation fees 60% of tuition and accommodation fees 70% of tuition and accommodation fees Period One semester after admission One semester after admission One semester after admission Sejong Scholarship 50% of tuition and accommodation fees 60% of tuition and accommodation fees 70% of tuition and accommodation fees Period One year after admission One year after admission Two years after admission Seoul Category : Scholarship TOPIK II level 4 : 50% of tuition and accommodation fees TOPIK II level 5 : 60% of tuition and accommodation fees TOPIK II level 6 : 70% of tuition and accommodation fees Category : Period TOPIK II level 4 : One semester after admission TOPIK II level 5 : One semester after admission TOPIK II level 6 : One semester after admission Sejong Category : Scholarship TOPIK II level 4 : 50% of tuition and accommodation fees TOPIK II level 5 : 60% of tuition and accommodation fees TOPIK II level 6 : 70% of tuition and accommodation fees Category : Period TOPIK II level 4 : One year after admission TOPIK II level 5 : One year after admission TOPIK II level 6 : Two years after admission The accommodation fees are based on a shared room of two residents. Regarding students at Sejong Campus : only those who completed 12 or more course credits in the previous semester and has a GPA of 2.5 or higher are awarded. International Language Institute Scholarship Qualification : Students who obtained Grade 4 or higher grades after completion of Korean education course for two or more semesters in International Language Institute of Hongik University Scholarship : 30% of tuition and accommodation fees the accommodation fees are based on a shared room of two residents for regular semesters Period : One semester after admission TOPIK Scholarship Qualification : A currently enrolled student who obtains TOPIK II level 4 or higher, but must also be higher than the TOPIK level the student had at time of admission. The student has to submit their TOPIK transcript manually Scholarship : KRW 100,000, and KRW 40,000 for each eventual upgrade Conditions regarding multiple scholarships Hongik Global Scholarship cannot be awarded in simultaneously, and the one with most benefits will be awarded if multiple qualifications are met. But, the TOPIK scholarship is an exception and can be received with other Hongik Global Scholarships.
