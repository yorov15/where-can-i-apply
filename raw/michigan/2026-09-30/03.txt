International Students International students not only thrive at U-M, they bring a world of perspectives to a world-class university. Michigan offers you the unmatched combination of excellence and opportunities in more than 280 undergraduate degree programs, top faculty, and impressive facilities. International students on temporary visas should expect to pay the full cost of attendance. At both the undergraduate and graduate level, U-M students come from 120+ countries, representing a highly diverse global community. Costs & Aid International undergraduate students who hold temporary visas such as the student visa are required to show proof of enough financial resources to meet their expenses while attending U-M as shown below. Lower Division LSA (0-54 credits toward program) Tuition & Fees* $67,096 Living Expenses** $17,220 Books, Course Materials, Supplies & Equipment $1,220 Transportation $400 Miscellaneous Personal Expenses $2,458 Total Budget $88,394 *Tuition and fees listed are based on approved rates for the 2026-2027 academic year. Rates are approved each June by the U-M Board of Regents and estimated budgets are updated at that time. Tuition and fees may be higher or lower depending on a student's program of study; these estimated budgets use information from the College of Literature, Science and the Arts. Upper Division LSA (55+ credits toward program) Tuition & Fees* $71,798 Living Expenses** $17,220 Books, Course Materials, Supplies & Equipment $1,220 Transportation $400 Miscellaneous Personal Expenses $2,458 Total Budget $93,096 In addition, international undergraduate students who hold temporary visas such as the student visa are not eligible for U.S. federal financial aid and have very limited opportunities for University of Michigan scholarships. You may wish to contact the Education USA Office nearest to you for information about alternative funding options that may be available in your area or check for local funding opportunities with your school counselor or advisor.
