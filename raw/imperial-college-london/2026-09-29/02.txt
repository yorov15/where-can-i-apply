Entry requirements Accepted qualifications Accepted qualifications Entry requirements Accepted qualifications Find the minimum entry standards for undergraduate study at Imperial. We accept a wide range of UK and international qualifications for entry to our undergraduate courses. All of our accepted qualifications are provided below. Please note that some courses require higher entry requirements than the minimum standard listed below. Specific course requirements are provided on the relevant course page in our Study course listings . All accepted qualifications The following grades allow Imperial to decide if an international qualification is equivalent to 3 UK GCE A Levels. These have been determined using guidance from UK ENIC .
