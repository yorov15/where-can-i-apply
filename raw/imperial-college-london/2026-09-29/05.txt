fees Tuition fees Undergraduate Tuition fees Bursaries, grants and scholarships Loans Paying your tuition fees We charge tuition fees for every year that your course lasts. How much you pay depend on a number of factors, including: Your fee status (Home or Overseas) Your course or department The year you started your course at Imperial What financial support you may be eligible for towards your fees Home fees The UK government sets the maximum Home fee that universities can charge each year. In November 2025, the government confirmed the Home tuition fee levels for the 2026-27 and 2027-28 academic years: 2026-27 academic year : Maximum fee for standard full-time courses will be £9,790 per year . 2027-28 academic year : Maximum fee for standard full-time courses will increase to £10,050 per year . Work placement year (sandwich year) : Up to 20% of the maximum standard full-time fee. Overseas study year or Turing year : Up to 15% of the maximum standard full-time fee. The tuition fee loan, which will be known as the Lifelong Learning Entitlement from 2027, will continue to cover 100% of the tuition fee. Overseas fees The tuition fee for Overseas students is set by Imperial and is available on our course pages . Tuition fee status For more guidance on whether you are likely to pay the Home or Overseas fee rate, see information about fee status and how this is assessed. Tuition fee policy Compliance with our Tuition Fee Policy forms part of your contractual obligations with Imperial College London. Check our Tuition Fees Policy . Tuition fees Tuition fees by course While the Home fee is the same for all of our undergraduate courses, the Overseas fee varies depending on your course of interest. Find fees by course Fee status Your fee status determines whether you pay the Home or Overseas rate of tuition. We assess this after you apply. About your fee status How to pay If you are responsible for paying your own fees, you have a choice of methods to make your payments. How to pay Payment in instalments Payment by instalments may be an option if you are self-funding and you cannot afford to pay your tuition fee charges in one go. Payment in instalments Sponsored students If you have gained sponsorship, you will need to follow our process to ensure your sponsorship award is accepted by Imperial. Sponsored students Tuition fee loan Home students can apply for a loan from the UK government to cover the entire cost of tuition for every year of study. Tuition fee loan Student visa If you're applying for a student visa, you will need to show you meet the financial requirements set by UK Visas & Immigration (UKVI). Money you need Tuition fee refund If you withdraw from your course or suspend your studies, you may be eligible for a tuition fee refund.
