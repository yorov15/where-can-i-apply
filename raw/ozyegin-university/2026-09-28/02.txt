Prospective students who graduated from a high school that is equivalent of Turkish schools (and eligible Turkish and TRNC students) can directly apply to OzU with the scores of OzU-YOS or an equivalent examination; or with the diploma scores. International student admissions to the undergraduate programs are held in accordance with the Ozyegin University Directives on International Student Admissions . Qutoas for the program is announced by OSYM in the OSYS Higher Education Programs for International Students Guide every year. Students are admitted at the beginning of the academic year. Applications and registrations are held within the dates announced in the Academic Calender . You can find more information about the programs, conditions and quotas on the I nternational web page. All undergraduate students should complete the English Preparatory Program. You can find more information about the English Proficiency requirements here .
