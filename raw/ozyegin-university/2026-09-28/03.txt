tuition waivers to newly-admitted undergraduate students. Tuition waivers awarded at the time of enrollment are called "Admission Scholarships/Discounts." In addition to admission scholarships, eligible students may benefit from only one of the additional tuition discounts, provided that they meet the specific requirements. Students are not permitted to combine multiple tuition discounts. FIBA Employee Discount Full-time employees of FIBA Group and their children who are placed in or admitted to the undergraduate programs of Özyeğin University in the 2025-2026 academic year are awarded a 25% tuition waiver . The discount is valid during the periods defined under "Duration and Renewal of Scholarships and Discounts," provided the parent remains a full-time employee. To maintain the discount, eligible students must submit updated proof of employment (issued by the relevant institution) to the Student Services Directorate ( student.services@ozu.edu.tr ) before the announced payment dates of each semester . ÖzÜ Employee Discount Full-time employees of Özyeğin University and their children who are placed in or admitted to undergraduate programs in the 2025-2026 academic year are awarded a 50% tuition waiver . The discount is valid during the periods defined under "Duration and Renewal of Scholarships and Discounts," provided the parent remains a full-time employee. To apply, eligible students must send a petition to the Student Services Directorate ( student.services@ozu.edu.tr ) before university registration . The employment status of ÖzÜ employees is confirmed by the Human Resources Department each semester. Family Discount The Family Discount is awarded to each first-degree relative (siblings, parents, or spouses) who are concurrently enrolled as undergraduate students at Özyeğin University with "registered" status. To apply, students must submit: A petition explaining their eligibility An official civil registry extract (vukuatlı nüfus kayıt örneği) These documents must be submitted to the Student Services Directorate ( student.services@ozu.edu.tr ) before the end of the add-drop period of the relevant semester. Conditions: Applies to siblings born to the same parents or legally adopted. The Family Discount is applied as a 5% tuition waiver , calculated after all admission scholarships/discounts have been deducted. This discount is valid only for semesters when both relatives are enrolled. Duration of Scholarships and Discounts The duration of tuition waivers and discounts is a maximum of 2 (two) years in the English Preparatory Program and a maximum of 5 (five) years in undergraduate programs , not exceeding 6 (six) academic years in total . If students are placed in an upper semester of the undergraduate program due to credit transfer or course exemption, the number of transferred semesters is deducted from the total scholarship period .
