TSU has 20 years of experience teaching international students. The Faculty of Philology over the years has conducted pre-vocational training programs for students from all over the world: Spain, Italy, Germany, USA, Belgium, Poland, China, Turkey, South Korea, Tunisia, Taiwan, Mongolia, Egypt, and Turkmenistan, and other countries. TSU organized Summer Schools with intensive training in the Russian language and implemented programs of academic student exchange from universities in Italy, Germany, the USA, Czech Republic, Poland, Belgium, and other countries. Since 2010 our University has implemented the Bachelor's double diploma program. The Master's program "Russian as a Foreign Language" operates for Russian and international undergraduate and graduate specialists in philology. Educational programs for international students Pre-courses Department Undergraduate (profile "Russian language") training period - 4 years dates - September 1 - July 3 applications are accepted until July 25 tuition fee - 207,000 rubles Master's program"Russian as a Foreign Language" accepted Russian and international graduates with a Bachelor's degree or a specialist degree (in the humanities) training period - 2 years tuition fee - 224,000 rubles Postgraduate studies accepted Russian and international graduates with a Master's degree (the humanities) training period - 3 years. Short courses of Russian language for international students. This is an internship, which lasts about 3 months. The cost of courses depends on the number of credits. Usually, the cost ranges from 20,000 to 30,000 rubles. Summer and Winter Schools Classes are taught by Russian language teachers of the Department, who have special training and considerable experience in working with international students. You can receive detailed information about Summer and Winter Schools by request to studentcenter@mail.tsu.ru The TSU Faculty of Philology organizes: residence cultural program: excursions to museums, theaters, and the countryside (with the help of International Student Services Centre ) visa support health insurance.
