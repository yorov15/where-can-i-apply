If you're completing an academic or university preparatory high school program, you may be eligible for admission. Check the minimum academic requirements for your country's high school system. All applicants must also meet our English language requirements and any prerequisites for their chosen program area. Minimum requirements by country Search by country name Expand All Singapore Minimum Admission Requirements: Singapore-Cambridge General Certificate of Education Examinations (Advanced Level) with three H2 Level examinations (excluding Knowledge & Inquiry). Applications from students following the Integrated Programme are also considered. Transfer credit may be awarded for your Advanced Level/H2 subjects. See: Transfer Credit Information Slovak Republic Minimum Admission Requirements: Maturitnej Skuske/Maturita Slovenia Minimum Admission Requirements: Matura/Secondary School Leaving Certificate Solomon Islands Minimum Admission Requirements: University of the South Pacific Foundation Year Somalia Minimum Admission Requirements: First Year completed at a recognized university. Individual consideration is also given to applicants with excellent results in secondary school graduation qualifications.
