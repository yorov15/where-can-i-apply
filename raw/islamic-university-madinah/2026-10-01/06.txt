1 (External Scholarships and Internal Scholarships) 1448 AH Undergraduate Admission Guide For Full and Partial Scholarship Students 2 3 The Islamic University of Madinah was established by Royal Decree No. 11 dated 25/03/1381 AH (corresponding to 06/09/1961), and classes commenced on Sunday, 02/05/1381 AH. The University seeks to fulfill its mission as a global Saudi institution that provides Islamic, Arabic, and other sciences to students from around the world, while promoting scientific research, community service, and the optimal use of technology in contributing to the dissemination of Islam's eternal message. Since its establishment more than sixty years ago, the University has welcomed students from over 170 countries. Currently, students from more than 140 countries are enrolled, representing approximately 85% of its regular student population. Scholarships at the Islamic University of Madinah represent one of the university's educational initiatives aimed at providing distinguished academic opportunities for qualified students from different countries around the world, within an integrated university environment that supports academic achievement, skill development, and cultural exchange among students from diverse backgrounds. Through its scholarship programs, the university seeks to attract academically outstanding students and enable them to enroll in various academic disciplines and programs while benefiting from the educational, research, and student services provided by the university in accordance with high academic and administrative standards. Through these scholarships, the Islamic University continues its global educational mission, which has extended since its establishment, by preparing graduates who are academically qualified and capable of contributing positively to their communities, promoting knowledge, and strengthening the values of moderation, cooperation, and cultural communication among nations. Full Scholarships (External Scholarships) These scholarships are granted to non-Saudi students residing outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to pursue full-time undergraduate studies at no cost. Financial Benefits Monthly stipend. Two-month arrival allowance. Three-month graduation allowance for shipping books. A discounted economy class ticket when traveling at the end of each academic year via the nearest route to the country of residence provided the student has not obtained a travel ticket from another party. Post office. Commercial bookstores. Student service centers. Sports clubs and facilities. Key Benefits Full exemption from tuition fees. Important Notes These scholarships do not include financial benefits such as stipends or graduation allowances. University housing, tickets, and meal-related services are not included. Admission Requirements 8 Faculty of the Holy Qur'an: Admission to the Faculty of the Holy Qur'an requires complete memorization of the Holy Qur'an, confirmed through a successful oral examination. Scholarship students are not permitted to transfer from one faculty to another after admission. Admission is final for the faculty and program to which the student is admitted. Applicants who graduated from schools within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, even if they apply for an external scholarship, will be treated as internal scholarship applicants for all admission and selection purposes. They must submit the General Aptitude Test (GAT) and, for science faculties (such as IT, science, and engineering), the Achievement Test (Qiyas). Applications that do not include these requirements will not be considered. For scientific faculties: Applicants for external scholarships whose previous studies were not conducted in English, or who are not from countries where English is an official or primary language, must provide an accredited certificate proving English proficiency (such as IELTS, TOEFL, or their equivalent). Instruction in theoretical colleges is conducted in Arabic, while instruction in scientific colleges is conducted in English. The University has no offices or agents anywhere in the world authorized to receive applications. Applicant Responsibility: Applicants are solely responsible for completing the application process themselves and for securely storing any application numbers or credentials provided. Specific Faculty Requirements Important Regulations 9 The Arabic Language Placement Test aims to assess the linguistic proficiency of admitted students in order to place them in the most suitable academic pathway, whether in the Arabic language program or direct admission into a college. Applications for internal scholarships are subject to competition based on the weighted score and program preferences, according to seat availability.
