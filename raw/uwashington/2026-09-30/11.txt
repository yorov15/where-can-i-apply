We look forward to connecting with you! × Husky Promise Office of Admissions Costs & Financial Aid Husky Promise The Husky Promise is our guarantee that we will not let financial challenges stand in the way of Washington residents' ability to discover your potential and achieve a UW degree. It assures that full tuition and standard fees will be covered by grant or scholarship support for eligible students. You will not have to pay back these grants and scholarships, and if tuition increases, your grants will increase as well. The Husky Promise ensures that the UW remains affordable and accessible for Washington residents. Learn more about how the Husky Promise works.
