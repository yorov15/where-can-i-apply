Please note that the LSE receives far more applications for financial assistance than it can support so unfortunately not all applications to the Undergraduate Support Scheme will be successful. Eligibility To be eligible for consideration for this award you must meet the following criteria: Overseas student with an offer on any programme. Scholarship Amount The value of this scholarship is: Award amounts vary depending on individual circumstances but are usually between £15,000 and £25,000 per year. Any award made will be renewed for each year of your programme, subject to satisfactory academic progress. How to apply For more information on applying for this award, please visit our How to apply for undergraduate funding page.
