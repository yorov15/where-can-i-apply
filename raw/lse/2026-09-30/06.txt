The number, value, eligibility criteria and type of scholarships vary from year to year. All scholarships are awarded on the basis of financial circumstances in the first instance, and secondly take into account academic merit. By submitting your completed LSE Undergraduate Scholarship Application, you will be considered for any LSE scholarships for which you are eligible. For more information and to apply, please visit the scholarship page for each award.
