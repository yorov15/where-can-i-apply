Further decisions are transmitted on a weekly basis and can be found on UCAS Hub. 13 January 2027 (18.00 GMT) UCAS equal consideration deadline. LSE deadline for receipt of applications to be considered 'on-time' From January 2027 Newsletters sent to all applicants holding an offer of a place. Further newsletters are sent on a monthly basis throughout spring and summer Early March 2027 LSE invites applicants to sit the Undergraduate Admissions Assessment (UGAA) Late March 2027 Undergraduate Admissions Assessment (UGAA) 12 May 2027 UCAS deadline for final decisions on all applications Spring 2027 Applicants who are holding a firm offer at LSE will be invited to create their LSE Public account, as well as submit a Student Verification Form (SVF) and a Financial Undertaking Form (FUF) 2 June 2027 UCAS Reply deadline Mid June 2027 Firm Overseas offer holders will receive pre-CAS emails Late June 2027 Unconditional Firm overseas offer holders begin to receive their CAS 30 June 2027 Deadline for undergraduate offer holders studying the International Baccalaureate to submit extenuating circumstances 23 July 2027 Deadline for undergraduate offer holders to request a deferral 30 July 2027 Deadline for undergraduate offer holders studying any qualification other than the International Baccalaureate to submit extenuating circumstances 30 July 2027 Deadline for undergraduate offer holders to submit certified copies of previous qualifications and/or English Language qualifications to the Undergraduate Admissions Office 12 August 2027 Official publication of A-level results Mid to late August 2027 Confirmation period; LSE aims to make final decisions on the applications of all offer holders whose results we have received Late August/early September 2027 3 September 2027 Welcome information is sent to all unconditional firm (UF) offer holders Deadline for undergraduate offer holders to send verification of spring/summer 2026 academic results to the Undergraduate Admissions Office 3 September 2027 Deadline for undergraduate offer holders to inform the Undergraduate Admissions Office of the results of any re-marks.
