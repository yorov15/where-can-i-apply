(2) The application requirements, exam principles, application forms, and exam dates for the exams to be held by units that accept students with Special Talent Examination and other issues are determined by the proposal of the relevant Unit Administrative Board, and by decision of the Senate until the end of April each year. Special Talent Exams can also be held outside Kyrgyzstan upon the proposal of the relevant unit and the approval of the Rectorate. Admission Requirements for International Students As international applicants are considered citizens of all countries, including Turkic republics and communities, except the Kyrgyz Republic and the Türkiye or have a refugee document issued by the UN. Academic Records Requirements for International Students Conditions for admission: Diploma of secondary education, giving the right to study in any higher education program within the framework of the criteria determined by the Ministry of Education of the Kyrgyz Republic and the Turkish Republic. The average score of the certificate/diploma must be at least 70 out of 100, at least 7 out of 10 and at least 4 out of 5. An applicant who has a diploma of higher education and submits an application with this diploma must have grades in the transcript not lower than CC.
