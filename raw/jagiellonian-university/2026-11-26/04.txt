Do you hold a Polish citizenship? In that case, you cannot take part in admission for foreigners (even if you are a citizen of another country at the same time). Go to the website with information for Polish citizens : rekrutacja.uj.edu.pl Documents required at the IRK registration stage When submitting your application in the IRK system, you may be asked to upload specific documents, depending on the programme you have selected. In most cases, this will be proof of language proficiency at the required level. Information on whether documents must be uploaded at the registration stage can be found on the relevant programme page . Documents required upon enrolment To complete enrolment, you must present a complete set of original documents. To ensure the procedure runs smoothly, particular attention should be paid to documents whose issuance may be time-consuming. These may include: apostille or legalisation of international secondary education document and, in certain cases, a certificate confirming knowledge of the language of instruction at a minimum level of B2 or NAWA's Director individual recognition of foreign education. If you are unable to attend enrolment on the designated date, you may appoint a r epresentative who will complete all enrolment formalities on your behalf. Some documents, such as NAWA's Director individual recognition , are provided only in electronic form and signed with a qualified electronic signature by an authorised person. If your document has been issued in this format, please send it within the enrolment deadline to the email address indicated in the IRK system or provided by a student officer. Further information about the enrolment procedure is available on the website . Valid passport or ID card (for EU citizens) At enrolment, you must present a valid passport for inspection. Citizens of the European Union Member States may also present a national identity card. Documents generated from the IRK system Personal questionnaire The personal questionnaire can be downloaded from the IRK system only after receiving the status "qualified". Before that, you should check whether all information in the "Personal Forms" tab has been completed, including details of the secondary education, and whether the photograph has been accepted by the administrator. The questionnaire is available in the "Applications" tab under the link "Documents and further steps". It must be printed and signed in all designated places. Statutory representative's declaration of consent Applicants who are under 18 years old on the date of enrolment must submit the document "Statutory representative's declaration of consent" - a declaration of consent to undertake studies signed by a statutory representative. A statutory representative is a parent or legal guardian. On the basis of this document, a minor may sign the personal questionnaire, the declaration of knowledge and acceptance of payment conditions for studies and, at later stages, apply for a place in dormitory or for financial support. Further information for minors can be found on the website . Important: If you are enrolling in more than one programme, prepare separate declarations for each of them. Power of attorney To appoint a representative, you must prepare and sign a power of attorney. A template of this document, available in various language versions, can be found in the "Applications" tab under the link "Documents and further steps". Additional forms In the "Applications" tab, in "Documents and further steps", you will be able to download the form "Requirements related to citizenship and country of certificate issuance". It summarises information concerning citizenship and previous education and indicates whether and which additional documents must be submitted upon enrolment. Depending on the programme, in the "Applications" tab there may also be available other forms, for example a "Key to the Future" project form. All forms available for a given application must be downloaded, signed and submitted upon enrolment. Documents entitling the holder to undertake first-cycle or long-cycle studies Certificate of completing the secondary education You may apply for studies in Poland on the basis of a Polish Matura certificate or a foreign secondary school certificate or other document confirming completion of secondary education which in the country of issue entitles you to apply for studies. The certificate should include information concerning eligibility to continue education at higher education level. If such information is not included in the document, you should provide a document confirming these rights. If you will not have your secondary school certificate by the enrolment deadline, you may present instead a certificate of completion of school together with a declaration that you will present an original certificate with its copy by 25 September 2026. The declaration must be signed during the enrolment at the student office. Note: Foreign certificates which in the country of issue entitle the holder to undertake only specific types of studies or impose additional requirements on the applicant may not entitle the holder to study in Poland. Information on whether and under what conditions your certificate entitles you to study in Poland can be checked using the NAWA Qualificator. Important: Documents issued outside Poland must be: have an apostille or be legalised; translated by a sworn translator into Polish or English (depending on the language of the programme). You can find more information below in the section "certified translation" recognised by the Director of NAWA or a School Superintendent - this applies to documents issued outside the EU, OECD and EFTA, Ukraine full secondary, China gaozhong certificate, IB and EB diplomas You must submit the original documents together with their copies. After verification, only copies of the documents will remain in the student office. Apostille or legalisation Foreign certificates entitling to undertake studies must have an apostille or be legalised. This certification allows a document issued abroad to be used in Poland. An apostille is an official confirmation of the authenticity of a public document. It confirms the authenticity of the signature, the role in which the person signing the document acted and the authenticity of the seal with which the document has been stamped. It is usually issued in the form of a sticker, stamp or additional sheet attached to the original document and may be obtained in countries which are parties to the Hague Convention abolishing the requirement of legalisation of foreign public documents. A list of countries where an apostille can be obtained, as well as the institutions that issue them, is available here . Important: The apostille must be issued for the original document. You must submit the original document together with a copy. After verification, only the copy will remain in the student office. If the apostille has been issued in a language other than Polish it must be translated in accordance with the rules for certified translations described below. The apostille requirement does not apply to International Baccalaureate and European Baccalaureate diplomas. Legalisation , similarly to an apostille, confirms the authenticity of a foreign document. It must be carried out if it is not possible to obtain an apostille because the country that issued the document is not a party to the Hague Convention referred to above. In such cases, the document must first be submitted to the relevant authority in the issuing country responsible for legalisation of documents for use abroad, usually the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Ministry of Education, and then to the Polish consulate, which will complete the legalisation process. Important: The apostille or legalisation must be obtained at the time of enrolment. Citizens of the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland and the United Kingdom who hold certificates not requiring NAWA's Director individual recognition statementmay submit the apostille by 25 September 2026 after signing an appropriate declaration during the enrolment at the student office. NAWA's Director individual recognition statement or Decision of a School Superintendent In the case of certificates issued abroad which are not recognised in Poland by law, it is necessary to obtain NAWA's Director individual recognition statement confirming that the certificate entitles the holder to undertake studies in Poland. Recognition decisions issued by School Superintendents under the regulations in force until 30 June 2025 will be accepted. Information from the Director of NAWA is not required for certificates issued in EU, OECD and EFTA countries or in countries with which Poland has concluded agreements on mutual recognition of education, including Ukraine full secondary education certificate and China gaozhong certificate. Detailed information on the statement issued by the Director of NAWA and other requirements applicable to foreigners can be found on the website . If you do not yet have the secondary school document for which NAWA's Director individual recognition statement is required, and will instead present a certificate confirming completion of secondary education upon enrolment, the NAWA statement must also be attached to that certificate. Important: The time required to obtain NAWA's Director individual recognition statement via the SYRENA system may be up to two months, therefore it is recommended to begin the procedure well in advance. It will not be possible for you to enroll without this document. Certified translation (sworn) For programmes conducted in Polish, all documents issued in a language other than Polish should be translated into Polish by: a Polish sworn translator entered on the list of sworn translators of the Polish Ministry of Justice; a sworn translator from an EU, OECD or EFTA country where the institution of sworn translator operates; the competent Consul of the Republic of Poland for the country in whose territory or legal system the document was issued; an accredited diplomatic mission or consular office in Poland of the country in whose territory or legal system the document was issued. The translation may be prepared by a translation agency provided that it is additionally certified by a Polish diplomatic mission or a sworn translator. You must present the original translation together with a copy upon enrolment. After verification, only the copy will remain in the student office. If you are applying for programmes conducted in foreign languages, documents issued in a language other than Polish or English should be translated by a sworn translator into one of these languages. If the document bears an apostille, it must also be translated. Important: A translation certified by a notary is not accepted. Documents confirming knowledge of the language To begin studies, knowledge of the language of instruction at a minimum level of B2 is required. Some applicants are required at enrolment to present a document confirming knowledge of the language of instruction in accordance with the list published by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education. Detailed information on situations in which this requirement applies is available on the website . Depending on the selected programme, additional language criteria may apply. Detailed information on language requirements for a given programme can be found on the programme page in IRK system . Important: For programmes conducted in Polish language at the Faculty of Medicine, a certificate confirming knowledge of Polish at a minimum level of C1 is required already at the stage of submitting the application. The list of accepted documents confirming knowledge of the language of instruction at the required level is available on a separate website . Insurance All foreigners in order to undertake studies must have health insurance . It is recommended that the document presented upon enrolment be valid for at least the first year of studies in Poland and cover treatment costs up to EUR 30 000 for the entire period of validity. A foreigner must be insured throughout the entire period of studies, therefore when the insurance presented during enrolment for the first year expires, a new confirmation of insurance must be promptly delivered to the student office. Other documents Additional documents may be required for enrolment if their possession was previously declared by you in the IRK system. For example, this may be a document entitling you to undertake studies without paying tuition fees or a decision of the Director of NAWA granting a scholarship . If the qualification criteria indicate the necessity to present documents confirming fulfilment of additional formal requirements, they must also be submitted upon enrolment. Web Content Display Web Content Display Documents submitted after enrolment Certificate from doctor of occupational medicine It applies only for selected programmes. If during studies students may be exposed to harmful, burdensome or hazardous factors, they will receive at enrolment a referral for examinations carried out by a doctor of occupational medicine. Important: Examinations carried out before receiving the referral or carried out outside Poland will not be accepted. Applicants are obliged to submit the medical certificate to the student office conducting the given programme within the deadline specified in the schedule for the relevant application round. Information on whether a certificate is required for a given programme can be found on the programme page in IRK Check the list of harmful, burdensome or hazardous factors to which students of selected programmes may be exposed. The list of medical centres with which the Jagiellonian University has concluded agreements and where the examinations indicated in the referral may be carried out free of charge is available on the website . Documents related to the protection of minors If your programme includes classes or other activities involving contact with minors for example internships in schools or hospitals, it will be necessary to submit documents specified in the Act on Counteracting Threats of Sexual Crime and Protection of Minors. Information on whether and to what extent the above obligations apply to a given programme can be found on the programme page in the IRK system .
