Do you hold a Polish citizenship? In that case, you cannot take part in admission for foreigners (even if you are a citizen of another country at the same time). Go to the website with information for Polish citizens : rekrutacja.uj.edu.pl Web Content Display Web Content Display Important: the IRK system differs between universities. When using the Jagiellonian University IRK system, please note that it may function differently from other IRK systems. You may apply for studies even if you do not yet possess the document entitling you to undertake higher education (a school-leaving certificate or diploma). However, please remember that you must present this document upon enrolment within the deadline specified in the admission schedule . If you require assistance with the admission process, please contact the Admissions Office via email at welcome@uj.edu.pl . Step 1. Create an account in IRK system Go to irk.uj.edu.pl , select the programme you are interested in, and then find the "Create an account" link in the top right-hand corner. Important! Provide an email address that you actually use. After creating your account, you will receive an email with an activation link, that you must click to use the IRK system. After activating your account, log in to the system. You can now complete your personal and educational details in the "Personal forms" tab. Basic personal details Provide information about your personal data, place of birth and citizenship. The data must match your identity document - once you submit your application, you will no longer be able to change it yourself. International applicant Complete the information regarding your status in Poland. The list displayed in the system presents conditions which, if met, may allow you to study without paying tuition fees. Selecting specific options will display a field that enables you to upload the specified document you must upload it in order to save the form. Meeting any of these conditions is not required to study at JU. If none apply to you, select "I do not meet any of the listed conditions". You can find a description of when you may be exempted from tuition fees on the following page . Address and contact details Provide at least one telephone number at which you can be reached. The University will use this number if urgent contact regarding your applications is necessary. Please also note that correspondence from the University may be sent to the postal address you have provided. You can edit these details. After enrolment, remember to update them in the USOSweb system as well (students should report any changes to the student office). Photograph Upload a photograph to the IRK system that meets the requirements for a Polish identity card. The photo should be current and of good quality, showing the entire head and the top of the shoulders. The head must not be tilted; the face, shown in its entirety, should occupy 70-80% of the frame. A plain, matte white background is required, and the photo must be in natural colours (not black and white or sepia). You must not wear head coverings (unless required for religious reasons), dark glasses, or anything else that hinders identification. This photograph will be used on your student ID, so it must be suitable for an official document. For this reason, every photo must be approved by an administrator. Without an uploaded and approved photo, you will not be able to generate your personal questionnaire, which is one of the required enrolment documents. Important: Photos are approved by university staff. If you upload a photo over the weekend before your enrolment date, please expect it to be approved only on Monday morning. It is better to upload your photo before the admission results are published - less stress for you! Education Start by providing details of your secondary school - this is required even if you are applying for a Master's programme. Next, in the "Documents" section, enter information about your secondary education (and higher education, if you are applying for second-cycle/Master's studies). You do not need to upload scans of these documents at this stage; however, you must provide basic details (place and year of issue). The remaining information must be completed before enrolment. Additional personal data If you do not have a PESEL number, complete the details of your identity document. Step 2. Check the schedule and list of documents Pay close attention to the admission schedule - if you cannot apply in the first round, plan to participate in a subsequent round. Important: Additional admission rounds are launched only for programmes where places are still available. Before submitting your application, familiarise yourself with the list of required documents . Ensure that you will have all the necessary documents in advance by the deadline specified in the schedule . Obtaining or preparing certain documents may take longer (even several weeks, for example for apostilles or recognition statements from the Director of NAWA), so start collecting them as early as possible. Remember that some documents are required during online registration, while others are only required if you are qualified for admission. Detailed information can be found in the IRK system. Step 3. Submit an application If you are not yet sure what you want to study, we invite you to explore our study offer on the website: Study offer . Before applying, find the programme you are interested in in the Jagiellonian University IRK system (the "Offer" tab). Carefully read the information on the programme page, in particular the qualification criteria. Make your decision and click the green "Apply" button. When submitting your application, complete all displayed forms the answers you provide may affect the assessment of your application. You can edit the data in additional forms yourself until the end of the admission round. After completing all forms, remember to save your application. The list of submitted applications can be found in the "Applications" tab. Step 4. Pay the application fee Each application requires a separate application fee. The fee must be paid no later than the last day of submitting applications in a given round, so that it is credited by the deadline specified in the schedule . All application fees are paid to the same individual bank account number provided in the IRK system. The due amount, the bank account number for the transfer and the option of quick payment (including by credit card) can be found in your account under the " Payments " tab. More information is available on the page: Fees . Step 5. Take part in examination For admission to most programmes, international applicants participate in an interview assessing their aptitude for study and their proficiency in the language of instruction at a minimum B2 level. Interviews are conducted online, most often via MS Teams. The interview results form the basis for the ranking list - grades from your certificates or diploma are not taken into account. Interview and examination dates can be found in the schedule . The admission team will provide you with the interview details in your IRK account no later than the day before the start of the entrance exam sessions. Please remember to: check the examination dates in the admission schedule and plan your time accordingly; regularly check messages sent via the IRK system; ensure you have a good internet connection - connection problems may affect the quality of the interview; prepare an identity document (passport or EU citizen ID card) - the examination team will verify your identity before the examination; verify the scheduled meeting time: examination dates are always given in Polish time. Keep this in mind if you are in a different time zone. If you experience any problems related to participating in the examination, reply to the invitation message in the IRK system. Admission for some programmes may also include written entrance examinations (for example, at the Medical School for Foreigners), fitness tests (conducted for candidates applying for physiotherapy and paramedic science), or document assessment (for some programmes taught in foreign languages). Information on this can be found in the IRK system. Step 6. Check the admission results Admission results will appear in the IRK system on the date specified in the schedule. After logging in, in the "My account" section under "Applications", you will see your position on the ranking list and the status: qualified, waiting list, or not qualified. Step 7. Qualified? Enrol to your studies! If your status is "qualified" - congratulations! It means that the University invites you to commence your studies. The final step is to confirm your intention to study, i.e. enrolment. Enrolment means submitting all required documents to your student office. Important: If you do not submit the documents by the deadline specified in the schedule, you will not be admitted to the programme. Detailed information about enrolment can be found on the page: Enrolment . Please also check the information on: granting a power of attorney , enrolment of minor candidates . Important: Enrolment will not be possible if your photograph in the IRK system has not been approved by an administrator. The requirements for the photo are described in the section on creating an account in the system.
