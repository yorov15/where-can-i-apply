The instalment payment facility works as follows: until confirmation of place for the first year students, respectively by October 15 for students from other years of study, at least 25% of the annual tuition fee must be paid; by December 5 , at least 50% of the annual tuition fee; by March 15 , at least 75% of the annual tuition fee; by May 15 , 100% of the annual tuition fee to be paid.
