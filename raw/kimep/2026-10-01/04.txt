Please update your browser cache. ✕ Nostrification The Republic of Kazakhstan is a signatory to the Lisbon Recognition Convention, which guides the recognition of qualifications from countries other than Kazakhstan for students who wish to study here. If you want to know more about the Lisbon Recognition Convention, please click here. KIMEP University welcomes students from all corners of the world. Holders of foreign certificates and diplomas, wishing to student at KIMEP have to pass a nostrification/ recognition procedure during their first semester of study at KIMEP. This is required under Article 39 of the Law on Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan, dated 27 July 2007. KIMEP provides consulting support to students on nostrification and recognition procedure. KIMEP does not guarantee recognition/ nostrification and bears no responsibility if the Center of Bologna process and academic mobility decides not to grant the certificate of nostrification/ recognition to a particular student. 7 STEPS TO PASS THE PROCEDURE I step: Bring the following documents from you home country: Apostilled or legalized original diploma and its supplement/ transcript in case a surname was changed, the confirming document should be included *According to the Minsk Convention as of August 22, 1993, academic credentials received in CIS countries (with the exception of Georgian) are not apostilled nor legalized. II step: Translate to Russian or Kazakh and notarize the following documents in notary services in Almaty: Apostilled or legalized original diploma and its supplement/ transcript Passport (two copies for foreign students) in case a surname was changed, the confirming document should be included III step: Pay for the nostrification/ recognition procedure Nostrification/ recognition of the higher and postgraduate higher education = 10 MCI* Nostrification/ recognition of technical and professional education = 8 MCI* Nostrification/ recognition of the main secondary and general secondary education = 6 MCI* *In 2018 year MCI is 2 405 KZT. V step: Submit all required documents to the Public Service Center: Notarized translation of diploma and its supplement/ transcript into Kazakh or Russian Notarized translation of student's passport into Kazakh or Russian in case a surname was changed, the confirming document should be included Receipt about payment Filled-in application form for submission of documents Plastic binder for filing the documents VI step: Get the confirmation document on submitting nostrification package to the Public Service and submit it to KIMEP Admissions Office. VII step: Get the result of diploma nostrification/ recognition procedure from the Public Service Center (3 months at the longest) and submit it to KIMEP Admissions Office. Center of Bologna process and academic mobility The Center of Bologna process and academic mobility is the organization responsible for implementation of the nostrification/ recognition procedures in the Republic of Kazakhstan, as stipulated in Rules of verification and nostrification of education credentials, approved by the Order #8 of the Minister of Education and Science, issued on January 10, 2008. By decision of the Center for the Bologna Process and Academic Mobility starting from February 5, 2018, documents for nostrification/ recognition procedure are submitted by students only through the Public Service Centers.
