English Proficiency We require all students, regardless of citizenship, permanent residency, or curriculum type, to demonstrate English proficiency . First-Year Applicants If you did not complete Grades 10-12 in an approved English-speaking country, you must submit one of the following test scores: TOEFL IELTS Duolingo English Test ACT* SAT* Scores must be from tests taken within two years of your intended entry term. This requirement cannot be waived. * If you choose to have your SAT/ACT considered, it will be used for all aspects of review and cannot just be sent to fulfill English proficiency. Transfer Applicants Transfer applicants must meet one of our listed conditions to satisfy the minimum English proficiency requirement. If you submit TOEFL iBT, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test scores… Report your highest overall score and highest sub-scores, regardless of test date. Don't use MyBest for TOEFL. Scores must be taken within two years of your enrollment date. The TOEFL iBT Special Home Edition is accepted for fall 2027 only, at this time. We'll only need official scores if you enroll. TOEFL code: 1836 IELTS: Send electronically to "University of Illinois Urbana Champaign Undergraduate" Duolingo: Send electronically to "University of Illinois, Urbana Champaign Undergraduate Admissions" Official Transcripts First-Year Applicants Do not submit official transcripts when you apply. You will self-report all coursework and exam results. Official transcripts are required only if you choose to enroll. Transfer Applicants Transfer applicants must submit official transcripts with their application. We cannot accept transcripts emailed directly by students or sent from international institutions. We can accept electronic transcripts sent through approved third-party vendors . Academic Information First-Year Applicants You will self-report all academic information, including coursework and external exam results. Official documents are not reviewed or stored during the application process. To learn what details we need from your school system, choose your country or curriculum: Canada China GCSE-GCE (O/A levels) India International Baccalaureate Malaysia New Zealand Singapore South Korea Year 11 Education Systems (Peru/Eastern Europe) Transfer Applicants All transfer applicants must provide proof of secondary school completion that would qualify you for university study in your home country. China: Submit high school transcripts showing graduation month/year or a graduation certificate. United Kingdom: Submit grades from your most recent college semester (Fall grades for fall entry; Spring grades for spring entry). If Fall grades are not available, wait to apply or apply for spring entry. Malaysia: Submit SPM scores if taken. If not, A Levels or a GED is required. Canada: Quebec CEGEP students must apply as first-year applicants after completing two years of CEGEP. Polytechnic Students (Singapore and India): If you completed lower secondary (Year 10) and then studied two or more years at a polytechnic, you must apply as a first-year student. Special Circumstances If your country requires military service after secondary school , complete this requirement before applying. Admission cannot be deferred for pending service. Homeschooled students are reviewed individually. If you completed homeschooling outside the United States , you must earn a GED before enrolling. Illinois does not use private recruiting agents. The University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign does not partner with agents or other private organizations to represent the university for the purpose of recruiting or enrolling students. Students interested in Illinois should work directly with the Office of Undergraduate Admissions.
