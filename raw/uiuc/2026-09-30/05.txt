Start Your App What Goes Into Your Application Major Choices You'll choose your first-choice major and an optional second-choice major . Pro tip: Choose majors you're genuinely interested in, since this can affect your review. Browse Majors Essays You'll write answers to a few prompts about your interests, experiences, and goals. Pro tip: Use your own voice and answer honestly. There aren't any right answers. View Essay Prompts Activities, Honors & Awards Share what you've been involved in both inside and outside of school. Pro tip: Include clubs, jobs, family responsibilities, volunteering, or anything else that's meaningful to you. See Why This Matters Test Scores Illinois is test-optional. You can choose whether to include ACT or SAT scores. If you do include ACT or SAT scores… Report your highest ACT composite and/or highest SAT total (no super-scoring). Report your highest sub-scores, even if they're from different test dates. We don't use the ACT writing test. We'll only need official scores if you enroll. SAT code: 1836 ACT code: 1154 If you do not include ACT or SAT scores… You may need to submit an English-language test. If you submit TOEFL iBT, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test scores… Report your highest overall score and highest sub-scores, regardless of test date. Don't use MyBest for TOEFL. Scores must be taken within two years of your enrollment date. The TOEFL iBT Special Home Edition is accepted for fall 2027 only, at this time. We'll only need official scores if you enroll. TOEFL code: 1836 IELTS: Send electronically to "University of Illinois Urbana Champaign Undergraduate" Duolingo: Send electronically to "University of Illinois, Urbana Champaign Undergraduate Admissions" See Reported Score Ranges Pay the Application Fee The application fee is $75. If you qualify for a fee waiver, your counselor must send our fee-waiver form by the deadline. Learn About Fee Waivers Submit Your Courses & Grades After you submit the Common App, we'll email you a link to self-report this information . List your classes and grades exactly as they appear on your transcript. We only need an official transcript if you enroll. International Requirements Art & Design Applicants You must submit a portfolio. Portfolio Guidelines Questions? Contact the School of Art and Design or call 217-244-8462. Dance Applicants You must complete an audition. Audition Requirements Questions? Contact the Department of Dance or call 217-333-1010. Music Applicants You must submit a separate music application and complete an audition or interview, depending on your program. Requirements Questions? Contact the School of Music or call 217-244-7899. Theatre Applicants You must complete an interview and either an audition or a portfolio review, depending on your program. Requirements Questions? Contact the Department of Theatre or call 217-333-2371. Track Your Application A few days after you submit the Common App, we'll create your myIllini account.
