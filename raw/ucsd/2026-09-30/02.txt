First-Year Students Transfer Students A transfer applicant has been enrolled in a regular session at a college or university after high school, excluding summer sessions. UC San Diego enrolls transfer students at the junior level. Transfer Students International Students International applicants must meet the same requirements for admission as other first-year or transfer applicants in addition to specific international requirements. International Students Military-Connected UC San Diego is home to more than 2,300 military-connected students. We have many resources and services dedicated to helping you transition from the military to college life.
