Undergraduate Tuition and Fees for Academic Year 2026-2027* (All fees are in UAE Dirhams.) Tuition for all majors is AED 110,876 per academic year. In addition, technology fees may apply based on the courses registered for in a given semester. Tuition for all majors (12 to 16 credits) AED 55,438 per semester AED 110,876 per year Lab/Technology Fee Type A AED 1,552 per course Lab/Technology Fee Type B AED 1,700 per course English Bridge Program AED 36,012 per semester Student Activities Fee AED 422 + 5% VAT per semester Seat Reservation Deposit AED 5,000 (inclusive of 5% VAT) Application Fee AED 400 + 5% VAT Health Insurance Plans Plan Type Per Semester Plan I: For AUS-sponsored students and students without their own private insurance 600 + 5% VAT** Plan II: For all students who are not on Plan I 300 For All Students: Medical services at University Hospital Sharjah's Primary Care Clinic and Emergency Room 112.50 + 5% VAT Laptop Requirements Students admitted to the School of Business Administration are required in their first semester to purchase a designated laptop during orientation week. Students admitted to the College of Architecture, Art and Design are required at the outset of their third year to purchase a designated laptop. Students admitted to the College of Engineering are required in their first semester to purchase a designated laptop during orientation week. We are evaluating additional student laptop programs and support. Prospective students in any program should not purchase a laptop for use at AUS without checking first with their major school or program. Residential Halls Fees Students in our residential halls enjoy convenient access to everything our campus offers. There are separate campus residential halls for men and women. All residential halls are equipped with Internet access, computer labs, gyms, TV and entertainment rooms, vending machines and laundry rooms. Residence on campus is available for all full-time students who have paid or arranged for the payment of their room, tuition and other university fees. (Other student categories may be considered depending on the availability of rooms.)
