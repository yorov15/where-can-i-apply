Calendar and Deadlines for First-Year Applicants | Amherst College First-Year Application and Financial Aid Deadlines (for enrollment in Fall 2027) November 9, 2026 (Early Decision) Early Decision admission applications must be submitted through Common App or Apply Coalition, with Scoir, by this date. November 13, 2026 (Early Decision) All Early Decision applicants who intend to apply for financial aid should submit aid applications by this date. Early-to-mid December, 2026 (Early Decision) Decision notices to Early Decision applicants sent on or around this date. January 5, 2027 (Regular Decision) Regular Decision admission applications must be submitted through Common App or Apply Coalition, with Scoir, by this date. January 15, 2027 (Regular Decision) All Regular Decision applicants who intend to apply for financial aid should submit aid applications by this date. March 20, 2027 (Regular Decision) Decision notices to Regular Decision applicants sent on or around this date. April, 2027 (TBA) Admitted Student Day Open House April, 2027 (TBA) Admitted Student Two-Day Open House May 1, 2027 (Regular Decision) Admission Response Form for students admitted under Regular Decision must be submitted to Admission Office.
