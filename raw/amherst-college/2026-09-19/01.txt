International Students | Financial Aid | Amherst College Amherst College is "need blind" with respect to admission - Amherst will not take into account whether you have applied or intend to apply for financial aid in the admission process will meet 100% of your calculated financial need your financial aid offer will not contain any initially packaged student loans The College has a comprehensive program of financial aid that provides assistance in the form of scholarships and grants, student employment and loans for international and domestic students. Amherst meets the full calculated financial need of all admitted students. Current applicants can check the status of your application materials by checking the Financial Aid Portal "documents" link from the menu at the top left. Learn about Amherst's affordability and generous aid program, procedures and links to apply for aid, and frequently asked questions in the section specifically for International Students.
