The Non-Thesis Master's program takes a maximum of three semesters, excluding scientific preparation time. Students are required to pay the university's determined fee for each semester, regardless of whether they enroll for each term. The Thesis Master's program takes a minimum of four semesters, excluding scientific preparation time. Regardless of whether the student enrolls each term, they are required to pay the university's determined fee for each semester. If the program extends beyond four semesters, the student only pays the thesis fee for each subsequent term. The Doctoral program lasts for a minimum of eight semesters, and students are required to pay the university's determined fee for each semester. If the education extends beyond eight semesters, the student only pays the thesis fee for each subsequent term of continued education. The tuition fees are announced annually by the Board of Trustees, considering cost increases and the university's budget. If there is a change in the VAT rate, the tuition fee will be adjusted at the same rate.
