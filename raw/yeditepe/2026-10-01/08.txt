Established in 1996, Yeditepe University is an educational institution that stands out with its international recognition. The University continues its academic activities with a total of 75 undergraduate and 4 associate degree programs offered across 13 faculties and 1 vocational school . Accredited nationally and internationally, Yeditepe University offers multilingual education and facilitates global exposure for its students through over 400 Erasmus & Exchange agreements. Additionally, it provides dual degree programs, allowing students to obtain a second degree from some of the world's leading universities. With a strong alum network, innovative educational approach, and support for entrepreneurship and career development, Yeditepe University also offers a sustainable campus, extensive sports facilities, student and sports clubs, thus providing a rich social and cultural life alongside high-quality academic education. Rich Academic Programme Contents With 69 undergraduate and 4 associate degree programmes, our university prepares its students for the future in many fields such as technology, social sciences, health and law. Accreditations and Academic Collaborations Our university offers students and faculty members the opportunity to take part in international projects through national and international accreditations and academic cooperation agreements. Erasmus and Exchange Agreements Our university offers international education opportunities with more than 400 Erasmus and Exchange Agreements with the world's leading universities from 42 countries around the world. Opportunities for 7 Learning Foreign Languages English is the medium of instruction at Yeditepe University. Students also have the opportunity to enhance their personal development by learning additional languages such as German, Chinese, French, Spanish, Russian, and Korean.. Interdisciplinary Education In addition to the undergraduate programme you are studying, you can have two undergraduate diplomas by double majoring with another undergraduate programme of your choice or you can develop yourself in the field of your interest by participating in minor programmes. Green Campus in the Heart of the City Yeditepe University continues its activities in the field of education at the 26 Ağustos Campus, which was built to meet the educational, cultural, artistic, sports and all needs of the students. For students living in Istanbul, Yeditepe University offers a wide range of shuttle services. In addition to the paid shuttle service from all districts of Istanbul (except Çatalca, Silivri, Şile and Sultanbeyli), a free shuttle service is provided every hour between İçerenköy Metro Station and Yeditepe University on weekdays to facilitate students' access to the campus. International Education Opportunities through Erasmus and Exchange Agreements Yeditepe University offers its students an international education opportunity to get to know different cultures, observe the innovations in the world on site and share intercultural experiences through cooperation agreements signed with the world's leading universities from 42 countries in 6 continents. Thanks to our extensive international cooperation network, students from all faculties and departments can study abroad and do internships. Yeditepe University, listed in Times Higher Education's " World University ", " Clinical, Pre-Clinical & Health ", " Asia University ", " Young University " and " Impact Ranking " from the London-based higher education rating organization, offers its students the opportunity to study abroad with over 400 Erasmus and Exchange agreements. Student Centre Yeditepe University Student Centre aims to strengthen the individual and academic development of students, enrich the university experience in terms of personal, social and intellectual aspects, and provide our students with a sense of belonging and cohesiveness. The centre enables students to develop in every aspect with services such as new student counselling, psychological counselling and guidance, healthy nutrition and diet counselling, clubs and student activities, culture and arts, career development and sports management.
