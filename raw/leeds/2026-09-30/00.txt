English language requirements IELTS (International English Language Testing System) We specify English language requirements in terms of IELTS. We accept both IELTS Academic and IELTS for UKVI (United Kingdom Visas and Immigration) (Academic). The test must be taken at a test centre. We cannot accept results from an IELTS test that is over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. You must meet the requirements through a single IELTS test, or via a single test and One Skill Retake. We also accept a range of other English language qualifications, which are listed in the next section. Minimum requirements by study level The following are the minimum English language requirements for each level of study. Some of our courses have higher English language requirements. Check the entry requirements for your course via Course Search or, for postgraduate research programmes, the relevant faculty graduate school website. We offer pre-sessional English courses to improve your academic English language level and help you prepare for study in the UK. You may be able to use a pre-sessional to meet the English language requirement for your course. Undergraduate degrees IELTS Academic or IELTS for UKVI (Academic): 6.0 overall with no less than 5.5 in each component. TOEFL iBT (Test of English as a Foreign Language Internet-Based Test): 80 overall with no less than 18 in reading, 17 in listening, 20 in speaking and 19 in writing (for tests taken before 21 January 2026) or 4 overall with no less than 4 in each component (for tests taken on or after 21 January 2026). We cannot accept results from a test that is over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. You must meet the requirements through a single TOEFL iBT test taken in a test centre. We do not accept TOEFL MyBest scores or the TOEFL iBT Paper Edition. PTE (Pearson Test of English) Academic or PTE Academic UKVI: 60 overall with no less than 59 in each component. We cannot accept results from these tests that are over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. We do not accept PTE Academic Online. LanguageCert Academic or LanguageCert Academic SELT (Secure English Language Test): 65 overall with no less than 60 in each component. We cannot accept results from these tests that are over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. The test must have been taken in a test centre. We cannot accept results from a test that is over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. Oxford Test of English Advanced: 126 overall with no less than 111 in each component. We accept results from the full test of all four components or from individual tests of each component. We cannot accept results from a test that is over two years old at the start date of the course you intend to study. A suitable Cambridge English qualification: 169 overall with no less than 162 in each component. Pre-sessional English language courses If you require a Student visa, you will need to have a Secure English Language Test (SELT) unless you are eligible for a combined Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS). See our guidance on visas for Language Centre students . You do not need a SELT if you will study the pre-sessional online. The minimum grade required in your English language qualification depends on the length of the pre-sessional and the degree you intend to study afterwards. Please check the Language Centre website for further information about entry requirements.
