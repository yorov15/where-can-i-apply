These procedures can sometimes be intimidating, but we are here to help you every step of the way! If you are a student applying from abroad or holding foreign qualifications, you must go through certain specific steps before you can enroll. That is why we have created a dedicated summary document Admission procedures - Orientation and information and the webpage Internationl Student Office detailing what you need to do depending on your status, programme, and qualifications, with contacts directing you to the office or online service that best fits your needs. Read the summary Admission procedures - Orientation and Information (pdf) Click on the page International Student Office Please remember: Italian candidates holding a foreign qualification must follow all procedures as set for International students . Bachelor's and Master's degrees taught in English Study in English at Sapienza: your admission starts here! Are you ready to give your future an international edge? If you want to enroll in a degree programme taught entirely in English, you have to apply for an admission evaluation directly from home through our online pre-selection procedure. Our academic offer in English is wide and diverse, tailored to your ambitions: 8 Bachelor's Degree Programmes (First-cycle); 45 Master's Degree Programmes (Second-cycle) Please note: for 9 of these Master's degrees, the online pre-selection procedure is also required if you choose the curriculum taught in Italian. Ready to check all the details, academic entry requirements, deadlines, and scholarship opportunities? Click on the page Foundation Year The Sapienza University Foundation Year was conceived for international students who need to complete their basic education in order to pass an admission test in Italian state universities (in a range of disciplines, i.e. Medicine, Architecture, Science, Engineering and Humanities). The programme is held in Italian and primarily addresses to students who do not meet the minimum schooling requirements outlined in current legislation for enrolment at Italian universities. Click on the page PhD Programmes, Advanced Professional Courses, Specialisation Programmes If you wish to enroll in a PhD Programme, an Advanced Professional Course, or a Specialisation Programme, you can find information in the related calls for application.
