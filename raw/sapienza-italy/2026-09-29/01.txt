(art. 38 section 2) Tuition Payment Deadlines 1. Students can pay Sapienza's tuition fee in three instalments. 30% (first instalment), 35% (second instalment) and 35% (third instalment) respectively. 2. Deadlines . For restricted access degree programmes, the deadline for the first instalment is indicated in the call; for degree programmes with admission tests and for those enrolling in years after the first, the deadline is November 6, 2025. The second instalment is due by December 18, 2025. By the same date, you must check with Infostud that the Isee per il diritto allo studio universitario is actually present. If it is not present, even after having carried out the "Upload Isee" operation, you must immediately email your Student affairs office. If you have not acquired the Isee value by December 18, 2025, you will have to pay your tuition fees at the maximum value. Between December 19, 2025 and June 30, 2026, it will be possible to submit the ISEE value via Infostud after the deadline, but a fine will be incurred. This fine will start at a minimum of €50, increasing by €2.50 for each day of delay up to a maximum of €100. This fine is in addition to any further penalties for late enrolment fee payments. The third instalment must be paid by March 26, 2026. 3. Late payment penalties. A penalty of €65 applies to late payments; this amount doubles if the delay exceeds 60 calendar days. a. A penalty of €65 will be imposed on: anyone paying the first instalment of tuition fees from November 7 November 14, 2025 (if the tuition covers years following the first); anyone paying the second instalment of tuition fees from December 19, 2025; anyone paying the third instalment of tuition fees from March 27, 2026. b. A fine of €130 will be imposed on: those who pay the first instalment of tuition fees from January 6 January 13, 2026 (in the case of enrolment for years following the first); those who pay the second instalment of tuition fees from February 18, 2026; those who pay the tuition fees for the third instalment from May 26, 2026. 4 . Payments after June 30, 2026. Those who do not pay the outstanding instalments by June 30, 2026 will have to wait until the start of the new academic year to regularise their status. Pay your tuition fees via PagoPA From March 1, 2021, students enrolled in all our degree programmes must pay their tuition fees via PagoPA, already compulsory for public administrations.
