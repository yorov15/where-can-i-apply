Course catalogue All other candidates (EU or equivalent): deadline set on July 31, 2026. What is a pre-selection application? A pre-selection application is an online application allowing international students to be assessed well before the official Call for applications is published in the Course catalogue. Pre-selection is available for most English-taught Bachelor's and Master's programmes not requiring an in-person admission test, and for a limited number of Italian-taught programmes. This application is particularly important for those applying for a study visa to enter Italy. Most programmes will ask pre-selection applicants to provide: Passport High School and University Diploma Transcript of Records Language Certificate Reference Letter (optional) Additional supporting documents Each student can apply for a maximum of 2 programmes per academic year . Please note : if the programme(s) you are interested in is not available for pre-selection, we invite you to contact the programme Coordinator through the Contacts section of our Course Catalogue. Application process Here are the main steps international students have to follow to apply to one of our programmes: Complete a pre-selection application on MoveIn (if available for your programme, see list in the Admission requirements document) MoveIn Admission requirements Create an account on Infostud , Sapienza's student services portal Infostud Follow the instructions for the whole procedure contained on the International Student Office page and in your programme's "Call for applications" available in the Course catalogue (Call for applications are published not before late June. Read the "Apply Section"). Course catalogue Office for Students with International Qualification s Apply for a study visa on Universitaly (NON-UE STUDENTS ONLY) by June 30th, 2026 . Universitaly General requirements for admission Students applying for a Bachelor's programme must hold a high school diploma earned after at least 12 years of studies; Students applying for a Master's programme must hold at least a 3-year Bachelors degree from a recognized university in a relevant discipline; Additionally, all students must have at least an upper-intermediate knowledge of English OR Italian (B2 level) , depending on their chosen programme. Some programmes also require students to sit a mandatory admission test (e.g. Medicine and Surgery). IMPORTANT: Please note that final admissions are dependent on the successful outcome of all of the above steps, some of which are not controlled or managed by Sapienza University of Rome. Do make sure to follow all the regulations applying to your specific status (e.g. visa applicant, permanent resident, international degree holder, etc.) Language requirements Please carefully review the English language requirements for admission to English-taught programmes. Language requirements Pre-selection fee, tuition fees and benefits, scholarships All pre-selection candidates are required to pay a €30 application fee for each submitted application (up to a maximum of two applications per candidate per intake). No application fee waiver is available under any circumstances. Full tuition for our Bachelor's and Master's programmes is € 300-1500/year, according to country of residence (visit the page Tuition fees and Benefits for further details). We are able to offer such convenient rates thanks to public funding by the Italian Ministry of Higher Education. Tuition fees and Benefits Additionally, students can apply for various scholarships available at different levels, awarded on the basis of academic merit and/or family income.
