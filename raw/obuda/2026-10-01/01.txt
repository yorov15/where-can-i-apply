Obuda University seeks students who possess a strong background and interest in STEM fields, a good command of English, and a strong motivation to study at Obuda University. Applicants are requested to either have completed high school/senior secondary school before the time of application, or to currently attend the last year of high school/secondary school. In the latter case, the Matriculation / School Leaving Certificate has to be uploaded into the University's online application system by the 1st of August the year of application. Please note that only original or certified documents (original, or as certified copies, in English or in the original language with a certified English translation) are required to be uploaded to the online application system of Obuda University .
