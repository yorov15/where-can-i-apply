Out of our 16 undergraduate BSc degrees, 8 programmes are offered entirely in English. The programmes focus on scientific fundamentals, such as mathematics, physics, computing, mechanics, engineering, drawing, and chemistry; necessary requirements for chosen fields of engineering. The engineering faculties ensure thorough knowledge of practical engineering and, depending on the faculty, offer opportunities for specialisation. Each faculty divides their programmes into branches with compulsory courses and optional elective subjects. Depending on the BSc faculty, specialisations may include: geo-informatics, corporate strategy, info-communication technologies, instrumentation and automation, IoT, embedded systems and robotics, big data, business intelligence, cloud service technologies, IT security, software design and development, environmental public administration, green energy, textiles, and interiors and packaging. Experimental work executed in well-equipped laboratory facilities are core components of the training. The hours of instruction range from 26 to 32 hours per week, with some variation occurring during the last semester, when students work on final projects. Students typically take 5 - 6 exams each semester and are required to participate in active research and design within the project laboratories. This active research develops independent study, the ability to undertake independent research with minimal supervision. Upon successful completion of the curriculum and final projects, students will be granted a BSc degree which certifies its holder's competence to engage in either engineering production or in a technology-related field. Business Administration and Management Brief Course Description/Objective The aim of the training is to train students who, with their knowledge of economics, applied economics, and methodology, and the knowledge acquired in the framework of their specialisations, are able to understand, plan, and analyse the operational processes and economic relations of business organisations and institutions. Once they have acquired practical knowledge and experience, they are able to manage and organise the activities and processes of entrepreneurs and startups. Hence, students will be prepared to continue their studies at the Master's level. Specialization opportunities: Project management and B2B marketing; Digital manager.
