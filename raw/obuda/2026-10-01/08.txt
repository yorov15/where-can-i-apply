Brief Course Description/Objective: Our Preparatory Program is meticulously designed to support international students transitioning into their Bachelor of Science (BSc) studies in Hungary. Recognizing the diverse educational backgrounds and unique challenges faced by students from different countries, our program aims to bridge any gaps in knowledge and skills, ensuring a smooth and successful academic journey. The primary aim of this program is to equip students with the essential academic skills and knowledge required for their BSc studies. As the medium of instruction at the university will be English, our program places a strong emphasis on improving students' English language skills, focusing on both general and academic English. Through carefully selected courses in Calculus, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, and IT skills, we aim to solidify students' foundational knowledge in key STEM subjects. Courses in Economics, Entrepreneurship, and IT skills are designed to not only support academic success but also to foster an entrepreneurial mindset and essential professional competencies. Understanding that Hungarian is an important part of the students' new environment, we provide Hungarian language courses to help them integrate smoothly into the local culture and daily life. Study Option: Full-time Study type: In-person at OU campus Language: English Location: Budapest, John von Neumann Faculty of Informatics https://nik.uni-obuda.hu/en/home-english/ Curriculum: https://nik.uni-obuda.hu/en/pre-bsc/ Regular intake(s): September Tuition Fee: Non-EAA 3,000 EUR/semester EU/EAA 1,900 EUR/semester Entry requirements For more information and the list of the necessary documents visit https://uni-obuda.hu/required-documents/ Updated: 27.02.2026.
