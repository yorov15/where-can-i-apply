Expand all UCA offers an English-medium undergraduate education that combines academic excellence, practical experience, and a close-knit residential campus community. With international academic partnerships, dedicated faculty, and professional experience opportunities, students develop the knowledge and skills to pursue careers and further study. Small classes and dedicated faculty With a 1:9 faculty-to-student ratio, UCA offers a close-knit learning environment where students benefit from individual attention, direct engagement with faculty, and meaningful academic support. Practical experience and career preparation UCA undergraduate students are required to complete 1,440 hours of professional work experience through the Co-operative Education (Co-op) Programme. Across three consecutive summers, students gain hands-on experience with companies, public institutions and development organisations, applying classroom knowledge to real projects in fields such as IT, research, communications, finance and environmental science. Residential campus experience UCA's campuses in Naryn, Kyrgyzstan, and Khorog, Tajikistan, bring students together in diverse residential communities. Campus life includes academic, cultural, and extracurricular activities alongside study. Interdisciplinary learning UCA's broad-based liberal arts and sciences education connects learning across disciplines. Students build critical thinking, analytical, and technical skills alongside knowledge in their chosen field. A regional perspective with global relevance UCA's programmes connect the challenges and opportunities of Central Asia with wider global perspectives. Students build knowledge they can apply in the region and beyond. How to Apply? Expand all Create your online account, submit the application and required documents, then complete the admissions assessment and interview. SAT scores and IELTS/TOEFL results may be accepted in place of the relevant tests; shortlisted applicants can also apply for financial assistance. Step 1. Create your personal account Create an applicant account in UCA's online application system. Step 2. Complete the application form Complete all required sections of the online application form. Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all information provided in the application is accurate and complete. Applications containing incorrect or invalid information (for example, a date of birth in the future, incorrectly entered grades, or other inaccurate information) will not be considered. Step 3. Upload the required documents Upload all required supporting documents according to the admissions requirements. Required documents: A clear copy of your passport or national ID card. Photograph : One current photograph on a white background, taken within the last three months. Academic Transcripts : For international applicants only. Step 4. Submit your application Review and submit the completed application before the application deadline on 15 February, 2027 at 18:00 (GMT +6) . To avoid technical issues caused by a surge in applications near the deadline, applicants are strongly encouraged to apply early. There is no application fee to apply to UCA. Step 5. Take the UCA Admissions Test The UCA Admissions Test will take place on 6-7 March 2027. Invited applicants will receive further instructions, including their test-centre location, from the Admissions Office. The Admissions Test fee is USD 15. Applicants taking the test online are additionally required to pay a USD 38 online proctoring fee. This fee is charged directly by the external proctoring service and is not a fee charged by UCA. Step 6. Attend an interview, if invited Shortlisted applicants may be invited to an interview as part of the admissions process. The Admissions Office will provide details and instructions to selected applicants. Step 7. Apply for financial aid Applicants seeking financial assistance should submit the completed application and all required supporting documents by the stated deadline. Details about the application process and deadline will be communicated to shortlisted applicants who successfully complete the admissions test. Step 8. Complete EduGate registration, if applicable All admitted applicants (except citizens of Kyrgyzstan, Russia, Tajikistan, Kazakhstan, and Belarus) applying to programmes offered at UCA's Naryn Campus (Kyrgyzstan) must also register on the Government Education Portal, EduGate . EduGate registration is required only after all stages of the admissions process have been successfully completed. Applicants required to register must do so by the instructed deadline to be offered a place; failure to register means their application will not be considered further. The EduGate registration fee is separate from UCA's admissions test fee. Admission Requirements Expand all Review the academic qualifications, English and mathematics proficiency requirements, and supporting documents needed to apply to UCA. Eligibility Applicants should be in their final year of secondary school or have already completed their secondary education at the time of application. Documents Required for Application To apply, applicants are required to provide: A valid passport or other government-issued identification document One recent passport-style photograph on a white background. International applicants, except citizens of Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and Kazakhstan, are also required to upload their academic transcript as part of the online application. Applications submitted without the required transcript will not be considered. Admissions Test The UCA Admissions Test assesses applicants' English-language proficiency and mathematical skills, including reading comprehension, sentence meaning, quantitative reasoning, algebra, and statistics. Sample tests are available here: English sample test Mathematics sample test Applicants invited to the UCA Admissions Test should bring the following to the test venue: Personal Application Number Original passport or other government-issued photo ID The Admissions Test fee is USD 15 . Applicants who choose to take the Admissions Test online are additionally required to pay a USD 38 online proctoring fee for the proctoring platform. Admissions Test Exemption Applicants may submit valid IELTS, TOEFL, or Cambridge English results instead of taking UCA's English language admissions test. Please note: Applicants who submit an accepted English language test result are exempt only from the English language component of UCA's Admissions Test. They are still required to take the Mathematics test, for which the USD 15 Admissions Test fee applies. Tuition and financial aid UCA's financial aid programme ensures that admission is based on merit, and the ability to pay fees is not a consideration during the selection process. The total annual cost of studying and living at UCA is $8,000, covering tuition, learning materials, accommodation, meals, and access to modern facilities. Financial aid can be up to $6,500 and includes various internal and external grants and long-term instalment payment plans. Its size is determined individually and depends on the financial situation of the family. Full scholarships are awarded on a competitive basis to meritorious students from Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan. These include: Exceptional academic achievements in UCA's admissions test, 1st place award in Republican Olympiads in all subjects, Gold Certificate winners for the General Republican Testing (ORT). Relevant certificates and other documents should be submitted by shortlisted candidates to UCA's Admissions Office. Is it possible to apply based on the results of the ORT? To be admitted, you must submit an application and pass our internal exams. What are the employment prospects? UCA graduates have broad employment and further education prospects. They work for international and regional companies such as the Aga Khan Foundation, the World Bank, and the OSCE, and continue their education at prestigious universities abroad, and implement their own projects and startups. What opportunities are there for internships and exchanges? UCA provides students with extensive opportunities for practical experience, including mandatory internships during their studies, participation in research projects, and academic exchanges with international partners. Students can participate in semester-long exchanges at universities in Asia, Canada, and Europe (such as the University of Cambridge), participate in summer schools and cultural programs, and take joint online courses with international students. Is it possible to transfer from another university? Unfortunately, we do not offer transfers from other universities. If you plan to change universities, you must pass the UCA internal entrance exams and enroll in your first year. Does UCA issue an international diploma? UCA awards two diplomas: a national diploma from the country where the campus is located (Kyrgyzstan or Tajikistan) and a UCA diploma. The university is currently in the process of obtaining international accreditation, and the issued documents confirm the completion of higher education and allow graduates to continue their studies or build careers abroad. In which countries is UCA's diploma recognised? A UCA diploma is accredited in Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, meets international academic standards, and paves the way for graduates to pursue master's degrees and employment worldwide. Is it possible to enroll after 9th grade? No, you cannot enroll after 9th grade. To study at UCA, you must have completed the full course of high school and have a transcript available by the time of admission. Is it mandatory to live on campus? Yes, on-campus housing is mandatory. This ensures full immersion in the academic and English-language environment, and creates optimal conditions for studying, socialising, and participating in extracurricular activities.
