Download the brochure ANNUAL TUITION FEES EU and EEA Students €15,900 / year Non-EU Students €19,600 / year The previous rates are exclusive to Ecole Polytechnique; your years at Columbia University will follow their rates. Rates are subject to change. Tuition fees remain constant throughout the Program. Students may also be eligible for scholarships and financial aid. This aid is limited and generally does not cover the entire cost of tuition. We are committed to ensuring students understand the costs and funding for them if they choose to apply to Ecole Polytechnique. It is important for all of our students to think ahead and make a financial plan allowing them to pursue their studies with sufficient funding. Students should thus anticipate and plan to cover all of their living costs and tuition fees during the whole duration of the Program. While doing so, keep in mind that the academic workload is such that it is not possible to have a student job during the program. POSSIBLE CHANGE OF FEE STATUS It is not usually possible for your fee status to change once the academic year has started. If you become an EU national, or your country joins the EU then you may become eligible to pay fees at the EU rate for the next academic year. However, a change in fee status is not automatic.
