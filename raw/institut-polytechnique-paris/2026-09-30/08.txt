The Bachelor of Science delivered by École Polytechnique is a three-year undergraduate degree, taught exclusively in English. This intensive program is highly selective, multidisciplinary, and allows students to acquire solid knowledge and skills in several disciplines, with a double specialization (Mathematics and Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science and Mathematics and Economics). Backed by research, this rewarding and demanding program provides access to the cutting-edge facilities of the École Polytechnique's labs. Prerequisites The Bachelor's degree of École Polytechnique is aimed at international and French students with very high potential, who are passionate about science and hold a high school leaving certificate or equivalent.
