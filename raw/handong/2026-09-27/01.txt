Spring & Fall Undergraduate Admissions How to Apply All applications must be submitted through the online application system on the University's English website. In-person and postal applications will not be accepted. An application is considered complete only when the online application form has been submitted, all Required Documents have been uploaded, and the Application Fee has been paid. You may check all the announcements and results at the announcement board .
