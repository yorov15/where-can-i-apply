The Office of International Affairs is here to help! It is our responsibility to ensure that every international student's needs are met, and that you get the most out of your stay at NTU. Drop-in consultation hours : Monday to Friday, 1:30 PM to 4:30 PM National Taiwan University conducts its international student admissions in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Education. The University does not collaborate with any study abroad agencies or external institutions for recruitment purposes. Requests from such organizations for campus visits, meetings, or recruitment cooperation are, in principle, not accommodated. All official admissions information is published on NTU's Overseas Student Admission website.
