What academic requirements are there? What language proficiency certificates do I need to submit? Applying to NTU can seem daunting at first with the amount of information and options available. This page will guide you through all the steps you need to know before starting your application. Student Status Regulations, application processes, tuition fees, scholarships, and available programs differ depending on your student status, so it is very important to check before starting your application. Academic Requirements There are different academic requirements depending on the level of study you want to pursue. Language Proficiency Requirements Depending on your program, you may need to submit language proficiency certificates for either Mandarin Chinese or English. Application Timeline Take note of all the application timelines so you won't miss them! Next Up Student Status The Office of International Affairs is here to help! It is our responsibility to ensure that every international student's needs are met, and that you get the most out of your stay at NTU. Drop-in consultation hours : Monday to Friday, 1:30 PM to 4:30 PM National Taiwan University conducts its international student admissions in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Education. The University does not collaborate with any study abroad agencies or external institutions for recruitment purposes. Requests from such organizations for campus visits, meetings, or recruitment cooperation are, in principle, not accommodated. All official admissions information is published on NTU's Overseas Student Admission website.
