The applicants must obtain approval from their home country's government to study in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia if it is required by their countries. The applicant has not previously received a scholarship from any Saudi university or educational institution. Certificates and documents must be authenticated by the competent authorities determined by the university. The applicant must provide a certificate of good conduct from the security authorities in his country. The applicant must be medically fit, able to fulfill the study program's criteria and pass the medical examination as specified by the rules and guidelines. Non-Arabic-speaking applicants nominated for admission to any track of Common First Year or bachelor's degree programs will first be admitted to the Diploma Program of Proficiency in the Arabic Language. After passing this program, they will proceed to their bachelor's program. Apply online through the scholarship admission portal si.ksu.edu.sa. Acceptance is determined by a competitive process and the number of seats available based on nationality. After receiving final approval form the Ministry of Education and completing the visa-issuing process, students must submit the required original documents to the University to finalize their admission and begin their studies.
