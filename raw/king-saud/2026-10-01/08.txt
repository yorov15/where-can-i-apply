This commitment is further reinforced by Royal Decree No. (9589), dated 9/2/1444 AH, approving the Foundational Statute of King Saud University. In alignment with the goals of Saudi Vision 2030, the University strives to enhance its operational efficiency and strengthen its standing among distinguished universities both regionally and globally. Since its establishment in 1377 AH as the first university in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, King Saud University has remained at the forefront of educational institutions leading the advancement of science and research, and contributing to the preparation of nationally competent graduates capable of competing at regional and international levels. Driven by the University's belief in the importance of the admissions stage as the starting point of a student's academic journey, the University has been committed to developing its admissions policies and procedures in ways that uphold the principles of fairness, transparency, and equal opportunity, while raising the quality of its incoming students. Within this framework, the University's admissions system has undergone a significant qualitative transformation, transitioning from admission through common first-year tracks to direct admission into undergraduate degree programs. This shift serves to clarify students' academic pathways from the very beginning of their university studies, attract high-achieving students, and enhance the University's competitiveness and appeal. Furthermore, admission to the University through the National Unified Admissions Platform - as part of the broader integration framework among Saudi universities - will streamline the application process for students and enable them to explore their university options with greater clarity and ease. The Deanship of Admissions and Registration is pleased to present the Admissions Guide for Undergraduate Programs for the academic year 2026/2027. This guide outlines the general admissions requirements, available degree programs, applicant selection criteria, and the procedural regulations governing the admissions process. We ask Allah to grant our students success in choosing their programs of study, and that this academic year mark the beginning of a distinguished scholarly journey that contributes to building their futures and serving their nation. Questions About Admission Are students who obtained their high school certificates From inside or outside the Kingdom required to take Qiyas tests? All Saudi students applying for admission who hold a high school certificate in any track (General, Computer Science and Engineering, Health and Life Sciences, Business Administration, or Sharia) or its equivalent-whether from inside or outside the Kingdom-must take the General Aptitude Test and the Achievement Test, as both are part of the admission criteria for all bachelor's degree programs at the university. Can non-Saudi students be admitted? The University attracts distinguished international students of non-Saudi residents with valid legal residency in Saudi Arabia by offering limited scholarship seats in certain programs, in accordance with the regulations governing such admissions in Saudi universities. More details are available on the Study in Saudi Arabia platform: studyinsaudi.sa/en The University also offers an electronic admission portal for fee-based bachelor's programs, featuring a diverse range of academic programs across health, scientific, and business colleges. Admission is determined based on the approved admission criteria set by the National Unified Admission Platform. More information is available through the Electronic Admission Portal: ksu.sa/ouzdc What happens if a student does not achieve the required GPA in Academic Excellence Programs after completing the first common year? If the student does not achieve the required cumulative GPA specified in the Admission Guide for the academic year 2026/2027, they will be transferred to another academic program based on their academic indicators. What is the difference between admission to Academic Excellence Programs and other academic programs? Applying to Academic Excellence Programs is reserved for academically distinguished students and is subject to specific conditions and regulations, as outlined below: Medicine and Surgery Program for Academically Distinguished Students: Applicants must obtain a weighted score of at least 95% to apply. To continue in the program, the student must achieve a cumulative GPA of at least (4.5 out of 5) in the first-year courses as per the program's plan. Engineering and Computer Science Programs for Academically Distinguished Students: Applicants must obtain a weighted score of at least 90% to apply. To continue in the program, the student must achieve a cumulative GPA of at least (4 out of 5) in the first-year courses as per the program's plan. Is priority in admission given to students who obtained their high school certificate this year? No priority is given based on the year of obtaining the high school certificate, provided that applicants comply with the maximum allowable period since graduation as specified in the general admission requirements. Are students from the Business Administration track allowed to enroll in scientific or health colleges? Students from the Business Administration track are not allowed to enroll in scientific or health colleges. They are only eligible for admission to Business Administration programs and humanities colleges. Is the application process paper-based or electronic?
