Degree programmes in English ![Image 11: mano con smartphone e carta di credito](https://www.unitn.it/sites/default/files/styles/max_1300x1300/public/2025-01/IMG_ALL_1920X1080_tasse_2.jpg.webp?itok=EUW_WKWb) # Tuition fees for degree programmes in English [](https://www.unitn.it/en/study/fees-scholarships-accommodation/tuition-fees/non-eu-citizens/degree-programmes-english) Contenuto ## Academic year 2025/2026 Testo The amount of tuition fees for students residing outside the EU **depends on the admission score**, for the first year, **and on the credits earned**, for the following years. ### First year Tuition fees for the first year are based on the students' admission score: * 90 or more: exemption from tuition fees; * 70-89: €1.000; * 60-69: €2.000; * 50-59: €4.500. ### Second year Tuition fees for the second year are based on the number of credits earned during the first year, by 10 August: * 42 or more credits: exemption from tuition fees for the second year; * 36 to 41 credits: €1.000; * 24 to 35 credits: €2.000; * 6 to 23 credits: €4.500; * less than 6 credits: €6.000 ### Third year Tuition fees for the third year are based on the number of credits earned during the first and second year, by 10 August: * 84 or more credits: exemption from tuition fees; * 72 to 83 credits: €1.000; * 48 to 71 credits: €2.000; * 12 to 47 credits: €4.500; * less than 12 credits: €6.000. ### Further years of study If you graduate after the expected duration of studies (for example in 3+1), fees will be calculated based on credits earned by the 10 August deadline of the last year of enrollment: * 126 or more credits: exemption for the first year after the expected duration of the programme (3+1). If you take one more year of study, your fees will be € 1,000; * 108 to 125 credits: €1.000 for the first year after the expected duration; * 72 to 107 credits: €2.000; * 18 to 71 credits: €4.500; * less than 18 credits: €6.000.
