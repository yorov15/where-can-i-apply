The list below contains the education systems for which our University receives the highest number of applications and relative documents/entry qualifications needed to evaluate your application. The list is updated every year . If you cannot find your system of education here it does not mean you cannot apply, in this case contact the office managing your admission for more information. Documents must be submitted with your application and not sent by mail. EVALUATION METHODS All qualifications are evaluated according to the guidelines from CIMEA (national ENIC/NARIC centre), specifically we check:  official names of the awarding institution, qualification and teaching institution  accreditation of the foreign institution  nature and level of the qualification  length of the course and number of credits  academic rights the qualification grants in the education system in which it was awarded  authenticity of the document SPECIAL CASES TNE (Transnational Education) : TNE includes all higher education programs or educational services in which the teaching institution and students are located in a country different from the country in which the institution awarding the qualification is based. TNE institutions must be accredited/recognized both in the country where they are based and in the country whose education system they refer to. Specifically for studies conducted mainly in Italy, the recognition of your qualification is subject to the accreditation procedure of the institution operating in Italy, as established by Decree n. 214 of April 26, 2004 implementing Article VI.5 of the Lisbon Convention. Foreign schools in Italy and Italian schools abroad : please check the CIMEA website Qualifications in ARDI : if your qualification is listed in the ARDI database we will consider the information available there to evaluate your qualification.
