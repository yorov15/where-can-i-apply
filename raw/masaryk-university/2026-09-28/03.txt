Select one of the options to play the video: Enable cookies and play Open on youtube.com Masaryk University is prestigious and respected higher education institutions in Central Europe, offering high-quality programs recognized internationally. At MU, you can focus on one field, combine a major with a minor, or choose a specialization that fits your interests. With a wide range of accredited Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programmes you'll find the right path for you. Study in world-class laboratories, classrooms, and libraries on our ultra-modern campus. At the same time, enjoy the charm of historic university buildings in the heart of Brno. This combination of cutting-edge facilities and tradition creates an inspiring environment. With over 80,000 students Brno truly belongs to young people. The city breathes student energy, offering countless festivals, cultural events, parades, and vibrant nightlife. It's safe, welcoming, affordable - the perfect place to live and study.
