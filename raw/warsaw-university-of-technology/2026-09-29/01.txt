EXAMPLE: IRK identifier (5-digit number), your surname, your name IMPORTANT: Please start your payment description with your IRK identifier (you may find it in IRK My account - Account settings - Identification data ) . The tuition fee payment is to be paid only after you get a "qualified status" (if you need to take the entry exam in Maths - after you get a "qualified status" and pass the exam). The tuition fee should be paid in full for the first semester of studies. If necessary for a visa application, you may also pay the tuition for the 1 st year of studies. IMPORTANT: Remember that extra charges might be added to the transaction by your bank - you should cover these charges. The University should receive the full amount of tuition fees . While transferring money to the University account, make sure that you cover all the bank charges and that they do not get deducted from the tuition fee amount by your bank.
