Instead of immediately entering a specialized field, students will first receive a broad liberal arts and interdisciplinary education before declaring their major from the third year onwards. Admissions Gateway College offers Comprehensive Admissions through two tracks: Humanities & Social Sciences and STEM . Applicants are evaluated holistically based on their motivation and aptitude for interdisciplinary learning in a global environment, academic excellence including English proficiency, and their ability to collaborate effectively with people from diverse backgrounds. *Regarding Humanities and Social Sciences track International and Japanese students with educational background abroad who wish to apply for this track are expected to have Japanese language proficiency at CEFR B1 level or higher, as these fields might require them to communicate and conduct research in this language. You may apply for either Spring of Fall enrollment, but not both. It is backed by substantial funding from the government's Universities for International Research Excellence initiative. As Japan's first university selected for the initiative, Tohoku University will receive 15.4 billion JPY (approximately 100 million USD) this fiscal year, and some 10 billion JPY annually for the next 25 years.
