Beamline for Schools (BL4S) is a physics competition for high school students from all around the world
Participation in the competition is completely free of charge for all eligible students, teachers, and schools worldwide.
For data privacy reasons, students need to be aged 16 years or older on the day that the team coach submits their proposal.
