Registration for The Earth Prize 2027 is open until 10th of January 2027.
Submissions close January 31st 2027.
The Earth Prize is a $100,000+ environmental sustainability competition open to teenage students worldwide.
Participants must be between the ages of 13 and 19 at the time of competition, and be currently receiving education through a school, homeschool arrangement, or other educational programme.
Participation in The Earth Prize is free for all students and schools.
