Below you will find a complete list of example documents that are often required to complete your application. Our application systems will also specify which documents you need to upload. If a document is not required, you will not get a question about it. The documents you need to upload during the application process depend on your nationality, your country of prior education, your personal circumstances, and the bachelor's programme you have chosen. Still need to graduate? You are welcome to apply even if you have not yet graduated. Most prospective students begin their application while still in their final year of high school. However, please note that you must have obtained your high school diploma in the academic year preceding the start of your studies. List of example documents Identity Instructions Please upload a valid and legible copy of: the personal details page(s) of your international passport, or the front and back of your national ID card ( EEA students only ). Note: If you hold multiple nationalities and wish to have them registered, please also upload valid identity documents for each nationality. The University of Twente will only register nationalities for which valid identity documents have been submitted. Purpose of this document Your uploaded document will be used to verify your personal details. In addition, the information will be used to ensure that your name and date of birth match those stated on your official educational documents. If your personal details differ between your passport/ID-card and your educational documents (for example, if you have undergone a name change), please also upload an official document confirming this change. Example Application Fee Instructions During your application, information regarding the application fee will be provided within the application form. You will find a link to the website aanmelder.nl , a platform through which you can make the payment. Payment can be made using a credit card, PayPal, iDEAL, or bank transfer. Once the payment has been completed, you will receive an invoice. Please upload this invoice as part of your application. Secondary School Education Instructions As part of your application, you are required to upload your diploma certificate(s) and academic records. In some countries, the diploma certificate and final transcript of records are combined into one document. If this applies to you, please upload that combined document. Check the country list to see which documents are required for your specific country, as documents conditions and specific requirements differ from one country to another. Only if your country is not listed, please upload the following: If you have graduated: a copy of your diploma certificate and your official final transcript of records. If you have not yet graduated: your most recent transcript of records, showing the subjects you will be examined in during your final year. Other conditions: Documents must be uploaded in the original national language along with an official sworn translation in English or Dutch. If the original document is in English, Dutch, or German, no translation is required. Please ensure your transcript of records includes your full name, date of birth (preferable), the official name of your diploma, and the institution's name and logo. If your transcript consists of multiple documents, please combine these into one file in chronological order, and minimise the number of files you upload by merging documents where possible. Country List Purpose of this document Your upload(s) will be used to verify whether you have obtained your secondary school diploma and to assess the grades you achieved, the content of your programme, and the official duration of your studies. It will also help determine the level of your education, as educational systems vary between countries. Additional Degree(s) Instructions If you hold an additional educational qualification, such as an (international) foundation year, the first year of university, or a completed higher education degree, please upload the relevant documents. Each of these examples has its own document conditions and specific requirements. Please refer to the country list to check which documents are required for your situation. Please note that specific examples cannot be provided, as each institution uses its own document formats and structures, which can vary considerably. Country List Purpose of this document Your eligibility for admission will be determined through a comprehensive assessment of both your secondary school qualifications and any higher education degrees you have obtained. Uploaded documents will serve to verify these qualifications, providing detailed insight into the level, content, grades, and official duration of your prior studies from recognised institutions. IDW statement Instructions During the application process, you may be asked to provide the International Credential Evaluation (IDW) document. Uploading an IDW statement is not mandatory. If you have such a document, please upload it. If you do not have one, simply skip the IDW question in the application form without uploading anything. Purpose of this document An IDW statement is an official document that assesses an applicant's previous education completed outside the Netherlands and and compares it to Dutch education levels. It helps determine whether the obtained diploma meets the required level for admission. International students can, but are not required to, apply for an IDW statement through the IDW website . Do not use the date at the bottom of the certificate, as that is the result announcement date . English Language Test: TOEFL Instructions Please make sure to upload a clear copy of the 'TOEFL iBT Test Taker Score Report' certificate. We do not accept a print screen from the online result. In addition to that, please order the official score report at ETS using our institutional code 7025 (TOEFL) and choose for the department 'undergraduate schools' (this also applies if you are applying for a master's programme). In that way, we can verify your result online. Please be aware that we will use this information to access your data using the ETS database. Example English Language Test: Cambridge Instructions Please ensure you upload a clear copy of the official Cambridge certificate (both front and back). Screenshots of the online result or the downloaded 'statement of results' are not accepted. Additionally, please provide your Candidate ID number, which consists of three letters followed by six numbers (e.g., EPA123456). Furthermore, please share your results through the Cambridge Candidate Results Website . You will need to log in with your personal account on the website. After logging in, select the option 'send my results' to submit your results digitally to the University, allowing us to verify your results via the University's account. Please be aware that the University of Twente will use this information to access your data via the CIE Direct Site (database). As stated on the Cambridge website: "Candidates receive their ID number and secret number with their confirmation of entry for the exam. If candidates do not know these details, they should contact their centre." Information about additional test(s) Not the required grade, subject or level? If you do not have the required grade, subject, or subject level, you might be eligible to resolve your deficiency by taking an additional test if your overall level is equivalent to the Dutch VWO level. The University of Twente accepts certificates for Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry from CCVX , James Boswell-Bèta (both online and physical tests from James Boswell-Bèta are accepted; please contact them about the online possibilities), or the Dutch 'Staatsexamen'. The OMPT and Korteweg-de Vries tests are not accepted by the University of Twente. If you have a deficiency in English or Dutch, you may be able to complete the required language test. Please do this as soon as possible before the final deficiency deadline . The applicable language test and deadline depend on the admission requirements of your programme. Read more about the language requirements for bachelor's programmes . Additional subject tests must be passed with a positive result and should be taken before the deficiency deadline . Please submit your application to the University of Twente before making a reservation for one of the tests, to prevent potentially unnecessary costs. The University of Twente assesses on an individual basis whether you are eligible for an additional test and will inform you by email after you have submitted your application. If you are unsure whether you meet the additional admission requirements, you can continue your application by answering 'No, I still have to complete a *subject* test'. After submitting your application, you will be informed by email. The Netherlands has different Mathematics subjects. For the social sciences bachelor's programmes, VWO Mathematics A is required. For the technical bachelor's programmes, VWO Mathematics B is required. For the subjects Physics, Chemistry and/or Biology, you have to take the VWO level; there are no sublevels like Mathematics.
