In Czechia, it can also be more affordable than you might expect. Home page Plan Your Studies Scholarships and Finances How much will you pay? If you study in Czech at a public university, you do not pay tuition fees. If you choose a programme in English, costs vary by university and subject. You may also be able to reduce your costs through scholarships , university support or part-time work . Your costs depend mainly on the language of study, the university and your subject. As a general guide: study in Czech: tuition-free at public and state higher education institutions study in English or another foreign language: usually 0-6,000 EUR per year medicine, arts and some specialised fields: usually 8,000-25,000 EUR per year private universities: set their own tuition fees scholarships and part-time work: can help lower your overall costs Tuition fees are only one part of the total cost. Before you apply, also think about your monthly budget for accommodation, food, transport, health insurance and everyday expenses. Tuition fees Study in Czech tuition-free If you choose a degree programme taught in Czech , you can study tuition-free at public and state higher education institutions , no matter where you come from. Good to know: you will usually pay an admission fee additional fees may apply if you exceed the standard length of study private universities set their own tuition fees learning Czech can open the door to tuition-free education and a wider choice of study programmes Find out More About Ways to Learn Czech Study Tuition-Free Study in English or another foreign language If you prefer to study in English, you can choose from hundreds of degree programmes across Czechia. In many cases, tuition fees are still relatively affordable. Typical tuition fees: most programmes: 0-6,000 EUR per year medicine, arts and some specialised fields: 8,000-25,000 EUR per year The exact amount always depends on the university and the programme, so it is worth comparing your options before you apply. Explore study programmes and compare tuition fees Private universities Private universities set their own tuition fees. The amount depends on the institution, programme, study level and field of study. There are several ways to support your budget in Czechia, from government schemes and university funding to mobility grants and part-time work. Scholarships for Degree Studies Scholarships for Degree Studies Government scholarships The Czech Government offers scholarships for students from selected countries to study at public universities in Czechia. They support the standard length of full-time degree studies in Czech, usually including a preparatory language year, and selected programmes in English in priority fields. Check eligibility and details Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters are prestigious international master's programmes run by a group of universities . Scholarships can cover tuition fees and contribute to travel, visa and living costs. Explore Erasmus Mundus scholarships and Czech universities involved JCMM scholarships The South Moravian Centre for International Mobility (JCMM) supports talented international students and researchers in Brno . These scholarships help with tuition fees and living costs for non-EU students studying in Czech or English. Explore JCMM scholarships in Brno University Scholarships University Scholarships Many Czech universities offer their own financial support. This may include: merit-based scholarships for excellent results tuition fee waivers or reductions social, accommodation or emergency support support for students with specific needs regular financial support for PhD students The amount and conditions are always set by the university's internal rules , so the offer can vary from one institution to another. Scholarships for Study Stays Scholarships for Study Stays If you are not planning a full degree, you may still be able to come to Czechia through a funded study stay, exchange or research programme. Scholarship awards under intergovernmental exchange programmes These scholarships, offered by the Czech Ministry of Education, support study or research stays at public universities in Czechia for 2-10 months at Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral level. Applications are submitted through authorities in eligible countries. Check details Summer Schools of Slavonic Studies These scholarships support 3-4 week Czech language courses organised by public universities in Czechia for students and professionals interested in Czech language and culture. Explore summer school scholarships Erasmus+ Erasmus+ supports study exchanges and traineeships for 2-12 months for Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral students from programme and partner countries. Grants contribute to travel and living costs. Explore Erasmus+ opportunities CEEPUS CEEPUS supports mobility within Central and South-Eastern Europe for 1-10 months at Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral level through university networks. Explore Bavarian-Czech scholarships Fulbright Scholarships Fulbright offers scholarships for U.S. students and researchers to study, teach or conduct research in Czechia, usually for around nine months. Think about: accommodation food public transport health insurance study materials phone and internet everyday expenses Part-time work during studies International students can work while studying in Czechia. A part-time job can help you cover part of your living costs, gain experience and build useful contacts for your future career.
