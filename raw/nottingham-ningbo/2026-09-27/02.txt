If you are applying from China's Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions, please refer to the dedicated how to apply page for China's HMT students . If you are applying from overseas, please read the following sections on this page carefully to prepare and submit your application on our Application Hub. Email or post applications will not be accepted. Application Hub Application deadline for September 2026 entry: 30 June 2026 Due to the exceptionally high volume of applications received, UNNC would like to inform you of an update to our undergraduate admissions policy for 2026 entry. UNNC will formally close admissions at 23:59 (Beijing Time) on 21st June 2026. Any student that applies after this time will be put onto a waitlist. Applications can be made pending final results or during the final year of school / university education. Conditional offers will be made where appropriate and students will have until 15 July 2026 to provide their results such as English language test scores and/or diploma certificates. The university will make exceptions for students studying qualifications where results will only be officially released after 15 July 2026 including the HKDSE, GCSE and A-level results. Please note you should not submit any supporting materials (including essays) that you would like to be returned to you. It is the University's policy not to return any application materials. What should my application include? Your application should include the following materials: One recommendation letter on headed paper, signed and/or stamped Transcript of school exam results (completed or results so far) High School Certificate English language test score report if already received (e.g. IELTS or PTE) Personal statement One photocopy of the main pages from your passport One 2 inch color bareheaded photo with white background (Note: the head must occupy two thirds of the photo; photo dimension is no less than 320 × 240 pixels, the aspect ratio is 4:3; the file size is between 100kb - 500kb; jpg format) Identification document for applicants from China's Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions Hong Kong - a copy of Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card & Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Residents Macao - a copy of Macao Permanent Identity Card & Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Residents Taiwan - a copy of China's mainland travel permit for Taiwan residents, front and back sides Documents submitted in a language other than English must be accompanied by a complete English translation made by an accredited translator. You should be prepared to provide original documents or original certified copies of supporting documents at registration and upon request at any time. Failure to do so may result in your candidacy being rejected. * Applicants have the right to express their dissatisfaction with the processing of their application. If you wish to make a complaint, please email: compliance@nottingham.edu.cn.
