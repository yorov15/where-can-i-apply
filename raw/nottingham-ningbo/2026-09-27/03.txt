Scholarships How to apply Course search Make an enquiry Undergraduate tuition fee Chinese mainland students: 110,000 RMB per year China's Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan students: 110,000 RMB per year International students: 120,000 RMB per year Students who choose to spend two years studying at the University of Nottingham in the UK (available on certain courses) will need to pay the international fee (or EU fee where appropriate) at the time of starting their course at the University of Nottingham UK. Accommodation fee Accommodation on campus costs approximately 11,000 - 20,000 RMB per academic year for oversea students, depending on the type of accommodation . Estimated total annual costs The estimated total annual costs, including tuition fee, accommodation fee, compulsory insurance and living in Ningbo but excluding any scholarship awards, is approximately 150,000 - 190,000 RMB . Please note that installment plans are not available to commencing students, therefore a student's registration for degree study will only be completed once the full payment of each year's tuition fee has been made. If you have completed at least one year of study at UNNC and encountered financial hardship, an installment plan application is available and will be reviewed on individual basis. Please visit the student finances pages in the University Life section to learn more about installment plan and the available financial aids . Undergraduate scholarships If you are a student from the Chinese mainland, the Li Yip Yio Chin Scholarship and Excellent Freshmen Scholarship are applicible. Please visit our Chinese language site for more details. If you are a student from China's Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan regions, please refer to the fees and scholarships page for China's HMT students . If you are a student from anywhere else in the world, please refer to the scholarships page for International students .
