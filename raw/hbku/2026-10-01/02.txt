Program Overview The Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering prepares students to address complex engineering challenges through a rigorous curriculum that combines fundamental theory, practical experience, and emerging technologies. The program provides a strong foundation in mechanics, materials, thermo-fluids, energy systems, manufacturing, and engineering design while integrating modern tools such as artificial intelligence, data analytics, and smart technologies. Students develop analytical, experimental, and problem-solving skills through coursework, laboratories, design projects, internships, and research opportunities. The program offers technical electives in Engineering Design, Smart Systems and Manufacturing, and Energy Systems, enabling students to tailor their education to their professional interests. Supported by the University's interdisciplinary ecosystem and research institutes, graduates are prepared for careers in industry, entrepreneurship, research, and advanced studies, contributing innovative and sustainable solutions to national and global engineering challenges. Program Objectives The Mechanical Engineering program aims to: Provide students with a quality education grounded in fundamental principles and engineering practices, preparing them for real-world challenges. Empower students with knowledge, skill sets, and professional attributes to become leaders and innovators in the field of mechanical engineering. Serve the industry, government agencies, and local community in Qatar through targeted outreach activities, innovative consulting, and research solutions. Language English Program Duration 4 Years Certification Bachelor Program Design Career Pathways Outcomes Admission Requirements Additional Information Program Structure The program is a four-year, full-time degree comprising 129 credit hours delivered in English. Students begin with foundational courses in mathematics, physical sciences, computing, and engineering principles before progressing to core mechanical engineering subjects. The curriculum combines classroom instruction, laboratory experiences, design projects, and experiential learning to develop both technical and professional competencies. Students may pursue specialized career pathways in Engineering Design, Smart Systems and Manufacturing, or Energy Systems, supported by electives, research opportunities, and industry engagement. The program also incorporates general education courses, communication skills, interdisciplinary learning, and hands-on experiences that strengthen teamwork, leadership, innovation, and critical thinking. This flexible structure allows students to align their studies with their academic interests and career aspirations while preparing for professional practice or graduate education. Curriculum Overview The Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering is a 129-credit-hour, four-year program that combines a strong foundation in mathematics, physical sciences, and core mechanical engineering with hands-on laboratories, design projects, industry engagement, and a summer internship. Students develop skills in analyzing, designing, and operating mechanical systems while exploring emerging areas such as AI-enabled engineering, robotics, smart manufacturing, sustainable energy, and flow assurance. The curriculum offers flexible Career Pathways in Engineering Design, Smart Systems & Manufacturing, and Energy Systems, allowing students to align their studies with career goals. Graduates are well prepared for employment in leading organizations across the energy, manufacturing, infrastructure, and technology sectors or for advanced graduate studies. Course Categories Core Courses: 99 credit hours Technical Elective Courses: 12 credit hours University Core Curriculum (UCC) Electives: 18 credit hours Total Credit Hours: 129 credit hours Summer Internship: Required Duration: 4 years (full-time) Program Highlights Strong foundation in mathematics, physical sciences, and core mechanical engineering principles Hands-on learning through laboratories, design projects, industry engagement, and a summer internship Flexible curriculum allowing students to tailor their studies to career interests Development of teamwork, communication, leadership, and critical-thinking skills through collaborative and experiential learning opportunities Strong industry relevance, with graduates employed by organizations such as QatarEnergy, QatarEnergy LNG, Qatar Steel, Shell, SLB, McDermott, Honeywell, Siemens, Wood, Stantec, NOC, and others. Study Plan Number of Career Pathways 3 1st Pathway Engineering Design Computer-Aided Design (CAD) Sustainable Design Bio-inspired Design Human-centered Design AI for Product Design 2nd Pathway Smart Systems and Manufacturing Robotics and Automation AI and Machine Learning Smart Manufacturing Smart Materials Resilient Materials and Systems Autonomous Systems 3rd Pathway Energy Systems Sustainable Energy Advanced Cooling Technologies Energy Transition and Climate Change Mitigation Flow Assurance Natural Gas Engineering Program Educational Objectives PEO1: Have broad mechanical engineering experience involving increasing complexity to address the evolving needs of the private and public sectors in Qatar, the surrounding regions, and beyond. PEO2: Pursue their intellectual development through continuous learning such as professional development and graduate education. PEO3: Demonstrate effective teamwork, leadership and communication skills in practical industrial settings. PEO4: Practice their career with a high degree of professional ethics and contribute to their local and professional communities. Student Outcomes The ability to identify, formulate, and solve complex engineering problems by applying principles of engineering, science, and mathematics. The ability to apply engineering design to produce solutions that meet specified needs with consideration of public health, safety, and welfare, as well as global, cultural, social, environmental, and economic factors. The ability to communicate effectively with a range of audiences. The ability to recognize ethical and professional responsibilities in engineering situations and make informed judgments, which must consider the impact of engineering solutions in global, economic, environmental, and societal contexts. The ability to function effectively in a team whose members together provide leadership, create a collaborative environment, establish goals, plan tasks, and meet objectives. The ability to develop and conduct appropriate experimentation, analyze and interpret data, and use engineering judgment to draw conclusions. The ability to acquire and apply new knowledge as needed, using appropriate learning strategies. Academic Requirements Applicants seeking admission to the Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering should have a strong high school academic record with evidence of advanced coursework in math and science. Applicants are required to take the IELTS or TOEFL exam to demonstrate their proficiency in the English language. Applicants are highly encouraged to take the SAT or ACT exam. Application requirements A completed online application form: admissions.hbku.edu.qa Academic transcripts Final transcripts of all high school years (grades 10, 11, and 12) that are available at the time of application. Transcripts in languages other than Arabic or English must be accompanied by an official translation. Applicants should submit a copy of their high school transcripts as part of the online application. Applicants who are admitted to the program based on copies of or incomplete transcripts will be required to provide original transcripts upon enrollment to register for classes. English Language Requirements Standardized Test Results Official copies of standardized test results must be sent directly to HBKU. Please refer to the institutional codes below: SAT: 7675 ACT: 7019 TOEFL: 4981 IELTS: No code required. Students should ask the IELTS center where they were tested to send the IELTS TRF to Hamad Bin Khalifa University. Applicants should also submit copies of their test results scores as part of the online application. Required Documents Letters of recommendation Applicants should provide two letters of recommendation, including one from the school counselor, and one from a math or science teacher. Applicants should include their referees' names and email addresses in the online application. Referees will receive an email requesting them to complete their references. Personal statement of interest Applicants should submit a personal statement as part of the online application. The personal statement should explain why the student is applying for the undergraduate major in Mechanical Engineering, and how this will contribute to their future goals (minimum of 300, maximum of 500 words). Resume/Curriculum vitae Applicants should submit their resume or CV as part of the online application, including academic and extracurricular achievements, such as honors, awards, leadership, volunteer work, athletic involvement or any other relevant activities. Applicants are highly encouraged to mention hobbies and personal talents as well. Identification document All applicants should submit an electronic copy of their passport as part of the online application. Nationals and Residents of Qatar should also submit their valid Qatari ID.
