This page outlines the financial support provided to graduate students at HBKU. Information on financial support for Bachelor's degrees is not included on this page. For more information, please contact: fao@qf.org.qa Scholarships Graduate student scholarships may include a tuition waiver applied to reduce the tuition costs for the students and a stipend paid directly to the student. Generally, graduate students receiving a stipend would also be provided with a tuition waiver. Tuition Waivers Tuition waivers are standardized across HBKU. Self-sponsored graduate students are only eligible for a discounted tuition rate of 2,000 QAR per credit hour based on financial need. These guidelines do not apply to students funded by external sources. Students should follow the policies of their sponsors as applicable. Program Stipend Monthly Rate Annual Rate Duration Qatari PhD 11,000 QAR 132,000 QAR 45 months International PhD 9,000 QAR 108,000 QAR 45 months Local PhD 5,000 QAR 60,000 QAR 45 months Qatari Master's 9,000 QAR 108,000 QAR 21 months International Master's 7,000 QAR 84,000 QAR 21 months Local Master's 4,000 QAR 48,000 QAR 21 months Conditions Stipends are awarded to full-time graduate students who are serving at the university as RA and/or TA, depending on the availability of funds. Stipends for local students are limited. Full-time working professionals and part-time workers who exceed 40% of full-time hours are not eligible for stipends. Students receiving a stipend shall work as research assistants (RAs) with their primary supervisors for 10 hours per week. Students are allowed to work a maximum of 20 hours per week in total (i.e., 10 hours beyond their RA commitment). Students receiving a stipend are eligible to serve as Teaching Assistants (TAs) for 10 hours per week at an hourly rate of 50 QAR for the duration of the semester in which they are assigned to teach. Students receiving a stipend are eligible for paid employment through the Student Employment Program (SEP) for up to 10 additional hours per week. Students are responsible for covering the cost of their housing, whether they choose to live in QF dormitory facilities or in external accommodations. This expense will be paid from their monthly stipend or personal funds. HBKU does not provide direct payment for student housing. Stipend awards do not depend on the student's marital status or the number of family dependents. International students will be provided with a single round-trip airfare during their candidature, consisting of one inbound ticket upon starting their academic program and one outbound ticket upon graduation. Stipends funded from external sources, including research grants, are excluded from the rates shown in the table. Stipend exceptions can be considered for academic degree programs, on a case-by-case basis, depending on the availability of funds, and with the approval of the dean and the provost. Scholarship Eligibility Tuition waivers and stipend awards depend on the availability of funds. Tuition waivers and stipend awards are not guaranteed and are only awarded on a merit or need basis. Frequently Asked Questions Are scholarships automatically awarded to admitted students? No. Scholarships are offered based on merit or financial need and available funds. What is the application process for a scholarship? You are considered for scholarships during the admissions process; no separate application is needed. Do working professionals qualify for financial support? Full-time professionals and those working over 40% of full-time hours are not eligible for stipends. Discounted tuition may be available based on need. Is the scholarship renewable each year? Scholarships are reviewed annually and depend on academic progress and available funds. How will I be notified if I receive a scholarship? You will be notified by email with details about your award. Are meal plans included in the stipend? No, meal plans are not separately included. The stipend is a fixed amount meant to cover general living expenses, including meals. Can I receive external funding alongside HBKU scholarships? External funding may affect eligibility. Refer to the scholarship agreement for details. Are there GPA or academic performance requirements to maintain the scholarship? Yes. You must maintain good academic standing as specified by the scholarship agreement. Do I lose my scholarship if I defer my admission? Deferral may affect eligibility. Contact your college for guidance. Who can I contact for more information about scholarships? Contact your college for guidance.
