Apply online Application dates Submitting your application - we're here to help! Apply to UNSW is easy - we will guide you through the process with our step-by-step guide below. Ensure you are an international student You are an international student if you are NOT: an Australian or New Zealand citizen (including dual citizens) a permanent resident of Australia a holder of a permanent Australian humanitarian visa. International students currently undertaking one of the following qualifications need to apply for undergraduate courses through Universities Admissions Centre (UAC) : an Australian Year 12 in or outside Australia an International Baccalaureate Diploma a New Zealand National Certificate of Educational Achievement (NCEA) Level 3. If you are NOT any of the above, you can follow steps 2-4 below and apply as an international student. Find your degree Explore our 400+ degree and double degree options via our degree pages . Not sure where to start? Take our degree match fun quiz to get personalised recommendations. Explore degrees Check your entry requirements You need to meet both the academic and English language requirements of your desired degree to study at UNSW. Some degrees may also have special requirements. You can check the specific entry requirements for international students for the program you want to apply via our degree pages . Accepted qualifications English requirements Submit your application Now you are all set to make your application! Applying is easy via the UNSW Applicant Portal . Simply fill out the online form and upload all the required documents to complete your application. Alternatively, you can find a local UNSW agent in your region, and they will guide you through the process. For answers to common questions and step-by-step guides on how to register and manage your application in the UNSW Applicant Portal, visit the FAQ page . Apply online Find a UNSW agent I've submitted my application. What's next? 1. Track your application After you have submitted your application, you can easily track its progress via the UNSW Applicant Portal . You can also upload any additional documents required. If additional documentation is required, there will be delays in receiving an outcome. Please refer to international admissions information for offer round schedules. If you have questions about your application, you can contact the Nucleus Student Hub . I have an existing application - how do I access it? To access your application or offer, log in to the UNSW Applicant Portal using your existing login details. You may be prompted to set a new password on your first login - check your inbox for a reset email, including your junk or spam folder. Learn how to access your migrated application or offer in the UNSW Applicant Portal: Click ' Get Started' to view the step-by-step guide. 2. We'll send you a letter of offer We will notify you of the outcome of your application via email. If your application is successful, you will receive a full offer or a conditional offer if you need to complete more steps. If you are receiving assistance with your application, your nominated agent will also receive a copy of the email. 3. Accept your offer If you receive a full offer, you will also receive an email with a link to the UNSW Applicant Portal . Once you have accepted and paid your deposit, you will receive an electronic confirmation of enrolment (eCoE). Learn how to accept or defer your offer in the UNSW Applicant Portal: Click ' Get Started' to view the step-by-step guide. 4. Enrol online You can then enrol in your degree and courses online at myUNSW .
