Admission Criteria l Document Screening ▶ The document screening evaluation involves a comprehensive assessment of the applicant's academic abilities, study plans, potential for growth, and personal qualities based on all submitted documents. ▶ Each applicant will be evaluated individually by two or more evaluators, and the results will be comprehensively reviewed. ▶ After the document screening, applicants are classified as either 'Document Screening Accepted' or 'Rejected' , and the interview evaluation will be conducted only for those who pass the document screening (Accepted). * If necessary, the interview evaluation may be omitted. In this case, the final decision of ' Accepted' , 'Candidate', or 'Rejected' will be based on the document review results. l Interview ▶ The interview is a qualitative assessment designed to evaluate the applicant's academic potential and personal attributes through questions based on their submitted documents. ▶ Only applicants who pass the document screening (Accepted) are eligible for the interview. ▶ Interviews will be conducted online. If necessary, in-person interviews may also be conducted on-site. l Final Admission Decision ▶ The final decision of 'Accepted' , 'Candidate' , or 'Rejected' will be determined in descending order of total scores (50% document and 50% interview) through the deliberation of the Admissions 7.
