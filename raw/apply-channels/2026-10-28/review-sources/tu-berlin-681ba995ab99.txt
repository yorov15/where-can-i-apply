If your foreign educational certificates according to anabin permit direct admission, you can apply for a bachelor's program. In addition, you need a proof of German language skills at least at level DSH-2. Application for the first degree semester of a bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) Expand all Collapse all Selection process and advance quotas From the total number of study places available in the first degree semester of a bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC), study places are first deducted for applicants who were admitted in an earlier selection process during a period of service (e.g., Voluntary military service/Bundesfreiwilligendienst) and were unable to take up their studies due to completion of the service (= Preferred admission). The study places reserved for advance quotas are then deducted from the remaining study places. The advance quotas amount to a maximum of 20 percent in total and are reserved for certain application groups. These application groups include: Nationals of countries other than the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway and stateless persons with an international university entrance qualification (International advance quota) cases of exceptional circumstances, applicants who have already successfully completed a first degree at a recognized university in a member state of the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway (Second degree advance quota), Minors residing with a legal guardian in Berlin or Brandenburg, Applicants who belong to a group of people who it is in the public interest to support, and Professionally Qualified Students In the main quota, the remaining study places are allocated 50 percent according to average grade and 50 percent according to waiting period. The waiting period is all semesters after acquiring a university entrance qualification and during which applicants are not or were not enrolled at universities in a member state of the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway. Nationals of another member state of the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway are equal to Germans. This means that they are considered on a joint ranking list with prospective students who apply with a German university entrance qualification. Please note: If you have multiple nationalities, you must register and apply using your nationality from the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway. Otherwise, your application will be rejected or any admission granted will be withdrawn. Nationals of countries other than the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway & stateless persons have equal status with Germans, if they can prove that they are married to a citizen of another member state of the European Union (excluding Germany) or Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway who is working in Germany. Enclose all the following documents, summarised in a PDF file, with your application in the application portal of TU Berlin: Passport, marriage certificate and proof of the spouse's nationality and proof of the spouse's employment in Germany. The admission process starts at the earliest in mid-August each year for a winter semester or in mid-February each year for a summer semester. In the event that study places in bachelor's programs with restricted admission (with NC) have not been allocated after the completion of the admission procedure, these study places will be allocated by means of a lottery system . Here you can find past selection cut-off criteria . International advance quota Within the International advance quota 5 percent of available study places in the first degree semester of bachelor's programs with restricted admission (with NC) are allocated to applicants with an international university entrance qualification who are nationals of countries other than the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway and stateless persons. An additional 4 percent of available study places are reserved for nationals of countries other than the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway and stateless persons who have completed the TU Berlin's Preparatory School. Graduates of the TU Berlin's Preparatory School are: Persons who are enrolled at TU Berlin and have currently passed the assessment test ("Feststellungsprüfung") at the Preparatory School of the TU Berlin. Persons who have applied for the External assessment test or Supplementary test for TU Berlin in the pre-semester in due time and form and have passed it in the current semester at the Preparatory School of TU Berlin. Please note: If you have passed the assessment test in a winter semester and your desired bachelor's program is not offered in the summer semester for the first degree semester, then you will be considered as a "graduate of the TU Berlin's Preparatory School" when applying for the following winter semester. The prerequisite, however, is that you have not applied at TU Berlin for the first degree semester of a bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) for the summer semester. You may only submit one application for a bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) for the first degree semester. A ranking list will be formed, and the selection will be based only on the average grade. The waiting period has no affect on the selection. In cases of equal average grades, applicants with a scholarship and applicants who have been granted asylum in Germany pursuant to Section 25 (1) of the Resident Act (Aufenthaltsgesetz) will be given priority. Second degree advance quota Your application will be considered in the Second degree advance quota if you can prove that you have already successfully completed a first degree at a recognized university in a member state of the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway. Further information can be found at: Application for Second degree advance quota Special requests and Special requirements Nationals of the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway, as well as others with equivalent status to German citizens, can claim special requirements and/or make special requests. Further information can be found at: Special requests Re-registration at TU Berlin If you were previously enrolled at TU Berlin and would like to apply for the same bachelor's program with the same study and examination regulations, then you are regarded as a re-registrant. Please note the special information for re-registration . Application for the first degree semester of a bachelor's program with open admission (no NC) Expand all Collapse all Admission process Regardless of your nationality, with an international university entrance qualification you must always apply to TU Berlin. This also applies if you are interested in a bachelor's program with open admission (no NC). You cannot enroll without prior application and admission. The admission process for the first degree semester of a bachelor's program with open admission (no NC) starts at the earliest in mid-June each year for a winter semester or in mid-December each year for a summer semester. If you have applied in due time and form, the following apps will then be available at your tuPORT: "Notices and certificates": There you will find your letter of acceptance , which you can save and print out yourself. The letter of acceptance will only be available as a download at this app. It will not be sent to you by e-mail or postal mail. "Registration and Re-registration": There you will find an overview of all documents you need to upload for the enrollment. "My account balance": There you will find the amount of the semester to be paid, the reason for payment and the bank details of TU Berlin. MINTgrün orientation program The MINTgrün orientation program offers you the opportunity to get to know the bachelor's programs at TU Berlin over two semesters. Applications are only accepted for the winter semester. If you are interested in the MINTgrün orientation program, please submit your application with all the required documents via the application portal of TU Berlin. For your application, select "Bachelor's degree" as your „Abschlussziel", then select „BA zulassungsfrei 1.FS" as the "Bewerbungsangebot" and the bachelor's program "Physics." Within the application portal of TU Berlin, you then indicate that you would also like to be enrolled in the MINTgrün orientation program. If you have applied on time and in the correct form, you will receive admission and simultaneously enroll in the bachelor's program Physics and MINTgrün orientation program . Bachelor's degree program with a teaching option If you are interested in a bachelor's degree program with a teaching option at TU Berlin, you should initially only apply for the major subject. You will choose your minor subject during the enrollment process, and it will then be assigned to you. Re-registration at TU Berlin If you were previously enrolled at TU Berlin and would like to apply for the same bachelor's program with the same study and examination regulations, then you are regarded as a re-registrant. Please note the special information for re-registration . Application for a higher degree semester Expand all Collapse all Degree semester placement If you have already earned study and examination credits and would like to apply for a higher degree semester, you need a "degree semester placement" ("Fachsemestereinstufung") in a higher degree semester in addition to all other required application documents. Here you can find out how to obtain a „ degree semester placement ". Selection process The selection process in a higher degree semester of a restricted admission bachelor's program (with NC) begins mid-September each year for the winter semester and mid-March each year for the summer semester. How to apply? Expand all Collapse all Application for a first degree Prospective students with foreign educational certificates need a preliminary review documentation ("VPD") from uni-assist as proof of their university entrance qualification. Apply for the VPD at uni-assist as early as possible, because processing of your VPD application can take 4 to 6 weeks. Only if you are applying for the first semester of a bachelor´s program with restricted admission (with NC), you must register with hochschulstart.de . You will receive an applicant ID (BID) and a BAN, which you must enter in the application portal of TU Berlin. Prioritisation: The maximum number of applications that can be submitted to hochschulstart.de for all universities in Germany is twelve. The order of your applications (prioritisation) is very important. If you are offered a place at a university, any other offers will immediately and irrevocably lapse, depending on the coordination rules. The correct prioritisation is your sole responsibility. If you do not actively change the prioritisation, the order in which you submit your application will also be the order of prioritisation. Further information can be found in the information on prioritisation and in the online help section at hochschulstart.de . Register on the application portal of TU Berlin. Applications can be submitted via the application portal of TU Berlin until the end of the respective application deadline . The process is paperless and takes place online. In the „Checklist for application and registration" you will find the link to the application portal of TU Berlin and an overview of the documents to be uploaded for the application: Checklist for application for a bachelor's program and MINTgrün orientation program -> Prospective students with an INTERNATIONAL university entrance qualification Please note the maximum number of possibly applications. The number of possibly applications depends on your nationality: Nationals of countries OTHER than the Europaen Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway and stateless persons: You may only submit a maximum of three applications to TU Berlin: - for one bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) for the first degree semester (International advance quota) - for one bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) for a higher degree semester - for one bachelor's program with open admission (no NC) for the first or higher degree semester Nationals of the European Union, Iceland , Liechtenstein or Norway: You may only submit a maximum of five applications to TU Berlin: - for up to three bachelor's programs with restricted admission (with NC) for the first degree semester - for one bachelor's program with restricted admission (with NC) for a higher degree semester - for one bachelor's program with open admission (no NC) for the first or higher degree semester Here you will find detailed explanations about the status of your application .
