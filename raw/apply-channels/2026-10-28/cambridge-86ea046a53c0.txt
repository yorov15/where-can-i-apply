Cambridge Application (International) My Cambridge Application is an additional form that you need to complete if you're applying to study an undergraduate degree at Cambridge. Applicants to the Graduate Course in Medicine do not need to complete My Cambridge Application. Once you submit your UCAS application, you'll get an email from us within 48 hours with instructions and a link to your My Cambridge Application. The form is personalised, so we'll only ask you the relevant questions for your application. For most applicants, the deadline will be 22 October 2026 (6pm UK time) . If you're a mature student applying in our January round , the My Cambridge Application deadline is 20 January 2027 (6pm UK time). If you're applying for the Foundation Year, you can visit the Foundation Year website to find out more about what happens after you've submitted your UCAS application. Before you start the form You should prepare any documents and information you need in advance. For 2027 entry, Cambridge will not download or review any additional documents uploaded to UCAS, so you will need to provide documents required for Cambridge via My Cambridge Application or via the College you applied to. You may need: £60 for the application fee if you're applying from outside of the UK, Ireland, Channel Islands or Isle of Man. Some applicants won't need to pay this fee. Check whether you need to pay A transcript . Getting a transcript can take several weeks, so we recommend asking for it as early as possible. Details of the topics you studied at high school, if you're leaving school this year or you left last year. For some courses, you may be asked to discuss these at interview. Find out more about what to expect at interview Scores and dates of any English language tests you've taken, such as IELTS Scores and dates of your SATs, if they're required qualifications for your application, for example if you're applying from the USA where we'd usually ask for a combination of AP tests and the SAT and/or ACT. If you've taken the SAT alongside other qualifications that would be sufficient for entry on their own (eg A levels or IB), you don't need to declare your results and they won't be considered A Cambridge specific personal statement. This is optional. For example, if you are applying to a course that is only offered at Cambridge, you can give information about why you want to study that course specifically Details of how your studies will be funded, if you're an international student Completing My Cambridge Application form General tips: The form takes about one hour to complete, but don't leave it to the last day as you will need to prepare some information and upload a photo Your answers are saved automatically You can return to complete your application at any point before the deadline. You can change your answers before you submit the form. Make sure to check them before you submit the form Check the help text in the form if you're not sure how to answer a question View previews of My Cambridge Application form: Document Preview of My Cambridge Application (UK) Document Preview of My Cambridge Application (International) Additional personal statement You can write an extra personal statement for My Cambridge Application. This is optional, but we recommend that you add a Cambridge-specific statement if the course you're applying for is significantly different from your other UCAS choices. You'll have 1200 characters for your Cambridge-specific statement. This is much shorter than your personal statement. You could use the statement to: highlight what interested you in the course you're applying to explain why you want to study a particular Cambridge course if you're applying to a course that is only offered at Cambridge tell us about the ways you're exploring your subject interests (if you haven't been able to cover this fully in your UCAS personal statement) You should not: repeat information that you included in your UCAS personal statement use the statement to explain why you applied to a particular Cambridge College Find out what Cambridge looks for in a UCAS personal statement . Help with passwords, signing in, and technical issues If you're not getting our emails, or they are going to your junk or spam folder, add the following email addresses to your email contacts or safe sender list: applicationhelp@ug.admin.cam.ac.uk no-reply@apply.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk If you forget your password or sign-in details, you can reset your password. If you don't get an email after you reset your password, wait a few minutes and check your junk or spam folders. If you can't submit the form, check that you've completed every question in all of the sections. Completed sections will say 'completed' and completed questions will have a tick icon next to them. You should then be able to submit the form. If your email or any other contact information changes, you can update your details in the UCAS Hub or by contacting UCAS customer services . Get help with the My Cambridge Application form Please contact applicationhelp@ug.admin.cam.ac.uk if: you don't get an email 48 hours after submitting your UCAS form the link to My Cambridge Application in your email doesn't work you lose the email we've sent you with the link to My Cambridge Application you can't complete the form online for any reason If you notice an error in the information you've given after submitting the form, you can email: your chosen College or the College you've been allocated to applicationhelp@ug.admin.cam.ac.uk if you made an open application and have not yet been allocated to a College In your email, you must include your full name, UCAS personal ID number, and the course you applied for.
