International Higher Education Entrance Qualification Admission Requirements Admission requirements for applicants with an international university entrance qualification for bachelor's programs and the Preparatory School Admission Requirements First apply, then enroll University entrance qualification ("HZB") and preliminary review documentation ("VPD") Information on special qualifications Admission Requirements ... for admission to a bachelor's program or the Preparatory School are: submission of the application within the application deadline , a university entrance qualification certificate, verified by preliminary review documentation from uni-assist, language skills at the level required by TU Berlin, and application in the proper form (application process, applications and documents, official certification and translation) First apply, then enroll Regardless of your nationality, with an international university entrance qualification you must always apply to TU Berlin. This also applies if you are interested in a bachelor's program with open admission (no NC) or if you are already enrolled at TU Berlin and would like to switching to a different bachelor's program. You cannot enroll without prior application and admission. Please bear in mind that you are responsible for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of your application documents and their submission within the deadline. It is not possible to extend the application deadline or submit documents after the end of the application deadline. Incomplete applications or applications that have not been submitted in due form by the end of the application period cannot be considered and will be rejected. There is no "conditional admission" or "provisional admission" at TU Berlin. You will only be admitted if you have submitted your application in full and in due time, fulfill all requirements, and study places are available. Application for the Preparatory School Application for bachelor's programs University entrance qualification ("HZB") and preliminary review documentation ("VPD") Expand all Collapse all What is a university entrance qualification? If you would like to study at TU Berlin, you have to be able to prove that your school education qualifies you = university entrance qualification ("HZB"). Eligibility may already be based on your school leaving certificate. However, it may also result from the combination of school leaving certificate, university entrance exam and/or university studies you have acquired in your home country. Please note: Time already spent studying at a German university is not a factor in the awarding of a university entrance qualification. If you cannot be admitted directly to the desired bachelor's program at the TU Berlin because your educational certificates only permit admission to the Preparatory School ("Studienkolleg"), you must take an assessment test ("Feststellungsprüfung") at the Preparatory School before starting your bachelor's program (indirect university entrance qualification). Depending on your educational certificates, you may either take up studies in all subjects (general university entrance qualification) or only specific subjects (subject-specific university entrance qualification). Please check here to see which bachelor´s program or focus course at the Preparatory School matches your subject qualification (PDF) . How do I check my university entrance qualification? Please check anabin whether your university entrance qualification certificate entitle you to study directly or only to attend a Preparatory School in Germany. Here you will find the assessment proposals of the Central Office for Foreign Education ("Zentralstelle für ausländisches Bildungswesen/ZAB") for the respective application semester. Search under "Schulabschlüsse mit Hochschulzugang" and/or "Bildungswesen" by the country of your educational system. On the uni-assist website you can also find out quickly and simply if you are qualified to study in Germany using your educational certificates. Direct university entrance qualification anabin valuation: "Direkter Zugang" (direct admission) "für alle Fächer" (all subjects) or "fachorientiert" (subject-specific) You can apply for direct admission to a bachelor's program. Indirect university entrance qualification anabin valuation: "Feststellungsprüfung/Studienkolleg" (assessment test/Preparatory School) "für alle Fächer" (all subjects) or "fachorientiert" (subject-specific) You can apply to the Preparatory School to take the assessment test. Application for direct admission to a bachelor´s program is only possible after passing the assessment test. No university entrance qualification anabin valuation: "Keine Zulassung" (no admission) You have acquired neither an indirect university entrance qualification nor a direct university entrance qualification. You can find options for acquiring a German university entrance qualification on the website of the responsible Berlin's Senatsverwaltung (Second-chance Education) "VPD": How to prove my university entrance qualification If you have acquired foreign educational certificates, you will need a preliminary review documentation ("VPD") from uni-assist as proof of your university entrance qualification ("HZB"). The VPD is a certificate containing information on the educational certificates you presented to uni-assist, how uni-assist evaluates these educational certificates, and how uni-assist rates your grade in the German system of grades. Apply for the VPD for TU Berlin at uni-assist as early as possible, because processing of your VPD application can take 4 to 6 weeks. Here you will find the current processing times from uni-assist . You can apply for the VPD at any time, regardless of the application semester you select at uni-assist. The VPD is valid for one year after the date of issue. Without a VPD issued for TU Berlin, it is not possible to apply to TU Berlin! uni-assist charges handling fees for creating the VPD which must be paid before your application can be processed. After positive verification, uni-assist will issue the VPD available to download in your My assist account under "My Inbox". Once you have received the VPD for TU Berlin, apply directly via the application portal of TU Berlin by the end of the respective application deadline . In the „ Checklist for application and registration " you will find the link to the application portal of TU Berlin and an overview of the documents to be uploaded for the application. How to apply for preliminary review documentation ("VPD") at uni-assist: Log on to the online portal My assist on the uni-assist website. Select „Technische Universität Berlin" and the current application semester. Select the following degree program: "Bachelor and Preparatory School". (Please note: If you wish to apply for a bachelor's program and a master's program at TU Berlin at the same time for the current semester, you must apply for the VPD at uni-assist for the "Master's program".) Select the first degree semester or the higher degree semester. Upload all foreign educational certificates required for the evaluation of a university entrance qualification in their original language to uni-assist. If your educational certificates were not issued in German or English, you must also upload a certified translation into German or English. Incomplete and/or provisionally issued educational certificates cannot be considered. In principle, you must upload the following educational certificates to uni-assist: Secondary-school final transcript and school-leaving certificate, where available, including summary of subjects and grades and/or Proof of completed studies, including an overview of subjects and grades as well as an average grade calculated by the institution of higher education. Please note the information on applying for Second degree advance quota . Additionally, if required: Semi-annual and annual reports from the final two to four years of school including overview of subjects and grades Proof of a university entrance examination or attendance of a pre-university course Assessment test certificate („Feststellungsprüfung") from a state or state-recognized German Preparatory School + your foreign educational certificates qualifying you for indirect university entrance Proof of successfully completed academic years at institutions of higher education in the form of overviews of subjects and grades APS certificate, if you have acquired Chinese, Indian or Vietnamese educational certificates You can find more details about the required educational certificates at anabin and in the country-specific information provided by uni-assist . Binding decisions regarding the recognition of foreign educational certificates can only be made after verification by uni-assist. TU Berlin does not conduct any prior checks. Please note: uni-assist does NOT check language certificates for TU Berlin. These must be uploaded directly and only via the application portal of TU Berlin. If you are currently attending the Preparatory School at TU Berlin, please contact the TU Berlin Preparatory School directly for information regarding proof of VPD. Information on special qualifications Expand all Collapse all Assessment test certificate ("Feststellungsprüfung") from a Preparatory School With the assessment test certificate ("Zeugnis über die Feststellungsprüfung") from a state or state-recognized Preparatory School in Germany, you have acquired a direct subject-specific university entrance qualification. It is only vaild for bachelor's programs that are assigned to the subject-specific focus course specified in your assessment test certificate. Please check here to see which bachelor´s program or focus course at the Preparatory School matches your subject qualification (PDF) . The assessment test certificate must qualify you for admission to a bachelor´s program at a university in Berlin. If it is only valid for universities of applied sciences, a university other than TU Berlin or for a federal state other than Berlin, it cannot be considered. The assessment test certificate is not a German Abitur or a German university entrance qualification. To apply to TU Berlin, you need a VPD issued for TU Berlin by uni-assist. This is because the assessment test certificate is only valid in combination with your foreign educational qualifications, which are also listed on your assessment test certificate. In addition, the new overall grade is calculated by arithmetically averaging the average grade of your foreign educational certificates and the average grade of the assessment test. Please note: An application for a bachelor´s program at TU Berlin not compatible with the focus course indicated on your assessment test certificate is only possible if you can prove that the focus course indicated on your assessment test certificate is compatible with the bachelor´s program you wish to study as offered in the federal state where you took your assessment test. Valid proof can be provided by an official document from a university in the federal state where you took your assessment test. International Baccalaureate (IB Diploma) In order for an IB diploma to be accepted as a direct university entrance qualification certificate, the requirements of the " Vereinbarung über die Anerkennung des International Baccalaureate Diploma/Diplôme du Baccalauréat International " (current resolution of the KMK on the recognition of the IB diploma) must be fulfilled for the relevant semester of application. This includes, among other conditions, that all subjects in the IB program were taken for two consecutive years. In order to review whether all subjects were taken continuously, you must submit not only the IB diploma certificate but also your transcripts from your last two years of school with an overview of your subjects and grades. The country of acquisition of the higher education qualification certificate is Switzerland, regardless which country you acquired the IB diploma in. At anabin you will find the IB Diploma under the keyword "International Baccalaureate". If you have not yet received your final IB diploma/your IB results during the current application period, you can still apply for a VPD. You can find out what you need to submit online on the following uni-assist website -> Assemble your documents -> International qualifications -> IB Diploma The European Baccalaureate The European Baccalaureate ("Europäisches Abitur") from special European Schools is treated as a German educational certificate as long as an additional certificate is submitted pursuant to the annex of the KMK resolution of 14.06.2018. This certificate must contain the average grade converted to the German grading system. Additionally, you require proof of German language skills. Here you will find essential information about applying and enrolling with a German university entrance qualification . Professional Qualification If you do not have a school-based university entrance qualification but have a professional qualification, you can apply under certain conditions pursuant to Section 11 Berlin Higher Education Act (Berliner Hochschulgesetz - BerlHG).
