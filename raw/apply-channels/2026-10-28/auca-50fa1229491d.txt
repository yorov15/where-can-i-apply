ADMISSIONS EXAM SCORES Official English Test Certificate (only one English test certificate must be submitted): AUCA Test of English Language Proficiency or Official TOEFL Certificate (paper-based or internet-based) - REPORT TO AUCA INSTITUTION CODE 0419 or Official IELTS Certificate - REPORT TO AUCA INSTITUTION CODE 8306 FOR UNDERGRADUATE APPLICATIONS AND 8305 FOR GRADUATE APPLICATIONS Official Admission Exam Results (only one exam certificate must be submitted): AUCA Admission Exam or SAT Reasoning Test (SAT I) - REPORT TO AUCA INSTITUTION CODE 5825 or ACT Test Plus Writing - REPORT TO AUCA INSTITUTION CODE 5479 HIGH SCHOOL DOCUMENTS Copy of high school diploma or vocational education certificate or if currently enrolled in high school, a document from current school certifying that an applicant is in the final year of study (Note: Upon admission to AUCA, the original copy of graduation certificate or diploma with appendix must be submitted to the Office of Admissions). Official copies (notarized photocopies) of any and all high school and university transcripts / marksheets (Note: Upon admission to AUCA, applicants who have graduated from educational institutions outside of Kyrgyz Republic must present a document confirming they have obtained the equivalent of the Kyrgyz Republic's state diploma of secondary education, equivalent to 11 years of school. Upon arrival to the university, the document issued by the foreign academic institution and its notarized Russian translation (including transcripts / marksheets) must be submitted to the Ministry for Education of the Kyrgyz Republic in order to confirm the level and content of study completed. The Ministry will issue a certificate recognizing the applicant's education, which should be submitted to the Office of Admissions to be kept on file). PASSPORT COPY AND PHOTOS Copy of passport information pages and for non-Kyrgyz citizens living in Kyrgyzstan, a copy of the Kyrgyz Republic visa page (international students must check in with the International Affairs Office within 2 days of arrival to submit copies of their Kyrgyz Visa and complete Ministry of Internal Affairs registration paperwork) 2 COLOR PHOTOGRAPHS (3 x 4 cm - white background) ORIGINAL OF REPUBLIC TEST CERTIFICATE ( KYRGYZ CITIZENS ONLY ) It is required for Kyrgyz citizens to submit an original of their Republic Test results. It is important that the Republic Test is taken in the year of applying , and the main test score is not less than the one set by the Ministry of Education for the year of applying. Submitting the certificate will not guarantee your admission to AUCA.
