Required for Questbridge Applicants See QuestBridge page Required for First Year Students Common Application or Apply Coalition with Scoir* Northwestern supplement (with additional writing supplements) Official secondary school transcript (s) from all schools attended and school report from most recently attended school Counselor recommendation At least one teacher recommendation Early Decision Agreement (for Early Decision applicants) Official proof of English language proficiency (for applicants whose first/primary language is not English or whose secondary schooling has not been in English) Accepted English proficiency exams: Duolingo English Test (DET) IELTS, including IELTS Indicator TOEFL iBT, including TOEFL iBT Special Home Edition but not TOEFL ITP Plus for China Solution Non-refundable $75 application fee or fee waiver Required for Transfer Students Common Application or Apply Coalition with Scoir (including Northwestern supplement) Official university transcript(s) from all universities attended, must demonstrate at least one year of full-time undergraduate study Official secondary school transcript(s) from all schools attended and final transcript noting graduation College report Academic evaluation Transfer Early Decision Agreement (for Early Decision applicants) Non-refundable $75 application fee or fee waiver Optional and Program-Specific First-Year and Transfer Applicants SAT and/or ACT scores Program-specific applications/materials for applicants to: Bienen School of Music Integrated Science Program (ISP) Mathematical Methods in the Social Sciences (MMSS) School of Communication Musical Theatre Certificate Program More About These Elements Identities and Affiliations Additional guidance based on your background(s).
