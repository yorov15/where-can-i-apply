At the University of Nottingham, we believe excellence deserves reward. If you achieve AAA grades or your international equivalent , you will automatically be awarded a scholarship of £5,000 to be deducted from your first year tuition fees. Enrol. Confirm grades. Automatically receive £5,000 scholarship. No scholarship application needed. Eligibility You must: be an international fee-paying student be starting year one of a full-time undergraduate degree programme (RQF level 4) that is UK campus-based (excludes Bachelor programmes in Veterinary Medicine and Surgery, Pharmacy, Medicine or Foundation Year) be studying towards one of the following awards: BA, BSc / MSci, BEng / MEng, LLB, BArch, MMath, or MNutr have achieved AAA grades or international equivalent , assessed at the point of successful admission be self-funded, and not in receipt of any other University award or sponsorship by an external body The university reserves the right of awarding scholarships and some exceptions may apply. See full terms and conditions . Students progressing to the University from Kaplan (University of Nottingham International College or Digital Pathways) are not eligible for this scholarship scheme. However, students may be eligible for Kaplan - University of Nottingham specific scholarships.
