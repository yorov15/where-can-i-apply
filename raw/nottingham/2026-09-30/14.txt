Tuition fees 2027/28 The UK Government has confirmed that the maximum tuition fee for full time UK undergraduate and Initial Teacher Training students studying in England will increase to £10,050 for the 2027/28 academic year. The university will charge the maximum approved fee, which is an increase of £260. For more information, please read our FAQs.
