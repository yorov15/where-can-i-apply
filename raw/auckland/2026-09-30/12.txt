Home Ngā akoranga | Study Scholarships and awards Types of scholarships Scholarships for international students You are currently on: Undergraduate scholarships for international students Undergraduate scholarships for international students All faculties University of Auckland International School Leaver Scholarship All faculties A Scholarship of up to $20,000 toward compulsory tuition fees for new international students who have attended a New Zealand secondary school or kura, and who are enrolling in the first year of an undergraduate degree at Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland. University of Auckland Malaysian Accommodation Award A $5,000 Award to assist an international student from Malaysia with the costs of accommodation in a Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland Hall of Residence. University of Auckland Foundation Pathway High Achiever Award All faculties An Award of up to $20,000 towards compulsory tuition fees to assist high achievers from our Foundation Pathway partners who are enrolling in their first semester of study. University of Auckland Pathway Partner High Achiever Award All faculties An award of up to $10,000 towards tuition fees for international students from our Pathway Partner NCUK enrolling in the first year of an undergraduate degree. University of Auckland ELA High Achiever Award ELA An Award of $5000 for ELA FCert EAP students who wish to enrol in undergraduate studies at the University of Auckland. University of Auckland Summer Research Scholarships All faculties and LSRIs Summer Research Scholarships are a great way to gain valuable research experience, work with leading researchers at the University of Auckland, enhance your career opportunities and help you think about pursuing postgraduate study. International applicants must be enrolled at a New Zealand university. Applications for 2026-2027 have now closed. Chew Liong Kim & Tan Chee Chin Scholarship An $8,000 Scholarship to support a domestic or international student enrolling in the second or subsequent year of an undergraduate degree who has achieved academically but who is experiencing hardships or who may have been disadvantaged due to their personal circumstances. Apply now . Pong, Tams and Ko Scholarship A Scholarship of up to $8,000 to support domestic and international students with disabilities. Apply now . Faculty specific scholarships and awards Faculty of Arts and Education Graeme Edwards Award in Music An Award of up to $8,000 to support international or domestic undergraduate or postgraduate music students undertaking study in organ or early keyboard. Beatrice Ratcliffe Entry-Level Scholarship in Music A Scholarship of up to $5,000 for international and domestic students commencing full-time undergraduate study in the School of Music. Michael Joseph Savage Memorial Award in Music A $4,000 Award to support a domestic or international student enrolled in the second or subsequent year of a Bachelor of Music. Veza Family Entry-Level Scholarship in Music A Scholarship of up to $5,000 to support a domestic or international student enrolled in the first year of a BMus or BMus conjoint degree. Faculty of Engineering and Design Bachelor of Design Game-Changer Scholarship A $5,000 Scholarship to support an international or domestic Bachelor of Design Student with a passion for design who wants to be a game-changer in the field. Colombo Plan Scholarship in Engineering A Scholarship to assist an international student who is a citizen of a Colombo Plan member country with a contribution of $3,000 towards undergraduate or postgraduate tuition fees in Engineering. Business School Dean's Asia Scholarship in the Faculty of Business and Economics A Scholarship to assist international undergraduate students from Asia enrolling at Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland for the first time, undertaking full-time enrolment in the first year of a Bachelor of Commerce, Bachelor of Property or an associated conjoint degree in the Business School. The Scholarship covers the tuition fees for up to 360 points of study. Business School, Faculty of Engineering and Design, Faculty of Science Kiwiplan Scholarship A Scholarship of up to $4,500 for the final year of undergraduate study in Computer Science, Information and Technology Management, Information Systems or Software Engineering. Faculty of Law Marylyn Eve Mayo Endowment Scholarship A $6,000 Scholarship to support an international or domestic student enrolled full-time in Part III of an LLB who is experiencing financial hardship. Other scholarships and awards Many other University of Auckland scholarships are open to international students. Search our comprehensive scholarships database for an up-to-date list of all of the scholarships and awards available to future and current University of Auckland students.
