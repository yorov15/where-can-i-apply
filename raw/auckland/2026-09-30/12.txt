Home Ngā akoranga | Study Applications and admissions How to apply You are currently on: Undergraduate application closing dates Undergraduate application closing dates It is important to get your application in on time. Late applications Applications will be accepted after the closing date for most programmes but will be considered on the basis of academic merit, if places are available. International students The application process should be started as soon as possible so there is enough time to apply for a visa. Special admission Applications must be received no later than the published closing date and no later than 1 December to be considered for special admission. Applications received after 1 December will only be considered if places are available.
