All fees are subject to annual reviews and indexation. This means that they may increase each year during your studies. International students are also required to pay the Services and Amenities Fee . Fees for students enrolled in a Study Abroad program may vary. International students will be issued with an invoice via ANUHub for each semester or session that they enrol in. If changes are made to course enrolment/s, additional invoices may be issued. Invoices must be paid by the relevant payment deadline. Students who are sponsored and have registered their sponsor with the Enrolments and Fees Office will have their invoice sent directly to their sponsor.
