Please check your application data carefully before submitting your application as programme choices cannot be changed once you have submitted your application. Application Fee The application fee, which is non-refundable and non-transferable, should be settled upon the submission of an application; otherwise, the application will not be further processed. While the recommended payment method is by Visa or Master Card, you can also click here to view the details on other accepted payment methods. Please note that other than the specified application fee, you are not required to pay any money to any persons in connection with your application. Confirmation of Your Application After submitting your online application, you will be given a PolyU application number as an acknowledgement of receipt. Please quote this number in your future correspondence with us. Upload Additional Documents Applicants are required to upload scanned copies of the academic certificate(s)/ transcript(s) for qualifications as claimed in their application, as well as any supporting document(s) specifically required for the applying scheme / programme. Failure to upload the required documents may affect your chances of admission or prolong the application process. If these documents are not in English or Chinese, you are required to provide official translated documents in English; otherwise your application may not be considered. If your results are not yet available upon submission of your application, please indicate the forthcoming date(s) which the results will be available. For applicants taking IB or GCE/ International A-Level, please ask your school to send us the predicted public examination results for our preliminary assessment . Once the results are available, please update the relevant sections of the online application form and upload the document(s) indicating your latest results. Applicants may login to eAdmission anytime to upload the transcript/ score report for new qualifications or academic results. "Academic Referee" Section For documents like "Predicted Score Report" or "reference letter" which are considered as "confidential" by your school / institution, please (1) Fill in "Academic Referee" section under eAdmission and then a system generated email will be sent to your designated school counselor or referee. They are able to provide the predicted scores via the online system*, or (2) Ask them to send us the relevant documents by email or by post (Student Admissions and Scholarships Office, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Kowloon, Hong Kong, China). * The system generated email will only be sent to the counselor / referee after applicants submitted the application and paid the application fee. Counselors / Referees are normally given one to two weeks to submit the required documents via the designated link. If counselors / referees need more time to prepare for the documents, they could do so by submitting the supporting information to our university by email. In the email, the applicant's name and PolyU application number must be clearly quoted for keeping track of record. For non-local international applicants, please send to polyu.intl@polyu.edu.hk . For non-local Mainland applicants, excluding current Gaokao applicants, please send to polyu.njee@polyu.edu.hk . Interview Arrangement While interviews are generally not mandatory, Academic departments may notify shortlisted applicants of the interview arrangement individually by email. Applicants are advised to attend the interviews as scheduled since re-arrangement may not be possible. If they fail to do so, their applications may not be further considered. Application Result If you are given an offer or a conditional offer, you will be informed via email and/or SMS. You may also check your application result online. If there is no news by mid-July, you should assume that your application is unsuccessful. Acceptance of Offer Upon receiving an admission offer from our University, you may confirm your acceptance by indicating your decision on the electronic Notice of Offer via eAdmission system and paying the initial fee by the deadline stipulated on your debit note. While you may receive more than one offer, you can only accept one offer at any time and each programme choice can only be accepted once. If you have accepted an earlier offer, the initial fee paid will be automatically transferred towards the later offer upon indicating your acceptance of the later offer. Submission of Student Visa Application After your offer acceptance is confirmed, PolyU will help you apply for a student visa. Please submit the completed student visa application package to PolyU within one month after paying the initial fee (i.e. first debit note). Click here for further details. Verification of Qualifications As the final stage to confirm your admission, you will be required to send the original official transcripts and score reports directly to our University, or ask the official bodies to send us electronic transcripts or certificates. Failure to do so or any discrepancies found may lead to nullification of your offer and registration in our University.
