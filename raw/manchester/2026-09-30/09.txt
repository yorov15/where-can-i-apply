Applying to Manchester There's a lot to consider when you decide to apply for university - we want to help you through the process and make it as straightforward as possible. On the following pages, you'll find everything you need to apply for undergraduate study at Manchester. Below you will find the key application dates for applying for 2027 entry. Application timeline for 2027 entry 1 September 2026 Completed applications can be submitted to UCAS You can send your completed application to UCAS for consideration. 15 October 2026 (18:00 UK time) Medicine and Dentistry deadline for 2027 entry UCAS deadline for applications for Medicine and Dentistry courses for equal consideration. Your academic reference must be complete before your application can be submitted to UCAS. You should ensure that you meet your school/college deadline which is likely to be earlier than this date. 13 January 2027 Equal consideration deadline Applications for all 2027 entry undergraduate courses, except those with a 15 October deadline, should arrive at UCAS by 18:00 (UK time) on 13 January 2027. Your academic reference must be complete before your application can be submitted to UCAS. You should ensure that you meet your school/college deadline which is likely to be earlier than this date. This is UCAS's 'equal consideration' deadline - this means course providers must consider all applications received by this time equally. Applications received after this deadline will be considered where vacancies exist, once the on-time applications have been considered. 25 February 2027 UCAS Extra opens Applicants who have used all five choices and are not holding any offers may be able to add another choice in their application. 12 May 2027 University decision day If you applied by 13 January 2027, this is the deadline for universities to decide whether they will make you an offer. You'll be given a deadline by UCAS to accept or reject the offer. 2 June 2027 Replying to an offer If you received your university decision by 12 May 2027, you must reply to the offer by today. 30 June 2027 Application deadline for 2027 Any applications received after 18:00 (UK time) on 30 June 2027 will be automatically entered into Clearing. 2 July 2027 Clearing opens Clearing opens today for eligible applicants, and vacancies are displayed on our website and in the UCAS search tool. Applicants can release themselves into Clearing It will close on 18 October 2027. Find out more about clearing 14 July 2027 University decision on offer If you applied by 30 June 2027 and you're still awaiting a decision, the Universi ty has until 14 July 2027 to decid e if we'll make you an offer. 21 July 2027 Reply deadline for applicants If you received your offer by 14 July 2027 , you must reply by today.
