Understanding student finance When calculating the cost of coming to university, you will need to consider two main elements - tuition fees and living costs while you study. The information you submit on your application to study at the University will be used to determine your tuition fee status. Find out more about tuition fee status We have provided guidance on what you need to know about additional finance and living costs but you can also find further information on the government's dedicated website for student finance .
