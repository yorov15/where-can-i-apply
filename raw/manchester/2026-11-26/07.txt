2027/28 entry Social responsibility lies at the heart of our University. For September 2027 entry, we are offering Humanitarian Scholarships to help people fleeing conflict and persecution take up the opportunity to study at Manchester. These scholarships are intended for individuals at immediate risk who have been forced to flee their homes as a direct result of armed conflict. For 2027 entry, two scholarships are available for three-year undergraduate degree programmes only . What the scholarship covers If you're selected, the scholarship will cover: your tuition fees; living expenses, including accommodation and food; visa costs. Key dates for 2027 entry Apply for a course via UCAS From 1 September 2026 Apply for the scholarship From 3 June 2027 Scholarship application deadline 30 June 2027 at 5pm (BST) Results announced By 19 July 2027 Start your studies September 2027 Who can apply for the scholarship? You must meet all of the following criteria to apply: you're at serious risk because of war or conflict; you've had to leave your home country, or move within it, in the last three years because of conflict; you've been unable to start or complete a university degree; you've never studied in the UK or EU; you hold an unconditional offer for an eligible three-year undergraduate course at The University of Manchester starting in September 2027; you can explain why you want to study your chosen course; you've performed well in your previous studies. You cannot apply for the scholarship if: you're currently living in the UK or the EU; or you're a national of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar or Sudan. Due to the UK Government's visa brake policy , the University is unable to sponsor nationals of these countries for a Student visa up to and including September 2027. English language requirements You don't need to show proof of your English language ability when you apply through UCAS. However, you must have met the English language requirements for your course and hold an unconditional offer before you apply for the scholarship. The English language requirements you need to meet are stated in your offer letter. The University does not accept the Duolingo English Test. See our course profile pages and English language requirements for information on accepted English tests and scores. Please note: the scholarship does not cover pre-sessional English courses at the University. How to apply Step 1: Apply for a three-year undergraduate degree programme Apply for a three-year undergraduate course starting September 2027 at The University of Manchester via UCAS . Please note: the University cannot cover the cost of the UCAS fee. Courses you can apply for: You can apply for most three-year undergraduate degrees - browse our courses here . Check the qualifications accepted for your country on our international webpages . You can submit your UCAS application from 1 September 2026. Courses you cannot apply for: medicine, dentistry, nursing or architecture degrees; courses with a Foundation Year - you must have the academic qualifications required for direct entry to a three-year undergraduate degree course. Important: some courses may not accept applications after the UCAS equal consideration date (13 January 2027). If you apply after this date, you must email the Admissions Team to check if they are still accepting applications. You can find their email address on the 'overview' tab of each course page. Find out how to apply for an undergraduate course . Receiving an academic offer does not guarantee that you'll receive a scholarship. Step 2: Apply for the Humanitarian Scholarship You can view a sample application form to help you prepare. A link to the application form will be added here before applications open on Thursday, 3 June 2027. Application deadline: Wednesday, 30 June 2027 at 5pm (BST). You must hold an unconditional offer for an eligible three-year undergraduate course at The University of Manchester when you submit your scholarship application. Selection process No interview is required. We will only assess applications from candidates who hold an unconditional offer for an eligible three-year undergraduate course when the scholarship application deadline closes. We will email all applicants with the outcome of their application by Monday, 19 July 2027. All decisions are final. Alternative funding opportunities Other scholarships and funding at the University can be found on our funding pages . If you're an asylum seeker or refugee in the UK, check the Article 26 Sanctuary Seeking Scholarships . If you're on a Ukraine visa scheme , you can apply to Student Finance England for tuition and living costs. Have a question? Ask Atlas, our ChatBot.
