We meet the full demonstrated need of admitted students, making it possible to attend Wesleyan regardless of one's financial circumstances. Wesleyan is committed to reining in growth in the cost of attending over time. The University offers a three-year option to make it possible for students to graduate in six semesters, with summer academic work. This accelerated course of study is not for everyone, but it can save families over $50,000. A Wesleyan education can be surprisingly affordable. For more information, see our financial aid website. Tuition $75,916 The fee charged to all undergraduate BA students Food and Housing $21,660 The fee charged to all undergraduate BA students; encompasses room and dining costs. Sixty-five percent credit applied for off-campus students. Student Activity Fee $404 A fee set by students to support student activities Matriculation Fee $300 A one-time fee charged to all first-time students to support orientation activities Green Fund Fee $50 An opt-out fee set by students to fund sustainability projects on campus (Opt out request found in the 'Student Finances' dashboard in Workday through the end of drop/add) Fall semester standard charges will be billed the week of July 6, 2026 with payment due August 14, 2026. Health insurance will be billed in July, if applicable. Remember to complete the annual health insurance requirements on the Health Center website by August 15th. Spring semester standard charges will be billed the week of January 4, 2027 with payment due January 20, 2027 (prior to the start of classes). All other billing will contain miscellaneous charges/credits. Payment is due within 30 days of each charge assignment. A $200 late payment penalty may apply if payment is not received by the due date. ________________________________________________________________________ FEES FOR INDEPENDENT STUDY, EDUCATION IN THE FIELD AND CREDIT FROM UNACCREDITED INSTITUTIONS Students engaged in independent study, enrolled only in education in the field or taking a course at an unaccredited institution while on leave during the fall or spring semester will pay a per-credit tuition charge based upon one-quarter of the prevailing semester tuition rate. For the 2026-27 academic year the per-credit charge is $9,490. Students engaged in independent study, enrolled only in education in the field or taking a course at an unaccredited institution during the summer will pay a per-credit tuition charge based upon the prevailing summer tuition rate. For summer, 2026 the per-credit charge is $4,528. For summer, 2027 the per-credit charge is $4,745 ________________________________________________________________________ FEES FOR COMMUNITY SCHOLARS Individuals in this category will pay a per-credit tuition charge based upon one-quarter of the prevailing semester tuition rate. For academic year 2026-27 the per-credit charge is $9,490. ________________________________________________________________________ FEES FOR HONORS THESIS ENROLLMENT ONLY Eligible seniors in their final semester will pay a per-credit tuition charge based upon one-quarter of the prevailing semester tuition rate. For academic year 2026-27 the charge will be $9,490.
