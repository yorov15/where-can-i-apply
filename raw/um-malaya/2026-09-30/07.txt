Thus, we are pleased to invite full-time undergraduate students to apply scholarships, financial assistances, and loans offered by government, private and statutory body agencies as followings: The PTPTN Education Loan Scheme (PTPTN) The PTPTN Education Loan Scheme was established for the purpose of providing educational loans to students pursuing studies at local Higher Education Institutions (IPT). This education loan will allow students to finance all or part of their tuition fees and living expenses throughout their period of study. This scholarship aims to ease the financial burden of higher education while offering a clear and secure career pathway upon graduation.
