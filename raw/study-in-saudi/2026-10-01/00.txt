Frequently Asked Questions Explore our most common questions and answers to help you understand registration, application procedures, and platform usage. What stages does a scholarship application go through? After submission, the application is forwarded to the selected educational institution for review. Once approved by the institution, it is processed by both the institution and the Ministry of Education to complete the final admission procedures. I made an error in my application data. How can I edit it? Contact information can be updated at any time. However, other application data cannot be modified after submission. How can I get a scholarship? You can apply for a scholarship through the educational institution where you wish to study by submitting an admission application. The institution will review your application and issue an admission decision accordingly. What does an "Under Process" application status mean? This status indicates that your application is currently under review by the relevant educational institution and is being processed according to the approved procedures. Please continue to monitor your application status on the platform until a final decision (acceptance or rejection) is issued. I cannot update my new passport information. What can I do? You can update your passport number only if your account is new and no applications have been submitted yet. If you have already submitted an application using your current passport number, it cannot be modified.
