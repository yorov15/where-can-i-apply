What stages does a scholarship application go through? After submission, the application is forwarded to the selected educational institution for review. Once approved by the institution, it is processed by both the institution and the Ministry of Education to complete the final admission procedures. I made an error in my application data. How can I edit it? Contact information can be updated at any time. However, other application data cannot be modified after submission. I'm trying to register but I see "You cannot apply as you have a previous scholarship." What should I do? A student cannot receive two scholarships for the same academic degree. I forgot my password / username. What should I do? You can recover your password by clicking "Recover Password" and following the on-screen instructions. Can I edit my data after registration and submitting the application? Students may update qualification data and certain other specified fields. Basic data such as residency number, passport or Gulf ID number, gender, and nationality cannot be updated. What are the requirements for a long-term educational visa to study in Saudi Arabia? The following are required: The applicant must be 16 years of age or older. Submit a valid passport with at least 6 months of validity remaining. Obtain admission from a licensed and accredited educational institution in the Kingdom. Prove financial ability to cover tuition and living expenses, or provide a financial guarantee. Medical approval from the guardian and companions of the visa holder for those under 18 years of age. What are the requirements for a short-term educational visa to study in Saudi Arabia? The following are required: The applicant must be 16 years of age or older. Submit a valid passport with at least 6 months of validity remaining. Obtain admission from a licensed and accredited educational institution in the Kingdom. Prove financial ability to cover tuition and living expenses, or provide a financial guarantee. Medical approval from the guardian and companions of the visa holder for those under 18 years of age. Where can I apply to study in Saudi Arabia? You can apply through the "Study in Saudi Arabia" platform at: https://studyinsaudi.sa What are the requirements to study in Saudi Arabia? The following are required: The applicant must be 16 years of age or older. Submit a valid passport. Submit a medical report proving freedom from infectious diseases. Attested certificates and required documents per the educational institution's requirements. How can I get a scholarship? You can apply for a scholarship through the educational institution where you wish to study by submitting an admission application. The institution will review your application and issue an admission decision accordingly. What does an "Under Process" application status mean? This status indicates that your application is currently under review by the relevant educational institution and is being processed according to the approved procedures. Please continue to monitor your application status on the platform until a final decision (acceptance or rejection) is issued. Can I change the email registered on the Study in Saudi Arabia platform? Students registered with an email address cannot edit their registered email. However, students residing in the Kingdom can update their email through their personal profile. Can I cancel an application that is "Under Process"? A student may cancel a study application only if no action has been taken on it by the educational institution. I cannot update my new passport information. What can I do? You can update your passport number only if your account is new and no applications have been submitted yet. If you have already submitted an application using your current passport number, it cannot be modified. Can I request the complete deletion of my profile from the platform? A student can submit a support ticket to request the deletion of their profile from the system. Why was my study application rejected? The application was rejected because one or more of the admission requirements for the program or educational institution were not met. Can I update my data after the university returns my application? Students may update qualification data and certain other specified fields. Basic data such as residency number, passport or Gulf ID number, gender, and nationality cannot be updated. My aptitude or achievement test scores are not showing in my account. What can I do? Click the "Update Data" button in your personal profile to retrieve the required information. Quick Links Home Institutions Scholarships & Visa Guide Living in Saudi Get Help FAQ Need Help?
