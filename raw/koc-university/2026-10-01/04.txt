The statistics presented below are drawn from last year's international undergraduate applicant and enrolled-student data and are designed to offer prospective applicants a reliable, research-based overview of our admissions landscape. Our goal is to provide meaningful context-not to predict individual outcomes, but to support you in making informed decisions about your academic future. Please note that the information shared here is for guidance purposes only. While Koç University sets clear minimum academic requirements for undergraduate entry, meeting these standards does not guarantee admission. Each application is reviewed holistically, and the weight given to standardized test scores, national exam results, high school GPA, personal achievements, English proficiency, and supporting documents varies according to an applicant's unique background and academic profile. This individualized assessment is a key strength of our admissions philosophy and allows us to evaluate talent in its many forms. Key Facts and Figures Application Requirements Koç University remains deeply committed to fostering a diverse, dynamic, and globally engaged student community. Each year, we welcome students from a wide range of cultures, education systems, and socioeconomic backgrounds. Our internationalization efforts continue to expand, as reflected in the growing number of countries represented on campus, the increasing presence of first-generation university students in our cohort, and the broader range of entry qualifications now recognized by our admissions committees. As Türkiye's top-ranked university in major global rankings, Koç University seeks not only excellence but also equity and opportunity. We encourage prospective students to review the data below in that spirit-using it as a helpful reference while understanding that every application tells its own story and is evaluated with care, attention, and respect for individual merit.
