Chancellor's Scholarship for Elite Students (100% Discount on Tuition Fees) The United Arab Emirates University offers this scholarship as one of its leading initiatives to attract top-performing high school graduates. This competitive scholarship aims to support academic and scientific excellence and is awarded to new students with outstanding academic performance, providing a full tuition waiver in accordance with the criteria and conditions specified below: Eligibility Conditions: Achieving a minimum of ( 98% ) in the General Secondary School Certificate (Grade 12) or its Fulfilling all general admission requirements and the specific qualifications of the intended college/academic program. Demonstrating English proficiency through one of the following approved tests with a minimum score: TOEFL ITP: 590 points TOEFL IBT: 105 points TOEFL CPT: 233 points Academic IELTS: 7.5 points Demonstrating mathematics proficiency for all colleges ( except College of Business and Economics, College of Humanities and Social Sciences, College of Education, and College of Law) by obtaining one of the following scores: SAT-MATH: 730 points ACT-MATH: 32 points Demonstrating Arabic proficiency (for the College of Law only ) by achieving a minimum score of ( 6.5 ) in the Al Ain Arabic Language Test . Scholarship Benefits: A 100% discount on tuition fees only (the scholarship does not cover housing & admission application fees). Scholarship Continuation Conditions: Maintaining a cumulative GPA of no less than ( 3.8 ) throughout the study duration at UAEU. Registering for a minimum of ( 15 ) credit hours per regular semester. The student must not hold another scholarship. The duration of the scholarship must not exceed the normal semesters required for graduation according to the study plan of the academic program, unless otherwise stated. Any change in the academic program, major, or study load, including course withdrawal, may affect the continuation of the scholarship. The scholarship continues until graduation, provided the student complies with the mentioned conditions and terms. The university reserves the right to revoke the scholarship in case the student commits any academic or behavioral violations that breach the integrity code or university regulations. Application Process: New international students: graduates of public and private schools and international students may apply via the university's electronic admission system ( link ). Current students: enrolled students at the university may submit an appeal or upgrade request for the scholarship via the portal ( link ). Application Deadlines: First semester of the academic year: May, June, and July. Second semester of the academic year: September, October, and November. Chancellor's Scholarship for Children of UAEU Employees (100% Discount on Tuition Fees) In appreciation of the efforts of academic and administrative staff in supporting the educational journey, and in affirmation of the university's role in promoting family stability and encouraging employees' children to pursue higher education, the United Arab Emirates University offers the Chancellor's Scholarship for Children of University Employees. This scholarship aims to enable children of faculty members and staff to continue their undergraduate studies through a full tuition waiver, in accordance with the approved criteria and conditions. Eligibility Conditions: Completion of High School (Grade 12) Certificate, or its equivalent, no more than one year prior to the date of submitting the admission application. Fulfilling all general admission requirements and the specific qualifications of the intended college/academic program. Providing a "To Whom It May Concern" letter issued by the Human Resources Department at the university, stating the parent's job title and confirming their employment status at the time of application. Scholarship Benefits : A 100% direct discount on tuition fees only (the scholarship does not cover housing & admission application fees). Scholarship Continuation Conditions: Maintaining good academic standing throughout the study duration at UAEU. The student must not hold another scholarship. The duration of the scholarship must not exceed the normal semesters required for graduation according to the study plan of the academic program, unless otherwise stated. Any change in the academic program, major, or study load, including course withdrawal, may affect the continuation of the scholarship. The scholarship continues until graduation, provided the student complies with the mentioned conditions and terms. The university reserves the right to revoke the scholarship in case the student commits any academic or behavioral violations that breach the integrity code or university regulations. Application Process: Applications are submitted via the university's online admission system ( link ). Application Deadlines: First semester of the academic year: May, June, and July Second semester of the academic year: September, October, and November Rules & Guidelines General Rules A student may be awarded only one scholarship and cannot combine more than one scholarship. The scholarship covers tuition fees only. The scholarship covers minor programs if they are included in the approved college plan. The scholarship does not cover repeated courses. The duration of benefiting from the scholarship must not exceed the number of regular semesters required for graduation according to the academic program's study plan. The academic performance of scholarship recipients is evaluated at the end of each semester, and this evaluation is a condition for the continuation of the scholarship. The scholarship applies from the time it is granted; it is not retroactive, and the student is not entitled to claim it for previous academic years. No financial refund may be obtained in exchange for the tuition discount. The student is required to sign the scholarship contract before benefiting from it, and signing the contract is considered acknowledgment of full compliance with the conditions. Students enrolled in the university who meet the conditions may apply for the scholarship within a period not exceeding one academic year from their high school graduation. The university reserves the right to amend or cancel the scholarship conditions with the approval of the University's Board of Trustees, provided that the student is notified of any changes. Cancellation, Appeals, and Reapplication The Scholarship Committee is authorized to upgrade a scholarship to a higher category if the student's cumulative GPA improves during two consecutive academic semesters, based on a request submitted by the student and in accordance with the approved rules and criteria. The university may temporarily suspend or permanently cancel a scholarship in accordance with approved regulations and systems, if the student fails to meet the continuation conditions, including GPA and credit hour requirements, or commits any academic or behavioral violations. The scholarship is canceled if the student changes the college or the academic program; the student may submit a new application for a scholarship, which will be considered in accordance with the prevailing rules and criteria at that time. The student has the right to submit an appeal to the Scholarship Committee to reinstate the scholarship, provided they present evidence of an exceptional circumstance that affected their academic performance and meet the previous scholarship criteria. The relevant committee will review the appeal, and its decision is final. If the appeal is accepted, the scholarship will be reinstated starting from the next semester. The student may submit a new application for another scholarship in case of cancellation for any reason, subject to the evaluation of their academic performance and the approved criteria. The student must submit the appeal to the relevant committee within ( 30 days) from the date of the official cancellation notice. Appeals submitted after this period will not be considered unless documented reasons are provided and accepted by the committee, in accordance with the university's approved policies.
