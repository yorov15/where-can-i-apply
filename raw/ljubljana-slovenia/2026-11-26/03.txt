Degree Programmes The degree programmes and enrolment requirements for the first year of bachelor's and single-cycle master's degree programmes are published in the electronic publication of the Call for Enrolment in Undergraduate and Single-Cycle Master's Degree Programmes for the 2026/2027 Academic Year (Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation, 30 January 2026, Vpis v visokošolski študij | GOV.SI). These programmes are conducted by higher education institutions of the University of Ljubljana and other universities, as well as by public and independent higher education institutions with concession. Information on the individual degree programmes of the University of Ljubljana and on studying is available to applicants on the websites of the faculties and academies: www.uni-lj.si/en/university/member-faculties-and-academies-ul. To enrol in certain degree programmes, candidates are required to pass tests of ability, capacity and skills. The degree programmes at the University of Ljubljana that require such tests, and their test dates, are published in the Call for Enrolment and on the website www.uni-lj.si/en/study/bachelorsand-single-cycle-masters-study/call-for-enrolment. The degree programme in English: University Degree Programme in Business and Economics, full-time study (UL, School of Economics and Business) - the programme can be selected during the application periods for citizens of non-EU member states and for Slovenians abroad. Enrolment Places For citizens of non-EU member states, the number of available places for the first application period is published in the Call for Enrolment and on the website www.unilj.si/en/study/bachelors-and-single-cycle-masters-study/call-forenrolment/citizens-of-non-eu-countries-who-have-completedsecondary-education-abroad. The candidates with international/ temporary protection and its applicants in RS, international citizens with permanent residence in RS, who (or their guardians) are tax residents of the RS, and non-EU citizens who completed secondary school and the Matura in Slovenia should apply for enrolments places for citizens of the RS and citizens of member states of the EU. Enrolment places for Slovenians abroad are announced separately. Slovenians abroad who are citizens of a non-EU Member State may submit only one application in both the first and second application periods, either for places designated for Slovenians abroad or for places designated for citizens of non-EU Member States. Interview for Applicants from Countries with a Higher Migration Risk Applicants from countries with a higher migration risk who require a visa to enter the EU must complete a direct interview to assess their eligibility for enrolment before further evaluation. Detailed information will be provided by the faculties and the academies after the application deadline. First Application The first application period: between 18 February and 18 March 2026. Candidates can submit an electronic application in Slovenian or English (portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/) in all application periods. It can be submitted: with the user's name and password (SI PASS), with a qualified digital certificate, smsPASS, e-ID card, or by means of an AAI account. There is no age limitation for enrolment in RS. In the first application period, candidates may list up to three degree programmes in order of preference. They will be admitted to the highest-ranked programme for which they meet the requirements and reach the minimum points. Number of Applicants and Enrolment Limits in the First Application Period The number of applications for individual degree programmes and information on enrolment limits will be published on 25 March 2026 on the website www.uni-lj.si/en/study/bachelorsand-single-cycle-masters-study/call-for-enrolment. Proof of Meeting the Enrolment Requirements Applicants with completed secondary education abroad must attach all required documents electronically to the e-application at portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/: • E-copy/scan of the final certificate(s), representing general requirement for access to higher education in the country of issue without legalization. The copy shall be verified by the issuing institution at the request of the higher education institution. If the higher education institution is unable to verify the authenticity of the document, applicants will be asked to send original of the final certificate(s) legalized according to the system of legalization in the country of issue. • E-copy/scan of certified translation of the final certificate(s) into Slovenian or English. If a translation is required, the applicant will be asked for it subsequently in the e-application. • E-copies/scans of annual report cards, transcripts, diploma supplement or other evidence on the content and duration of education (for all years of pre-university education) and certified translation into Slovenian or English for the last two years of (upper) secondary education. If a translation of the certificate(s) is required, the applicant will be asked for it subsequently in the e-application. • E-copy/scan of short chronological description of the entire education, relevant to the enrolment application and recognition procedure of foreign education, prepared by the applicant. • E-copy/scan of the passport page with personal data. Attachment is optional unless requested by the institution to verify identity or citizenship. • E-copy/scan of the evidence of the Slovenian language examination at B2 level if enrolling at the University of Ljubljana on degree programmes of Academy for Theatre, Radio, Film and Television, Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of Medicine (exam passed at ALTE-approved Centres for Slovenian Language Examinations), Faculty of Law, Faculty of Health Sciences, the Professional Degree programme in Business Administration at the School of Economics and Business, the Administration programme at the Faculty of Administration, the Journalism programme at the Faculty of Social Studies, the Slovenian Language programme, Psychology, Comparative Literature and Literary Theory at the Faculty of Arts, Pedagogical Mathematics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, and the Interdisciplinary Professional program Biopharmaceutical Engineering (UL FFA, BF, FKKT, FRI). Adequate certificates of B2 level Slovenian: • certificate of passing the B2 level Slovenian language exam (for the UL Faculty of Medicine at ALTE-approved Slovenian Language Examination Centres) • final certificate of primary school in RS or of a foreign primary school with Slovenian as the language of instruction • Matura certificate or certificate from the final year of a secondary vocational programme showing completion of the Slovenian language subject • certificate of completion of bilingual secondary education (in Slovenian and in a foreign language) or a certificate of completion of foreign secondary education with Slovenian as the language of instruction • diploma of higher education in RS and a declaration, that the applicant has completed the study programme in the Slovenian language The International Baccalaureate Diploma (IB) and The European Baccalaureate Certificate are considered equivalent to the general Matura certificate in the RS, and no recognition procedure for foreign education is required for them. The candidates can attach an electronic printout from the website where the results of the International Baccalaureate were published. For candidates completing the International or European Baccalaureate in Slovenia this year, the secondary school will submit the diploma to the Higher Education Application and Information Service. Candidates holding an IB certificate must submit the required documents themselves and undergo the recognition procedure. Deadlines for Submitting Documents All required documents must be attached by candidates as soon as possible after they receive them. The decision on the recognition of foreign education may take up to two months from the date the application is considered complete. Candidates with incomplete applications or recognition requests will be individually notified of the required documents and the deadline for submission via the eapplication at portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/. First application period: by 31 July 2026. Applicants who have already completed secondary education must submit their final certificate(s) by 18 March 2026. Other evidence of having fulfilled the entry requirements (e.g. proof of B2 Slovenian language proficiency) must be submitted by 31 July 2026 at the latest. Applicants whose final certificates are issued after 1 August and whose recognition decision is issued by 22 August will also be included in the selection procedure. Second application period: by 3 September 2026. Slovenian Language Exam Candidates may take the Slovenian language exam at any language school in Slovenia or abroad that offers it on a level B2. For the UL Faculty of Medicine, the exam must be passed at ALTE-approved Slovenian Language Examination Centres. At the University of Ljubljana, the exam can be taken at the Centre for Slovene as a Second and Foreign Language. Contacts: centerslo.si/en/, phone: +386 1 241 86 47, e-mail: centerslo@ff.uni-lj.si Selection Procedure If the number of applicants significantly exceeds the number of available enrolment places, a decision on the limitation of enrolment will be made by 25 March 2026 at the latest. Candidates will be selected according to the criteria of each degree programme specified in the Call for Enrolment. The evaluation of secondary school achievement from foreign certificates is carried out by VPIS UL. Decision on the Result and Enrolment Each candidate can access the decision on the result of the selection procedure through their user account in the eapplication at portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/. First application period: by 28 August 2026. Enrolment deadlines: candidates must enrol by 30 September 2026. Faculties and academies will invite them to enrol either in writing or electronically. Second Application The second application period: 2 and 3 September 2026 Candidates submit an electronic application in Slovenian or English (portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/): with the user's name and password (SI PASS), with a qualified digital certificate, smsPASS, e-ID card, or by means of an AAI account. The admission procedure is conducted by faculties and academies, which publish available enrolment places on their websites at the start of the application period. The candidates may submit a maximum of three different applications in which they may indicate one study wish. The second application period is open to candidates who have: • a decision on the recognition of foreign education issued in the first application period at the University of Ljubljana this year and are not enrolled after the selection procedure in the first application period, • a decision on the recognition of foreign secondary educational qualifications issued before 2005, • the International Baccalaureate Diploma or the European Baccalaureate Certificate, • finished secondary school in SFRY before 25 June 1991, • decision on the recognition of foreign secondary education issued by other universities and independent higher education institutions in Slovenia with permanent validity. Tuition Fee Non-EU citizens must pay a tuition fee, usually in full at enrolment for the current academic year. The fee does not include compulsory health insurance, which must be arranged before the start of studies. Tuition fee for full-time studies is waived for citizens of countries with which the Republic of Slovenia has concluded bilateral or multilateral agreements on educational cooperation or reciprocity (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, and Kosovo); for citizens with permanent residence in Slovenia who themselves of their guardians are tax residents of Slovenia; persons with international or temporary protection and its applicants; and non-EU citizens who completed secondary education and the Matura in Slovenia. Slovenians abroad who are scholarship holders of the Public Scholarship Fund of the RS are also exempt from tuition fees. All students are required to pay the tuition fee for part-time studies. The pricelist is available on the website www.unilj.si/en/study/tuition-fees-and-other-study-contributions.
