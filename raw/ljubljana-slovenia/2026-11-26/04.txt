Interview for applicants from countries with a higher migration risk Applicants who are citizens of countries with a higher migration risk and who require visas to cross the European Union external borders, must undergo a direct interview on their suitability for enrolment in the degree programme in accordance with the enrolment conditions before further professional and administrative consideration. Candidates will receive more detailed information about the interview from higher education institutions after the application deadline.
