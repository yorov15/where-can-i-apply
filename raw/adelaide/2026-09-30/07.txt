If you're an international student who did not meet the admission requirements for your chosen degree, our pathway programs may be suitable for you. You'll develop your skills and become prepared to study at Adelaide University, whether you have already pursued tertiary education or have no prior qualifications. Each program provides a range of different subject choices, intakes and experiences. You should consider the available options carefully to decide which programs is best suited to your individual circumstances. English Language Centre (ELC) The English Language Centre (ELC) provides English language pathways to Adelaide University for students who don't meet the English language requirement for direct entry. ELC also offers a range of General English language programs, as well as short courses for international students and visiting groups. English Language Centre (ELC) Eynesbury College Eynesbury College provides pathways into a wide range of Adelaide University bachelor degrees and select master's degrees. You can choose to study Foundation Studies, Diploma or Graduate Certificate programs. Eynesbury College Kaplan International College Adelaide Kaplan International College Adelaide provides a range of pathways into Adelaide University. You can choose to study Foundation Studies Programs, Degree Transfer Programs or Pre-Master's Programs as pathways to undergraduate and postgraduate degrees at Adelaide University. Kaplan International College Adelaide TAFE SA You can package a variety of Technical and Further Education SA (TAFE SA) programs with Adelaide University programs and gain credit towards your Adelaide University studies.
