Library Current students Staff Portal Giving Contact us Compare degrees Search English Language Centre (ELC) Home Study English Language Centre Study Show menu Providing English language programs to international students Get your English skills up to scratch at Adelaide University's English Language Centre (ELC). Our Pre-enrolment English Program (PEP) and General English for Academic Purposes (GEAP) courses are designed for international students and visiting groups who want to improve their English. If you're preparing to launch into a university degree but don't meet the minimum English requirements or are looking to enhance your professional skills - we'll ensure you get the support you need. Pre-enrolment English Program (PEP) The ideal preparation for students that have not met the minimum English requirements to enter their Adelaide University undergraduate or postgraduate program. Discover PEP General English for Academic Purposes (GEAP) A versatile program for students looking to improve their English skills. The program can be completed for professional development purposes, to earn credit towards a degree at your home university, or as a pathway into an Adelaide University degree. Explore GEAP Why choose the ELC? We'll work with you to achieve your goals within and beyond the classroom. As an ELC student you'll benefit from: Quality teaching: our passionate teachers will give you the individual attention and support you deserve. Excellent facilities: learn in our modern Hub Central complex, a space designed for students with 24-hour weekday access to computers, meeting rooms, cafes and restaurants in the heart of Adelaide. Valuable connections: meet students from around the world and access ELC Social Club activities and events to help you make new friends while experiencing Australian culture. Tangible results: we are committed to helping our students achieve their goals beyond the classroom, whether that be university study, work or personal interests.
