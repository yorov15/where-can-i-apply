Library Current students Staff Portal Giving Contact us Compare degrees Search Scholarships Adelaide Merit Scholarship (15%) Home Study Scholarships Study Duration Standard full-time duration of the recipient's undergraduate or postgraduate Coursework Program Residency International Student What best describes me New student Level of study Undergraduate, Postgraduate Type of scholarship High achiever/academic The Adelaide Merit Scholarship provides a 15% tuition fee reduction for international students starting an undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degree and is automatically awarded at the time of offer to applicants who have achieved a credit average and shown strong academic commitment. This scholarship supports international students commencing undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degrees who are committed to their academic achievement. Eligibility To be eligible for this scholarship you must: be an international student be applying to study a CRICOS registered bachelor degree (minimum 72 units / 1.5 years equivalent) after the application of any credit/RPL, or a CRICOS registered master degree (minimum 48 units / 1 year equivalent) after the application of any credit/RPL, at Adelaide University; satisfy one of the following in the qualification used as the basis of admission to the Adelaide University degree: Undergraduate only: A minimum ATAR of 75 for students who have completed the Australian Senior Secondary Certificate of Education (SSCE) (or international equivalent for any other secondary certificate). Successful completion of a competency-based vocational qualification in Australia with an overall non-graded pass. Undergraduate and postgraduate: A minimum overall Grade Point Average (GPA) of 4.5 (on a 7.0 scale) for an award completed at Adelaide University (or with a comparable score for a qualification completed at an Australian University). A minimum overall Grade Point Average (GPA) of 5.0 (on a 7.0 scale) or comparable. A minimum overall Grade Point Average (GPA) of 4.5 (on a 7.0 scale) or equivalent from a program completed at an approved Adelaide University pathway provider. not be applying to study one of the Excluded Programs (see below); not be an Australia Awards recipient; and not be an International Sponsored Student entitled to a tuition fee waiver equal to or greater than the scholarship Important information Minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) to maintain: The scholarship recipient must maintain a minimum Program Term GPA of 4.5 on a 7.0 scale (Minimum GPA) in reviews of academic progress carried out each compulsory Study Period. The scholarship may be suspended if the recipient's GPA falls below the Minimum GPA. If a recipient's overall GPA falls below the Minimum GPA, the scholarship will not be applied to the next invoice. If a recipient's GPA returns to the Minimum GPA or above, the scholarship will recommence and will be applied to the next invoice. How to apply No scholarship application is required. When you or your Education Agent submit an application to study, Adelaide University will automatically assess your eligibility for the scholarship. If you are successful, you will be awarded the scholarship on your Offer of Admission, and your tuition fees will be reduced.
