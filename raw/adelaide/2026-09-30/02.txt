We're here to help You've chosen your degree - now it's time to apply! Whether you're preparing your application yourself or applying through one of our authorised Education Agents, we're here to help at every step. All international applications to study an undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degree at Adelaide University are submitted online through our Online Application System . Please note that a non-refundable application fee of AUD$150* applies for every application submitted to Adelaide University. We've put together the information below to help you prepare, submit and track your application status. Apply now Before you apply Expand all Prepare your documentation You'll need to provide certified copies of your official academic documents as part of your application. This includes academic transcripts and graduation certificates for all secondary school, tertiary studies and professional qualifications (e.g. short courses). If your official documents are in a language other than English, you will also need to provide certified translations of these documents, in addition to certified copies of the original non-English documents. To find out more about document certification, contact us . Check the application closing date for your chosen degree Before you apply, it's a good idea to familiarise yourself with the application closing date for your chosen degree. Please note some degrees have different application deadlines. We recommend you check the specific deadline for your chosen degree on the program page. Find my degree Review any additional academic entry requirements It's important to make sure you understand the eligibility criteria and entry requirements for your chosen degree. Look out for any prerequisites or assumed knowledge subjects, as well as additional entry requirements like interviews or auditions. For more information, review the entry requirements for your chosen degree. Learn about English language requirements International students seeking admissions to coursework degrees must meet our minimum English language requirements. You can demonstrate your level of English language proficiency by providing the results of an accepted English language test (e.g. IELTS, TOEFL iBT). In some cases, you can also meet minimum language requirements using your previous academic qualifications (e.g. International Baccalaureate) or through practical application of your English skills in a professional environment. If you don't meet the minimum English language requirements, you can still apply for a program. If you meet the academic eligibility requirements for admission, we can send you a conditional offer packaged with a Pre-Enrolment English Program (PEP) pathway through our English Language Centre. Visit the entry requirements webpage for or more information. Apply now Please ensure you have the following documents ready to upload: Certified copies of your academic transcripts and graduate certificates (if applicable) for all secondary school and tertiary studies (if these are in a language other than English, certified translations must also be provided) A copy of your passport. Please note that a non-refundable application fee of AUD$150 applies for every application submitted to Adelaide University. You may also need to provide: Professional qualifications (e.g. short courses) or professional memberships you may have Official English test results from IELTS, TOEFL iBT, Pearson or Cambridge CAE/CPE or another approved objective test Syllabus of subjects if you wish to be considered for advanced standing (credit transfer). Expand all Applications requiring an Education Agent You may be required to apply through an Adelaide University authorised Education Agent. We have a wide network of Education Agents around the world that can assist you with the preparation and submission of your application, as well as your student visa application. Students from the following regions require an Education Agent to apply: The Middle East North Africa Sub-Saharan Africa South Asia (Bangladesh, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka) Philippines Latin America. Find an Education Agent near you Changing your Education Agent If you have lodged an application though an Adelaide University authorised Education Agent but want to change to a different partner, you'll need to contact us to nominate a new agent. Applications without an Education Agent If you're applying without an Education Agent, you can lodge your application directly through our Online Application System. Apply now Next steps Once you've submitted all required documents and paid your application fee, our International Admissions team will begin assessing your application. The team will carefully review your application and provide an outcome as soon as possible. Right now, we're experiencing a high volume of applications, so it may take up to two weeks for us to complete the assessment. If your application requires a credit assessment, the process may take a little longer - up to four weeks. Once your application has been assessed, Adelaide University will contact you or your Education Agent with an outcome. If you are successful, you'll receive your offer directly by email or via your Education Agent. * Note: Application fees will not be charged for research degrees, 100% online programs, and non-award programs, such as Study Abroad and Exchange or standalone English Language Centre programs.
