For: First-time applicants (new or current students): Must have achieved at least TOCFL Level 3 (B1 CEFR equivalent) or above. Re-applicants (current students, second time or above): Must have reached at least TOCFL Level 3 and advanced by at least one level (except for those who have already reached the highest level, Level 6). Must have an average academic score of at least 70 and a conduct score of at least 80 in the previous academic year (two semesters). Award: 50% tuition and fees waiver for one academic year (two semesters). Apply: New international students: Apply together with the admission application. Current students: Applications are accepted annually in July . Learn More >> Regulation >> TOCFL Test Registration Fee Subsidy Provides annual TOCFL test registration subsidy for eligible international students and overseas Chinese. For: NTUT international students who have completed the TOCFL and demonstrate improvement in Chinese proficiency or achieve at least B1 (TOCFL Level 3) on their first attempt. Award: Up to NTD 2,000 per student for the TOCFL test fee (rolling review). Apply: Submit required documents to OIA after obtaining the official TOCFL score report. Contact: Amanda (hwang@ntut.edu.tw) , Overseas Student Advising Section, Office of International Affairs.
