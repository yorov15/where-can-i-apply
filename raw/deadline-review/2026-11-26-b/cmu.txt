Applying Early Decision is for students who have done their research and know they want to make Carnegie Mellon and Pittsburgh their home. You'll receive your admission decision by mid-December, giving you more time to celebrate your accomplishments, plan for college and enjoy your senior year.
Early Decision Timeline
Deadline: November 2
Notified by: December 15
Regular Decision is for students who are still considering their options and aren't ready to make an enrollment decision. It’s common for students to apply Regular Decision to a number of institutions and then compare universities.
Regular Decision Timeline
Deadline: January 4
Notified by: No later than April 1
