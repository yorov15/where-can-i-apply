Interviews and InitialView
We recommend that first-year applicants request an interview, though interviews are optional and not guaranteed. These conversations are a great way to showcase your academic and personal successes, demonstrate your knowledge of Rice, and learn more about the student experience.
Applicants can request an interview through their Rice Admission Student Portal after submitting their application to Rice. All interviews for the 2026 - 2027 application cycle will be conducted virtually. A member of the Rice Alumni Volunteers for Admission (RAVA) team or a current student will meet with you to learn more about your accomplishments and academic interests, as well as answer your questions about life at Rice.
Applicants are assigned to an interviewer based upon availability – we do not accept requests for specific interviewer types. Interviews conducted by alumni and current Rice student interviewers are weighted equally in the evaluation process. In admission cycles of high demand, we may be unable to fulfill all interview requests. However, our inability to match you with an interviewer will not impact your admission decision.
