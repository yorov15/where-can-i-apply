Singapore-Cambridge GCE A Levels Apply Now (Applications are now closed) Here is an overview of the admissions requirements for GCE A-Level holders: General At least 18 years of age as of 1 January of the year of matriculation, OR Have completed a minimum of 12 years of formal education beginning with Primary 1 or equivalent Academic 3 H2 content-based subjects and General Paper (GP) taken in the same sitting A pass grade in Project Work Mother Tongue Language (applies to Singaporeans and Permanent Residents only) Meet one of the following requirements set by the Ministry of Education (MOE): Achieved 'S' grade or higher in H1 MTL or General Studies in Chinese or H2 Mother Tongue Language & Literature (MTLL) taken at 'A' level, or an MOE-approved MTL-in-lieu Pass in MTL B syllabus taken at 'A' level Scored D7 or higher in Higher MTL taken at 'O' level Have an MOE-approved MTL-exemption A Mother Tongue subject (Chinese/Malay/Tamil) taken at a separate sitting of the GCE A-Level examination is acceptable. H1 non-Tamil Indian Language (Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu) or H1 Foreign Language (French, German, Japanese) may be taken in lieu of MTL. If you do not meet the MTL requirement, you may still apply to NTU. But note that if we offer you a place, you will be required to fulfil the minimum MTL requirement before you are allowed to graduate. Minimum subject requirements Check the minimum subject requirements and 'A-Level' cut-off grades of the programmes you wish to apply for. How we compute your university admissions score (UAS) In computing your University Admission Score (UAS), we consider the following: For applicants who sat the GCE A-Level examination from 2025 3 H2 content-based subjects General Paper (GP) H1 Mother Tongue Language and/or the fourth H1/H2 content-based subject may be considered if it improves the UAS. For applicants who sat the GCE A-Level examination in 2024 or earlier We will use your Project Work, as well as H1 and H2 content-based subject grades to compute your UAS. H2 Knowledge & Inquiry (KI) can be use in lieu of GP and the H1 content-based subject in the UAS computation. We will compute your UAS using the 90-point scale. We will proportionately weigh that score to the revised 70-point scale. We will not consider H3 programmes in the computation of your UAS. If you have taken H3 programmes, you may be considered for admission under our Aptitude-based Admissions (ABA) scheme. For more information on how we compute your UAS, please refer to our FAQs . International Baccalaureate diploma Apply Now Here is an overview of the admissions requirements for IB Diploma holders, whose diploma is awarded by the International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO). Direct access to IB results We require direct access to your official IB Diploma transcript via the IBO. Access must be granted by: The application closing date of 19 March 2027 for students who sat the November 2026 or earlier exam session End of June for students sitting the May 2027 exam session We will not process your application without direct access. We encourage you to provide access before your IB results are released in January or July, by submitting your transcript requests through your school's IB coordinators. After the results are released, your request will incur a processing fee and must be submitted directly to the IBO via the IBO request for results service . Important information for May exam session If you are sitting for the May 2027 IB Diploma exams, you may submit your application by the closing date of 19 March 2027 and provide your final IB results within three (3) days of their official release in July 2027, except for the following programmes : Medicine Arts (Academic Discipline & Education) Science (Academic Disciplines & Education) Important information for Singapore citizens and PRs only If you have not completed a minimum of 12 years of formal education, please complete this form with the details of your schooling history from Primary 1 and upload it to your application. All Singapore citizens and Permanent Residents applying to NTU must fulfil one of the following minimum requirements under the Ministry of Education (MOE) mother-tongue policy : Obtain a grade D7 for Higher MTL at Singapore-Cambridge GCE O-Level Pass in MTL 'B' or a grade S for H1 MTL or H2 MTL Language and Literature or General Studies in Chinese at Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level, or an MOE-approved MTL-in-lieu* Pass in MTL Language A or Language B at Standard Level or Higher Level at IB Diploma, or MOE-approved MTL-in-lieu* Provide an MOE-approved MTL-exemption* If you do not meet the MTL requirement, you may still apply to NTU without prejudice to your application. However, if you are offered admission and have not been granted an exemption by MOE, you will be required to fulfil the minimum MTL requirement before you can graduate. * For students who have been away from Singapore's school system for some years, we will submit an application for MTL-in-lieu or MTL-exemption on your behalf if you are offered admission. MOE does not accept direct applications. You will need to provide your academic transcript for the last 2 years of studies prior to university admission for MOE's assessment. If you have previously been approved to pursue a MTL-in-lieu or are exempted from MTL, please upload the MOE approval letter to the 'Checklist' after you have submitted your application. If you have misplaced the letter, you must notify us when you submit your application and request a replacement from MOE Customer Service at https://www.moe.gov.sg/feedback/ . Update your application with the letter once you've received it. Minimum subject requirements Check the minimum subject requirements of the programmes you wish to apply for. As our IB applicant numbers are limited, we are unable to provide a meaningful grade profile. English-language proficiency requirements Applicants holding certain high school qualifications are required to submit an approved English language proficiency test, unless otherwise stated under their qualification category. English language proficiency test results must be submitted by the application deadline for the relevant admission exercise. Late submissions will not be considered. NTU accepts the following English language proficiency tests: Test Score IELTS Academic; IELTS UKAI Academic Overall score - 6.5 Writing - 6.5, Speaking - 6.5 TOEFL / TOEFL iBT Home Edition Total Score - 90 or 4.5 Writing - 25 or 4.5 Verbal/Speaking - 25 or 4.5 Pearson Test of English (PTE) Academic Overall score - 55 Speaking - 55, Writing - 59 C1 Advanced (formerly Cambridge English Advanced (CAE)) Overall score - 170 Writing - 170 Speaking - 170 SAT Total score - 1250 Reading and Writing - 600 ACT with Writing in a single sitting Composite - 30 English - 30 Writing - 7 Aptitude Scholastic Test (AST) English Score - 220 For IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, C1 Advanced, and AST , only test scores obtained within two (2) years prior to the application deadline will be accepted. SAT and ACT scores are valid for five (5) years from the test date. Submission of an approved English language proficiency test is mandatory for applicants who hold, or are currently pursuing, any of the following qualifications: PRC Gao Kao Taiwan General Scholastic Ability Test (GSAT) South Korea College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT) or General High School Diploma (Ilbankye Kodung Hakkyo) Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) Vietnam High School Graduation Certificate Thailand Mathayom 6 Indonesia Ijazah SMA Any qualification where English was not studied at both junior and senior high school levels, or where English was offered only as a second language.
