As a full-time undergraduate student, you have to pay tuition and miscellaneous fees for Semesters 1 and 2 of each academic year. These fees are payable as long as you're actively enrolled and have not applied for a leave of absence. Miscellaneous fees help to meet part of the costs of providing insurance, student programmes and amenities, IT services and academic-related services that are not covered by the tuition fee. For full-time undergraduate programmes, the fees are fixed for your entire candidature under a cohort-based system. For detailed guidelines on tuition fees, please click here . To support students, Singapore's Ministry of Education provides tuition grants that cover a substantial portion of tuition fees to eligible students. Tuition Grants Singapore's Ministry of Education (MOE) provides a tuition grant that covers a substantial portion of the full tuition fees to eligible students. If you are eligible, you will only pay the subsidised tuition fee. Expand all Who is eligible and what are the terms of the grant Local and international full-time undergraduate students who gain admission to NTU on a subsidised fee basis are eligible for a tuition grant and GST subsidy from the Ministry of Education (MOE). The grant covers the normal duration of a degree programme. This is usually three or four years, depending on the programme. For students who transfer from other universities or are re-admitted to NTU the tuition grant available will depend on the number of semesters of tuition grant already used for any previous programme(s). For example, if you have spent 2 semesters at a local university, and then transfer to NTU to pursue a 4-year (i.e., 8-semester) undergraduate programme, you will be eligible for the MOE tuition grant for only 6 (i.e. 8 minus 2) semesters at NTU. Permanent Residents and international students who accept the MOE tuition grant are required to sign the Tuition Grant Agreement, which obliges you to work for a Singapore entity for three (3) years upon graduation. How to apply Singapore citizens If you are Singaporean, you do not need to apply for the MOE tuition grant. You will automatically receive a tuition grant, unless you have already used the maximum tuition grant for a bachelor's degree or higher degree at a local university. You have to fill in a tuition grant declaration form after accepting admission into your new programme and obtaining your matriculation number. Permanent Residents and international students If you are a Singapore Permanent Resident or an international student interested in obtaining the MOE tuition grant to subsidise your tuition fees, you have to indicate that you are opting for the tuition grant in your admission application form. If you are offered a tuition grant together with a university place, you will need to indicate whether you accept the offer with or without the tuition grant before the closing date in July . If you accept the admission offer with a tuition grant, you will need to register for the tuition grant after you have obtained your matriculation number, International Student Pass and Singpass. Singapore Permanent Resident or an international students are required to sign the Tuition Grant Agreement, which contractually obliges you to work for a Singapore entity for three (3) years upon graduation. Be ready for your tuition grant application in 2 steps. 1. Get your Singpass ready If you do not already have a Singpass account, register for one as soon as you have your Student's Pass. Refer to Singpass Registration User Guide for Students . 2. Gather your sureties' information and photo identity documents (ID) in advance. TWO sureties of any nationality are required. The guarantors must be aged 21 and above but below 65. They must not be an undischarged bankrupt. Surety must not be a tuition grant/SO applicant or recipient with outstanding tuition grant obligations.
