Awards Important Links Stories Contact us Freshmen Portal Scholarships and awards Recognising academic excellence, celebrating achievements and alleviating financial barriers At NTU, we believe that a university education should not be limited by financial resources. To enable you to pursue your dream without worrying about your finances, we offer a wide range of scholarships. Scholarship opportunities Merit-based Recognising academic excellence Need-based Alleviating financial barriers Talent-based Celebrating outstanding non-academic achievements As part of our commitment to affordability, we also offer numerous financial aid assistance schemes. Explore more How to apply for a scholarship Our scholarship application window follows the same timeline as the admissions application window and applications for scholarships need to be submitted before you know the outcome of your admission application. Some scholarships, such as the University Engineering Scholarship and the Lee Kuan Yew Scholarship to Encourage Upgrading (LKY-STEP) Award are administered by a secretariat that rotates amongst the universities. For the latest secretariat and details on how to apply, please refer to our FAQs.
