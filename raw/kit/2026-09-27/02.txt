Application FAQ Application Deadlines Application deadlines There are different application deadlines depending on admission procedures for the degree program and citizenship of the applicant. Freepik For German citizens, EU citizens and educational residents: Bachelor's degree programs: Limited capacity No limited capacity Winter term: July 15 Winter term: September 15 Summer term: January 15 (only HS) 2 Summer term: March 15 (only HS) 2 Master's degree programs: Limited capacity 1 No limited capacity 1 Winter term: July 15 Winter term: September 30 Summer term: January 15 Summer term: March 31 1 For exceptions, please see chart 2 FS = first semester, HS = higher semester For international, non-EU citizens: Bachelor's degree programs: Limited capacity No limited capacity Winter term: July 15 Winter term: July 15 Summer term: January 15 (only HS) 2 Summer term: January 15 (only HS) 2 2 FS = first semester, HS = higher semester Master's degree programs: Limited capacity 1 No limited capacity 1 Winter term: July 15 Winter term: September 30 Summer term: January 15 Summer term: March 31 1 For exceptions, please see chart All application deadlines are fixed preclusive deadlines if there are no other deadlines mentioned in the statues and regulations of the degree program. All documents need to be submitted to the Studierendenservice by this preclusive deadline (postal mailings need to be received by the Studierendenservice by this date; the date of mailing is of no relevance!). Should the deadline be on a weekend or on a holiday, §3 section 5 of the Hochschulvergabeordnung takes precedence over §193 BGB! Please consult the list of the degree programs below to find out whether a degree program has a limited capacity or not.
