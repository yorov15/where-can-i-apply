Application portal Losverfahren (lottery) WT 2026/27 Paperless application process at KIT Information for Alt-Abiturient*innen The following study programs with limited capacity still have capacity for WT 2026/27, thus the open places will be distributed via a Losverfahren (lottery): Bioengineering B.Sc. Biology B.Sc. Geoecology B.Sc. Information Systems B.Sc. Application deadline for the Losverfahren: October 05, 2026 Every person who meets the application criteria can participate in the Losverfahren via the KIT application portal and apply for a place in the lottery. Application at KIT is a paperless process. All documents need to be submitted online in the password-protected part of the application portal. A submission of paper copies is not necessary. At the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, application deadlines are the same for both Alt-Abiturient*innen (former school graduates) und Neu-Abiturient*innen (recent school graduates)!
