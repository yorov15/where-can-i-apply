Admission quota for the 2026-2027 academic year Division Contract-based School of Computer and Information Engineering (SOCIE) 450 SBL (Business Administration) 125 SBL (Logistics Management) 125 Total quota 700 * The quota is subject to change. Applicants who are admitted to the SOCIE will be assigned to one of two departments (CSE and ICE) by the regulation of IUT after completing the first year of the education program at IUT. Applicants who apply to the SBL MUST choose between Business Administration (125 students quota) and Logistics (125 students quota). Applicants CANNOT apply for both majors at the same time. Among Pre-University students who pass the final examination, up to 120 students (30% of 400) for SOCIE, up to 37 students (30% of 125) for Business Administration, and up to 37 students for Logistics (30% of 125) can be admitted. The admission quota may not be completely filled in case the applicants' scores of the entrance exam (IUT Entrance Examination Admission), the Pre-University final exam (Pre-University Final Examination Admission), or the evaluation of document review and interview (International Student Special Admission / Korean Nationals Residing Abroad Special Admission) are lower than the passing score set by the Admission Committee of Inha University. 2. Eligibility for the 2026-2027 Admission 2.1 IUT Entrance Examination Admission Those applicants who satisfy all of the following are eligible for the IUT Entrance Examination Admission: - domestic or foreign lyceum, college or high school graduates or graduates-to-be in the first half of the year 2026. - those applicants who hold a Korean passport are not eligible for IUT Entrance Examination Admission. Applicants with dual citizenship (Korean and other countries) are regarded as Korean citizens. Those applicants who pass the entrance exam should meet the English Proficiency Requirement: Division Minimum Requirement for Official English Test Score* SOCIE (School of CIE) IELTS : 5.0 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 50 or higher SBL (School of Business and Logistics) IELTS : 5.5 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 71 or higher * Please note that those who have completed secondary education (High school) or higher education (University) taught in English in the country that uses English as their native or legal official language shall be exempted from submitting the official English test score. 2.2 Pre-University Final Examination Admission Those applicants who satisfy all of the following are eligible for the Pre-University Final Examination Admission: domestic or foreign lyceum, college or high school graduates, or graduates-to-be currently enrolled as Pre-University program students. Applicants with a Korean passport are not eligible for Pre-University Final Examination Admission. Applicants with dual citizenship (Korean and other countries) are regarded as Korean citizens. Those applicants who pass the Pre-University Final Examination should meet the English Proficiency Requirements: Division Minimum Requirement for Official English Test Score* SOCIE (School of CIE) IELTS : 5.0 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 50 or higher SBL (School of Business and Logistics) IELTS : 5.5 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 71 or higher * Please note that those who have completed secondary education (High school) or higher education(University) taught in English in the country that uses English as their native or legal official language shall be exempted from submitting the official English test score. The Pre-University students who pass the final exam this year but do not meet the qualification of lyceum/college/high school diploma or equivalent before the beginning of 2026-2027 academic year, will be allowed to enter IUT only after obtaining their diploma from their lyceum/college/high school or equivalent. 2.3 International Students Special Admission 2.3.1 International Students Special Admission Those students who satisfy all of the following are eligible for the International Student Special Admission: domestic (Uzbekistan) or foreign lyceum, college or high school graduates or graduates-to-be in the first half of the year 2026. applicants who hold a foreign passport (other than Uzbekistan). Those students who hold a Korean passport are not eligible for International Student Special Admission. Applicants with dual citizenship (Korean and other countries) are regarded as Korean citizens. Those applicants who pass the International Student Special Admission should meet the English Proficiency Requirements: Division Minimum Requirement for Official English Test Score* SOCIE (School of CIE) IELTS : 5.0 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 50 or higher SBL (School of Business and Logistics) IELTS : 5.5 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 71 or higher * Please note that those who have completed higher education (University) taught in English in the country that uses English as their native or legal official language or domestic (Uzbekistan) higher education (University) taught in English shall be exempted from submitting the official English test score. 2.3.2 Korean Nationals Residing Abroad Special Admission Students who are eligible for the qualification of Korean Nationals Residing Abroad Special Admission for 2026 at Inha University or have completed the entire elementary, middle, high school education outside Korea. Please see the link below - //admission.inha.ac.kr/cms/FR_CON/index.do?MENU_ID=160 Those students who pass Korean Nationals Residing Abroad Admission should meet the English Proficiency Requirements: Division Minimum Requirement for Official English Test Score* SOCIE (School of CIE) IELTS : 5.0 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 50 or higher SBL (School of Business and Logistics) IELTS : 5.5 or higher / TOEFL iBT : 71 or higher * Please note that those who have completed higher education (University) taught in English in the country that uses English as their native or legal official language or domestic (Uzbekistan) higher education (University) taught in English shall be exempted from submitting the official English test score. 3.
