Instructions for paying the tuition fee online Currently, IUT students can transfer tuition fees using the Click.uz , Payme.uz mobile applications. Instructions for the Click.uz App Go to the application, select the section "Education" Find in this section - Inha (by text or by clicking on the logo), the name of the University. Type of payment: ➡️ Contract payment ➡️ For loss of ID card ➡️ Payment for entrance exams. To make a payment, you must enter the ID (personal student ID) Note: it is possible to pay an amount from 200 thousand to 6 million soums. Instructions for the Payme App Go to the application, select the section "Education" Find the Inha logo in this section. Light background of the logo ➡️ payment for students, dark background ➡️ payment is possible for applicants (abiturient) For applicants(abiturient), enter the existing applicant ID and payment amount. For students, enter the student ID, indicate the required payment amount and make the payment. IMPORTANT! Please note that you must enter your ID correctly.
