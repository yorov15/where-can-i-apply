The government grant fully covers tuition fees for the entire undergraduate program, for 4 years. The grant is available to applicants who are successfully admitted to the Korean Program. Eligibility: - Applicants must enroll in Inha University in Tashkent on a contract basis. - After enrollment, students are eligible to participate in the government grants competition. - The national exam (DTM) must be taken in September-October. - Based on the exam results, a ranking is formed, and the top candidates are transferred to tuition covered by the government grant. 2.Academic Scholarship Inha University in Tashkent rewards students with high academic performance. Academic scholarship is available only for students of Korean Program and granted every semester to students with the highest GPA. Each semester, a student ranking is formed based on GPA. The calculation is done separately for each undergraduate year. Number of scholarships per semester: Faculty of SOCIE (Computer and Software Engineering): 4 students receive 100% tuition coverage for one semester 6 students receive 50% tuition coverage for one semester Faculty of SBL (Business and Logistics): 2 students receive 100% tuition coverage for one semester 3 students receive 50% tuition coverage for one semester Requirements: At least 16 academic credits earned in the previous semester GPA: 4.0 or above - 100% scholarship GPA: 3.7 or above - 50% scholarship Scholarships are granted for one semester. Rankings are updated every semester. 3.Scholarships and Grants from Partner Companies and Sponsors Each year, students at Inha University in Tashkent are awarded scholarships and grants from leading companies and organizations. These grants may fully or partially cover tuition fees, provide monthly payments, or be merit-based scholarships. Some scholarships may also include future employment or professional cooperation. Examples of grants and scholarships already received by students: Scholarship from the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan, scholarship from Shinhan Bank, state scholarship of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan, named scholarship in honor of Islam Karimov, grant from Sturgeon Foundation, other corporate and named scholarships. Information about competitions and conditions for receiving such grants is announced during the academic year.
