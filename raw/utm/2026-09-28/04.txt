UTM National Postgraduate Fund Award Description This initiative aims to diversify financial aid channels and attract students to pursue postgraduate studies at UTM in the form of partial tuition waivers amounting to RM 1,000 UTM National Postgraduate Fund Requirements Eligibility Criteria - To be eligible for this financial aid, applicants must meet the following criteria: General Requirements 1) Must be currently in 'Active' status. 2) Must be a Malaysian student. 3) Must not be currently working or receiving any form of financial aid from other sources. Academic Requirements 1) PhD Students : Must be in the 1st to 6th semester and 45 years old or below. 2) Master Students: Must be in the 1st to 4th semester and 35 years old or below. 3) Applicants must have obtained a minimum CGPA of 3.60 (for Master by Mixed-Mode & Taught Course). 4) Preference will be given to students with good publications or an outstanding academic track record. Financial Requirements 1) Students with a household income of RM 5,000.00 and below will be given priority.
