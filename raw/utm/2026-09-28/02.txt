More Information about IEP UTM IEP UTM Session Calendar ELS Language Centres has been a trusted partner of UTM since 2015. The ELS Certified Intensive English Programme (CIEP) is approved by the Malaysian Ministry of Higher Education, allowing university admission without the need for IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE tests. ELS is conveniently located in D07 (CICT Building) on the UTM Skudai campus, and in Kuala Lumpur near KLCC, just 2 km from the UTM KL campus. ELS also offers the flexibility to study online from your home country while you apply for your student visa. Cambridge Linguaskill Test Linguaskill is a 100% online and computer-based test, which enables test takers to be remotely proctored and have their English competency levels tested wherever they are. However, we would like to inform you that UTM will only accept the Linguaskill Online Language proficiency test results if the candidate registers for the test with Malaysian test center specifically under SASBADI (Institution: MY959) which is the exclusive and authorized Linguaskill Agent by Cambridge Assessment English in Malaysia. The decision to use a VPN to register for the Linguaskill test with a Malaysian test center is up to the individual student. * Candidates for Cambridge Linguaskill test make take the online test only if they are located in Malaysia, Singapore and Philippines . Candidates outside these three countries are not permitted to book and take the online test Candidates may click the link below to register for the Cambridge Linguaskill test : https://e4c.sasbadi.com/linguaskill/register/ra-utm1-c1 OTHER EXEMPTION FOR UNDERGRADUATE INTERNATIONAL STUDENT : 1.
