English Proficiency Requirements | Columbia Undergraduate Admissions To be considered for admission to Columbia, you must be comfortable with rapid and idiomatic spoken English. Proficiency can be demonstrated in several different ways, including through English Language Proficiency exams. Please note that demonstration of English language proficiency, including potentially through English Language Proficiency exams, is required. If there are questions around your English language proficiency when applying, you may be contacted for more information. Options Other than English Language Proficiency Exams There are several different ways to demonstrate that you are proficient in English on your application without an English Language Proficiency exam: Your home language is English. Your primary language of instruction at school has been English for the duration of your secondary school career. You earned one or more of the following subsection scores on the SAT or ACT: 700 or higher on the Reading and Writing section of the SAT (either paper or digital administration) 29 or higher on the English or Reading sections of the ACT English Language Proficiency Exams If you meet one or more of the above criteria, you have fulfilled Columbia's English language proficiency requirement and do not need to submit scores from an English Language Proficiency exam. If you do not meet the above criteria, you must take one of the below English Language Proficiency exams and provide your score to Columbia as part of your application. TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) IELTS (International English Language Testing System) DET (Duolingo English Test) Cambridge English Qualifications
