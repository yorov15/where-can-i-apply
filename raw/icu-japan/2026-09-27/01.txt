Average Scores of Successful Applicants (2022-2026) While the minimum requirement for English proficiency is IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 79, there are no such criteria for other standardized tests. The numbers below are not the minimum requirements but just average scores among successful applicants in the past years. Average /Full Marks SAT 1,386 /1,600 ACT (Composite) 30.4 /36 IB Full Diploma 38.3 /45 TOEFL iBT 105 /120 IELTS 7.51 /9.0 *For the 2026 admission cycle, the reported average TOEFL iBT score includes results converted from the new 6-point score scale introduced on or after January 21, 2026. IMPORTANT NOTES: Not only test scores but all the application documents are equally important. The screening committee examines all application documents carefully and holistically. The average TOEFL iBT score includes some results from tests taken on or after January 21, 2026.
