The selection process is highly competitive and only the candidates with the highest scores and the best course track can succeed in passing it. Scholarship amount : €8,000.00 (gross) + exemption from student fees for each academic year. Beneficiaries are only required to pay the regional fee and the revenue stamp per academic year. Duration : The scholarships have a maximum duration of: 2 years for master's degree programmes; 3 years for bachelor's and/or single-cycle degree programmes At the end of the above-mentioned period, the beneficiaries will be required to pay the student tuition fees due until graduation. In order to enter the selection process, candidates: must not hold the Italian citizenship (unless it is a dual citizenship); must not hold a non-Italian secondary school diploma (for those applying to bachelor's degree courses and/or single-cycle degree courses) or a foreign degree diploma (for those applying to master's degree courses); must not be resident in Italy at the time of application; apply to an English-taught degree at the University of Padova within the deadline. The application for the degree course of your interest automatically makes you eligible to compete for the award of the scholarship provided you meet the requirements listed above. Therefore, there is no need to submit a separate application or any additional documentation other than the documents required to apply for the chosen degree programme. The Teaching Committee of each degree course selects the admitted candidates who will be awarded a scholarship, based on academic excellence in their field of study, the quality and results of their previous academic career. The scholarships are mainly reserved for candidates from the first call; on an exceptional basis, the Teaching Committees may decide to award the scholarship to candidates from the second call. The scholarship results will be published on the University website according to the deadlines specified in the Admission Notice. Candidates selected for a Padua International Excellence scholarship will receive a specific notice outlining the procedures and deadline for accepting the financial benefit. in order to receive the scholarship applicants must also not be recipients of other scholarships and must complete their enrolment at the University of Padua. The academic requirements to keep the scholarship are indicated in the selection notice. The scholarship will be allocated in 2 (two) installments of € 4,000.00 gross each per academic year upon fulfillment of the academic requirements specified in the selection notice. The scholarship may be revoked if recipients: do not earn at least 20 CFU (ECTS credits) by the 30th of November of the following academic year. decide to transfer to another university or apply to withdraw from their studies In the event of the scholarship being revoked, you will be required to return the installments you may have already received.
