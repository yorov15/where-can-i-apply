By "international student" or "international degree-seeker" we mean anyone who holds a qualification obtained abroad and wishes to enrol in a degree programme at our University. Start early and stay ahead! The first call for application for Academic Year 2027/2028 will open on 15 September 2026 for most of the degree programmes offered by the University of Padua. Apply Apply for a full degree programme Bachelor's degrees To be eligible for admission to a bachelor's or a single-cycle degree, you need a minimum 12-year school record . If you have been awarded a final diploma after a shorter programme of study (i.e. less than 12 years), in order to become eligible you must: have attended at least 1 (in case of 11 years of schooling) or 2 (in case of 10 years of schooling) years of academic studies and passed all the exams OR have obtained a post-secondary qualification at a non-University higher education institution , certifying the additional one or two years of education OR have successfully completed one- or two-year preparatory courses (Foundation courses) held at an Italian university to compensate for the missing years of schooling. Foundation courses can also be used to fill other additional requirements that are necessary to access the programmes, but they cannot replace a missing upper secondary school diploma. The University of Padua also offers a Foundation Year programme . The pre-school year can be counted in the 12 years of schooling if it is officially recognised as an integral part of the school curriculum at national level, provided that attendance is compulsory and includes teaching the language of the country (reading and writing) and the basic elements of arithmetic. If in the country where you obtained/are going to obtain your qualification an entrance exam is required to access university (e.g. Selectividad in Spain, Prova de Aferiçao or Prova Geral de Acesso ao Ensino Superior in Portugal, Vestibular or Exame Nacional do Ensino Médio in Brazil, Gao Kao in China), you must also provide a certificate which states that you have passed said test. As an alternative to passing the university entrance exam (where required), you must: have attended an additional one (in case of 12 years of schooling) or two (in case of 11 years of schooling) years of academic studies in another country and/or different foreign system and passed all the exams OR have obtained an official Italian or foreign post-secondary qualification at a non-University higher education institution in a subject related to the chosen course with a minimum duration of one or two years OR have successfully completed Foundation courses held at an Italian University. These additional certificates only allow enrolment to the first year and cannot be further evaluated for shortening of your degree programme. Foreign qualifications eligible for admission to the courses You can refer to our country-based list of qualifications in order to verify whether your entry qualification makes you eligible for admission to the degree programme(s) you are interested in. Master's degrees To be eligible for admission to a Master's degree, you will need a Bachelor's degree or equivalent foreign qualification with specific curricular requirements obtained from a higher education institution accredited according to the national educational accreditation system of the country where it is located. If your qualification is jointly awarded by two or more universities (e.g. the awarding and the teaching institutions are not the same and pertain to different national education systems), each institution must be accredited according to the national educational accreditation system of the country where it is located. Foreign qualifications eligible for admission to the courses You can refer to our country-based list of qualifications in order to verify whether your entry qualification is suitable to access the degree programme(s) you are interested in.
