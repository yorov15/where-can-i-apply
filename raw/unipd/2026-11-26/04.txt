Check out a list of documents required to submit your application In order to submit your application, you will need to upload the following documents: • Valid Passport/Valid ID card (only for EU - or equivalent - applicants) ; • Italian Residence permit (only for non-EU applicants already residing in Italy) ; • Academic transcript/Marksheet ; • Upper secondary school certificate/Academic qualification (if already obtained) Please verify country-based specific entry title requirements at the following link ; • Curriculum Vitae ; • Motivation Letter ; • Language proficiency certificate A full list of accepted English language certificates , minimum scores and exemptions is available at the following link ; • Other specific documents required for the chosen degree programme Please note that all documents must be uploaded in a .pdf format. This is a general list, some programmes may require different or specific documents. Once you add a degree programme to your application, the system will add tasks to the "Checklist" section. Refer to these task for detailed information on the documents you have to upload. IMPORTANT : Diplomas and Transcripts must be official (i.e. provided with the stamp of the Institution and the signature of the person in charge of issuing the document) and written/translated in Italian OR in English . If your documents are issued only in a language other that Italian OR English, you will have to upload them together with official translations in Italian OR in English. If you are admitted to a degree programme, you will have to submit other documents in order to complete your enrolment. For more information, click here IMPORTANT No legalised documents are required to submit your application.
