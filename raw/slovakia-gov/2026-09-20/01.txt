Governmental scholarships of the SR for foreign students Selection criteria The age limit is determined as of September 1 of the year in which the application for a government scholarship is submitted for the 1st and 2nd levels of study (bachelor's/master's/combined bachelor's and master's programs): the applicant must be at least 18 years old and not older than 26 for the third level of study (doctoral studies): the applicant must be at least 23 years old and not older than 35 years old. Submission of all required documents in accordance with: https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/required-documents Proof of academic performance during the entire previous secondary or higher education, with a minimum grade of 80%. Selection of a field of study that is supported for the given country. Successful admission to a public university in the Slovak Republic. Completion of the admission process for full-time study in accordance with the conditions of the specific university. A list of public universities is available at this link: https://www.portalvs.sk/en/informacie-o-vysokych-skolach A prerequisite for admission to a university is proficiency in the Slovak language at the B2 level; if the scholarship recipient does not speak Slovak, they are required to complete a 10-month language and professional training program before beginning their university studies, during which they will receive a scholarship.
