Governmental scholarships of the SR for foreign students FAQ Who can apply for this scholarship program? To apply for the provision of Slovak governmental scholarship, you have to meet the conditions, which can be found on the website https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/wizard (only select your country). If you do not find your country there, this means you can not apply. What are the age requirements for applicants to bachelor's, master's, and PhD programs? Applicants for bachelor's or master's degrees must be 18 years old by the 1st of September, otherwise, they will not be able to apply. The age limitation is set to be for bachelor's and master's degrees 18 to 26 years old by the 1st of September. And for PhD, applicants must be 23 to 35 years old by the 1st of September (if a student is by 1st of September 27 years or 17 years old, he/she cannot apply for bachelor's and master's degrees) How can I apply? Those who meet the conditions and would like to apply must register from the website https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/registration Please remember your email and password given in registration to be able to log in. After registration you will receive confirmation email with activation link (check also the spam folder). Click on the activation link first to activate your account then you will be able to log in from https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/ After log in you can fill the online application form and attach all required documents. You can find also manual in your account which might help you with filling the form. What are the financial conditions? Information about grants can be found here https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/documents (download Information on Current Financial Terms). Is it necessary to take standardized tests such as the SAT in order to apply for a Governmental Scholarship, or can applicants submit their application without these test scores? No, the SAT is not required to apply for a Governmental Scholarship. Applicants are instead required to submit the documents listed here: https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/required-documents What is the language of study? If you do not speak Slovak, mark in your account, section 2. Basic data, that you do not speak Slovak language (reply"no" to the question "My knowledge of the Slovak language is sufficient to pass the study:"). If your application for the provision of the scholarship is accepted you will take part in the Slovak language and academic preparatory course which starts in September and lasts for 10 months, after finishing the course you can apply to university. The study at university under this scholarship programme is conducted in the Slovak language. However, there are also study programmes that combine both English and Slovak, and this is allowed and supported under the Government Scholarship programme. A limited number of programmes are taught in English only, and only those that are tuition-free are supported by the Government Scholarship. For example, Medicine requires payment of annual tuition fees and is therefore not covered by the scholarship. It is also important to note that although it may be possible to study a programme either in English or as a combination of English and Slovak, proficiency in the Slovak language at B2 level is mandatory. Applicants who do not meet this requirement must complete a Slovak language and academic preparatory course, which starts in September and lasts for 10 months. If I am awarded a Government Scholarship and complete the one-year language preparation, does this guarantee my automatic admission to a university in Slovakia? No, completing the Government Scholarship language preparation does not automatically guarantee admission to a university. A requirement for admission to a Slovak university is proficiency in the Slovak language at the B2 level. If a scholarship recipient does not speak Slovak, they are required to complete a 10-month language and professional training program before beginning their university studies, during which they receive a scholarship. However, after completing this preparation, applicants must still successfully go through the standard admission process for full-time study, in accordance with the specific requirements of their chosen university. Admission is therefore not automatic. A list of public universities in Slovakia is available here: https://www.portalvs.sk/en/informacie-o-vysokych-skolach I have not received an activation link which I need to log in to the website. Please check also the SPAM folder. If you have not find it there, contact helpdesk@studyabroad.sk . Which secondary school reports should I submit if I am graduating this year? You are required to submit report from all completed years of study, as well as the mid-year report for your final year. The final-year report card can be submitted by 31 August. What are allowed formats for uploads? Permitted formats for photograph: png, jpg, jpeg, gif. Permitted formats for other documents: 'png','jpg', 'jpeg', 'gif', 'doc', 'docx', 'pdf', 'rtf', 'txt', 'zip'. Maximum permitted size of one upload is up to 30MB. To save attachments click on "Save data and continue". Save uploaded files one by one. If I do not currently hold a valid passport and am unable to obtain one in time due to time or financial constraints, is it possible to use an alternative form of identification, such as a national biometric ID card, for my application? Yes, in such cases you may use a national biometric ID (national identity card) as proof of identity when applying. However, if you are selected, you will be required to obtain a passport for visa processing. A copy of your passport will need to be send additionally upon your arrival to Slovakia. What is Signed application form? Once you finish with filling the online application form, go to section 1. Applications in your account, download PDF file named "Document", print the last page of it "Confirmation", sign it and attach the scan of it as "Signed application". What should I do if I cannot find the study programme I want to apply for in the list? The list of study programmes includes only those for which the scholarship is offered. If you cannot find the programme you wish to study, please do not select a related or similar programme from the list. In the electronic application, the study programme must be entered exactly as stated in your application to the university. Since the name of the field of study is selected from a dropdown menu in the "Education" section, is it necessary to also indicate the study programme elsewhere? If the name of the study programme differs from the selected field of study, it can be specified in the section "Other information you would like to provide" under "Additional information" (e.g. after selecting the field of study "Teacher Training in Academic Subjects," you can specify the exact study programme in the "Additional information" section). Is it necessary to send the original application and the required documents to the Ministry of Education, Research, Development and Youth of the Slovak Republic in paper form as well? Only the applicant for the Government of the Slovak Republic scholarship who has been awarded the scholarship sends the original signed application and the required documents to the Ministry in paper form. There is not any link "new application" visible in my account. The link "New application" is visible only during the official term for applying. It is not possible to apply before or after the official term that is why the link "New application" is not displayed in the account. Term for applying can be found here https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en/application When will the results regarding the awarding of the Governmental Scholarship be announced? Applicants will be notified whether they have been awarded a Governmental Scholarship by the Ministry in electronic form no later than 15 July. The notification will be sent to the email address provided by the applicant in their application. Do I have to attach publications? Publications are not required from applicants for bachelor or master degree. It is an optional attachment for those who apply for doctoral (PhD.) degree. What does "joined 1st and 2nd degree (bachelor and master)" mean, and does it apply to General Medicine studies? "Joined 1st and 2nd degree" refers to study programmes that are not divided into separate bachelor's and master's stages, but are offered as one continuous programme. This applies e.g. to General Medicine studies. In the case of General Medicine, the programme lasts 6 years and leads directly to a master's level qualification (MUDr.), without obtaining a bachelor's degree along the way. Therefore, it is not possible to apply only for the 2nd degree (master's level) within this field of study, as the programme must be completed as a whole. Where should I apply for a visa if I come from a country currently experiencing unrest, such as Afghanistan or Palestine? For visa-related matters, please contact the relevant embassy or consulate in your country.
