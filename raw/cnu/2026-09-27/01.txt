for International Students (degree seeking) International students who wish to seek a degree from CNU should follow these procedures. An applicant should submit an application form (After completing the online application, print it out)and required documents (Study Plan, Self-introduction letter, Affidavit of Financial Support and other required application materials) to CNU via registered mail. Please refer to the Admissions Guide for further details. Visa Process for Successful Applicants CNU will send the following documents to the email address of each successful applicant: Certificate of Admission Visa Issuance Number Business Registration Certificate Visa Application Process: Apply for a D-2 student visa at the Korean Embassy in your home country. Obtain the visa. Arrive at Chungnam National University (CNU), Korea. Upon arrival, register to obtain your Alien Registration Card (ARC) through the Office of International Affairs. Important Note: All matters concerning immigration and visa status must be handled through the Office of International Affairs at CNU. If you encounter any problems or have questions regarding your visa or immigration status, please contact or visit the Office of International Affairs.
