( http://ipsi.cnu.ac.kr ) Eligibility Requirements An international student should receive the Test of proficiency in Korean (TOPIK) score level 3 or higher. Minimum accepted scores are : TOEIC 700, TOEFL (iBT 71), TEPS 600, New TEPS 327, IELTS 5.5 from a recognized English proficiency test. ( e.g. TOEIC, TOEFL, TEPS) Level 3 or higher on a test of Korean proficiency at the CNU Language Education Center Level 4 or higher on a completion of Korean Language course at the CNU Language Education Center Evaluation of Academic Proficiency The admission will be determined by initial document review & the interview Evaluation is done based on an overall evaluation of applicant´s attitude, academic proficiency and aptitude for desired major. The basis of the evaluation is the documents submitted by the applicant. Application Fee (Non-refundable) KRW 60,000 Required Documents To be considered for freshman admission, submit the following: Essay of your study objective Graduation certificate and transcripts of high school : Graduation certificate of high school and transcripts Copy of passport Copy of Alien Registration Card (if available) Government-issued Family Census Register Certificate for Level 3 or higher TOPIK or Certificate of English proficiency test (TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS, TEPS) Certificate of Korean proficiency test at the CNU Language Education Center Completion certificate for Level 4 or higher on a Korean Language course at the CNU Language Education Center Affidavit of Financial Support (available on the web site) Sponsor's Bank Certificate with a balance of more than KRW 16,000,000 Sponsor's Certificate of Employment or Certificate of Business Registration Additional material, if any, required by the department.
