These programmes are known as Numerus Clausus programmes. It concerns: Aerospace Engineering Architecture, Urbanism and Building Sciences Clinical Technology Computer Science and Engineering The application deadline for all Numerus Clausus programmes is 15 January (23:59 CET). After completing your application in both Studielink and the TU Delft online application portal (Osiaan) , you will receive further information about the selection procedure for your chosen programme. The results of the selection process are announced in mid-April. If you apply for a Numerus Clausus programme, you may also apply for one additional BSc programme as a backup . This may be another Numerus Clausus programme or a regular (non-Numerus Clausus) BSc programme. The following deadlines apply: 15 January (23:59 CET) for a backup application to another Numerus Clausus programme. 1 April (23:59 CEST) for a backup application to a regular BSc programme. If your backup application is for a regular BSc programme, it will only be processed after you have withdrawn your Numerus Clausus application and informed TU Delft by email that you wish to proceed with your backup application. Important: Applicants from EU/EFTA countries can participate in the placement procedure until 15 August . Applicants from non-EU/EFTA countries can participate in the placement procedure until 15 June . After this date, there is generally insufficient time to complete the visa and residence permit process before the start of the academic year. For detailed information about the selection and placement procedure, please consult the Selection and placement regulations If you have general questions about admission and application, please visit our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) page.
