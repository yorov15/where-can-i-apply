Please note : your application must be fully completed and submitted before this deadline. This means that: all required documents must be uploaded; the application fee must be paid. your application must be officially submitted through the online application system. Applications that are incomplete or submitted after the deadline cannot be considered. The programmes with mandatory matching are: Electrical Engineering Applied Physics Applied Mathematics Marine technology Molecular Science & Technology Nanobiology 1 June (23:59 CEST) Confirmation Statement deadline 1 July (23:59 CEST) Full payment deadline 1st week September Start of first semester. Numerus Clausus BSc programmes 15 October - 15 January Application period for Numerus Clausus BSc programmes. 15 January (23:59 CET) This application deadline applies to applicants with an international secondary school diploma, including international secondary school diplomas obtained in the Netherlands. Please note: your application must be fully completed and submitted before this deadline. This means that: all required documents must be uploaded; the application fee must be paid; your application must be officially submitted through the online application system. Applications that are incomplete or submitted after the deadline cannot be considered.
