We strongly recommend not using external agencies or representatives who offer application services. Choose your programme carefully You may apply for one BSc programme per academic year. If your chosen programme has a Numerus Clausus selection procedure, you may also apply for one additional backup programme. Re-applying after a previous application If you were admitted in a previous academic year and wish to apply again, you must submit a new application, including a new application in Studielink. Your student number will remain the same. Application deadlines Application deadlines vary by BSc programme. Please make sure to check the deadline that applies to your chosen programme. Once the deadline has passed, it is no longer possible to submit an application for that programme. Application fee A non-refundable application fee of €100 applies to all applicants. Instructions on how to pay this fee will be provided during the online application process. Complete applications only We can only process complete applications submitted through the online application system. Applications that are incomplete or submitted by post or email cannot be considered. Processing time Once we have received all required documents and your application is complete, we will assess your application and inform you of the outcome within 6 weeks. We kindly ask you not to contact us about the status of your application during this 6-week period, as this will not speed up the processing of your application. Applicants to Numerus Clausus programmes will receive their ranking number on 15 April. Emails sent by TU Delft may be filtered into your spam or junk mail folder. Please check your spam folder regularly to ensure you do not miss any important communication regarding your application.
