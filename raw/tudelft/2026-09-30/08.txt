Follow the steps below if you have been admitted to TU Delft. If you have been admitted to TU Delft and have received a (full) scholarship from an external provider, follow these steps to submit it for TU Delft approval. Without an approval from TU Delft, you will need to transfer the amount in your financial letter before the payment deadline. Requirements Approval deadline Additional Conditions Requirements Submit your scholarship letter before 1 June to contactcentre-esa@tudelft.nl , including your student number. The letter must contain the following details: Name of the scholarship provider Date of issue Student's name (as shown on passport) Date of birth Start and end date of the scholarship (duration) Programme of study at TU Delft Amount of the scholarship Email address for TU Delft to send the tuition fee invoice Approval deadline TU Delft must approve the scholarship letter by 15 June . If the letter does not meet the IND's requirements, the scholarship will be rejected, and you must transfer the fees and costs before 1 July : Tuition fees Living costs (as stated in the Financial Letter) Additional Conditions If the scholarship does not cover the full first-year living costs , you must transfer the remaining amount before 1 July . All students must pay the required service fees (housing and residence permit/visa) before 1 July . Tuition fees must be fully covered for the entire duration of the master's programme which is 2 years . Scholarship providers should account for an annual tuition fee increase of 2-3% .
