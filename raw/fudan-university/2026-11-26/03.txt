1 Fudan University 2027 Undergraduate Admission Information for English-taught Programs Founded in 1905, Fudan University is a comprehensive research university with a world -class reputation for academic excellence. It ranked 26th globally in the 2027 QS World University Rankings. The university currently offers 12 primary academic discipline categories-philosophy, economics, law, education, literature, history, science, engineering, medical science, management, arts, and interdisciplinary studies-with 12 disciplines ranking in the global top 1‰ according to ESI. Fudan operates across four campuses: Handan, Fenglin, Zhangjiang, and Jiangwan. Programs Please refer to Fudan University 2027 English -taught Undergraduate Programs. Eligibility Requirements 1. General Requirements: (1) Non -Chinese citizens holding a valid foreign ordinary passport. 2 (2) High school graduate (or above) with qualifications eligible for university admission. (3) Physically and mentally healthy, with no criminal record. (4) Excellent proficiency in the language of instruction. 2. Nationality Eligibility In accordance with relevant regulations, including the Nationality Law of the People's Republic of China, the Measures for the Administration of School Recruitment and Cultivation of International Students (Joint Decree No. 42 of the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the Ministry of Public Security), and the Notice on Regulating the Enrollment of International Students in Higher Education Institutions in China (Jiao Wai Han [2020] No. 12): (1) For applicants who were born in a foreign country and held foreign nationality at birth , t he applicants ' parent/s (Chinesecitizen side) must have obtained foreign permanent residency before the applicants' birth. In addition, the applicant must have a record of actual residence abroad (outside Chinese mainland/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan) for at least 2 years between May 1, 2023, and April 30, 2027 (calculated in cycles from May 1 to April 30 of the following year, 9 months of actual residence per cycle counts as one year, verified by entry/exit stamps). (2) For applicants who emigrated from China and acquired foreign nationality, the applicants must have obtained foreign 3 nationality before May 1, 2023, and completed the procedures for renouncing Chinese nationality. In addition, the applicants must have a record of actual residence abroad (outside Chinese mainland/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan) for at least 2 years between May 1, 2023, and April 30, 2027 (calculated in cycles from May 1 to April 30 of the following year, 9 months of actual residence per cycle counts as one year, verified by entry/exit stamps). Application Rounds Round 1: December 1, 2026 - February 26, 2027 Round 2: April 1 - April 15, 2027 Application and Admission Procedures Step 1 Take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA). This assessment is organized by the China Scholarship Council on behalf of the Ministry of Education of China. Students must take the assessment in advance and obtain the score report. Students can visit the official CSCA website ( https://csca.cn/home) to check specific test schedules and content. For the required CSCA test subjects, please refer to Fudan University 2027 English-taught Undergraduate Programs. Step 2 Fill Out the Online Application. 4 Please register on and then log into Fudan's Online Application System for International Students: https://istudent.fudan.edu.cn/apply for application. Please choose "Personal Application → Undergraduate" and then choose your intended program. Fill out the application and upload the application materials (please refer to Application Materials section). Once completed, please make sure to click the "Submit" button. Step 3 Pay the Application Fee. The application fee is 800 RMB (online payment, non refundable). The application is considered invalid without the application fee. Step 4 Take the Interview. Following the university's preliminary review, eligible applicants will be invited to participate in an interview. The interview will focus on assessing the applicant's comprehensive competencies and language communication skills. Step 5: Check Admission Results. Applicants can check admission results for Round 1 and 2 in the application system starting from late April 2027 and late June 2027 respectively. Step 6: Download Admission Notice and Check Scholarship Results. 5 Starting in July 2027, admitted students can download the official electronic admission not ice through the application system and check their email promptly for scholarship results.
