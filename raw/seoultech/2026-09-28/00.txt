Admission Guide Application Period Spring Semester : (1st Round) September (2nd Round) Novemb er Fall Semester : (1st Round) March (2nd Round) June Application Guidelines & Forms Guidelines Forms Guidelines Forms Application Submission Submit Application Admission Process Online Application Payment of Application Fee Submission of Original Documents (by post or in person) Documents Review Interview (Online or in person) Announcement of Admissions Result Submission of Financial Documents (such as bank statement) Tuition Payment Issuance of Certificate of Admission Visa Application and Entry to Korea Course Registration and Orientation Start of Semester Eligibility for Application 1. Applicant and applicant's parents must hold citizenship from another country other than Korea 2. (Undergraduate course Freshman) Applicants who have completed or expected to complete high school prior to admission (Undergraduate course Transfer) Applicants who have completed or expected to complete at least two years of a bachelor program or who have completed or expected to complete 2-3 years at college 3. Applicants must meet one of the following language requirements: A. Applicants who have obtained TOPIK Level 3 or higher B. Applicants who have completed Level 4 of the SeoulTech Korean language program C. Applicants who have completed Level 4 of a Korean language program of IEQAS university in Korea D. Applicants who have completed the Intermediate - Advanced course of Korean Homeland Education program E. Applicants who have completed intermediate level from King Sejong Institute 4. Exceptions English-Track in undergraduate course - Dept. of MSDE, Dept. of ITM(IT Management), Dept. of Business Administration, Dept. of Global IT Convergence - English score: TOEFL iBT 71, IELTS 5.5, or above (It can be applied differently by department. Refer to the admission guidelines) * Applicants from countries where English is an official language are exempt from English proficiency requirements. - Korean proficiency: TOPIK Level 3 or higher only for applicants to the Dept. of IT Management(ITM) Department of Creative Writing (Undergraduate & Graduate) - Korean proficiency : TOPIK Level 6 Required documents Index Common documents Additional documents Undergraduate Freshman - Online Application form - Financial certification - Bank deposit more than KRW 20,000,000 - Agreement of verification of academic records - Score report of language proficiency - (Optional) Alien registration card (If already in Korea) - A copy of the applicant's and both parents' passport - Document proving parent-child relationship (e.g., birth certificate) - (Applicants for college of Art and Design)Portfolio more than 10pages - Apostilled or Embassy confirmed)High School graduation certificate and grade transcript Transfer - Apostilled or Embassy confirmed)High School graduation certificate and grade transcript - Certificate of (Graduation / Enrollment) from Previous University ※ Submission of the bank balance certificate is required after the admission results are announced. ※ Required documents are subject to change.
