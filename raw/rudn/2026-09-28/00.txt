Applications to the Institute of Russian Language for the purpose of studying Russian may be submitted at any time; however, depending on the submission deadline, applicants may be offered either two or three semesters of study. Documents are accepted: - In person - By Russian Post - Via the Public Services Portal (ruID) To apply for the 1st year of Bachelor's / Specialist's / Master's / PhD programs, foreign citizens must submit the following documents: - A copy of the educational certificate or qualification, along with a translation into Russian (if required) - A scanned copy of the passport or other identity and citizenship document, along with a translation into Russian (if required) - Documents confirming the applicant's individual achievements - Consent to the processing of personal data - A copy of a medical certificate confirming no contraindications for studying in the Russian Federation and no HIV/AIDS - Copies of other documents - for example, proof of victories in international Olympiads or professional competitions - 1 photo List and Form of Entrance Examinations for the Admission of Foreign Citizens for the 2026/2027 Academic Year, Conducted Independently by RUDN University (Selection is conducted for fields of study within the faculties/ institutes/ academy/ schools) For all entrance examinations: - Maximum score: 100 - Minimum score: 30 In 2026, RUDN University will admit foreign citizens to Bachelor's, Master's, and Specialist's programs based on competitive selection through entrance examinations in the form of an interview (in Russian/English) and an evaluation of the portfolio submitted by the applicant. The following will be considered: - Grade point average (GPA) from the previous educational institution, with special emphasis on results in core subjects relevant to the chosen field of study; - Participation in competitions and Olympiads in specialized disciplines; - Recommendations from academic departments of Russian/foreign universities or academic supervisors; - Recommendations from Russian embassies/consulates abroad or foreign diplomatic missions; - Recommendations from public organizations or associations of foreign graduates of Russian (Soviet) universities; - Availability of a certificate confirming the level of Russian language proficiency. For Master's programs, in addition to the documents listed above, the following will also be considered: - A motivation letter; - Academic and professional achievements confirmed by diplomas and certificates; - The position of the applicant's previous educational institution in world rankings (if it is ranked among the TOP-500 in the ARWU, QS, or THE institutional rankings, or among the TOP-200 in the ARWU, QS, or THE subject rankings). For questions regarding admission to Bachelor's, Master's, or Specialist's programs, please contact: intstudent@rudn.ru ! Original documents must be submitted upon arrival at the university. !
