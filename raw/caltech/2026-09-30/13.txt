Everything you need to know about applying to Caltech is here. Before you start exploring our detailed instructions on our supplemental application essays , academic requirements , deadlines , and letters of recommendation , remember these things: Caltech is looking for students with incredible potential in STEM. Potential . There is no prescribed way of getting into Caltech. You don't have to have scientific research or get a Nobel at 12; read more about what you should do to prepare for Caltech . Cost should never get in the way of you applying to or affording Caltech . If the cost of applying presents a challenge to you, we will waive the application fee. We're a small community and that means you can reach out to us. Connect with your regional admissions counselor , schedule a campus visit , or register for a virtual Techer Talk . Beginning in fall 2023, Caltech has enacted a policy on minimum age for on-campus housing for undergraduate students . There are two ways to apply to Caltech. Select the option that fits you best. Apply to Caltech by submitting one of the following applications: Common App or QuestBridge Application. Caltech does not have a preference between any of our 2 applications. Choose the option that best suits you. Caltech is also a proud QuestBridge Partner . Students may apply for Caltech through the QuestBridge National College Match . Ready to dive in?
