University of Sheffield International College offers you the best pathway to a degree from the University of Sheffield - one of the UK's leading universities. Off Flexibility The University of Sheffield International College provides high quality and flexible International Foundation Year programmes that are specifically designed to prepare international students for admission to undergraduate degrees at the University of Sheffield. Choose from one of the following routes: International Foundation Year in Business, Social Sciences and Humanities International Foundation Year in Science and Engineering The small classes and extensive student support ensure you get the most out of your time at the University of Sheffield International College. You can fast track your entry to the University by improving your English language skills alongside your academic subjects. Your pathway to an undergraduate degree Upon successful completion of your International Foundation Year programme, you can gain entry into a wide range of world-class undergraduate degree programmes at the University of Sheffield. On-campus location The University of Sheffield International College is located on the campus of the University of Sheffield so as a student of the University of Sheffield International College you will have access to first class educational, recreational and social facilities at the University. The campus is only a short walk from Sheffield city centre with access to great shops, cafes and entertainment. Find out more about the University of Sheffield International College .
