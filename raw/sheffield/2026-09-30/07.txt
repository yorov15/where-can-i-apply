Fixed fee guarantee We offer a special fixed-fee guarantee for international students taking courses longer than one year. International students will pay the same fixed annual tuition fee for each year of the same course. The tuition fee you pay in your first year will be the same for each subsequent year as long as you remain on the same course. If you change courses, your tuition fee may be different. This means that you will know exactly how much you will pay each year before you start your studies, making it easier to plan and budget for your time in Sheffield. Fee status The University of Sheffield charges different tuition fees depending on whether students are classed as Home or Overseas for fee purposes.
