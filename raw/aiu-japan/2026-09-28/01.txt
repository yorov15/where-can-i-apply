Partners Tuition & Fees Home Undergraduate Tuition & Fees Matriculation Fee & Tuition Matriculation Fee (One-time Payment) Students from Akita Prefecture: 282,000 yen Other students: 423,000 yen Annual Tuition 896,000 yen Total Students from Akita Prefecture：1,178,000 yen Other students：1,319,000 yen Question How much will the first year of school cost except matriculation fee? Answer Approximately 900,000 yen. Housing Housing 622,900 yen Following are included in the housing fee: 11.5 months of residence fee 8 months of board (3 meals/day) excluding Golden Week and winter/summer breaks. Internet usage fee Cleaning fee (at year end) Utilities/Heating/AC Miscellaneous fee for matriculation Student activity fee 60,000 yen (For the period of enrollment) Parent/Guardian Association dues 20,000 yen (For the period of enrollment) Insurance (Gakkensai & Gakkenbai) 4,660 yen (For the standard duration of the program) *Gakkensai:Personal Accident Insurance for Students Pursuing Education and Research *Gakkenbai:Liability Insurance for Students Pursuing Education and Research Insurance (Inbound Futai-Gakuso) 43,400 yen *Inbound Futai-Gakuso:Comprehensive Insurance for Students Lives Coupled with "Gakkensai" *This insurance is only for international students staying in Japan with the residence status "Student." Textbooks and English test Textbooks approx. 50,000 to 70,000 yen/semester English test approx. 50,000 yen Tuition Waiver AIU has a tuition waiver program for students who encounter difficulties affording tuition fees because of financial situations, natural disasters, etc. Tuition while Study Abroad Details about tuition while study abroad are available at the following webpage.
