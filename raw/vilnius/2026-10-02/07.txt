Find Your Programme Bachelor & Integrated studies Master studies Doctoral studies Prof. Rimvydas Petrauskas Rector of Vilnius University I warmly invite you to join our vibrant academic community. Studying at VU means engaging with centuries of tradition while embracing innovation and global perspectives. Here, you will find not only world-class education but also values that define us: openness, integrity, and a commitment to excellence. Our strong focus on interdisciplinary collaboration ensures that you gain both depth and breadth in your academic journey. Beyond the classroom, VU supports your career ambitions through partnerships with industry, research institutes, and global networks, helping you to transform knowledge into real-world opportunities. By becoming part of Vilnius University, you will enrich your future with knowledge, friendships, and opportunities that extend far beyond borders. Assoc. Prof. Dr Mariana Sueldo Department of Organisational Information and Communication Research, Faculty of Communication At Vilnius University, 'Hinc itur ad astra' ("from here to the stars") is not just a motto - it is my lived experience. As an Argentine-born scholar who pursued further studies, earned my Master's and PhD, and built a career here, I have seen how this spirit comes alive in a welcoming, intercultural community. Students and lecturers inspire one another, learning and growing together. This openness prepares our graduates to thrive globally with resilience, curiosity, and confidence, carrying VU's tradition and vision into their future careers. Aliya Adilbayeva Student from Kazakhstan at the Institute of International Relations and Political Science From an early age, I was surrounded by international student communities. However, I must admit that the atmosphere at Vilnius University is where I feel most confident being myself and where I can grow the most. As an international student, I see VU as my stronghold. The remarkable thing about VU is the setting where diversity is respected, and dialogue is elevated. My course mates always keep inspiring me and making me feel welcomed, and a part of the community. Being a student here has boosted my confidence enormously in my area of specialisation. I would recommend it to every newcomer without hesitation.
