The U.S.-CAEF program offers a prestigious scholarship for earning a bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Economics, Software Engineering, or Applied Mathematics and Informatics at the American University of Central Asia (AUCA) in Bishkek. What you receive: Full tuition coverage for 4 years Monthly stipend Housing allowance Coverage of travel expenses Additional opportunities: Networking with leading industry experts Fully funded summer regional internships Academic support and mentorship program Professional and personal growth events Access to the exclusive U.S.-CAEF alumni network Learn more at: https://uscaef.org/apply/ and on the program's social media pages.
