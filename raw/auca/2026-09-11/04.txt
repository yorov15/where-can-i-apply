Only citizens of the Kyrgyz Republic are eligible for merit-based awards. To qualify for these awards, a student must have demonstrated exceptional academic performance or other significant achievements. These scholarships are need-blind, meaning there is no requirement for the student to provide proof of financial need. Awards granting discounts must be received in the year of admission to AUCA. For the 2026-2027 academic year, the following individuals can receive merit-based scholarships: Winners of the Republican English Language Olympiad in Kyrgyzstan : 1st place - 100% 2nd place - 60% 3rd place - 50% Winners of Republican and International Olympiads in Mathematics and Informatics : 1st place - 100% 2nd place - 60% 3rd place - 50% (Note: Additional conditions may apply if there is more than one applicant for each place ) Holders of the "Golden Certificate" ORT - 100% Winners of the annual BRAIN RING competition conducted by AUCA among students of schools, gymnasiums, and lyceums in Kyrgyzstan: 1st place - 100% 2nd place - 80% 3rd place - 75% Winners of the annual vocal and artistic talent competition STREAM , conducted by AUCA among students of schools, gymnasiums, and lyceums in Kyrgyzstan: Top 3 places - 100% Top-scoring applicants (2 places) in the entrance exams for the programs "Anthropology" and "Sociology" - 100% (This support is available for students from all CIS countries.) Winners of the VICTORIA competition , conducted by AUCA among high school graduates on European knowledge: 1st place - 100% 2nd place - 60% 3rd place - 40% (Applicable when enrolling in the "Liberal Arts and Sciences: European Studies" program.) Winners of the annual mathematics olympiad conducted by AUCA among 10th-11th grade students: 1st place - 100% 2nd place - 90% 3rd place - 80% (Applicable when enrolling in the "Applied Mathematics and Informatics" program.) Winners of AUCA's law debate competition : 1st place - 80% 2nd place - 70% 3rd place - 60% (Applicable when enrolling in the "International and Business Law" program.) Winners of the TIFE project by AUCA : 1st place - 60% 2nd place - 50% 3rd place - 40% (Applicable when enrolling in the following programs: "Business Administration," "Economics," "Liberal Arts and Sciences: Social Entrepreneurship," and "Journalism and Mass Communications.")
