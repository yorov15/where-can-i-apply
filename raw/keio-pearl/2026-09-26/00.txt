contains all the necessary information on the admissions procedures for the PEARL offered by the Faculty of Economics, Keio University. In order to prevent mistakes in their applications, applicants should carefully read this page and the related material before preparing their applications. Update history 2026.07.31 Application Guidebook for September 2027 Entry released 2026.07.16 PEARL Online Information Session 2026 will be held on August 22（SAT） 2025.07.30 IMPORTANT NOTICE: CHANGE TO STANDARDIZED TEST REQUIREMENTS About the Faculty of Economics Visit the Faculty of Economics website from here Promotion Video Basic Overview (1) Date of Entrance September 22, 2027 (2) Degree Offered Bachelor of Arts in Economics (3) Year of Entry The 1st year of undergraduate-level university *The Faculty of Economics does not accept transfer students. (4) Duration of Study 4 years *Students who have satisfied prescribed conditions may allowed to graduate in 3.5 years. (5) Language of Instruction English (6) Admission quota Approximately 100 students through all three application periods (7) Screening Method Based solely on an evaluation of submitted materials. No interview is required. *Application to the PEARL should be made in English. (8) Required Test Scores All applicants must submit one (or more) of the following: a. International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma (or predicted grades) b. SAT scores *These scores cannot be substituted by any other test scores. All applicants must submit either (or both) of the following as well: d. TOEFL iBT® scores e. IELTS Academic Module scores *These scores cannot be substituted by any other test scores.
