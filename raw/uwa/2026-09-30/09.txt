Search scholarships Prepare required documentation Once you are shown the scholarships that you are potentially eligible for, review the scholarship details to determine whether there are additional requirements such as financial hardship evidence, educational disadvantage evidence or personal statements. Financial Hardship To be eligible to apply for scholarships from UWA based on financial need you need to prove that you are in financial hardship . You can demonstrate that you are in financial need by: providing your Centrelink Registration Number (CRN) when applying online; or completing relevant financial statements form/s that show financial hardship. Needs Based Scholarships Where possible, ensure you submit documentation to support your answers. For example, if applying based on a medical condition, you should provide medical certificates to support your circumstances. Personal Statements Some scholarships require personal statements as part of the application process. Ensure you read the conditions document to understand the required statement and remain within the word limit. Make sure all information is accurate, true and complete. Apply Complete your application and submit by the deadline. Our application system only requires you to submit one application, which will be used to assess you for all selected scholarships. You will receive an email confirmation of your application. Please note, the UWA Fogarty Foundation Scholarship and UWA Winthrop Scholarship will require a separate application. After you apply After applications close, eligible applications will be assessed against the published scholarship criteria and applications will be ranked by a panel in line with the individual scholarship conditions. Scholarships will only be awarded if there are applicants who are of sufficient need or merit and subject to funding of the scholarship. All outcomes will be sent to applicants within two months of the published closing date of the scholarship round. Accept your scholarship If you have been made a scholarship offer, you will receive an email with a link to the acceptance portal. You will have 2 weeks to accept the scholarship, after this time, the scholarship offer may be rescinded. Issues viewing offers If you can't see the offer in the portal but you received the email, log out and log back in with the email used for your original application. If this also doesn't work, try logging in with your student number and password. If you are still having issues, please contact us as soon as possible through askUWA. Scholarship payments After census date of each semester, each scholarship recipient will be assessed against the individual continuation criteria of their scholarship to determine whether payment will be made for that semester. As a rule, payments are made for Semester 1 from mid-April to mid-May and Semester 2 from mid-September to mid-October. Please ensure your bank details are up to date on StudentConnect to avoid any delays. Can I apply for scholarships if I'm already studying? Yes. Some scholarships are available to continuing students, while others are only for commencing students. Can I apply for multiple scholarships? Yes, provided you meet the eligibility criteria for each scholarship. Some scholarships can be held concurrently, subject to conditions. What if I miss the deadline to apply? Applications submitted after the closing date cannot be accepted. Do I need to accept the scholarship conditions? Yes. If you are offered a scholarship, you must accept the scholarship conditions via the online portal to receive payment. Can I ask for feedback if Im unsuccessful? Due to the high number of applications received, individual feedback is not usually available. Where can I get help? If you have questions about scholarships or the application process, contact the Scholarships team via AskUWA or by calling on 6488 3235 Need some help?
