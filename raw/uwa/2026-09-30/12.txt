Enquire now The University of Western Australia is offering 10%* off tuition fees for students from selected countries. The International Student Award promotes a diverse student community and helps more students from around the world achieve their study and career goals at UWA. If you're looking to start studying in 2027, you could receive 10%* off your tuition fees for the entire duration of your eligible undergraduate or postgraduate course. General Eligibility To be eligible for the International Student Award, you will need to: Be a citizen from Argentina, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, China, Colombia, Costa Rica, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Kenya, Maldives, Mexico, Nepal, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Sri Lanka, Uruguay, Venezuela Be applying to The University of Western Australia as an international student on an Australian student visa Study an eligible undergraduate or postgraduate course offered by the University and published in the UWA Handbook Be enrolled to study a full-time course Terms & Conditions How to apply When you submit your application to UWA, our Admissions team will automatically assess your eligibility for the Award. If you are eligible, your offer letter will include the International Student Award.
