Find our English language requirements Explore our English transition programs Frequently asked questions Expand All How do I apply? Applying to U of T is a two-step process. First, submit your application through the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC). Then you'll complete your next steps in the Join U of T or Engineering Applicant Portal. Learn more about how to apply. Do I need to submit an essay, reference letters or a personal statement? Not for most programs. Most program areas typically don't require essays, reference letters or personal statements as part of the application. However, some programs do ask for a supplemental application to get to know you beyond your grades. If one is required, you'll find the details in Join U of T or the Engineering Applicant Portal after you apply. When should I apply? Applications typically open in late September. You should apply as early as possible in the fall of your final year of high school. Some programs have limited space or supplemental applications, and applying early gives you more time to complete next steps, like submitting documents or supplementary applications. Review all application deadlines. Will I get transfer credit for AP or IB courses? If you've taken Advanced Placement (AP) or International Baccalaureate (IB) courses in high school, you may be eligible for transfer credit. Credits are assessed after admission, after we receive your official final results directly from the examining board. Transfer credit policies vary by faculty and campus. Learn more about transfer credits from high school.
