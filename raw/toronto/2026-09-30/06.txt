Understanding the costs and preparing in advance can give you confidence and allow you to focus on making the most of your academic experience. As a publicly funded institution, the university is not able to offer direct financial aid to international students. If you need financial assistance, it's important to explore funding options available in your home country, as many governments and organizations offer programs to support students studying abroad. Researching these opportunities early can help you plan ahead and manage your university expenses more effectively. Scholarships and awards All students, including international students, are automatically considered for scholarships as part of the admissions process. While there are many scholarships available for international students, they are highly competitive and most do not cover the full cost of tuition. We encourage you to have additional funding plans in place. Learn more about scholarships for international students Studying in Canada Before issuing a study permit, the Government of Canada requires that international students provide proof of financial support to show that you are able to support yourself while in Canada. Students studying at a Canadian university on a study permit are not eligible for financial assistance from Canadian government programs. We recommend that you consult with the funding agencies in your own country. Please note that documented protected persons or convention refugees are able to apply for Canadian government funding.
