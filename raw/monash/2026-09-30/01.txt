or honours course - international students Semester one entry Mid-year entry How to apply Apply directly to Monash Apply through VTAC if you're currently studying an Australian Year 12 in Australia or overseas, International Baccalaureate (IB) in Australia or New Zealand, or NCEA in New Zealand. If you're located in Crimea, Cuba, Donetsk, Iran, Luhansk, North Korea, Syria or South Sudan, you'll need to apply for a course through a registered Monash agent (or an IDP office in your region if a Monash agent isn't available). Courses offered and application dates You can search for courses offered at Monash . Non-year 12 applicants International students can apply for a place anytime throughout the year. Teaching starts: Semester one: early March Semester two: mid-July You can check the progress and outcome of your application by logging back into the applicant portal. Current year 12 or IB students Timely applications : Most Year 12 students submit their application and preferences to VTAC by late September. There are also late and very late application periods with higher fees. When you apply through VTAC, your course code should end with the number 3. This identifies you as an international student. If you're an International Baccalaureate student, you need to ensure you, or your school, has submitted the results release form to the International Baccalaureate Organisation (IBO). This is so VTAC can access your results. For a full list of dates and fees, see VTAC dates and payments . Change of preference : Once you apply through VTAC, you can add, remove or re-order your course preferences in your VTAC course application during the specified periods. See VTAC change of preference dates . Entry requirements You should ensure you meet all the entry requirements for the course and for general entry to university. Some courses have extra requirements such as a folio, personal statement, interview, etc. Course requirements Check the requirements of your preferred course General and academic requirements Minimum entry requirements (ie. English language, age) Interstate Year 12 and IB students You can check your IB score and prerequisite equivalents on the VTAC website and download the prerequisite subject list. Special entry program Monash University now accepts Gaokao for direct entry (pdf, 1.48 mb) into bachelor degrees. Credit for prior learning If you have passed units at other tertiary institutions, you may be eligible for a credit transfer. You need to apply before enrolling in a course. For details, see credit for prior learning .
