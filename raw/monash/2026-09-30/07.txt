What you need to provide The online application will ask you to provide details about the course you are applying for (see the lists below). For course details, see Find a course . International applicants course code name of the course course start date campus copy of documents specified in the course entry requirements (e.g. results, certificates, related employment) a copy of your passport evidence you meet the English language requirements official translations of documents in a language other than English $125 applicant fee (or a waiver code) Apply now Domestic applicants course code name of the course course start date campus copy of documents specified in the course entry requirements (e.g. results, certificates, related employment) evidence you meet the English language requirements (if you didn't complete high school in Australia) Apply now Documents and translations When submitting an application, you should attach copies of all relevant supporting documents. These could include secondary school results certificate, official academic record/transcript, and if applicable, IELTS or TOEFL test results and employer references. Originals or certified copies of your academic documents must be provided to Monash University upon request. If you don't provide them, your enrolment may be cancelled. This may occur at course enrolment or any time after starting your course. Tips for using the online application We recommend you have all your documents scanned and ready to upload when starting your application. For questions that aren't relevant to you and are not compulsory, leave the field blank and proceed to the next question. Making changes to your application You will receive a login ID so you can save your application to complete it later or change your information before submitting it. If you have questions or need help with your application, use the link at the bottom of the screen to view frequently asked questions and contact details. Application fee International applicants need to pay a $125 application fee and attach proof of payment. For payment options, see application fee payments . Fee waivers We may waive the application fee if: a Monash staff member has given you an application fee waiver code at an exhibition, seminar or interview session that you've attended, or you're sponsored by one of our listed sponsors (you should contact Admissions and advise them who your sponsor is).
