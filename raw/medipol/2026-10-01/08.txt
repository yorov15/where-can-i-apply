Fille The Application Form & Save It Your browser does not support iframes, but you can visit New Applications To apply, please complete the Online Application Form. Select the academic term and degree program you wish to apply for. Confirm your email address by using the validation code sent to you. Enter your passport number. Please note that each applicant is allowed only one application per term. Prioritize your program choices, as only one application per term can be approved. Carefully provide your personal details, contact information, and educational background on the form. Upload the required documents specific to the program or degree you are applying for. Please be aware that graduate programs offer both Fall and Spring intakes, while undergraduate programs only have a Fall intake. New Applications Apply Now Track Your Application Once you submit the online application form, it will be reviewed within a maximum of one week. The admissions team may request additional documents or require a response to an email as part of the evaluation process. Therefore, it's important to regularly check your inbox and monitor your application status through the portal. Please be aware that each program has a limited number of available seats. Prompt action is essential if your application needs further attention. If you do not receive any feedback on your application, please reach out to us. Our team will assist you as swiftly as possible. Contact email: [email protected] Track Your Application Deposit Payment If your application is approved, you will receive a Conditional Acceptance Letter via email. To receive your Official Acceptance Letter, you must pay the advance tuition deposit by the specified deadline. This non-refundable deposit confirms your intention to enroll at Istanbul Medipol University and will be deducted from your tuition fees upon registration in Istanbul. You can make the deposit payment online, through a bank transfer, or via credit card on campus. The Conditional Acceptance Letter email will include a link for online payment, which is also accessible on the Track Application page. Bank details for the deposit will be provided on the second page of your Conditional Acceptance Letter. Once the deposit is received, we will send you the Official Acceptance Letter along with the Enrollment Guide and important dates. Banking Information 2026 - 2027 Applications Are Open Now !
