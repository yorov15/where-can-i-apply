Tuition fees are determined annually by the University's Board of Trustees and are published on the University's official website. Only the information provided on our website is valid. Please do not rely on data obtained from third-party sources. Our team supports every student whose application is approved through the scholarship process. Students applying directly or via an intermediary institution to university, cannot be charged for their application. For students who do not meet the language proficiency requirement and are required to attend the Language Preparation Program, the applicability of scholarships and discounts may vary depending on the tuition structure of the relevant academic department. Students are advised to review the specific fee regulations applicable to their program. Family Members Scholarship If more than one family member is enrolled at our University, a 10% discount will be applied to the tuition fee of each student. Lump Sum Advanced Payment Lump sum advanced payment discount rate is 10%. This discount is applied to all students who wish to pay the tuition all at once or complete the annual tuition fee before the deadline stated in the student's offer letter. To be eligible for evaluation, students must: • Be enrolled in their academic program, excluding compulsory and optional preparatory classes, • Have taken the courses and credit load prescribed by the curriculum for the relevant academic year, • Have successfully completed all registered courses, • Not have taken resit examinations in the for grade improvement or due to course failure, • Have achieved a minimum Annual Grade Point Average (AGPA) of 2.91 out of 4.00, or its equivalent. In the calculation of the Annual Grade Point Average (AGPA), grades from transferred courses shall not be included. In cases where students have identical Annual Grade Point Average scores, the third decimal place shall be taken into consideration. Students who complete one or two academic semesters at another institution under a Student Exchange Program shall be included in the academic ranking. Students who have transferred courses during the relevant academic year may only be included in the academic ranking provided that they take additional courses sufficient to meet the required ECTS credit load. If a student entitled to a Merit Scholarship: • Fails to renew registration, • Withdraws from the University, • Transfers to another institution, or • Otherwise terminates their affiliation with the University, the scholarship entitlement shall not be transferred to another student. The relevant Faculty and School Boards shall conduct evaluations at the end of each academic year and report the top three ranked students to the Rectorate. Scholarship and discount rates shall be applied as follows: • First-ranked student: Full tuition waiver for the following academic year, • Second-ranked student: 50% tuition discount applied to the applicable tuition fee, • Third-ranked student: 25% tuition discount applied to the applicable tuition fee.
