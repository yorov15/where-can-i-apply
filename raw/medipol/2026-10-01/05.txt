Student Selection Criteria The students shall: • Be citizens or permanent residents of the country of their home university, or hold a valid temporary visa for that country • Have completed at least one (1) year of full-time study at the home institution • Have good to excellent academic performance records • Accepted Exams and Minimum Scores: - IELTS ACADEMIC (Overall): 6.5 and above (IELTS Online is not accepted.) - YDS / E-YDS: 70 - YÖKDİL / E-YÖKDİL: 70 - Cambridge C2 Proficiency: C - Cambridge C1 Advanced: B - Cambridge Linguaskill General: 160 - TOEFL IBT: 84 (TOEFL IBT Home Edition is not accepted.) - PEARSON PTE Academic: 71 (Not accepted online) - Oxford Test of English: 121 📌 Important Note: Internationally recognized language proficiency exams are accepted provided that their validity is verified by our institution. In Türkiye, only exams administered by state universities are considered valid. International exams administered in all authorized test centers abroad are accepted subject to verification. For further information, please visit: https://www.medipol.edu.tr/en/dil-okulu/sss Academic Achievements Report Academic achievement reports will be sent to the student's Home university at the conclusion of each semester, not later than four (4) weeks after the last day of final examinations. This will be achieved through a university transcript. Tuition and Fees Any fees, transportation and living expenses, health insurance, medical costs, food and/or any required meal plan, housing, books and other study costs are to be borne by the guest students or their Home institution. Exchange Liaison Both institutions will provide the name and detailed contact information of the Exchange Liaison who will be responsible for all matters during the student application process, academic counseling, and assisting students upon arrival. Accommodation International students have a variety of options to meet their accommodation needs throughout their educational life in Türkiye. Students can stay in university dormitories located within the campuses, in private dormitories, or in the apartments they rent in the city. Dormitories at Istanbul Medipol University with the capacity of 2,500 students are located on the Kavacık North and Kavacık South Campuses. Visa Support Services Students shall be responsible for obtaining any necessary visas and otherwise complying with all immigration laws and regulations of the country of the Host institution. The Host institution shall cooperate in such efforts, but shall not have any responsibility to assure the granting of any visas, permits or approvals. The Host institution will reasonably assist incoming exchange students in obtaining visas and other documents required by the Host country government immigration authorities to the extent permitted by law.
