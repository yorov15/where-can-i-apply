Accepted Exams and Minimum Scores: IELTS ACADEMIC (Overall): 6.5 and above (IELTS Online is not accepted.) YDS / E-YDS: 70 YÖKDİL / E-YÖKDİL: 70 Cambridge C2 Proficiency: C Cambridge C1 Advanced: B Cambridge Linguaskill General: 160 TOEFL IBT: 84 (TOEFL IBT Home Edition is not accepted.) PEARSON PTE Academic: 71 (Not accepted online) Oxford Test of English: 121 📌 Important Note: Internationally recognized language proficiency exams are accepted provided that their validity is verified by our institution. In Türkiye, only exams administered by state universities are considered valid. International exams administered in all authorized test centers abroad are accepted subject to verification.
