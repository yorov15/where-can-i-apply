The scholarship is divided into two main tracks: Recruitment Track: Designated for students wishing to enroll in specific colleges and majors, including: College of Arts and Sciences (scientific majors) College of Education (scientific and exact disciplines) College of Nursing College of Sharia and Islamic Studies Excellence Track: Intended for academically outstanding students across all colleges of the University, including those covered under the Attraction Track. Awards are granted based on seat availability, and applicants are selected on a competitive basis. Program Objective To attract academically distinguished students to Qatar University. Scholarship Details Scholarship Program Targeted Category Benefits Student Recruitment and Excellence Scholarship Residents of Qatar for Bachelor degree Exemption from tuition fees Exemption from textbook fees Requirements for application: The applicant must have a high school certificate or its equivalent. Applicant must receive final (unconditional) admission to Qatar University. Applicant should not get benefit from this scholarship and another sponsorship/scholarship simultaneously. Conditions for Continuity of Scholarship: To maintain the scholarship, the student must comply with the following conditions: The scholarship shall be effective from the semester of award. If the student does not pass his/her first semester, the scholarship will be terminated and the student will be informed by QU email. The student must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0. The student must successfully complete a minimum of 12 hours per semester. The student should not exceed the period for graduation according to his/her study plan. The student must remain registered continuously (in Fall and Spring) until graduation. Recipients of the scholarship are expected to adhere to academic regulations, policies, and procedures of Qatar University. The student Must abide to the General Conditions and Special Continuity Conditions to keep the scholarship. Attraction Scholarship Conditions The student must continue in the same college and major for which the scholarship was awarded. In case of violation of any continuation requirement, the University reserves the right to either terminate or continue the scholarship, based on the decision of the Scholarship Committee. Required Documents Applicants for this scholarship are not required to submit any documents except the documents required for admission. Application and Selection Process The applicant may apply to the scholarship through the online admission application in the "Scholarship Application" section during the announced application period . Scholarship applications will be evaluated after the deadline of the online scholarship application, where applicable, the applicant's scores in standardized tests, such as IELTS or TOEFL , as well as SAT, ACT, or QU Math Placer , may be considered, in addition to any other criteria deemed appropriate by the Scholarship Committee. Admission and scholarship decisions will be announced via email according to the announced timeline . Annual round trip air fare ticket Residence permit under QU sponsorship Requirements for application: The applicant must have a high school certificate or its equivalent. How to Apply International students applicants may apply to this scholarship through the online admission application in the "Scholarship Application" section during the announced application period. GCC States Scholarships (Embassies) Qatar University offers this scholarship to academically distinguished students of Gulf Cooperation Council states (GCC) who hold the High School Certificate or its equivalent from their countries or any other country but not from Qatar and are seeking to join an undergraduate program at Qatar University. The student must submit their application for admission to the undergraduate program at Qatar University in person. A number of scholarships are allocated to each GCC state and nominating candidates to these scholarships is left to GCC embassies. Such scholarships can be postponed to the second term in the same academic year but not to the next academic year. Scholarship Objective Attracting distinguished students from the GCC states to Qatar University. Scholarship Details Scholarship Program Targeted Category Benefits GCC States Scholarship Students from GSS states for Bachelor degree Exemption from tuition fees. Student housing (two students per room) including transportation to/from campus. Requirements for application: The applicant must have a high school certificate or its equivalent. The applicant must meet the admission requirements for the college that they applied to. Obtain a letter from the embassy addressed to Qatar University requesting consideration for this specific scholarship. Applicant cannot benefit from this scholarship and another sponsorship/scholarship simultaneously. Conditions for Continuity of Scholarship: The student must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0. The student must successfully complete a minimum of 12 hours per semester. The student should not exceed the period for graduation according to his/her study plan. The student must remain registered continuously (in Fall and Spring) until graduation. Recipients of the scholarship are expected to adhere to academic regulations, policies, and procedures of Qatar University. Must abide to the General Conditions to keep the scholarship. Application and evaluation procedures: The students may apply through the online admission application during the announced application period. Students should apply for scholarship nomination through the embassy of his/her country in Qatar after receiving the final admission decision at Qatar University. The nomination letters must be submitted directly to Qatar University. The embassies' nominations will be evaluated after the deadline of the scholarship application. The final admission and scholarship decisions will be sent to the students by email according to the announced scholarship timeline . The embassies will be notified. Talent Scholarship Talent Scholarship Requirements and how to apply Qatar University offers this scholarship to undergraduate non-Qatari students (including international students) who are talented in the fields of scientific research, technology and innovation, literature, acting for male, media, and athleticism . Applicants for this scholarship are selected on a competitive basis. Scholarship Objective Recruiting talented students in the fields of scientific research, technology and innovation, literature, acting for male, media, and athleticism . Scholarship Details Scholarship Program Targeted Category Benefits Talent Scholarship Talented students for Bachelor degree Exemption from tuition fees Exemption from textbook fees Monthly salary of 1,000 QR Guarantee to be admitted to the desired program of study upon meeting the minimum program admission requirements (except for medicine and dental medicine) For international talented students: Student housing (two students per room) including transportation to/from campus, annual round trip air fare ticket and residence permit under QU sponsorship. Requirements for application: The applicant must have a high school certificate or its equivalent. A minimum high school average of 80%. Applicant must meet the minimum admissions requirements of the program that he/she wishes to join. Applicant must submit the final transcript of the 11th grade with a minimum average of 80%. Applicant must submit the final transcript of the first semester of the 12th grade with a minimum average of 80%. Applicant must provide a minimum of two letters of recommendation to support the application from prestigious entities relevant to the area of talent. Applicant must submit a portfolio letter presenting his/her talent and proving the recent achievements in the field of talent, specifically in one of the following areas: scientific research (includes all literary and scientific fields), technology and innovation, literature, acting for male, media, and athleticism . Applicant should pass the personal interview with the committee. Applicants cannot benefit from this scholarship and another scholarship simultaneously, except for international students (terms and conditions apply). Applicants who have previously granted the full benefits of this scholarship are not eligible to benefit from it again for the purpose of acquiring a new academic degree. The scholarship should be utilized starting from the semester in which the scholarship was awarded. For international students, the start of the study can be postponed for one semester, if necessary, conditional of approval of the scholarship committee. If the student does not pass his/her first semester, the scholarship will be canceled and the student will be informed by QU email. Sport Talent Males Females Swimming - Football Football Volleyball Volleyball Basketball Basketball Padel Padel Table Tennis Table Tennis Conditions for Continuity of Scholarship: The student must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5/4.0. The student must successfully complete a minimum of 12 hours per semester. The student must regularly represent the university in the area of talent locally and internationally. The student should not exceed the period for graduation according to his/her program. The student must remain registered continuously (in Fall and Spring) until graduation. The student must commit to the continuous practice and development of the talent. Recipients of the scholarship are expected to adhere to academic regulations, policies, and procedures of Qatar University. Must abide by the General Conditions to keep the scholarship. Required Documents: Applicant must provide a minimum of two letters of recommendation to support the application from prestigious entities relevant to the area of talent. Applicant must submit a portfolio letter with a link to a video of large-size file supporting files (e.g. on Dropbox or OneDrive) presenting his/her talent and proving the recent achievements in the field of talent, specifically in one of the following areas: scientific research (includes all literary and scientific fields), technology and innovation, literature, acting for male, media, and athleticism . For athletic talent, the video must show the applicant playing within a team during a match. After passing the personal interview successfully, Applicants for the field of sport talent are required to submit signed history and medical information form along with the physical examination form filled by a health care provider (family doctor or general practitioner) and to pass a physical fitness examination at Qatar University. How to Apply The applicant may apply to the talent scholarship through the online admission application on "Scholarship Application" page during the announced application period. The applicant is requested to upload the required and supporting documents presenting his/her talent and achievements. Application and evaluation procedures: Students apply for admission and for scholarship online using the online admission application during the announced application period. Students enrolled in schools outside Qatar or in private schools inside Qatar (non-governmental Qatari school system) must upload the first semester certificate of grade twelve and the final certificate of grade eleven. If the student completed grade twelve, he/she must submit only the final certificate of grade twelve. Applicant must upload a minimum of two recommendation letters through the online admission application from recognized entities relevant to the area of talent to support the application. Applicant must submit a letter summarizing his/her talent, achievements, and awards received with a link to large-size files including videos and any other supporting documents (e.g. on Dropbox or OneDrive) to highlight his/her talent and to show the recent achievements in one field of the following talents: scientific research (includes all art and scientific fields), technology, innovation, literature, acting for male, media, and sports. The applicants' files will be evaluated and interviews will be conducted to assess the eligibility and qualifications of the applicant. The best applicants for each talent field will be short-listed. An initial conditional acceptance letter will be sent to the nominated students, within six weeks after the deadline of the online scholarship application, stating the conditions that must be met and the documents required to receive the final acceptance. For international students, after they send the required documents, the university will apply for their visas and they will be notified accordingly. The final admission and scholarship decisions will be sent to students by email according to the announced timeline . College of Education Diploma Scholarships Qatar University offers a number of scholarships for students who want to join the diploma programs offered by the College of Education . Benefits These scholarships include exemption from tuition fees only. Requirements for application : The applicant must meet the admission requirements for the college that they applied to. The applicant must be working under the ministry of education and higher education or any governmental educational organization. It should be the first time for the applicant to apply for a scholarship in the same category offered by the College of Education. Conditions for Continuity of Scholarship: The student must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.50 out of 4 at the end of each semester. The duration of studying shouldn't exceed two semesters excluding the semesters of absence and withdrawal. The student should not be absent or withdraw for two separated or consecutive semesters. If the student fails in meeting any of the conditions to maintain the scholarship, it will be canceled and the student will have to pay the tuition fees of the following semesters. After the student's scholarship has been canceled, he/she will not be eligible to receive that scholarship again, regardless of whether the situation is amended. In all cases, the academic policies will be applied to the student as shown in the Student Catalog. Required Documents: "To whom it may concern" Letter for the new academic year proofing that the applicant is working under the ministry of education and higher education or any governmental educational organization. Copy of ID card or passport (identification card).
