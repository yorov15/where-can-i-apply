Otherwise, the application will not be reviewed.）; -Offered by the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China in the form of full or partial scholarships under educational cooperation and exchange agreements or memorandums reached with governments, institutions, universities and relevant international organizations of respective countries. Application timelines vary by country (generally between November and March). Please consult the Chinese Embassy or Consulate-General in your home country and relevant local authorities directly. Admission documents will be issued to applicants by the scholarship receiving authorities (relevant local authorities and Chinese Embassies/Consulates-General abroad); -For detailed information, please visit the website of the China Scholarship Council ( http://www.csc.edu.cn/ ); -For applicants of the Chinese Government Scholarship (Bilateral Program / Category A) who need to apply for a pre-admission letter from our university, please select Chinese Government Scholarship Category A in the application system. Within two weeks upon submission of your application, our university will issue a pre-admission letter to applicants meeting the basic requirements. This pre-admission letter is solely to facilitate your application for this scholarship and does not constitute the final admission decision of the university or the scholarship authority.
