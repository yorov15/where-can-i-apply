Turkish Proficiency (Bachelor's & Associate) The students who will study in Turkish are required to provide B2 level Turkish language proficiency. If student does not have a valid Central Turkish Proficiency Exam result (the institute must be authorized by YÖK), they must sit for Bahçeşehir University's Turkish language proficiency examination after they completed the enrollment process in İstanbul. Required score for undergraduate programs in BAU Turkish proficiency exam is 60 . Exemption Rule : The students who studied last 3 years of their secondary education in a national high school located in a country where Turkish is the official language will be exempt from the Turkish exams. The programs that requires Turkish proficiency are listed below.
