It is motivated by the invaluable experience gained by the donors throughout and upon completion of the IBA programme - both academically, and with regards to the expansive international network they derived from it. Requirements You are a prospective student, planning to study International Business Administration at Erasmus University Rotterdam; You are a student with a truly entrepreneurial spirit who would demonstrably benefit from financial support; Upon acceptance you will become an outstanding ambassador of the Entrepreneurial Spirit Scholarship and the Rotterdam School of Management (RSM) , which includes supporting marketing initiatives; You commit to stay involved after graduation as a mentor for the new incoming students accepted into the scholarship programme. Applicable to: Nationalities: EU/EEA Partcipating faculties: Rotterdam School of Management (RSM) Level of study: Bachelor (prospective) Scholarship / Grant in € € 2.694 statutory tuition fee + € 700 book fee Application and deadline Find all information about the application.
