Admission scholarships Candidate : Separate application for Admission Scholarship is not required as applicants are automatically selected based on their admission evaluation results. Applicants can check the nomination result at the time of the admission announcement (1st semester only). Amount : 10-100% of Dues (including entrance fee) will be waived for remarkable freshmen 2. Scholarship for Excellent Korean Language Professionals Candidate : Scholarship will be granted to the successful candidates who have graduated or are expected to graduate from overseas high schools if they meet the following Korean language proficiency (1st semester only). Qualification and Amount - TOPIK Level 6 or Completion of Level 6 at SLI: 100% tuition waiver - TOPIK Level 5 or Completion of Level 5 / Preparatory course for degree(Basic Korean for college) at SLI: 50% tuition waiver ※ Scholarship shall be provided even if the successful candidate additionally meets the above requirements after enrollment and before the beginning date of the semester / Among successful candidates, those recommended by SLI may receive the scholarship 3. Scholarship for Excellent English Language Professionals Candidate : Scholarship will be granted to the successful candidates if they meet the following English Language proficiency(1st semester only) Qualification and Amount - IELTS 8.0 / TOEFL iBT 110 or higher : 100% tuition waiver - IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL iBT 95 or higher : 50% tuition waiver ※ Scholarship shall be provided even if the successful candidate additionally meets the above requirements after enrollment and before the beginning date of the semester. 4. Merit-Based Scholarships Qualification and Amount: Scholarship is granted for 2nd~8th semester based on the rank of academic performance in the previous semester (average GPA) ※ Candidates must have completed at least 12 credits (9 credits for the 7th semester) in the previous semester without any failing (F) grades Condition Amount Note Top 6% of last semester 70% tuition waiver Recipients are selected separately for freshmen and others (2-4th year) Top 6% or above and below 18% of the previous semester 50% tuition waiver Top 18% or above and below 30% of the previous semester 30% tuition waiver 5.
