While international students are not eligible for federal financial aid or federal loans, Northwestern has a variety of other options for financial assistance. If you're a current or returning student, jump down to our resources for current students . Need-aware Admission We have a need-aware admission review for international students, meaning that a request for financial aid and the amount of financial aid you require may factor into your admission decision . We review all admitted students who have applied for financial aid, and students with demonstrated financial need will receive institutional aid in the form of scholarships and grants. Required Annual Application You must apply in your first year: As part of the need-aware aid policy, you must apply for and receive financial aid for your first year if you plan to apply for assistance in later years. This means if a student does not apply for aid as a first-year student, they cannot apply in subsequent years. Reapply each year: If a student receives financial aid their first year, they must apply again for their second, third, and fourth years. Application requirements vary between the first year and subsequent years; more information about this can be found on our continuing students' financial aid application page . What International Students Can Expect Need-Based Aid Awards International students who apply for financial aid will be considered for need-based aid by indicating on their admissions application that they wish to be considered for this limited assistance program. Each year, a small group of first-year international students receive need-based aid. These packages: Meet 100% of a student's demonstrated financial need Consider the full cost of attendance (tuition, room and board, fees, health insurance, books and supplies, personal expenses, visa renewal costs and travel allowance) Consist only of grants and scholarships, which do not need to be repaid Are loan- and work-free Apply only to undergraduate study Please note: Need-based aid is not available to international transfer students. For students whose permanent residency is finalized , students qualify for aid under Northwestern's domestic aid budget. Northwestern cannot assume responsibility for economic changes such as currency fluctuation, and we cannot replace lost support that a student may have expected to receive from friends, relatives or government and corporate grants. Resources for Current Students We've compiled a brief list of some of the resources our students on financial aid can utilize. Any student on need-based financial aid, regardless of citizenship status, might qualify or be considered for the below programs. US Tax Support for International Students International students are required to file U.S. taxes, even if they do not earn income. The Office of International Students and Scholars Services (OISS) has compiled a resource for international students filing their taxes in the US and a list of Tax FAQs . Apply for Second, Third, and Fourth Years All students who would like to receive financial aid must apply each year. Check out our application instructions for continuing students for more information. Emergency Aid If you have unexpected expenses while you are actively taking courses, our office may be able to help with those costs. After reviewing our website for circumstances that qualify, submit your request for Emergency Aid as soon as possible. Continuous Housing Students who cannot travel home for winter recess may opt into Continuous Housing, which allows them to stay in the dorm after courses end in December. More information about continuous housing can be found on the Residential Services website. Summer Financial Aid Students can take courses at Northwestern over the summer term, and they can use financial aid to help with the cost of tuition and fees. Learn more about our summer aid policy . Aid for Health Insurance All students must have health insurance coverage, and international students are required to use the Northwestern University Student Health Insurance Plan. Students may apply for a health insurance grant to help with the cost of this plan. Budgeting Students who receive refunds of financial aid should budget their funds wisely. Check out our financial wellness resources to learn more about budgeting strategies. Start-up Grants Some students may qualify for additional assistance in the form of a start-up grant, which is disbursed just before the first term of freshman year. Our office will contact you directly if you qualify for this funding. Finding a Job on Campus Some students may be eligible for on-campus work , such as for Dining Services, campus Libraries, or Athletics. There may be other ways our office can support you throughout your time at Northwestern. Please contact our office to learn more about what may be available. We're here to help.
