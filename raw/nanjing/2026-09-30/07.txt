Category - Undergraduate student: CNY 10,000 one-time reward - Master student: CNY 20,000 one-time reward Criteria and Eligibility 1. Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens in good health and friendly to China. 2. Applicants for undergraduate programs must have a senior high school diploma and be under the age of 30; for master's degree programs must have a bachelor's degree and be under the age of 35; for PhD degree programs must have a master's degree and be under the age of 40; 3. Applicants must abide by the laws and decrees of the Chinese government ； be a student of Nanjing University have No violation of college rules and regulations during the period of applying for and receiving scholarships ； have a good learning attitude and a good academic record of the highest degree. 4. Applicants cannot be recipients of other scholarships offered by Chinese government, local governments or other organizations simultaneously. Application Time 2024.07.01-2024.07.31 Please submit application materials as required within the specified time, otherwise the application will be deemed invalid .
