Your Journey to KFUPM Starts Here Begin your online application by going to apply.kfupm.edu.sa You may choose your admissions track directly on the website and then complete the corresponding form directly on there. Important dates for admissions will be posted on the official admissions website. Selecting a major Application Your admission to a particular major depends on: A. The weighted average score B. The availability of seats in your chosen major. Students are assigned disciplines according to their grades - from the highest downwards - until all seats in each discipline are filled. This process is repeated with all students in all the options they have selected, with the aim of finding the best option that corresponds to each student's weighted average score. If you are admitted into a major other than your first choice, it does not mean that you have completely missed the opportunity. As soon as a seat becomes available in one of your top choices, due to a withdrawal, your major will be updated automatically, and this process continues until the start of the academic year. If you reject the major you have been accepted in, you will not be offered that major or any other lower-preference major on your list of options. You will be placed on the waiting list and, depending on availability, you will be offered the higher-preference majors from your list of options. If you do not respond with acceptance or rejection within the specified period, the acceptance will be considered void, and you will lose your chance permanently. Note: If your preferred major is not available, we recommend you accept the proposed major so that you do not miss your opportunity to obtain admission to the university. We will inform you if you meet the admission criteria and tell you which major you have been admitted to. You must confirm the major within 24 hours, and you must complete your profile information, or else your acceptance will be withdrawn. How can I register for KFUPM's Tahsili Test? You can register for the test through the official website of the National Center for Assessment (Qiyas). The test will be offered on two dates: November 8 and December 15, 2026. 2. Are students who graduated from high school more than two years ago eligible to apply? Yes, applicants must be graduating in the current academic year or must have graduated within the previous two years. 3. Is an English proficiency test required before registering for the KFUPM's Tahsili Test? No. However, an English proficiency test is required as part of the university admission process. Applicants who do not achieve the required score will be excluded from consideration. All required test results must be submitted by February 2, 2027. SAT Track 1. Can I apply through both the Primary Admission Track and the SAT Track? Yes, you can apply through both tracks. If you are admitted through the Primary Admission Track, your application to the SAT Track will be automatically withdrawn. 2. How long is an SAT score valid? There is no specific validity period for SAT scores when applying through this track. 3. Is the high school grade included in the SAT Track admission criteria? No. High school grades are not considered when evaluating applicants for the SAT Track. 4. Do I need to submit scores from the General Aptitude Test, the Achievement Test, or an English proficiency test? No. These scores are not required when applying through the SAT Track. Admission is based on the SAT score, in addition to the track-specific requirements. 5. What SAT score is required for admission? There is no specific score that guarantees admission. However, applicants must achieve a minimum SAT score of 1350 to apply. Selection is competitive and is based on the SAT score and performance in KFUPM's mathematics test. 6. Does KFUPM accept SAT Superscoring? No. Superscoring is not accepted. The total SAT score must come from a single test sitting. 7. Is the mathematics test conducted in person or online? The mathematics test is conducted online. Applicants will be contacted with details of the test date and arrangements after the registration period ends. 8. What topics are covered in KFUPM's mathematics test? Olympiad Track 1. Does being a Mawhiba student qualify me for direct admission through the Olympiad Track? No. Direct admission requires the applicant to have placed among the top three winners in a competition recognized by KFUPM. 2. Do special awards in the ISEF competition qualify applicants for direct admission? No. Special awards at ISEF are not accepted for admission through the Olympiad Track. Only the Grand Award is recognized, provided the applicant has placed among the top four winners. 3. Do I need to submit scores from the General Aptitude Test, Achievement Test, or an English proficiency test? No. These scores are not required for admission through the Olympiad Track. Admission is based on achieving a qualifying position in a competition recognized by the university, in accordance with the track's requirements. 4. Are winning certificates accepted if the competition took place more than two years ago? Yes. Winning certificates are accepted regardless of when the competition took place, provided that the competition and the applicant's winning position are recognized by the university. Vacancies Admission Track 1. Is KFUPM's Tahsili Test score included in the weighted score for the Vacancies Admission Track? No. The KFUPM's Tahsili Test score is not included in the weighted score for this track. The weighted score is calculated using the General Aptitude Test score, Achievement test score, and high school grade. 2. Is the English proficiency test score included in the weighted score? No. The English proficiency test score is not included in the weighted score. However, achieving the required English proficiency score is still an admission requirement for this track. 3. Can I apply if I do not have a recommendation letter? No. A recommendation letter is required when applying through the Vacancies Admission Track. Applicants are therefore advised to request the letter in advance, before the end of the academic year and school closures, to ensure it is available during the application period. 4. If I am not admitted through the other admission tracks, can I apply through the Vacancies Admission Track? Yes. You may apply through this track if you are not admitted through the other tracks, provided that you meet its requirements. Applicants will be ranked for seats that become available due to withdrawals, based on the admission criteria and available capacity.
