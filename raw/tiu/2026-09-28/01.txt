International Applicant FAQs Do I need a visa to study in Uzbekistan? Most international students require a student visa (type "O'T-2") to study in Uzbekistan. Citizens of some CIS countries may be exempt. TIU's International Office will issue an official invitation letter to support your visa application once you've been accepted. Does TIU provide accommodation for international students? Yes. TIU offers student housing options both on and near campus. International students are given priority for university accommodation. Alternatively, we can help you find private rental options in Tashkent at affordable rates. What is the cost of living in Tashkent? Tashkent is one of the most affordable capital cities in the region. On average, students spend approximately $300-500 USD per month on rent, food, transport, and personal expenses - significantly lower than most European or Asian cities. Can I work while studying on a student visa? Part-time work opportunities may be available depending on your visa conditions. TIU also offers on-campus work-study positions and internship placements through our Career Centre. Contact the International Office for the latest regulations. Is there an orientation programme for new international students? Absolutely. Every September, we run a dedicated International Orientation Week that includes campus tours, city tours, registration support, SIM card and bank account setup, and social events to help you settle in and make friends before classes begin. Are scholarships available for international students? Yes. TIU offers merit-based scholarships for high-achieving international applicants covering up to 50% of tuition fees. Additional financial aid and fee discounts may be available. Visit our scholarships page for full details and eligibility criteria.
