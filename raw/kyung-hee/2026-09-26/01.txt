Various on-campus and off-campus scholarships are available every semester for enrolled students. However, if a student is selected for more than one scholarship, the beneficiary will only be awarded the scholarship with a higher value. If a student is supported by GKS program or receives scholarships that cover full tuition, they will be disqualified from certain scholarships.
