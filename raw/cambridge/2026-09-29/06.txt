We offer a wide range of financial support to help students manage the costs of studying at Cambridge. On this page: Cambridge Bursary for UK and EU students Outreach scholarships for UK students: Stormzy Scholarship for Black UK students F1 Scholarship for Engineering students from underrepresented backgrounds Gordon Edge Scholarship for science or technology students from counties in North West England and North Wales Geography Scholarship for Black UK students Faculty awards and grants College funding Financial support for international students Music awards Support with childcare costs Cambridge Bursary Scheme As a Cambridge student, you could receive up to £3,500 a year of free financial support. You do not need to repay the money. Eligibility is based on household income. You could be eligible for higher amounts or extra funding if you meet certain criteria. Care leavers, for example, could receive £8,350 a year. You do not need to apply for the Cambridge Bursary separately. You're considered automatically if you apply to Student Finance for a loan. You do not need to take up the loan to receive the bursary. Find out what funding you could get (Cambridge Students website) Outreach scholarships The Cambridge Trust offers scholarships for UK undergraduate students from underrepresented groups. Find out more about the work to identify and support these groups on our widening participation pages . Contact ugscholarships@admin.cam.ac.uk if you have questions about the outreach scholarships. Stormzy Scholarship The Stormzy Scholarship is for Black UK students from low income and/or disadvantaged backgrounds. It supports study of any undergraduate course at Cambridge. Find out more about the Stormzy Scholarship Formula 1 ® Engineering Scholarship The Formula 1 Scholarship supports eligible students to study Engineering at Cambridge. It is for UK students from a background underrepresented in Engineering. This includes: female students students from a lower income background students from underrepresented ethnic minorities Find out more about the F1 Scholarship Gordon Edge Scholarship The Scholarship supports a UK student to study a science or technology degree at Cambridge. It is open to students from particular counties in North West England and North Wales. Find out more about the Gordon Edge Scholarship Geography Scholarship The Scholarship is for a Black UK student from a low income and/or disadvantaged background to study at Cambridge. This Scholarship will not be open for applications in 2026. Check back in Summer 2027 for updates.
