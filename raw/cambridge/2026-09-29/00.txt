Courses Colleges Fees and funding Applying Open Days and Events Find out more International students: Our journey to Cambridge The University of Cambridge is a diverse international community and welcomes talented students from around the world. It's a great place to be a student wherever you're from. 25% of our undergraduate students (around 3,207 students) are from outside the UK, representing around 91 countries.
