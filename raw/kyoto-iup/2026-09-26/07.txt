Preparatory Course 11. Qualifying Tests 12. Financial Support 13. Accommodation 14. Others 6. Japanese Language Proficiency Q Q6-1. Can I apply for Kyoto iUP without Japanese language proficiency? A Yes. Japanese language proficiency is not required at the time of application. Q Q6-2. Do applicants with Japanese language proficiency have an advantage in the selection process? A Japanese language proficiency is not required at the time of application. Therefore, Japanese language proficiency doesn't affect the selection process. However, if you wish to submit your JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) score for reference, you may do so. Q Q6-3. Can I submit my JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) score for reference? A Japanese language proficiency is not required at the time of application. Therefore, Japanese language proficiency doesn't affect the selection process. However, if you wish to submit your JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) score for reference, you may do so. Q Q6-4. Is there any support for studying Japanese before I enter the Preparatory Course? A If deemed necessary (based on their Japanese language ability), successful candidates will be provided with preliminary Japanese language lessons before the commencement of the Preparatory Course. The tuition and textbook fees will be covered by the university. Q Q6-5. I am confident in my Japanese language proficiency, but do I have to take the Preparatory Course? A All successful applicants must take a placement test to assess their Japanese language proficiency before starting the Preparatory Course in October. Students are divided into beginner to advanced-level courses based on their results. In addition to Japanese language education, the Preparatory Course also offers classes in foundation subjects to compensate for the differences in curricula between students' home countries and Japan.
