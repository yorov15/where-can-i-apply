Admissions(ERICA) Outbound Student Exchange Program Outbound Student Exchange Program Program Introduction The outbound student exchange program is a program in which the students of Hanyang University are sent to another affiliated university abroad, according to a bilateral exchange agreement between the two schools in regards to the student and their exchange of credits. The student is then able to study at the partnered university for a certain period of time, according to the agreement. Eligibility for Application 1. Eligible students : Seoul and ERICA campus students who have completed their second to the sixth semester - For those who are enrolled under a five-year degree, students who have completed their second to the eighth semester - For some partner universities located in China and Japan, graduate students are also eligible to apply. 2. Academic requirements : Must have an average GPA of 2.5 (Including F grades) - For graduate students, their average GPA for both their undergraduate and graduate studies must be higher than a 2.5 GPA in total. - For transfer students, their grades from their previous university and from Hanyang University must be higher than a 2.5 GPA in total. 3. Language Proficiency Scores - For English-speaking countries (the United States, Canada, Australia, United Kingdom, Ireland, etc.): IBT TOEFL score of 75 or above, or an IELTS (Academic) score of 6.0 or above - Europe/South America/Other Asian countries: IBT TOEFL score of 75 or above, or an IELTS (Academic) score of 6.0 or above, or an ITP TOEFL score of 550 or above - For Chinese-speaking countries and Japan: Students are not required to submit a language proficiency score to apply for this program. However, language proficiency scores may be required by different universities. In this event, the student must fulfill the required university's language proficiency requirements. (If a student has a language score from the new HSK and JLPT tests, then that student will earn extra points based on the foreign language performance conversion table.) *Please refer to the application notice in the future as one's eligibility may differ based on the partner university's standards. Selection Period Annually in January and August January : Fall semester selection of outbound students August : Spring semester selection of outbound students Selection Method A comprehensive evaluation of submitted documents - 100% (Cumulative Grades + Language proficiency scores + Study abroad plan) Details and Contacts Please refer to the website of the Office of International Affairs below for details regarding the program. https://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/exchangeout Contact information : (Seoul) 02-2220-2459~2460 / (ERICA) 031-400-4915 Hanyang ERICA Summer School Hanyang ERICA Summer School Program Introduction Hanyang ERICA Summer School (HESS) is a short-term education program started for foreign students living abroad in Korea during their summer vacation every year. The students who participate in the program will learn about Korea's politics, economy, society, history, and culture through classes at the university and other various cultural experiences outside of the university. HESS will be an opportunity for Hanyang University students and students from universities abroad to build academic and cultural exchange opportunities. Language Used During the Program All classes are conducted in English. Location of the Program ERICA Campus Eligibility Requirements Full-time university students at a foreign university (including prospective students), Hanyang University students Details and Contact Information https://ericasummer.hanyang.ac.kr/ Contact information : 82-31-400-4914 Short-term Program: Recognition of Language Study Credits Earned Abroad Short-term Program: Recognition of Language Study Credits Earned Abroad Program Introduction The language study abroad credit recognition program recognizes three credits (elective credits) if a student takes a language course for two weeks / 48 hours or more and receives a certification of completion at a foreign university or a university subsidiary language institute that issues a two to four-year degree. Eligibility for Application ① Students who have more than one semester worth of grades to present at the time of their application ② Must have an average Cumulative GPA above 2.0 ③ Students who have been enrolled in the university for more than eight semesters are ineligible to apply. Details and Contact Information ① Students will only be admitted once while they are enrolled at the university. ② Private language institute courses will not be acknowledged. ③ Students must register for this program before departing South Korea (Students' credits will not be acknowledged after their course completion under any circumstances) ④ Document(s) that must be submitted before departure - Language Study Abroad Credit Recognition Application Form which can be found on the HY-in Portal website - Admission Letter - A copy of one's passport and airline ticket ⑤ Document(s) that must be submitted after departure ⑥ Credit recognition period - Winter School semester (late February) : Students who submit their certificate of completion from September to February - Summer School semester (late August) : Students who submit their certificate of completion from March to August Please refer to the website of the Office of International Affairs below for details regarding the program. http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/credit Contact information : (Seoul) 02-2220-2457 / (ERICA) 031-400-4915 Short-term Program: Study Abroad Program for Summer / Winter School Short-term Program: Study Abroad Program for Summer / Winter School Program Introduction The study abroad program for summer and winter school is a program in which students pay their own expenses to participate in International Summer / Winter schools hosted by other universities affiliated with Hanyang University during the vacation period and receive recognition from Hanyang University through a study abroad program. Eligibility for Application ① Students who have more than one semester worth of grades to present at the time of their application ② Must have an average Cumulative GPA above 2.0 ③ Students who have been enrolled in the university for more than eight semesters are ineligible to apply. Details and Contact Information ① Selection period : Summer (March~April), Winter (September~October) ② Outbound universities : Will be selected amongst partner universities abroad that host International Winter / Summer Schools (These universities differ every year.) SNS: https://www.instagram.com/hanyanggloballions/ Contact information : (Seoul) 02-2220-2457 / (ERICA) 031-400-4915 Visiting Program - Student Financed Study Abroad Visiting Program - Student Financed Study Abroad Program Introduction After the student is admitted to a study abroad program at a foreign university, the program allows the student to study for one or two semesters (a year) at that university and accepts academic credits within a certain range. Eligibility for Application 1. Winter / Summer School - Must be a student who is enrolled at either the Seoul or ERICA campus, with an average GPA of 2.0 - From the time of their application, students must be an enrolled student or a student who is on a leave of absence who has completed their 1st to seventh semester only. - Soon to-be-graduating students and students who have delayed their graduation are ineligible to apply. 2. Regular Semester - Must be a student who is enrolled at Seoul or ERICA campus, with an average GPA of 2.5 - From the time of their application, students must be an enrolled student who has completed their second to sixth semester. They must also be an enrolled student even during their outbound period. - Soon to-be-graduating students and students who have delayed their graduation are ineligible to apply. Selection Procedure 1. Registration on the HY-in portal website based on the fixed registration schedule (Annually in June and December) 2. Submission of the application form and other required documents to the students' affiliated department's administration team 3. The students' affiliated department's administration team gathers all relevant documents together and sends them out to the Office of International Affairs. 4. The Office of International Affairs sends out a list of finalized students to the individual departments' administration teams. Details and Contact Information Please refer to the website of the Office of International Affairs below for details regarding the program. http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/visiting Contact information : (Seoul) 02-2220-2460 / (ERICA) 031-400-4915 Study Abroad Scholarship Study Abroad Scholarship Program Introduction Since 1981, Hanyang University has been the first Korean university to implement this system to train talented students to have a more international outlook and global abilities. This is a program that provides scholarships to Hanyang University graduates that wish to study abroad for their master's / doctoral degrees from other graduate schools. Eligibility for Application 1. A student with a Bachelor's degree from Hanyang University, with an average GPA of 3.5 for both their undergraduate and master's courses (Master's degree can be obtained at both their home university or at other universities) 2. A student who has not passed three years since obtaining their master's or bachelor's degree (excluding military service period) 3. Students who are scheduled to enroll into a master's and doctoral degree course of study abroad during their period of application (This program will begin its period of application in May.) 4. Students who are under the age of 35 5. Students who do not have any study abroad restrictions 6. Students who are capable of speaking the main language of the university abroad. If the university is located in an English-speaking country, the student must have a TOEFL IBT score of 91 or more, and an IELTS score of 6.5 or more (same for those majoring in the field of arts and sports) For countries that speak other languages, the student must submit other language proficiency scores implemented by that country (However, English scores may be additionally required if needed.) Details and Contact Please refer to the website of the Office of International Affairs below for details regarding the program. https://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/scholarship Contact information : 02-2220-2460
