Admissions(ERICA) Outbound Exchange Program Outbound Exchange Program Program Introduction Outbound Exchange Program is a program where you pay the tuition fee to Hanyang University and spend one or two semesters studying at a partner university that has bilateral agreement. The earning credits from the host university can be transferred back to HYU. * Students can go abroad to a partner university where the following conditions are all fulfilled. A. both General and Student Exchange Agreements with HYU are valid B. a balance in the exchange numbers is achieved Eligibility All full-time HYU undergraduates are eligible to apply, if they : 1. Academic Requirements A. have completed 2 to 7 graded semesters by the time of exchange recruitment opens (December/July). (2 to 9 semesters for those who are majored in Architecture) B. Master's degree students may be acceptable to some of partner universities in China or Japan. *ONLY qualified to study abroad in their third semester. (1st semester: Enrollment 》 2nd semester: Application for exchange 》 3rd semester: Study Abroad as exchangers 》 4th semester: Return to study at HYU) C. Students are NOT eligible if they : 1) are expected to graduate or extended the status of graduation 2) are double-majored (minor or multi-fields are not included) 3) have already participated in the exchange program for one year (two semesters) 4) are applying for two consecutive semesters (a full-year selected students are not included) 2. Grades (4.5-point scale) A. attain a Cumulative Average Point(GPA) of at least 2.5 B. For Master's students: Cumulative Average Point(GPA) of at least 2.5 for both undergraduate and graduate C. For Transferred students: Cumulative Average Point GPA of at least 2.5 from both previous and current university 3. The students who participate in the program will learn about Korea's politics, economy, society, history, and culture through classes at the university and other various cultural experiences outside of the university. Hanyang International Summer School is used as an opportunity for Hanyang University students and students from universities abroad to build academic and cultural exchange opportunities. Language Used During the Program All classes are conducted in English. Location of the Program Seoul Campus Eligibility to participation Full-time university students at a foreign university (including prospective students), Enrolled students at Hanyang University or students on a leave of absence (excluding students expected to graduate/students on graduation extension) Course registration and precautions 1.Course registration A. Separate notification of course registration and available subjects through Hanyang Portal in May of every yea B. Application is made through the system of application for reception in the same way as the regular semester. C. All courses available in the international seasonal semesters are English-only A type courses (recognized as English-only courses) 2.Precautions A. You can apply for the same course with the general season semester, but you cannot apply for a course that is lectured at the same time. B. Other credit recognition programs (language training, student financed, field training, etc. during vacation) cannot be recognized at the same time. C. When taking the same course number, the same course classification is applied to the same curriculum, but in some cases, when taking higher year (3rd / 4th year) courses it might not be applied, so even if you take the same course, the course classification may vary for each student, so you must check it with the administrative team of your department in advance. D. For retake courses is accepted only if you take a course with the same course number. Eligibility for Application ① Students must have enrolled in at least one semester at the time of application ② Students who have an average Cumulative GPA above 2.0 ③ Students who have been enrolled in the university for more than eight semesters are ineligible to apply Details and Contact Information 1. Students are allowed to apply only once while enrolled at the university 2. Programs from private language institute are not accepted 3. Students must register for the program prior to departure (Student's credits will not be accepted after completion under any circumstances) 4. Documents that must be submitted before departure - Language Study Abroad Credit Recognition Application Form from HY-in website - Certificate of Admission - Copy of Passport / Airline ticket 5. Documents that must be submitted upon arrival - Original Certificate of Completion 6. Credit transfer period - Winter School semester (late February): Students who submitted their certificate of completion from September to February - Summer School semester (late August): Students who submitted their certificate of completion from March to August Please refer to the website of the Office of International Affairs below for details regarding the program. The selection opens in May-June per year, the awarding period for 1 to 2 years depends on the evaluation. The details are subject to change, students must refer to the website once the application opens. Eligibility All full-time HYU undergraduate degree graduates are eligible to apply, if they : 1. have Bachelor's degree from HYU and attain a cumulative average point of at least 3.5 on 4.5 point scale (both bachelor's and master's for master's degree holder). 2. obtained their final education within three years except for military service period 3. accepted the admission and scheduled to enroll in the year of applying 4. are under the age of 35 5. do not have restrictions on travelling and studying overseas 6. have TOEFL IBT score of above 91 or IELTS academics score of above 6.5 ※ conditional admission is not acceptable, and an official letter of admission must be prepared to submit during the application period.
