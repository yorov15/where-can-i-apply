Graduate Students Types of Aid Cost of Attendance Net Price Calculator Financial Aid FAQs Your Cost of Attendance includes direct and indirect costs. Direct costs are billed by Soka and include tuition, living expenses, and health insurance - a total of $57,016 for 2026-27. Students who waive the health insurance fee have a direct cost of $54,486. Indirect costs - books and supplies, transportation, and personal expenses - vary by student and are not paid directly to the university. An estimated health insurance fee of $2,530 is included in the personal expenses row. Students with qualifying coverage through their own plan or a parent's plan may waive this fee.
