Every Soka student studies abroad for a semester. It's included in tuition and built into the four-year plan for earning your degree. You'll spend a semester immersed in a language, culture, and community outside your comfort zone - and you'll grow in ways a classroom can't replicate. Learning beyond borders Study abroad at Soka isn't optional enrichment. It's a graduation requirement*, and it's been that way since the university opened in 2001. After two years studying a language of your choice - Chinese, French, Japanese, or Spanish - you'll spend a semester living and learning in one of 11 possible countries. Every program on the approved list has been vetted for academic rigor, language immersion, and student support. *Students who are unable to travel abroad due to health conditions or immigration status may complete equivalent coursework on SUA campus. Built into your regular tuition Study abroad is covered by your standard cost of attendance. There's no supplemental program fee or separate application cost. Housing, academics, and support services are all coordinated through SUA's approved program network. Language Offerings Select a section... View Spanish Options What to expect Expand All Collapse All When does study abroad happen? Junior year, fall or spring semester. Your four-year academic plan is built around it, so your semester abroad won't disrupt your progress toward graduation. What are the language requirements? You'll need to complete four semesters in your target language, reaching at least the 202-level before you go. Language proficiency tests are required both before departure and after you return. How do I choose a program? SUA maintains an approved list of programs across 11 countries. You'll choose from that list; outside programs aren't permitted. Your language track narrows the options, and from there, you'll work with the study abroad office to find the right fit. Where will I live? Housing is guaranteed and coordinated by SUA so you can focus on learning. The type of accommodation varies by program. You might live with a local family, in dorms with other students, or in an off-campus apartment. Can I earn full academic credit? Yes. Programs carry between 12 and 16 credits per semester, which count toward your degree. What if I'm on academic probation? Students on academic probation are not eligible to participate until their standing is restored. Will there be internship or service-learning opportunities as part of my semester abroad? It depends on the country and program. See the program options in your target language for more information. Do you provide information for LGBTQ+ students about safety abroad? Yes, we provide information by country and region regarding rights and safety for LGBTQ+ students. Study Abroad staff The Office of Study Abroad helps you find the right program, navigate logistics, and prepare for departure.
