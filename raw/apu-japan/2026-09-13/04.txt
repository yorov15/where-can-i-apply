Standard Tuition Fees Until Graduation Tuition payments are semester-based and includes a fixed tuition (referred to as Tuition A) and a tuition that varies based on the semester a student enrolls (referred to as Tuition B). Tuition B will be calculated based on the overall annual tuition of the current semester a student is in. Tuition for each individual differs according to the awarded APU Tuition Reduction Scholarship percentage. The fee includes a moving-in fee ( 32,000 JPY ), security deposit ( 98,000 JPY ), and rent ( 56,800 JPY per month * ) for two months. All amounts are subject to change. * The rent amount before enrollment is a flat rate of 56,800 JPY per month, regardless of the AP House building in which you will live. If this amount differs from your actual rent, the difference will be used to cover subsequent monthly rent payments or will be refunded when you move out of AP House.
