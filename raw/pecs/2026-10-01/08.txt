Language Proficiency Requirements - University of Pécs Academic year 2026/27 English-taught Programs Meeting the language proficiency criteria outlined below does not automatically guarantee admission. In addition, applicants to the following faculties: • Faculty of Health Sciences • Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology • Faculty of Pharmacy • Medical School will have their English language proficiency assessed through an online test, an interview or both conducted by the university, regardless of submitted certificates. However, uploading test results can support their application. Applicants may be exempt from the language examination requirement if they meet any of the following criteria: • They obtained their secondary education in English in one of the following countries: o United States of America o United Kingdom o Ireland o Malta o New Zealand o Australia o Canada o Singapore o Republic of South Africa • They hold an International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma. Individual IB certificates may be considered for exemption from the English test. When applying, please include: o Grade 11 results o First semester grade 12 results (Progress Report November) o Official predicted grades Ensure that subject levels (HL, SL, A or B) are clearly indicated. • They hold Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel, or OxfordAQA International Examinations: o (I)GCSE + A-levels: student is exempted from the English test. o Cambridge, Pearson, and/or OxfordAQA International A-levels with grades A-C provide additional admission points.
