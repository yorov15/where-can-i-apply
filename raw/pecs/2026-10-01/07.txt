1 General admission requirements for Undergraduate (BA/BSc, OTM and Preparatory) programs for international students at the University of Pécs Are you an international student wondering whether you are eligible to apply for a bachelor's programme at the University of Pécs? This document lists the most commonly accepted nonHungarian secondary school qualifications that meet the minimum admission requirements. Important notes The University of Pécs's study programs have a selective admission procedure. This means that meeting the admission requirements does not guarantee a place. • The inclusion of a diploma on this list does not guarantee admission. Grades, subject relevance, and academic performance are also considered. • For applications to the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, an apostilled and notarised copy of degree certificates is required for documents issued in the following countries: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cameroon, Ghana, and Pakistan. Additional legalisation may also be requested for documents issued in other countries. • If your country is not listed, your diploma may still be accepted if it: • Certifies at least twelve years of education (or eleven in systems where that is the standard), • Grants access to higher education in the issuing country, and • Suggests you are academically prepared for university-level study. Admission decisions are made only after submitting a complete online application. • This list applies to diplomas obtained after at least two years of formal study in the relevant curriculum. Diplomas obtained after one year or through online schools will be reviewed individually, taking your full educational background into account. 2 Table of Contents International diplomas applying, please indicate which subjects you took your written and oral examinations. International Baccalaureate (IB) • The International Baccalaureate Diploma (IB DP) is required for general admission. • Individual IB certificates are not accepted. If you have followed the IB curriculum, you are expected to obtain the IB diploma. Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel or Oxford AQA International Examinations • Proof of at least two different GCE A-level (Advanced Level) exam results and at least three different GCSE exam results with grades A-C (9-4) (a total of five different subjects), or • Proof of at least three different GCE A-level exam results and at least one GCSE exam result with a grade of A-C (9-4) (a total of four different subjects), or • Proof of at least four different GCE A-level exam results, in which case no GCSE exam results are required. A GCSE exam result may be substituted with an AS-level result, provided that the AS-level subject is different from the A-level subject. 5 US high school diploma • High School Diploma and Official Transcript, • GED: High School Equivalency Certificate and Official GED Transcript. A Student Transcript is not acceptable. AP results may be counted as extra points. AP exam results must be certified by an official certificate issued by the College Board. International Foundation (or Access) programs for university admission • A strong general academic curriculum and very good results are required for admission, with your national diploma being the primary qualification in the assessment. • When applying, also include your national secondary school diploma and transcript and your academic offer letter from the university that admitted you on condition of completing the foundation programme. Caribbean Advanced Proficiency Examination (CAPE) • The CAPE is required with three double units in general/academic subjects and an overall grade of I-V. 6 Non-Hungarian national diplomas Unless otherwise stated, the qualifications listed below refer to general/academic education streams.
