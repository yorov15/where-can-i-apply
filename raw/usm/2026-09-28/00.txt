Skip to main content Undergraduate International Programmes How To Apply Fees Download How To Apply ONLINE APPLICATION All application must be submitted via online at International@USM. You will need to register to create an account or to log in if you have previously applied online. The system will automatically generate a reference number which will be send to the registered email. THINGS TO BE PREPARED FOR ONLINE APPLICATION: Official certificates, transcripts, mark sheets or school report from each secondary or college attended Translated copy of certificates (if issued other than English or Malay Language) IELTS/TOEFL/MUET/CAE/CPE/PTE/Authorization Letter from an institution in which the medium instruction is English Copy of passport (Details page only in A4 size) Student Pass (if study in Malaysia) Letter of scholarship (if any) Receipt of processing fee payment. A processing fee of RM100 is applicable for every application submitted. Payment can be made using the following mode: USM Online payment via EPayment@USM Undergraduate Student Admission Unit Academic Management Division, Registry, Level 2, Chancellory Building, Universiti Sains Malaysia, 11800 Penang, Malaysia.
