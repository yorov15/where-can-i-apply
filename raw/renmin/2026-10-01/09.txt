Notice Regarding Application Fee Payment The university performs daily maintenance on the financial payment system. During maintenance, the online application and payment functions of Online Application System for International Student s （https://international.ruc.edu.cn/application ）will be temporarily unavailable from 11:00 PM to 1:30 AM daily. Additionally, due to university financial operations, the online application and payment function of the Online Application System for International Students will be temporarily unavailable from 10:00 PM on December 28, 2025, to 12:00 AM on January 1, 2026. Applicants are advised to schedule their applications appropriately, avoiding the financial system maintenance periods. During maintenance, please save any completed or submitted information. Applications will be considered successfully submitted once the payment function resumes and the application fee is paid. We apologize for any inconvenience caused. II. Statements Our university has never established any co operative relationships with intermediaries or third -party companies regarding international student admissions. All admissions information is released exclusively through our official channels. Applicants should consult or submit applications directly th rough official channels to guard against false advertising and fraudulent activities. Our university does not collect any form of "agency fees" or "guarantee deposits." Any promises of admission or charges made under the guise of "partner agencies" are unrelated to our institution. Do not pay application fees to any individual or unofficial account. If in doubt, verify information through official contact channels. Report suspicious activities promptly to our university or local police authorities. Thank you for your interest and support! We look forward to welcoming you to campus!
