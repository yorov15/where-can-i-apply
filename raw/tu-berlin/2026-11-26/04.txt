The following provides an overview of the documents you need to upload when applying and registering, organized by degree/program type . This overview aims to make things easier for you by providing you in advance with the information you need concerning application and registration. The required documents must be submitted with the respective application via your personal TUB account (tuPORT) on the TU application portal. Access application portal/Browser Please use the Edge or Chrome browser. Only these browsers allow you to view the page in its entirety. Please check well in advance of the application deadline whether you have access to the TU portal (tuPORT). If you already have an active TU account, have successfully logged into the TU portal (tuPORT), and can see the "Study & Teaching" app, you can start your application right away. If you are not yet able to log in to the TU portal (tuPORT), please request access in good time at the beginning of the application phase: link to create application access This does not oblige you to apply, but it does give you the opportunity to get help in case of errors. At the end of the application period, the server may be overloaded, meaning that access may not always be possible immediately. If you encounter technical problems, please contact support as soon as possible. Please note: You cannot submit an application without access to the TU portal (tuPORT). This is how you help us review your documents faster: Format and size of uploads: Please note that failure to comply with these guidelines may result in applications or registrations not being submitted or processed. Please pay attention to the readability of your scanned documents and avoid to send fotos of your documents. If you do not have a scanning device at home, you can also use an appropriate smartphone app. Sign forms: Forms must be signed by hand or using the freehand function in PDF readers. Digital signatures are not accepted. PDF format A file name may not exceed 40 characters and may not include umlauts, other special symbols and spaces max. 4 MB per file One file per document (all pages belonging to a file are to be sorted in the correct order, all scans must be legible and the right way up) Please note that TU Berlin reserves the right to request original documents or officially certified copies of uploaded documents on a random basis or in cases of doubt concerning the authenticity or suitability of documents. If the documents required for the application or registration were not issued in German or English, a certified translation into German or English must also be included. Here you will find detailed explanations about the status of your application . Information on data protection : Be sure to mark your personal document as a copy. You can black out any unnecessary data. Your full name, date of birth, and if applicable, registered address, such as when applying for consideration in the quota of minor applicants, are needed. Underage students and applicants are capable of acting independently in all matters concerning their studies at their respective university, as long as they have reached the age of 16 (Section 9 (4) Berlin Higher Education Act). Re-Enrollment If you were previously enrolled in the same degree program with the same study and examination regulations at TU Berlin, you are a re-registrant. As a re-registrant wanting to re-apply for the first semester you need to submit the Credit transfer application - part A with the placement into the first term of studying ("Fachsemester") from the relevant examination board as well. Further information you can find here . Applying for a higher semester For an application for a higher semester, the following documents have to be submitted to the TU Berlin Graduate Admissions Department in due form and time incl. the approval of the examination board. Credit transfer application - part A with the placement into the appropriate term of studying ( "Fachsemester" ) if you want to apply for a lateral entry in a Master's degree course ("Quereinstieg") Credit transfer application - part A with the placement into the appropriate term of studying (" Fachsemester" ) if you want to continue a formerly cancelled enrolment into a Master's degree program at TU Berlin ("Wiedereinschreiber") Clarification of definitively failed exam results If you have definitively failed an exam during your previous studies at a German university - meaning you have lost your eligibility to take the exam - and would like to apply for a master's program at TU Berlin, you will need a decision from the relevant TU Berlin examination board stating that the definitively failed exam is not a reason to deny your application to the new program. The reviewed Application for Recognition of Previously Obtained Credits - Part C must bear the stamp and signature of the examination board or a digital signature from the examination board. The Application for Recognition of Previously Obtained Credits - Part C with the current decision by the examination board must be uploaded with the application to the TU portal (tuPORT).
