Find more information here . Swarthmore financial aid is renewable, and returning domestic students must reapply for aid each year. Nov. 1 - Questbridge National College Match Nov. 15 - Fall Early Decision Jan. 4 - Winter Early Decision Jan. 4 - Regular Decision April 1 - Transfer Applicants Applicants who apply by the deadline will receive their financial aid decision at the same time as their admission decision. Important Information for Prospective International Applicants If you're an international applicant, wait to submit your financial aid application until after you've received your admission decision. There's a two-week document processing period built into the January 4 deadline for your CSS Profile and other financial aid documents. Missing the financial aid deadline will affect your aid offer and your ability to matriculate. Learn more about how to apply for financial aid as an international applicant . Prospective international students should not submit their financial aid application until admission has been decided; see the International Student Application Instructions for further details. Financial Aid Application Deadlines for Current Students Dec. 1 - Recommended deadline for students using the College's payment plan Decisions delivered by Late May 2026 March 1 - Deadline for students not using the College's payment plan Decisions delievered by Late June 2026 Additional Tips for Domestic Applicants Double-check your application via your financial aid checklist in mySwarthmore before and after submitting to make sure it's complete. Allow for online processing time as your financial aid checklist does not update immediately after uploading documentation to IDOC Make sure to include all required income documents, including: Full U.S. federal tax return (with all schedules) W-2s, 1099s, or other income statements The information you enter on the CSS Profile and FAFSA should come directly from official documents. Do not round or use estimates. Save a copy of everything you submit, just in case you need it later. If you're planning a leave of absence or studying abroad: You still need to meet all financial aid deadlines, even while away from campus. Missing the application deadline will affect your aid eligibility and possibly your ability to return to campus. Need more time? If something unexpected happens (like a personal emergency or natural disaster), contact the Financial Aid Office . We may be able to offer deadline flexibility under certain circumstances.
