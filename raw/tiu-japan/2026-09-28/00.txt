Tuition, Fees, and Scholarships Enrollment Procedures Home Admissions How to Apply How to Apply Application Flow for the E-Track Program Application Flow for Bachelor Program Step 1 Before Applying 1) Read the Application Guidelines. 2) Confirm your application eligibility. 3) Choose your major. 4) Check the Application Schedule. Step 2 Application 1) Register My TIU Page account (Online). 2) Pay the application fee. 3) Complete your Online Application (My TIU page). 4) Send the physical documents to our office, ensuring they arrive before the deadline. Common Application Mistakes and Important Notes regarding Physical Documents Step 3 Screening TIU reviews all completed applications. Step 4 Announcement of Results 1) Check your admission results. 2) Confirm your admission and scholarship results on your My TIU Page if admitted. Step 5 Enrollement Procedure & Visa 1) Pay the enrollment fee. 2) Submit Certificate of Eligibility (COE) related documents. (Click here for COE Flow) 3) Pay tuition and fees. 4) Receive Certificate of Eligibility (COE). 5) Apply for a student visa at a Japanese embassy or consulate. Step 6 Departure to Japan 1) Receive information about orientation. 2) Arrive in Japan before the start of the semester.
