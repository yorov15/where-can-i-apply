The online form and the physical materials must be received by the E-Track Admissions Center by the deadline. Late materials will not be accepted. Please send documents well before the deadline to ensure they arrive on time. Applicants from Bangladesh, Nepal and other countries that have had longer wait times for visa issuance are encouraged to apply for the first or second application period of the spring or fall intake, respectively. Applying for the third application period may significantly increase the risk of late visa issuance. Rejected applicants and admitted applicants who withdraw cannot re-apply for the same intake, but can apply for the next intake. Alternative Payment Schedule for Applicants from Bangladesh, Myanmar, Nepal, Uzbekistan, Sri Lanka and Pakistan Tuition and Fees Applicants of the above nationalities must pay the full amount of tuition and fees for the entire first year of study before applying for a COE. Tuition and fees may be paid per-semester from the third semester onward. TIU will inform the students to whom the advance payment of fees rule applies on the results announcement day.
