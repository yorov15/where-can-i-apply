Supplementary Information A one-time 40,000 JPY Alumni Association fee will be added to the first tuition and fees invoice. All applicants for the English Track Program may apply for a TIU Tuition Reduction Scholarship. Tuition and fees do not include accommodation. Applicants who have a sibling currently attending TIU or has graduated from TIU may be eligible for a 50% reduction on the enrollment fee. If multiple siblings enroll at the same time, one sibling pays the full enrollment fee while a 50% reduction is applied to the second sibling onward. Nationals of Bangladesh, Myanmar, Nepal, Uzbekistan, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka are required to pay the full annual amount of tuition and fees for the first year of study by the designated date. See below. Refund Policy If a successful applicant decides not to enroll in TIU after paying all stipulated fees, they are eligible to have the tuition, operations fee, and alumni association fee refunded back to them. However, the enrollment fee is non-refundable. To receive the refundable fees, a "Reimbursement Application Form" must be requested and submitted to the E-Track Admissions Center ( etrack-admissions@tiu.ac.jp) by the deadline. Under no circumstances will it be possible to request a refund after the deadlines have passed.
