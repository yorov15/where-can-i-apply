Instructions for CSCA Sichuan University's application period for the 2026 undergraduate programs is from January 1, 2026, to May 30, 2026. V. Submission Requirements Applicants to Sichuan University's undergraduate programs must provide CSCA scores. For Liberal Arts programs (Chinese-taught), CSCA scores (Chinese Language and Mathematics) are required. For Liberal Arts programs (English-taught), CSCA scores (Mathematics) are required. For Science/Engineering programs (Chinese-taught), CSCA scores (Chinese Language, Mathematics, and Physics) are required. For Science/Engineering programs (English-taught), CSCA scores (Mathematics and Physics) are required. For Medical programs (Chinese-taught), CSCA scores (Chinese Language, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry) are required. For Medical programs (English-taught), CSCA scores (Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry) are required.
