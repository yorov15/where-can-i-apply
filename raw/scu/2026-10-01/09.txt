Application Materials and Process Application Process Applicants shall apply online via the international student application website of Sichuan University at https://scu.17gz.org/member/login.do within the specified application period. Proof of Financial Source (or Proof of Guardian's Financial Guarantee). Proof of financial capability can be one of the following documents, including family bank deposit certificate and parents/guardian's income certificate, with a recommended amount of no less than 5,000 US dollars.
