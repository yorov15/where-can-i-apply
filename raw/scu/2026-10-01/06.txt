PART 1 Scholarship Details and Standards "Study at SCU" Outstanding Student Scholarship Undergraduate Students : Partial scholarship, up to a maximum of 20,000 RMB/year. Postgraduate Students : Full scholarship covering tuition, accommodation subsidy, medical insurance, and living expenses. PART 3 Applicant Eligibility Age Requirements Undergraduate applicants should generally be 18 to 22 years old. Academic Requirements Undergraduate applicants must hold a high school diploma. Nationality Requirements Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens with a valid passport.
