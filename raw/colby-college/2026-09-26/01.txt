Dates and Deadlines HOME APPLY In This Section College Profile Requirements Dates and Deadlines Applying for Aid First-Year Pathways Arts Supplement Gap Year Maine Commitment NESCAC Admissions Practices FAQ Colby has three admissions rounds, including two binding rounds of Early Decision: Early Decision I with a deadline of November 15 and Early Decision II with a deadline of January 4. Applicants for both rounds will learn of their decision about a month after the deadline. We also have a Regular Decision round with a deadline of January 4. If you're 100 percent sure that Colby is for you, consider applying Early Decision. We'll give you our decision quickly, but if you are admitted, we expect you to withdraw all other applications. If you're still weighing your options, Regular Decision applications are due January 4, and we'll let you know our decision by April 1.
