Applying for Aid Through the Colby Commitment, we pledge to meet 100 percent of each admitted student's demonstrated financial need without loans. Families with a total income of $200K or less with typical assets have a parent/guardian contribution of no more than $20K, and families with a total income of less than $75,000 (and assets typical of this income range) will have a parent or guardian contribution of $0. Learn more about financial aid at Colby. College Board CSS Profile Colby's CSS code number is 3280. The CSS Profile is required for determining eligibility for institutional funds. Parents' and students' 2025 income tax returns with all pages, schedules, and W-2s. Tax documents must be sent via the College Board's Institutional Documentation Service (IDOC). Aid recipients must reapply for financial aid each year. Aid may increase or decrease with changes in family circumstances. Students who do not apply for financial aid prior to admission will not be considered for College grant assistance until their junior year, unless their family financial circumstances change unexpectedly. Colby aid packages contain grants and work-study aid-but not loans. When you get your aid package you may see grants, both institutional and federal, as well as campus employment.
