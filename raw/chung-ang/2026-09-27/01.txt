CAU Dragon A Scholarship The CAU Dragon Scholarship is an opportunity for exchange students to work at on campus activities. The assigned mandatory hours are 10 or 12 hours per week and pay is given accordingly. This opportunity is only open to those universities that have an agreement regarding the Dragon A Scholarship.
