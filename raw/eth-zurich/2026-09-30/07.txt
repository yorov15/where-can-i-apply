Bachelorstudents are eligible after passing successfully the first year examination in all parts. Masterstudents (without an ETH-BSc) may apply after a min of 40 ECTS in the preceding master study year. Non-participation on examination(s) due to illness must be proofed to us with a medical certification, issued not older than one day after the planned exam.
