ETH Zurich offers Bachelor's degree programmes in the following subject areas: Architecture and Civil Engineering Engineering Sciences Natural Sciences and Mathematics System-oriented Natural Sciences Public Policy (Career Officer) The study programmes at ETH Zurich provide sound professional knowledge, practical experience and expertise in interdisciplinary cooperation as well as training in social skills and critical thinking.
