admission stage August 28 Deadline for signing tuition contracts for additional admission stage August 29 Publication of enrollment orders for additional admission stage Olympiads Today, many Olympiads are organized in Russia, in which everyone has the right to take part free of charge. Mostly the right to enter any Russian university is given only by a victory in the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren and Olympiads of I, II and III levels, which are included in the list of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia. Successful results of participation in the Olympiad (the status of a prize-winner or winner) gives an applicant the opportunity to either be enrolled in a university without an admission examinations, or count them as the maximum score for the Unified State Exam in the major subject of the Olympiad. The type of privilege directly depends on the level of the Olympiad and the status of the participant (prize-winner or winner). Each Russian university has the right to decide for itself which benefit to provide. All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren Organizers: the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia. Participants: Russian and international schoolchildren studying in Russian organizations of secondary general education. Benefits: The winners and prize-winners of the All-Russian Olympiad finals are enrolled without entrance examinations. Rosatom Subject Olympiad in Physics and Mathematics for schoolchildren Organizers: MEPhI and the State Atomic Energy Corporation ROSATOM. Participants: Students of 7-11 forms (grades) who are mastering general (basic and secondary) education in Russia and foreign countries. It is a multilevel Olympiad: I level (physics), II level (mathematics). Benefits: The winners of the final (full-time) round can count on benefits for admission to Russian universities. Joint Interuniversity Mathematical Olympiad for Schoolchildren Organizers: a group of universities, including RUDN University, National Research Nuclear University MEPhI, St. Petersburg State Electrotechnical University, the Far Eastern Federal University, the Ural Federal University named after the first President of Russia B.N. Yeltsin and others. Participants: Students of the 11th grade who are mastering general (basic and secondary) education in Russia and foreign countries. Benefits: Winners can count on benefits when entering Russian universities. Open Doors: Russian Scholarship Project by Association "Global Universities" OPEN DOORS Olympiad is your chance for early admission to a tuition-free Master's or Ph.D. program at one of the leading universities in Russia! PARTICIPATE Organizers Association of Leading Russian Universities "Global Universities" with the support of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation. Participants Foreign citizens, stateless persons, and compatriots living abroad who have completed or are completing the required level of education. Opportunities for Winners Tuition-free study at leading Russian universities Programs taught in English or Russian Simplified admission process Access to Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, and Postdoctoral tracks Additional privileges and opportunities offered by participating universities Eligibility Bachelor's track - applicants with completed secondary education Master's track - applicants with a Bachelor's degree or equivalent PhD track - applicants with a Master's or Specialist degree Postdoctoral track - applicants holding a PhD degree Olympiad Format All stages of the Olympiad are held online in English or Russian (participant's choice). Participants can join from anywhere in the world. Subject Areas The Olympiad covers a wide range of interdisciplinary fields, including: Engineering Physics IT and Computer Science Medicine Economics Linguistics and other areas.
