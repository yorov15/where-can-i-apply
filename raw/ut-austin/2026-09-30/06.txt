Texas Global International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS) ISSS supports international students in adjusting to life on the Forty Acres. This unit handles visas and immigration support services such and insurance and financial aid, and provides myriad opportunities for social activity and connection. Once you've been admitted to UT Austin, ISSS will introduce you to your new life in Texas. ISSS TOEFL/IELTS/DET Information International applicants must submit either an official Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL), International English Language Testing System (IELTS) or Duolingo English Test (DET) demonstrating an adequate knowledge of English. We cannot accept institutional TOEFL (ITP) scores, TOEFL Essentials scores or IELTS General Training scores. International applicants are exempt from this requirement if they: Are from a qualifying country, or Graduate from a high school in the United States or a qualifying country after completing at least three years of study. Minimum scores acceptable for admission are: TOEFL: 79 (120 scale) or 4 (6-point scale) internet-based test IELTS: An overall band of 6.5 on the Academic Examination DET: 115 minimum overall score TOEFL IELTS DET Qualifying Countries For purposes of the TOEFL or IELTS requirement, international applicants are exempt only if they are from a country where English is the sole official language and the primary conversational language of the country. In some countries, English is but one of the officially recognized languages or it is not the primary language used to communicate outside of the classroom. Students from the following countries are exempt from the TOEFL/IELTS requirement: American Samoa Australia Bahamas Barbados Belize Canada (except Quebec) Dominica Ghana Grenada Grand Cayman Guyana India Ireland Jamaica Kenya Liberia New Zealand Nigeria Singapore Trinidad/Tobago Turks and Caicos Islands United Kingdom U.S. Pacific Trust Submitting Scores The University's TOEFL code is 6882. There is no institutional code for the IELTS or DET examination. To fulfill the requirement with scores from the IELTS, please use the IELTS electronic score delivery service to send your scores to the "University of Texas at Austin" account. To send your scores to DET, select "University of Texas at Austin - Undergraduate Admissions". Questions? We've got the answers.
