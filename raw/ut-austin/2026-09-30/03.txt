Attending a university is a big financial decision for students and families. Tuition is an important piece and can vary depending on whether you're considered "in state" (a Texas Resident), applying from outside of Texas or depending on which academic college you're enrolled in - just to name a few. Cost of Attendance Net Price Calculator Calculating what you actually have to pay can feel complicated. We have a tool to make it easier. Our Net Price Calculator provides personalized estimates of the net price (what you will actually have to pay) of attending based on your major, personal finances, housing, estimated financial aid, possible VA Education Benefits and more. By gaining a better understanding of the net price, you'll know what to expect and you can feel more prepared to make your UT education possible.
