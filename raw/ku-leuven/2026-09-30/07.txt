Consult your tuition fee Your initial tuition fee consists of a fixed cost plus an amount per credit based on a calculation of 60 credits. Below you can find a representation of the fixed cost and the amount per credit that applies to the corresponding tuition fee amount indicated in the tuition fee calculator . Please note, these amounts may be subject to indexation and may therefore increase annually. From mid March onwards, where applicable, a recalculation is carried out based on the actual amount of credits mentioned in your ISP. More information can be found on the Calculation webpage. If you're planning to take up 18 ECTS or less , you may request a recalculation of your tuition fee based on the exact number of credits. You can submit your request via the online form for the 2026-2027 academic year.
