This estimate includes study materials, housing costs (rent, heating, water, electricity, etc.), food, clothing, laundry, medical & insurance costs, and other expenses - e.g. leisure, groceries, transport, etc. Please note that Belgium applies a general indexation mechanism, which has an impact on all expenses such as food, rent, transport, tuition fees, ... Given that costs are always on the rise, the average cost of your studies might evolve. Consult the current consumer price index in Belgium.
