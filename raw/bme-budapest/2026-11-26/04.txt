Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME) brings together students and faculty in an international atmosphere that fosters groundbreaking research and innovation. At BME, we offer over 50 English-language Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral programs in engineering, natural sciences, economics, and management. On this page, you will find detailed application guidelines and admissions information. You can study at BME with a scholarship or as a self-funded student, by paying tuition fees . Various scholarships help you cover tuition fees and your living expenses during your studies. Successful PhD students may receive additional support from research grants. Have you found a suitable degree programme ? The checklist below will guide you through the application process. Please note that for the 2027 spring intake not every degree programme is available . Please refer to the programme description on the Degree Programmes page . Application deadline The next application period (for programmes starting in February 2027) for fee-paying students is 15 October - 15 November, 2026. PhD programmes may have different application deadlines, please contact the relevant faculty for more information. Application requirements Please note that individual units may establish additional criteria for admission, such as specific academic backgrounds, oral examinations , or minimum language test scores higher than the general BME minimum requirements. For more information, please see the relevant programme pages . Below is the list of the documents you need to prepare for applying to BME's degree programmes. Preparatory programme a) CV and motivational letter b) copy of passport c) high School certificate d) proof of English language proficiency e) Receipt of the transfer of EUR 150 application fee BSc, OTM a) CV and motivational letter b) copy of passport c) high School certificate d) proof of English language proficiency e) Receipt of the transfer of EUR 150 application fee MSc a) CV and motivational letter b) copy of passport c) BSc certificate d) transcripts of previous studies e) two recommendation letters f) proof of English language proficiency g) receipt of the transfer of EUR 150 application fee BSc, OTM, MSc transfer a) CV and motivational letter b) copy of passport c) high school or BSc certificate d) transcript of previous studies e) transcript of current studies f) description of the subjects g) proof of English language proficiency h) two recommendation letters (for MSc applicants) i) receipt of the transfer of EUR 150 application fee PhD Please contact the relevant faculty . Applicants need to demonstrate their proficiency in English by submitting standardized English language tests results. Language requirements for Preparatory Programmes Students applying for Preparatory Programmes are required to prove English language skills at least B1 level by either of the following certificates: IELTS & IELTS Online min. 4.5 Cambridge B1 Preliminary (PET) min. 140 TOEFL iBT & TOEFL iBT Home Edition min. 42 Trinity Integrated Skills In English (ISE) min. ISE I TOEIC Listening and Reading* min. 275 and TOEIC Speaking and Writing* min. 120 Pearson Academic (PTE) min. 43 Duolingo English Test min. 60 OR: a Medium of Instruction Certificate OR: a successful English skills entrance exam in the BME E-admission system Language requirements for BSc or MSc Programmes Students applying for BSc or MSc Programmes are required to submit one of the following documents of English language proficiency (minimum level B2): IELTS & IELTS Online min. 5.5 Cambridge B2 First (FCE) min. 160 TOEFL iBT & TOEFL iBT Home Edition min. 72 Trinity Integrated Skills In English (ISE) min. ISE II TOEIC Listening min. 400 / Reading min. 385 and TOEIC Speaking min. 160 / Writing min. 150 Pearson Academic (PTE) min. 50 Duolingo English Test min. 100 OR: a Medium of Instruction Certificate In special cases (e.g., inability to take exams due to international sanctions, unavailability of service, etc.), exceptional permission has to be requested in advance for an internal (BME) English language test. For language requirements in PhD programmes, please contact the relevant programme coordinator or visit the appropriate Doctoral School programme website. Admission procedure When applying to our programmes, you are required to complete an electronic e-admission exam or placement test. For transfer applicants, the corresponding faculty will evaluate the preliminary studies and judge whether an admission test is necessary. NOTE: An admission test is not required for preparatory courses. Online application Before you apply, please carefully read the programme descriptions to check the entry criteria and language requirements. Create an e-admission account. Fill out the application form and upload the required documents. Upload the payment receipt for the application fee. See how to pay the application fee by card here . Submit your application. How to apply to BME - step-by-step guide for fee-paying students Need help with your application to BME? We've got your back! Our step-by-step tutorial video makes the process simple and stress-free. After you apply For detailed information about the application procedure , notification steps, and admission decisions, please see the Application Guide on the e-admission page. Support for admitted students Admitted international students are supported by BME's International Mentor Team . This dedicated team will help you navigate both BME and Budapest. Whether it's administrative tasks (such as how to obtain a health insurance card), finding accommodation , or getting around Budapest, the Mentor Team has you covered. They will assist you in making the most of your BME experience from the get-go. See our Student Services page for more.
