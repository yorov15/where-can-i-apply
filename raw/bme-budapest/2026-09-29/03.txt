The Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship is a programme for international students. The programme aims to promote cultural understanding and strengthen economic and cultural relations between Hungary and other countries. Please visit Tempus Public Foundation's website for more information and to check if this scholarship is available for your country. The Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship aims to strengthen the ties between Hungarian diaspora communities and Hungary by providing young people living in Hungarian diaspora communities with access to study programmes in Hungary. For more information, please visit Tempus Public Foundation's website for more information. The Scholarship for Christian Young People programme was founded by the Government of Hungary in the spirit of its solidarity policy towards persecuted Christian minorities. For more information, please visit the webiste of the Hungary Helps Agency . The Students at Risk Programme aims to support students who started their studies in Ukraine by helping them continue their studies and earning a bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree in Hungary. Please visit Tempus Public Foundation's website for more information.
