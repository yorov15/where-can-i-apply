Admission to Laurea programmes Students with non-italian qualification Documents Students with non-italian qualification Documents required for admission to Bachelor of Science programmes If you hold a non-Italian qualification , during the admission test procedures (test registration or recording an alternative test) and enrolment, you must submit documents to verify that you meet the admission requirements (see the summary table of required documents). All document required must be uploaded in the Online Services (Bachelor of Science and five-year single-cycle programmes: entrance or recovery tests and enrolment > Enrolment documents). If the required documents cannot be verified, Politecnico di Milano may revoke your enrolment. Politecnico di Milano accepts translations in Italian (preferred), English, French and Spanish. All students currently residing in Italy, who are attending a foundation course , must also upload: Foundation course enrolment certificate, specifying the start and end dates of the course, with the stamp and signature of the competent office All document required must be uploaded in the Online Services (Bachelor of Science and five-year single-cycle programmes: entrance or recovery tests and enrolment > Enrolment documents). At any time during the admission process, the International Students Office may request additional documents, including the CIMEA Statements, to complete further verifications of qualifications. The deadline to upload these documents corresponds to the test/alternative test registration periods . Further information: Apostille The Apostille must be obtained in the country where the qualification was issued (provided that the country has signed the Hague Convention ). You can find the list of competent authorities for each country at this link . Please check whether the electronic Apostille is available in your country. Apostilles are accepted for documents signed by: a Rector, Dean, administration office manager or other authorised representative appointed by the university/school, declaring the award of the qualification a notary public, declaring that they have checked the authenticity of the qualification/certificate Important notes: Apostilles are not accepted for signatures of a notary or other official declaring that a copy of the qualification/certificate conforms to the original (true copy). The legalisation of a qualification must not be confused with its official translation. The certificate of conformity of the translation or the declaration of conformity made by the translator, in the case of sworn translations, are not legalisations. Paper Apostille is no longer accepted. For all details regarding CIMEA Statement of Verification and Statement of Comparability , please check their website .. ATTENTION: CIMEA service availability may be limited during holiday periods. Please check their website for updates and plan accordingly. The certificate must be translated, if necessary, and verifiable through the methods described above. Students who have completed integration years to fill missing requirements For the following cases: 10 or 11 years of schooling missing APs missing university entrance examination Such students must complete the previous documents with one of the following documents: a) Certificate attesting the completion of 1 or 2 years of university studies, depending on whether the secondary school diploma was obtained after a total of 11 or 10 school years Such certificate must be issued by the competent office in the original language accompanied by an official translation if not available in English, French and Spanish (Politecnico di Milano accepts translations preferably in Italian, otherwise English, French and Spanish), and must be verifiable through one of the following methods: Legalisation from the competent Italian Diplomatic Representative; Electronic Apostille or paper Apostille , obtained from the competent authority in the country of origin (for further details, see the dedicated paragraph); Online database provided by the institution that issued the document. The link to the database and the methods of consultation must be provided by the institution itself. The certificate must state that the university year or years were completed by passing all the examinations required under the programme regulations. b) Certificate attesting the passing of a Foundation Course in Italy (with a duration of at least one Academic Year) at a recognised Italian university This document must be issued by the competent office of the university where the course was completed.
