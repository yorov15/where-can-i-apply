Admission to Laurea programmes Students with non-italian qualification Admission to Laurea programmes Students holding a non-Italian qualification Required qualifications and documents, reserved places, study visas and other information If you hold a non-Italian qualification , the admission procedure for Bachelor of Science programmes at Politecnico di Milano is the same as for all other students: you must take an admission test and, once you have passed it, complete the enrolment process by the relevant deadlines. In addition, regardless of your nationality or country of residence, you must meet certain specific requirements and provide the documents required to assess whether your educational background is suitable for admission. This page provides all the information you need to understand what you need to do to be admitted to Politecnico di Milano with a non-Italian qualification. Browse this page Reserved places for international students Admission requirements Documents to submit Language requirements Student visa Previous academic career evaluation Reserved places for international students For each Bachelor of Science programme, a number of places for first-year students is set each year. Some categories of international students have a specific number of places allocated to them, known as "reserved places". Find out below whether you are considered an "equivalent student" or fall within the "reserved places". Types of students Students who are considered equivalent to Italian citizens citizens belonging to European Union countries, as well as those coming from Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein and the Swiss Confederation, the Republic of San Marino and the Holy See; non-EU citizens legally residing in Italy (with EU residence permit for long-term residents, with residence permit for subordinate work, self-employment, for family reasons, for asylum, for subsidiary protection, for religious reasons) or residing in an EU country with an EU residence permit for long-term residents, in possession of an Italian or foreign qualification; candidates, wherever resident, with final secondary school diplomas issued by Italian schools (state or equivalent abroad), non-EU citizens holders of CLIQ quality system linguistic certificates in levels C1 and C2, holders of qualifications issued by foreign international schools, operating in Italy or abroad, subject to bilateral agreements on the recognition of qualifications; non-EU citizens with a residence permit for study reasons, in possession of an Italian or foreign qualification, enrolled in a Bachelor programme or Single Courses (related to the Bachelor programme for which the application is being made), to foundation courses at another Italian university, who wish to transfer to Politecnico di Milano, or enrolled in an Italian language course and willing to enrol in a Bachelor programme taught in Italian at Politecnico di Milano. In all the above cases, the residence permit must be valid at the time of enrolment at Politecnico di Milano. Students eligible for "reserved places" foreign candidates who require an entry visa to Italy for long-term stays for enrolment in higher education institutions, in possession of a foreign qualification; non-EU citizens who studied in Italy with a residence permit for study reasons, but who have lost the student status or have given up their studies (*) ; non-EU citizens in possession of a residence permit for study purposes obtained in order to attend Single Courses not pertaining to the three-year Bachelor programme for which the application is being made (*) . (*) in this case it is necessary to request a new study visa in order to be able to re-enrol at university In the case of dual citizenship, one of which is Italian or of another EU country, it is the Italian citizenship or that of another EU country that prevails for the purposes of admission and enrolment. Admission requirements If you hold a non-Italian secondary school qualification, check that you meet the requirements for admission to Bachelor of Science programmes before registering for the admission test. These requirements may relate to: the total number of years of schooling the successful passing an academic suitability test The requirements are set each year by the Italian Ministry of University and Research. To find out more, visit Universitaly.it , or download the Ministry's official document and its annexes ( Annex 1 and Annex 2 ). Politecnico di Milano will check that you meet the requirements based on the documents you must upload when you register for the test and enrol (see the dedicated section ). Your enrolment will be confirmed only once the submitted documents have been checked. Find out more about the requirements Total number of years of schooling completed To be admitted to a Bachelor of Science or single-cycle Master's degree programme, you must hold a secondary school qualification obtained after completing at least a total number of 12 or 13 years of schooling completed. See Annex 1 to find out what you need to do if your educational background is shorter than this. University exams used to fill any gaps in the admission requirements In some cases, you can use examinations already taken , at university or elsewhere, to fill any gaps in the admission requirements. For example, if: your educational background covers only 10 or 11 years you hold a US high school diploma , but have not obtained the required number of APs you come from a country where passing an academic eligibility test is required for admission to university, but you have not taken this test Examinations used to meet admission requirements cannot be used for an evaluation of the previous career for the purposes of shortening the course. For this purpose, Politecnico di Milano also accepts the successful completion of a foundation course lasting at least one academic year. Requirements for specific qualifications Some types of qualification are subject to specific requirements . In particular, see Annex 1 if you hold: a qualification obtained after completing fewer than 12 years of education a US high school diploma a qualification obtained in the United Kingdom a qualification without confirmation that you have passed an academic eligibility test If you hold an International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma , see Annex 2 , page 3. An IB Diploma is valid for admission to Italian universities if you were admitted to the IB programme after completing the penultimate year of secondary school required by your education system. If you started secondary school at an Italian institution, you can be admitted with an IB Diploma if you have completed: the first three years of secondary school the two years of the IB programme You must therefore have completed a total of 13 years of schooling . To find out more about the specific requirements for the qualifications listed above, see the summary table available on this website. Documents to submit To complete your enrolment at Politecnico di Milano, if you hold a non-Italian qualification, you must submit the required documents by the relevant deadlines to verify: that your qualification is valid for admission to a Bachelor of Science programme the authenticity of your qualification Visit the dedicated page to find out which documents you need to submit and by when: Go to Documents Language requirements To enrol in a programme taught in Italian , you must demonstrate adequate Italian-language proficiency; to enrol in a programme taught in English , you must demonstrate adequate English-language proficiency. You can demonstrate that you meet this requirement by uploading one of the accepted language certificates to Politecnico di Milano's Online Services, either during the test registration process or when you enrol. To find out which certificates are accepted, see the following pages: Italian language certificates English language certificates Alternatively, the requirement is considered met if: you are a citizen of a country where Italian or English is an official language, depending on the language of the programme you obtained a secondary school qualification after completing a course of study taught in the language of the programme ((in this case, the language of instruction must be explicitly stated on the required school documents) To take the test, you must understand the language in which it is delivered . If you do not understand the language spoken by the test supervisor during the admission test, you will not be allowed to take the test . If you take the remote TOL , you must also prepare your room and workstation as instructed. For further details, see the dedicated page: Language requirements Student visa If you are a non-EU student residing abroad and do not hold a valid Italian residence permit, you must obtain a study visa to enrol in a Bachelor of Science programme at Politecnico di Milano. If you have already obtained a study residence permit following a previous enrolment - at Politecnico di Milano or another Italian university - but have withdrawn from your studies or your student status has lapsed, you cannot use the same residence permit . You must apply for a new entry visa . See the dedicated study visa page to find out when and how to apply: How to apply for a visa Previous academic career evaluation If you have already attended, even partially, a university programme abroad, you can request a previous academic career evaluation of the examinations you have already passed, which may lead to a shortened programme of study . You cannot request recognition of examinations that you used to meet a missing admission requirement . To request the evaluation, upload the following documents to the dedicated application: your transcript of examinations passed the description of each course To complete your enrolment, you must also submit to the International Students Office a legalised or apostilled copy of your transcript of examinations passed.
