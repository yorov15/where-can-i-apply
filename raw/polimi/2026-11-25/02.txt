in Engineering: admission tests, requirements and deadlines Would you like to enrol in a Bachelor's degree programme in Engineering at Politecnico di Milano? On this page, you will find all the essential information about admission and enrolment . Read the Call for Admissions and the other resources available Find out more Requirements and documents To be admitted to a programme at Politecnico di Milano, you must pass an admission test and demonstrate adequate language proficiency in the language in which your chosen programme is taught. If you hold a non-Italian qualification , you must also submit documents to verify that you meet specific requirements. We recommend preparing them in advance to make sure you meet the enrolment deadlines. See the dedicated pages: 01 Students holding a non-Italian qualification Find out about the admission requirements for students holding a non-Italian qualification, and submit the required documents by the relevant deadlines More 02 Languages Find out which language skills are required for admission and to continue your studies More Admission test To be admitted to an Engineering programme, you must take an admission test . You can choose between: the TOL (Test On Line), provided directly by Politecnico di Milano one of the recognised alternative tests provided by external organisations: TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT Would you like to see the results of the ranking lists for enrolment in the 2026/2027 academic year? Click here Find out more about Engineering admission tests: Test On Line TOL The test provided directly by Politecnico di Milano More TOLC-I The test provided by the CISIA consortium (online, in Italian) More CEnT-S The test provided by the CISIA consortium (online, in English) More SAT The test provided by the College Board (online, in English) More Enrolment Have you already passed one of the admission tests? To be admitted to an Engineering programme and officially become a student at Politecnico di Milano, you must complete the enrolment procedure. Enrolment in degree programmes for the 2027/2028 academic year will begin in May 2027 and consists of four separate phases , according to the rules and timetable set out on the dedicated page . 1st year of 2027/2028 programmes Enrolment From May 2027, take part in the enrolment process for admission to the 1 st year of Engineering Bachelor of Science programmes in 2027/2028 Find out more Additional information Guides and reference documents to help you navigate your journey towards admission to Politecnico di Milano Bando di ammissione T he Call for Admissions contains all the official information you need to access Engineering degree programmes, including requirements, procedures, deadlines, exceptions and special cases. It is the reference document to consult for full details.
