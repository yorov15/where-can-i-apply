Viaggio nella ricerca Scopri Work with us You are here: Home Students Language requirements Students Language requirements English and Italian - Academic year 2026/2027 Degree programmes Politecnico di Milano has decided as prerequisite to access all Degree Programmes, the knowledge of the language in which the course is delivered. In particular, if you wish to enrol on a: course taught in Italian - you must demonstrate your knowledge of Italian before enrolling course taught in English - you must demonstrate your knowledge of English before enrolling. Master of science Master of Science programme or Laurea Magistrale equivalent to Master of Science the Politecnico di Milano requires certified proficiency in English according to the levels, by submitting a certification that is recognised by the Politecnico. Students of an Italian-language Laurea study programme See more... Students of an English-language Laurea study programme See more... Students of the Building Engineering-Architecture Laurea Magistrale Study Programme See more... Students of Laurea Magistrale Study Programmes See more... Politecnico di Milano does not accept certifications considered expired by the issuing institution. Please note that the Politecnico di Milano does not certify the knowledge level of the English or italian language and all original certificates required for inspection are not retained. The Politecnico di Milano only registers the possession of the admission requirements and cannot in any way certify to external bodies that the foreign language certification has been obtained. The student must retain these certifications independently.
