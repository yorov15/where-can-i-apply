Viaggio nella ricerca Scopri Work with us You are here: Home Prospective students Costs and scholarships Scholarships Costs and scholarships Scholarships Bachelor of Science Students admitted to a Bachelor of Science programme can apply for Diritto allo Studio (DSU) Scholarships: financial and merit-based scholarships assigned after a Call for Grant (also known as "Bando") issued yearly. Complete information can be found here: DSU Scholarships Politecnico di Milano offers Scolarships opportunities for international students who intend to apply one of the following Bachelors of Science: BSc in Process Engineering at Cremona Campus BSc in Industrial Engineering at Piacenza Campus Find out more Master of Science Students enrolled in a Master of Science programme have several scholarships opportunities. University Financial Aid - Diritto allo Studio Universitario (DSU) See the relevant page: DSU Scholarships Merit-based scholarships Thanks to the cooperation with public and private partners, every year Politecnico di Milano offers a number of merit based scholarships to the most talented international candidates admitted to a Master of Science programme to the 1st semester. Students obtaining a scholarship will have to pay only a non-refundable administrative fee of about €170. All scholarships include a full tuition-fee waiver, some of them will provide also an additional gross allowance up to 10,000€/year For the academic year 2026-27 the scholarship candidates will have to fill out an additional application form (no fee required). Only students who have submitted their admission application during the Early Bird to the first year of a Master of Science program taught in English, paying the application fee between October 1st 2025 and December 1st 2025 , are eligible to participate in this Call. Detailed information and deadlines are available in the document below. A valid certificate of English language proficiency is required. To be considered for a scholarship, this certificate must be submitted by February 21st 2026 Call for application for merit-based international scholarships (Academic Year 2026/2027) Scholarships assignment criteria The selection criteria are based on the evaluation of your previous education and its match with the requirements for our Master of Science programmes, on the documents provided upon application and on the scholarship application form. Obviously GPA (grade point average) and academic achievements (including the portfolio when requested) are of utmost importance. Other scholarships The Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation allocates every year a number of scholarships for international students . Please visit this page for further information. Additional scholarships are offered under the Invest your Talent in Italy programme to applicants to the 1 st semester (September intake) coming from the following countries: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Brazil, Colombia, Egypt, Ethiopia, Ghana, India, Indonesia, Iran, Kazakhstan, Mexico, People's Republic of China, Republic of Korea, Tunisia, Turkey and Vietnam.
