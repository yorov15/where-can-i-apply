Click on "APPLY NOW": On the main page, click on the "APPLY NOW" button to start your application. Complete the Application Form: Fill out the application form with your personal details, selected program, and other necessary information. Choose Exam Type: Select the type of entrance exam (online or offline). Exam Schedule for Scholarships To be considered for merit-based scholarships at Central Asian University , applicants must complete the scholarship examination. Below are the scheduled exam dates for the 2025-2026 academic year : Scholarship Exam Date • August 10, 2025 Important Notes: • Students must complete the university application process and pay the 25% contract down payment before registering for the scholarship exam. • Scholarships are awarded based on exam performance and ranking within the eligible applicant pool. • The number of scholarships and tuition coverage percentages may vary depending on the number of students enrolled in each department (program) . • Scholarship results will be communicated to successful applicants after each examination cycle. For further details on scholarship eligibility and exam registration, please contact the Scholarships Office at Central Asian University. Entrance Exam Subjects: Mathematics: Questions: 20 questions Time: 45 minutes Passing Score: 50% out of 100% Chemistry: Questions: 20 questions Time: 45 minutes Passing Score: 50% out of 100% Biology: Questions: 20 questions Time: 45 minutes Passing Score: 50% out of 100% English: Questions: 60 questions Time: 30 minutes Passing Score: 50% out of 100% Registration Confirmation: After submitting your application, you will receive a confirmation SMS and email with further instructions. Additionally, representatives from the admissions office will contact you. Take the Entrance Exam: Participate in the entrance exam on the scheduled date and time. Interview: After successfully passing the English exam, applicants are invited to a mandatory 10-minute interview with the Admissions Committee. This is a brief conversation designed to assess the applicant's motivation and readiness for academic study. Exemption from Entrance Examinations: For Applicants to the Faculties of Medicine and Dentistry: English Language : Applicants may be exempted from the English entrance examination upon submission of one of the following: A valid IELTS score of 6.0 or higher; A TOEFL iBT score of 60 or higher; A SAT English subject score of 585 or above . Biology and Chemistry : Exemption from subject-specific entrance examinations is granted to applicants holding a National Certificate with a minimum grade of C+ in both Biology and Chemistry . For Applicants to the Faculties of Business, Engineering, and Hospitality Management and Tourism: English Language : Applicants may be exempted from the English entrance examination with one of the following: A valid IELTS score of 5.5 or higher; A TOEFL iBT score of 46 or higher; A SAT English subject score of 585 or above . Mathematics : Exemption from the Mathematics entrance examination is granted with either: A SAT Mathematics subject score of 585 or above ; A National Certificate with a minimum grade of C+ in Mathematics. Important Notice: Applicants seeking exemption from entrance examinations or wishing to be considered for scholarship opportunities based on their SAT scores are required to designate Central Asian University as a recipient institution in their College Board account by entering the official university code 50232 . Official submission of SAT scores is mandatory for eligibility verification and further evaluation. Admission Decision: Based on your exam results and submitted documents, you will receive an admission decision via email and a phone call. Complete Enrollment: Follow the instructions in your admission email to complete the enrollment process. Contact Information: For additional questions or assistance, please contact the admissions office: Phone: +998 (71) 200 05 22 Email: admission@centralasian.uz We look forward to welcoming you to Central Asian University! The fee for the entrance exam, per subject, is 100,000 UZS for citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan (non-refundable, in accordance with the Admissions Policy), and 10 USD for international applicants (non-refundable, in accordance with the Admissions Policy). The fee covers the costs of administering the exam. Applicants can pay online using "Uzum Bank" online service. All relevant costs are incurred by CAU.
