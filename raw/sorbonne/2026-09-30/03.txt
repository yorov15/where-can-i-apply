Please note that application and enrollment procedures vary according to your profile and the course you are applying for. share bluesky facebook linkedin mail print Published on 16/09/2021 - Updated on 20/08/2026 First year of Licence (L1) French Language Proficiency A minimum B2 level in French is required to apply for a bachelor's degree program at the faculty of Science and Engineering at Sorbonne University. You must be able to provide a diploma or certificate recognized by the French Ministry of Higher Education attesting to your proficiency level (such as TEF, TCF, or DELF).
