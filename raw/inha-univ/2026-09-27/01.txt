View details Retaking Courses Retaking a course refers to repeating a course that has already been taken to improve the student's grade (allowed for only C+ or lower, including F grades, from the second semester of 2014). Only the course with the same course number and course name and the course allowed for re-registration are eligible for retaking. (A student may retake a courses with a maximum 24 credits courseload while enrolled, except if it is a mandatory course where the student received an F grade.)
