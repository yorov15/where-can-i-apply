beneficiary period are valid only for the relevant semester unless otherwise specified. Scholarship Application Criteria Based on the credits required by the school regulations (Article 39) for each semester. The grades obtained with seasonal semesters, on-the-job training, or special credits will not be reflected in the calculation of the average score. Any corrected grades will not be accepted. Restriction of Eligibility for Application Students who fall under any of the following subparagraphs can, in principle, apply for a scholarship in the next semester after the semester in which the reason occurred Returning students - Excellent Academic Scholarship, Tuition Aid scholarship, and Inha Sarang Scholarship Re-admitted students Disciplinary offender Students with poor work performance: Work scholarship Substitutes for tuition under Article 38 of the School Regulations, Outstanding Academic Scholarship, Tuition Aid Scholarship, Inha Sarang Scholarship Violators of School Regulations and related rules. Prohibition of Double Benefits Students cannot receive double benefits. Students eligible for dual benefits may choose a scholarship in his or her favor, and where he or she has received double benefits, he or she shall return one of scholarship to the school. Exceptions shall be made to the following scholarships: Work Scholarship Scholarship for Students with Disabilities Sibling Scholarship Student Loan Interest Exemption Scholarship Merit Scholarship Foreign Language Excellence Scholarship Service Scholarship Social Service Scholarship Special scholarships approved by the University President Restriction of Benefits In the case of any of the following, the scholarship student's qualifications will be forfeited during the remaining period of study: Those who have been suspended from school for more than 30 days. Those who have falsified information in applying for a scholarship. Those who violated the purpose of the scholarship programs. In the following cases, scholarship students' qualifications are suspended for one semester: Those who have been disciplined and suspended for less than 30 days Jeonseok Scholarship, Outstanding Academic Scholarship, and Outstanding Scholarship for Freshmen of recipients who have given up their courses under Article 5 of the Regulations for Course Registration (Course Withdrawal). Where a student, who changes his or her major according to Article 24 of the School Regulations, has benefited from the Outstanding Academic Scholarship or Tuition Aid Scholarship. Students who exceed the period of study prescribed in Article 8 of the School Regulations or take partial courses Students who have more than eight regular registrations (ten in architecture and twelve in medicine) including fails and interim leave among the beneficiaries of Freshmen Scholarships, the National Merit Scholarships, Global Scholarships, the Faculty's Children Sscholarships, and the North Korean Defector Scholarships. Those who violate the purpose of the scholarship programs. Scholarship Payment Method The Director of Student Affairs will issue a certificate of reduction or scholarship payment to the relevant college and related departments upon issuance of a tuition bill based on the list of scholarships approved by the University President. The scholarship payment certificate may only be used when the replacement or difference is paid for the semester tuition, and will not be exchanged for cash. However, an exception may be made to those selected after enrollment from among off-campus scholarships and on-campus scholarships. Replacement of Scholarship Student Where a selected scholarship student falls under Articles 10 through 12 of the Regulations on the Payment of Scholarships, he/she may be replaced with a qualified student. Course credits and course abandonment availability Name of scholarship Course abandonment unavailable At least 16 credits for 1st to 3rd grader (minimum 12 credits for 4th grader) → for Architecture majors, 16 credits for 1st grader to 4th, 12 credits for 5th grader → Outstanding academic scholarship Scholarships for freshmen such as Jeongseok, Top-ranking, Biryong, Asia Pacific Logistics, Global Finance, and Next-Generation Core scholarships Course abandonment available Minimum of 15 credits for the first to the third grader (Minimum 4 credits for the 4th grader) → For Architecture major, 15 credits for the first - the fourth-grader, nine credits for the 5th grader → Tuition aide, Inha Sarang, 1/2 of household difficulties, Volunteer service , Special selection for children in rural and rural areas, Faculty's children, Social care, Competition, Global, North Korean defectors. etc. First to third graders must apply for at least 16 credits and fourth graders for at least 12 credits. The earned credits from field training, the special exam for credit acquisition, or seasonal semester will be included in graduation credits, but not be inserted in the credit for calculating scholarships and academic probation. Dropping Courses Not Permitted: Even if a student applies for 21 credits or more and receives 18 credits or more due to dropping a course, he or she will not be able to receive any benefits.
