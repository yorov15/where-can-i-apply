Academics > Scholarships > Application for Scholarships Application for Scholarships 현재페이지명 /WEB-INF/jsp/k2web/com/cop/site/layout.jsp eng_JW_MS_K2WT001_S How to Apply The period of validity of the application for scholarships and benefits shall be valid only for the relevant semester unless otherwise provided. Off-campus Scholarships The selection criteria of off-campus scholarship providers are the highest priority. A separate notice will be made if students can apply. On-campus Scholarship In principle, students selected for the Outstanding Academic Scholarship will selected by each faculty (department) after grades are announced at the end of the semester without application, and then the tuition bill is reduced or exempted. (However, the application and selection will be conducted separately for each department (school).) For Sibling scholarships and Scholarships for Students with Disabilities, the application period and method will be separately provided. Other Guidelines Total scholarship benefits cannot exceed tuition fees (except for the scholarship for living expenses). Repayment of student loans from the Korea Scholarship Foundation and other institutions is the top priority.
