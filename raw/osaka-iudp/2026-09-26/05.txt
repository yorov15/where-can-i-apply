The first installment is due in May; the second installment is due in November. 1. Undergraduate/graduate students Your tuition fees shall be paid from your financial institution account at a member bank of the Japanese Bankers Association or Japan Post Bank by automatic bank account transfer on the day specified by the university. You need to register your account to the Tuition Payment Account Registration System. ・Tuition fee for the first semester: May 27 | Tuition fee for the second semester: November 27 ・If the payment due date falls on a non-business day, your payment will be processed on the next business day. ・Make sure you have enough money in your account to cover the amount due. ・To register or change your account details for automatic bank account transfer, please go to the "Tuition Payment Account Registration" page below. Tuition Payment Account Registration *The registration period for the first half of AY2026 : April 1 to 19, 2026 *The registration period for the second half of AY 2026: October 1 to 18, 2026 ・If you failed to register during the registration period, we will send you a bank transfer request form designated by our university. Please make payment via bank transfer by the specified date. (You will be charged the transfer fee.) 2. Auditor students, special auditors, research students Please pay at a financial institution counter via bank transfer using a bank transfer request form sent by the university by the specified date.
