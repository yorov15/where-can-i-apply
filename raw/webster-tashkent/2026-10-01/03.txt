Most students complete their bachelor's degrees within four years. Our students benefit from a unique blend of coursework, including the Global Citizenship curriculum, electives and classes within their major. Electives allow students to explore subjects beyond their primary field of study, while the Global Citizenship program equips them with a global perspective and a broad foundation in the arts, sciences, and humanities. To graduate, students must complete 120 credit hours, encompassing major-specific courses, electives and Global Citizenship requirements, ensuring a well-rounded education that prepares them for success in an interconnected world. Through this signature curriculum, you'll develop the essential competencies required to become a responsible and engaged global citizen, fully prepared to thrive in the interconnected world of the 21st century. Contact Admissions Webster Tashkent: Minors What is a minor? While "majors" are the bachelor's programs offered at American universities, "minors" are smaller specializations that students can choose to complete in addition to their major. They can be complementary to the student's major or very different. At Webster Tashkent, students may select one of the following minors: Minor in Business Minor in Economics Minor in Film, Television and Video Production Minor in International Relations Minor in Media Studies Minor in Psychology Additional Resources Founded in 1915, Webster is an independent nonprofit university with students studying at campus locations in North America, Europe and Asia, and in a robust online learning environment.
