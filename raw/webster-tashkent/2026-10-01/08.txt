Our comprehensive grants program provides financial assistance to help you achieve your educational and professional goals. For more information about our grants program or assistance with your application, please contact the Office of Research and Sponsored Programs. We are here to support you in your academic and research journey. How to Apply Applying for grants at Webster is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to submit your application: Analyze 1. Identify the appropriate grant for your needs by reviewing the grant descriptions and eligibility criteria. Preparation 2. Prepare your application, including a detailed proposal outlining your project, objectives, methodology, and budget. Apply 3. Submit your application through the Webster Grant Application Portal before the specified deadline. Types of Grants 1 Student Grants: - Undergraduate Research Grants: Designed to support undergraduate students in conducting independent research projects under the guidance of faculty mentors. - Graduate Research Grants: Provides funding for graduate students to undertake significant research projects as part of their degree requirements. 2 Faculty Grants: - Research Development Grants: Aimed at faculty members who are initiating new research projects or expanding existing ones. - Professional Development Grants: Supports faculty in attending conferences, workshops, and other professional development activities. 3 Collaborative Grants: - Interdisciplinary Research Grants: Encourages collaboration across different departments and disciplines to address complex research questions. - Community Partnership Grants: Facilitates partnerships between the university and community organizations for mutually beneficial projects. Resources and Support We offer various resources to help you succeed in your grant applications: Grant Writing Workshops: Attend our workshops to learn effective grant writing techniques and improve your chances of securing funding. Consultation Services: Schedule a one-on-one consultation with our grant advisors for personalized guidance and feedback on your proposal. Funding Databases: Access comprehensive databases of external funding opportunities relevant to your field of study or research. Apply Request information When To Apply?
