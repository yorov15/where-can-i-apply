Request Information Learn More Founded in 1915, Webster is an independent nonprofit university with students studying at campus locations in North America, Europe and Asia, and in a robust online learning environment. With its main campus in St. Louis, Missouri, USA, Webster University's network of faculty, staff, students and alumni forge powerful bonds with each other and their communities around the globe.
