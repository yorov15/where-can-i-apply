International Home > Admissions > International International Students at Webster University in Tashkent At Webster University in Tashkent, we are proud to be a global institution that welcomes students from all corners of the world. Our diverse and vibrant international community enriches the campus experience, fostering cross-cultural learning and collaboration. Located in the heart of Tashkent, Uzbekistan, we are surrounded by a rich history, thriving businesses, and a variety of cultural landmarks. Our university provides international students with access to high-quality education and valuable learning opportunities in a truly global environment. Why Choose Webster University in Tashkent? • Global Community: Webster University in Tashkent is part of the renowned Webster University network with campuses in Europe, the U.S., and Asia. Studying here allows students to experience an international education while living in one of Central Asia's most dynamic cities. • International Partnerships: We have strong collaborations with leading businesses and institutions in Uzbekistan, providing our students with internships, work experience, and career development opportunities. • Student Support Services: We offer a range of services specifically for international students, including visa support, academic advising, cultural integration programs, and language assistance. Our team is here to help you succeed both academically and personally during your time at Webster. • Cultural and Professional Opportunities: Tashkent is not only the capital of Uzbekistan but also a major hub for regional and international commerce. Our strategic location enables students to explore career prospects, internships, and partnerships with multinational companies and local businesses. Admission for International Students Webster University in Tashkent welcomes international students who meet the admissions criteria for our undergraduate and graduate programs. For detailed information on the application process, required documents, and language proficiency requirements, please visit our Admissions Page. Living in Tashkent Tashkent offers a unique cultural blend, with a rich heritage, modern infrastructure, and growing economic opportunities. As an international student, you will have the chance to explore historical sites, experience Uzbek culture, and engage with a community of students from around the world. Stay Connected Join the Webster University International Students' community and experience a world-class education in a city full of opportunities. Whether you're coming for your undergraduate or graduate studies, Webster University in Tashkent is here to support you in every step of your academic journey.
