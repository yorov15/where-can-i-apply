Undergraduate students are admitted according to their department preferences and majors are not assigned upon enrollment. Generally students begin to choose their majors and undergo sub-direction training after the second year. When applying applicants only need to fill in the name of the school or department and do not have to specify particular majors. 2. For detailed introductions of schools departments and majors please refer to the Admissions website of Peking University: www.gotopku.cn .
