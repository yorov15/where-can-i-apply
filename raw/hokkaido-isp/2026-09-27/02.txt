If they are revised before admission or during enrollment, the new amount will apply with immediate effect from the time of revision. *The full amount of the enrollment fee will be waived for students enrolling in the Bachelor's degree in the Integrated Science Program in October 2026. ISP Scholarship Programs Hokkaido University has a scholarship program for students enrolling in the Integrated Science Program (ISP). Details of that program are outlined below. a. Enrollment fee The university will waive the entire enrollment fee for the Bachelor's degree for students enrolling in October 2026. b. Tuition fees (i) We will waive the tuition fee for the first semester (October 2026 - March 2027) of the Bachelor's degree for students enrolling in October 2026. (ii) After the first semester, selection will be made on an annual basis, and tuition fees for one year or half a year will be waived. Please note such waiver is not offered to all students. Notes • The full waiver of the enrollment fee and tuition fee for the first semester is available to all ISP students except for those who receive MEXT scholarship. • This scholarship program is available during the initial 3.5 years of the Bachelor's degree and it is expected to be applicable to the Master's degree program. Other Financial Support In the case that a student is not awarded a scholarship (1) or (2) after admission, they are required to pay the tuition fees in full. However, students may apply for a full, half, or quarter tuition waiver if they are facing hardship in paying their tuition fees for financial reasons, and have demonstrated outstanding academic performance. In addition, there are scholarships available from private organizations, and those who wish to apply for such programs may apply for scholarships after being admitted to the Integrated Science Program. Details on how to apply for these scholarships will be disseminated after enrollment.
