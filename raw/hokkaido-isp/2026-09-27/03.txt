ISP students will spend their first 6months in one of the many student dorms, available in a range of sizes and costs. In subsequent years, students are expected to seek out accommodation by themselves. The university will, however, aid the students in their search, by offering advice and guidance. The rent and cost of living in Sapporo, in general, are much cheaper compared to other metropolitan areas in Japan. For more details of the accommodation, please refer to the Hokkaido University website .
