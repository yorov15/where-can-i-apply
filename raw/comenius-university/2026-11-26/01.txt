Documents Needed for Application HOW TO APPLY - STEP BY STEP ECTS Systems of Credits Study Guide for academic year 2026/2027 Full time study Post Graduate Study (PhD.) HOW TO APPLY - STEP BY STEP 1. Register on the Application Portal Scan the provided QR code or visit the Application Portal - AiS2 ePrihlas - UK The default language is Slovak, but you can switch to English (Top right corner on desktop, top left menu on mobile). Please click on "Registration" Create an account and remember your credentials - they will be required to access the application. Activate your account via the confirmation email - an activation email will be sent to the provided email address. 2. Select a Study Program Sign in and select the degree level (Bachelor/Master) and enter Management in the study field. Choose your study program: Bachelor Program (Am21) - €1,990/year Master Program (AMN21) - €2,490/year Double Degree Program (BMN24) - €2,490/year Add your selected program to your Briefcase . 3. Create and Complete Your Application Go to My Briefcase → Create a new application. After creating the application, click on My Applications again, and you will be presented with the option to Fill in the required information. At this stage, the status will be marked as Incomplete, allowing you to modify and complete it as needed. Start filling out the 10-step application . Some fields are not mandatory for foreign applicants - there is no need to worry if certain sections do not apply to you. Your progress is visible in the system (the status will be marked as Incomplete, allowing you to modify and complete it as needed), and assistance is available. At this stage, we can already see in our system that you are working on your application and are available to assist you if needed . Mandatory fields must be filled out, and the system will notify you about any missing required information. Step 9 requires you to upload the mandatory documentation. To be eligible for an invitation to the entrance exams, please upload your CV, Motivation Letter, and any other required documents. The final step is the summarization of your application. Please review all the provided information carefully and confirm your application to complete the process. 4. Pay the Application Fee After successful confirmation, go to My Applications and click on your application once more. Generate a payment order in the system (includes a QR code and credentials). Application Fee: €80 . Your application is NOT considered complete without this payment . Upload the proof of payment using the "Add to Application" option. IMPORTANT - Mandatory Documentation Curriculum Vitae (CV) - An up-to-date CV in English outlining your educational background and relevant experience. Motivation Letter - A letter in English explaining your interest in management, reasons for choosing our Faculty and future academic or career aspirations. Passport - A clear copy of your passport containing your photograph and personal information. Proof of Application Fee Payment - Confirmation of payment of the €80 application fee (see Step 5 - Pay the Application Fee). Educational Documents - Applicants must submit proof of their previous education, including the relevant school-leaving certificate or university diploma, depending on the level of study they are applying for. Applicants who have not yet completed their current studies may submit the required documents after graduation, in accordance with the applicable admission deadlines. Applicants who completed their previous education outside Slovakia must also provide official recognition of their educational qualifications . The recognition procedure and required supporting documents vary depending on the level of study and the country in which the qualification was obtained. For detailed information, please consult the relevant admission criteria and the official Education Recognition Portal . Next Steps - Entrance Examination and Admission Once your application is complete, including all mandatory attachments and payment of the application fee, you will receive an invitation to the entrance examination at the email address provided in your application. The entrance examination takes place online via video conference and assesses your practical English communication skills, comprehension, logical and analytical thinking, argumentation, academic readiness and motivation to study management. Please note that English language certificates, including IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge English or other internationally recognised qualifications, do not guarantee admission or automatically exempt applicants from the entrance examination . Applicants must fulfil the official admission requirements, unless they qualify for an exemption specified in the admission criteria. IMPORTANT - Conditional Admission and Enrolment Applicants who completed their previous education in 2026 or earlier are required to submit all relevant educational documents, including official recognition of foreign qualifications (nostrification), where applicable, no later than the official application deadline . Applications that do not meet these requirements may be considered incomplete. Applicants who are expected to complete their studies in 2027 may be admitted conditionally if they have not yet obtained their final educational documents or the required recognition of their qualifications. However, conditional admission does not guarantee enrolment . All outstanding educational documents, including the required recognition of foreign education, must be submitted no later than the official enrolment deadline. Failure to fulfil this requirement will result in the loss of the right to enrol in the programme. We strongly recommend starting the recognition process as early as possible. The procedure may take approximately 2-3 months, or longer during the summer, depending on the responsible authority and individual circumstances.
