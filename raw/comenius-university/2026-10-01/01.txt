After passing exams, they will be enrolled into the Faculty of Arts and they are not obliged to pay any tuition fees in case they manage to complete the programme within the standard length, i.e. in case of the B.A. programme within 3 years (with the exception of the programme East Asian Languages and Cultures - in this case the standard length is 4 years) and in case of the M.A. programme within 2 years. However, in case a student studies longer, he/she is obliged to pay 1400 EUR per academic year.
