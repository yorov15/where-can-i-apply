Choosing a degree When you decide what to study you should consider your interests and strengths. You should also check if the teaching and assessment methods used in the degree programme suit you. Making an application How to apply to Edinburgh, what you need to apply and when you should do it. Apply for part-time study For part-time study, you should apply directly to the University, not through UCAS. Fee status and admissions Any offer we make is made based on your tuition fee status. Competition may vary across different fee status categories, and we will review your application in relation to other applicants who have the same fee status as you. Our selection process How our colleges and schools select undergraduate students. After you apply What happens after you have applied. UCAS Clearing Under certain circumstances, you can make a late application for a place on one of our degree programmes through UCAS Clearing.
