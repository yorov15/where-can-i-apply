Asylum Seeker Scholarship The University will offer 2 awards for entry into academic year 2026-2027. The award offers tuition fee and living cost support to those most in need who are seeking asylum. Edinburgh Global Undergraduate Mathematics Scholarships The Edinburgh Global Undergraduate Mathematics Scholarship is awarded to international applicants who demonstrate outstanding academic performance. Global Talent Fund BIG Scholarships The BIG ('Backing Invisible Geniuses') Program offers prodigious young talents the opportunity to study at world-class institutions. Scholarships are open to students from around the world who have medalled in international Olympiads. The Defne-Muhtar Kent Education Foundation Scholarship at the University of Edinburgh The Defne-Mutar Kent Education Foundation Scholarship at the University of Edinburgh is a new Global Access Scholarship, launching in 2026. The Scholarship will offer sector leading financial support to an eligible undergraduate student joining the University from Turkey. The scholarship aims to support a talented student who may lack the means, or who is currently suffering significant financial hardship, to undertake international study. Royal (Dick) School of Veterinary Studies UK Graduate/International Scholarship The Royal (Dick) School of Veterinary Studies is offering two scholarships for UK Graduates and International students beginning their studies on the BVM&S full-time programme in the 2026-2027 academic year. Rosedale OSSD University of Edinburgh Scholarship Rosedale International Education and the University of Edinburgh are proud to announce the launch of the Rosedale OSSD University of Edinburgh Scholarship. Starting in the 2026 academic year, £10,000 will be awarded to one successful candidate during their first year of studies at the University of Edinburgh. This award will support students with living costs during their first year at Edinburgh, to aid their transition into higher education.
