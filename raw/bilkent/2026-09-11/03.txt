Admitted from Abroad Tuition Fees for International Students and Students Admitted from Abroad INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS AND STUDENTS ADMITTED FROM ABROAD WHO HAVE THE RIGHT TO REGISTER THROUGH THE HIGHER EDUCATION COUNCIL'S REGULATION FOR ADMISSION FROM ABROAD Tuition fee for the 2026 - 2027 academic year (including 10% VAT): - For students admitted before 2023 is 16,300 USD - For students admitted in 2023 and 2024 is 16,950 USD - For students admitted in 2025 is 17,600 USD - For students admitted in 2026 is 18,400 USD Students, who will send their tuition fees from abroad can make a wire transfer to the bank account of Garanti Bank provided below. During the process, the student's name and surname must be stated and the document showing that the wire transfer has been made, must be sent to the Registrar's Office. The annual tuition fee is paid in two equal installments, before Fall and Spring semester course registrations, by the deadline in the academic calendar. Garanti Bank / Bilkent Branch USD Account Number: 393-9088737 USD IBAN: TR10 0006 2000 3930 0009 0887 37 SWIFT CODE: TGBATRIS In line with the Higher Education Council's Regulation for Admission from Abroad , students who are eligible to register at Bilkent University and had acquired Turkish citizenship at birth but later renounced their citizenship status with the approval of Ministry of Internal Affairs, are required to pay the tuition fee for the students admitted from abroad even if they have the legal document which grants their previously acquired citizenship rights as stated in the citizenship law. Turkish nationals, who complete their secondary education in a foreign country are also required to pay the tuition fee of the students admitted from abroad. Students admitted to Bilkent University through transfer application, are also required to pay the tuition fee of the students admitted from abroad, if they have not registered to their previous institution through central placement.
