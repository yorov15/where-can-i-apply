Emory University Student Health Insurance Plan • All new and continuing degree-seeking and international Emory University students are required to have health insurance. Under this requirement, students will be enrolled in the Emory University Student Health Insurance Plan (EUSHIP) unless they opt out by providing documentation of enrollment in a comparable United States -based plan. • New students wishing to waive enrollment in the Emory University Student Health Insurance Plan will need to complete the annual waiver process via OPUS by August 14, 2026, for fall-spring enrollment. • The 2026-2027 cost for EUSHIP is approximately $2,796 a semester/$5,592 a year.
