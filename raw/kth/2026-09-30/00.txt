By choosing KTH, you gain access to a vibrant student life and a prestigious academic environment. We offer one bachelor's programme taught in English. It is a three-year programme in Information and Communication Technology. After graduation, you can proceed to a working career or continue studying a master's programme at KTH or elsewhere.
