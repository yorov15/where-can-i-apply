Exchange or Incomings If you want to have a limited stay at RWTH Aachen to study, you can apply as an exchange studend. The most important prerequisite is that you are enrolled at your home university and that you find a supervisor at RWTH Aachen. You can find more information on this topic at Admission Requirements for Exchange Students and Incomings .
