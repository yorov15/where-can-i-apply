Understanding Enrollment vs. Application It's important to distinguish between applying and enrolling at a university. For all academic programs, the process begins with submitting an application through RWTHonline by the specified deadlines. Once your application is reviewed and you're admitted, the next step is enrollment. This is where you confirm your acceptance of a spot at RWTH and officially become a part of our community. If you're unsure about which program to choose, try our online self-assessment tool for personalized guidance. Who Needs to Apply? Please consult our current RWTH information or course listings to determine whether your desired program has open or restricted admission . If your intended bachelor's program is not subject to restricted admission, you're guaranteed a spot at the university! However, regardless of admission status, you must complete the process using our application wizard. For both open and restricted admission master's programs, an online application is required. Be sure to submit all necessary documents to RWTHonline by the application deadline to complete your application process. Please be aware that these deadlines are final; applications received after the deadline will not be considered. International Prospective Students If you're applying from a country outside the EU or EEA, you must always apply for admission by the specified deadline to secure a spot at the university.
