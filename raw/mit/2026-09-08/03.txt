Our goal is to admit and enroll the best students from around the world regardless of their financial circumstances, and make sure they can afford an MIT education while they're here. Affordability + MIT 🌟 $0 Parental contribution for families whose total income is less than $100,000 with typical assets. 🌟 Full tuition paid for students whose families make less than $200,000 with typical assets. 🌟 88% of the Class of 2026 graduated from MIT debt-free.
