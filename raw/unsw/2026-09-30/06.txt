updates and FAQs Apply online View FAQs Menu Offers to study at UNSW Sydney are made in accordance with the following: Undergraduate and postgraduate offers for applicants from International Student Award (ISA) eligible countries are issued on a weekly basis. Offers for applicants from other countries are made according to offer round schedules (see below). Applications are prioritised according to academic merit and subject to program availability. Entry requirements can be found on individual degree pages or in the 2027 undergraduate entry requirements table. Issued offers will have a specified acceptance period. Offer deferrals are not automatically approved and must be requested via StudyLink. Program changes are not available and require a new application. It is recommended that multiple preferences are listed in the initial application. Offers for sponsored student applicants follow a different admissions process. Information on this page is correct as of the day of publication. UNSW reserves the right to change offer rounds and admissions processes as required without prior notice. 2027 Applications Applications to study at UNSW Sydney across all 2027 terms are now open. We encourage students who are interested in studying with us to apply early for their best chance to secure a place. International Admissions Diversity Pathway The International Admissions Diversity Pathway (IADP) recognises that educational opportunities vary around the world. Applicants from International Student Award (ISA) eligible countries applying for undergraduate programs at UNSW Sydney are automatically included in our new International Admissions Diversity Pathway, which provides applicants with an additional 5 points towards their International ATAR* when applying for admission to UNSW Sydney (excluding applicants for the Bachelor of Medical Studies/Doctor of Medicine). This new pathway will assist talented students to be competitive across a broader range of UNSW programs, expanding access and supporting a more diverse and globally connected student community. No separate application is required - eligible applicants are assessed automatically. A minimum score equivalent to an international ATAR of 75 is required. View full eligibility criteria, terms and conditions . Check the FAQs below for more information. *Maximum International ATAR of 99.95. For more information about applying for an Australian Student Visa, please visit this page . FAQs International Admissions Diversity Pathway What is an International ATAR? An 'International ATAR' refers to the conversion of your international curriculum score into an Australian Tertiary Admission Rank (ATAR) which is used for admissions into UNSW Sydney. The indicative international ATAR required for each undergraduate program can be found in the first column of the undergraduate entry requirements table . Do I need to apply separately to the International Admissions Diversity Pathway to have 5 points added to my international ATAR? No, there is no separate application needed. You will automatically be included in the pathway if you are an applicant applying with a passport from eligible markets. Do I require a minimum International ATAR to receive the International Admissions Diversity Pathway 5 additional points? Yes, you require a minimum of a 75 international ATAR to receive the additional 5 points. I have already applied for admission in 2027, do I need to re-apply to be considered for the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? No, all existing eligible applications will have the 5 additional points applied. There is no need to re-submit your application. If your application was already denied, but you would now be eligible for an offer with the additional 5 points added, your application will be re-assessed. Are any programs excluded from the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? Yes, applicants for the Bachelor of Medical Studies/Doctor of Medicine are excluded. Can I receive both the International Student Award or any other scholarships, AND the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? Yes. The International Admissions Diversity Pathway (IADP) is an admissions pathway, not a scholarship. Being considered under the IADP does not affect your eligibility for the International Student Award (ISA) or other scholarships. Eligible applicants may be considered for both the IADP and any scholarships for which they qualify. What if I don't receive the International Student Award (ISA), can I still receive the 5 additional points as part of the IADP? Yes. Receiving the International Student Award (ISA) is not a requirement for the International Admissions Diversity Pathway (IADP). While both initiatives use the same list of eligible countries, applicants are assessed separately for each. Eligible applicants may be considered for the IADP regardless of whether they receive the ISA. How will I know if I received the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? If you apply with a passport from an eligible country, the International Admissions Diversity Pathway will automatically be applied to your application. Is the International Admissions Diversity Pathway available for postgraduate programs? The International Admissions Diversity Pathway is available only for international undergraduate applicants. Are UNSW College or other foundation or diploma graduates eligible for the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? No, the International Admissions Diversity Pathway is only applicable to applicants entering UNSW directly. Why isn't my country listed as an eligible country for the International Admissions Diversity Pathway? The International Admissions Diversity Pathway (IADP) is administered under a NSW Anti-Discrimination exemption that allows UNSW to provide additional admission consideration to applicants from specified countries. The exemption was approved to support greater access to higher education opportunities and to promote a diverse international student community by recognising that educational opportunities are not equally available across all countries. The list of eligible countries forms part of the approved exemption and cannot be changed independently by UNSW. Applicants from countries that are not included on the eligible list are still encouraged and considered by UNSW. All applications will be assessed for admission under the University's standard admission requirements and processes. Applying to UNSW Can I apply to UNSW with incomplete academic results? UNSW will be making offers to eligible postgraduate programs on the basis of six out of eight or seven out of eight semesters studied. Students will need a weighted average mark (WAM) of 5 points above the advertised level to be eligible with 6/8 semesters studied, or a weighted average mark (WAM) of 2 points above the advertised level to be eligible with 7/8 or 5/6 semesters studied. Applicants who do not meet this rank will be placed on hold and may be made an offer once they upload their final results. Will UNSW accept predicted grades for full undergraduate offers? We have altered our policy around predicted grades to widen the qualifications that we accept and have improved the process of assessment. UNSW now accepts predicted grades for full offers for the following qualifications: International Baccalaureate (IB) A-Levels Indian CBSE Singapore A-levels NCUK South African NSC Students who are completing an approved external English language program can request an extension of their offer deadline until their course is finished. Key considerations: A buffer of 2 points above the standard entry requirement is required for a full offer based on predicted grades. Offers will only be issued where this is met or exceeded and all other requirements have been met. Students must provide final results when available, and no later than the UNSW term commencement date. If final results do not meet program entry requirements, students may need to: Transfer to a program with lower requirements, or Explore pathway options such as UNSW College Diplomas, or Seek study alternatives outside UNSW Required documentation: Forecast results must be presented in the following format: Be printed on official school letterhead and include the institution stamp and signature of the Principal, Registrar or Academic Director. Be dated after 1 May for Term 1 (February intake) and 1 November of the previous year for Term 3 (September intake). For IB Forecast only: IB Forecast results have the total predicted score as well as the individual scores for each subject. The total predicted aggregate must be the equivalent of 2 points higher than the total final aggregate required for admission. What are the eligibility criteria and additional requirements for UNSW applicants holding SAT, AP, or ACT results? Students who meet program entry requirements based on their SAT, AP, or ACT results are now eligible to receive a full or conditional on English offer ahead of their final high school result and proof of and completion of high school studies. Results and proof of high school completion will be required no later than the UNSW term commencement date and must be submitted as soon as they become available. This change aims to support students in lodging timely visa applications and re-establishes opportunity to commence their undergraduate studies as planned. Additional requirement - enrolment or expected graduation letter In addition to sharing SAT/AP/ACT results with UNSW via the College Board portal, students must submit a letter from their school meeting the following minimum criteria: printed on official school letterhead bears the official seal of the school includes the student's full name and date of birth specifies the current stage of study (e.g., Year 11/12) states the expected graduation date specifies the name of the final award or test (e.g., US High School Diploma, Chinese High School Graduation Certificate, Indonesian SMA Certificate) Eligibility This assessment policy is only available for students completing Year 12 high school qualifications recognised by UNSW for direct entry. It excludes: post-secondary diplomas foundation programs unregistered/informal home-schooling up to Year 12 GEDi Singapore polytechnics Canadian OSSD delivered online any other bridging or access program What is an International ATAR? An 'International ATAR' refers to the conversion of your international curriculum score into an Australian Tertiary Admission Rank (ATAR) which is used for admissions into UNSW Sydney. The indicative international ATAR required for each undergraduate program can be found in the first column of the undergraduate entry requirements table . Offer expiry What is an offer expiry? Most UNSW international offer holders will have a personalised offer expiry date to accept, decline or defer their study at UNSW. The offer expiry will be active from the date that you receive your offer and your offer will lapse after the offer expiry date. Offer holders will have until 11:59 pm (AEDT) to accept their offer on the acceptance deadline date. UAC International Offer Holders will also receive a personalised offer expiry and must accept within this period. Conditional offer holders will have: Offer expiry and conditions Most international student offer holders, including sponsored students, will now have 8 weeks (56 days) to accept their offer, regardless of whether the offer is unconditional or conditional, unless an earlier deadline applies. Where an earlier deadline applies, this will be clearly reflected in the offer of admission. For conditional offers: English language requirement: Offer holders must meet the condition of their offer by either submitting satisfactory English language results or confirming their enrolment in UNSW College's UEEC program , and accept their offer. Once satisfactory English language results have been received, an unconditional offer will be issued. The new unconditional offer will include the standard 8-week (56-day) acceptance period, unless an earlier deadline applies. If the decision is to package the applicant with the UEEC program, the acceptance will be processed accordingly. The applicant will only be able to finalise their acceptance, including the required enrolment steps, once satisfactory UEEC results have been confirmed by UNSW College. Genuine Student (GS) Requirement If you have received a conditional offer and have been notified that you need to complete a GS check, please follow the required steps outlined on the UNSW Genuine Student (GS) Requirement webpage. If you are required to complete the Genuine Student (GS) Declaration, you must submit the completed form before your acceptance can be finalised. If you are required to complete the Genuine Student (GS) Assessment and Financial Declaration, this condition must be satisfied before you can progress any further with your application or acceptance. Once the applicant has met this GS conditions, an unconditional offer will be issued. The new unconditional offer will include the standard 8-week (56-day) acceptance period, unless an earlier deadline applies. Where can I see my offer expiry date? You can see your offer expiry date on your offer letter and via the UNSW Applicant Portal . I missed the deadline to accept my offer. What should I do? You will need to reapply to study at UNSW. UNSW will only approve for an extension on an offer under special circumstances. This also applies for UAC International students who have missed the deadline. The below reasons are not accepted as reasons for extension of an offer: You are waiting on an outcome from another institution You are waiting for another UAC offer round after the offer expiry date You did not see your offer expiry date You just missed your offer expiry You missed deadline and program is now full If you have an extenuating circumstance, please contact your agent or UNSW regional representative. I won't be able to accept my offer within the expiry date. Can I have an extension? UNSW will only approve for an extension on an offer under extreme special circumstances. This also applies for UAC International students who have missed the deadline. The below reasons are not accepted as reasons for extension of an offer: You are waiting on an outcome from another institution You are waiting for another UAC offer round after the offer expiry date You did not see your offer expiry date You just missed your offer expiry You missed deadline and the program is now full I have been approved for an extension for my offer, where is the new expiry date shown? You will not receive another offer letter, however you can see the revised expiry date on UNSW Applicant Portal . I can't see an offer expiry date on my offer letter and on UNSW Accept Online. Does that mean I don't have an expiry date? Most UNSW offer holders will have a set time period to accept, decline or defer their study at UNSW. The personalised offer expiry is specified in the offer letter and UNSW Accept Online. If you are in one of the following cohorts, you will not have a personalised offer expiry: Sponsored Students UNSW College packaged offers Fully online (offshore) and PGRO students Internal Program Transfer (IPT) students HDR Students Non-award students, including Study Abroad and Exchange students Current UNSW Students applying for a new program Academic calendar UNSW is introducing a new academic calendar from 2028. What does this mean for my studies? UNSW will move to a new academic calendar in 2028. As a global top 20 university and ranked #1 in Australia for employment outcomes (QS World University Rankings, 2027), this new academic calendar is designed to meet the changing needs of the broader educational landscape and follows extensive feedback from students, staff and the community over the past 18 months. The new 'flex-semester' calendar is a semester structure for our undergraduate students and the majority of postgraduate students, centring on delivering the best learning experiences and outcomes for all our students throughout their lifelong education journey.
