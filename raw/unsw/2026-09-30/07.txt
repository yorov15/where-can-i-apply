Find your path At UNSW Sydney, we are committed to offering multiple paths to help you achieve your academic goals. If you do not meet the academic requirements for your chosen degree, a pathway program could help you on your journey to study with us. UNSW College, fully owned by UNSW Sydney, provides various pathway programs to support your smooth transition into university life. Whether you want to strengthen your academic skills, improve your English, or fast-track your studies, UNSW College is here to guide you. Explore the pathway that suits your goals and discover how UNSW College can help you succeed at UNSW Sydney. Find your path Explore our pathway programs Diplomas Stay on track to your university goals with our 12-month diploma programs, offering direct entry into the second year of any UNSW bachelor's degree. Foundation Studies Build a solid academic foundation to prepare for entry into the first year of any UNSW bachelor's degree. Pre-Masters Start your postgraduate journey in just 15 weeks. This program provides the academic and English language support you need to transition smoothly into a master's degree. Academic English Enhance your English skills and boost your confidence with our Academic English programs, designed to prepare you for university success. Got questions on which pathway is right for you? If you need help with deciding what pathway is right for you, contact us below. We're here to help.
