Apply separately for admission to a university or college in Taiwan. Step 2: Attend an interview conducted by the overseas mission. Step 3: Provide a copy of the admission notice received from the university or college to the overseas mission. Step 4: Successful applicants will receive a scholarship certificate from the overseas mission. Step 5: Arrive in Taiwan and register at the university or college. Step 6: Each university or college is responsible for distributing Taiwan Scholarship allowance to students. ::: Authority MOE MOE Official Website (Open in a New Window) MOFA MOFA Official Website (Open in a New Window) Contact info.
