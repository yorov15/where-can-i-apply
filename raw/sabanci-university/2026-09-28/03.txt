If you have results from international exams such as SAT, IB, ACT, or A-Level, you can check the minimum grade requirements under the 'International' menu. If you have a local diploma or school leaving certificate, you can find the relevant details by selecting the country associated with your diploma. Please note that SAT is not mandatory. If you are unsure about the acceptability of your qualification for entry to Sabancı University please contact us for further details. In addition to the requirements below, applicants will also need to fulfill necessary requirements for an equivalency certificate to be able to fully register at Sabancı University. Applicants are expected to bring their equivalency certificates with them when they arrive on campus for registrations. Please note that meeting the admission requirements below do not mean that applicants also fulfill equivalency certificate requirements. Applicants, whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) is not stated on their final diploma/transcript, are required submit a letter from their high school administration to declare their graduation cumulative grade point average. If one of the required exams or diplomas in this list are verifiable through the official online system for the exam or diploma, the application evaluation may be done through online confirmation, or through the submission of the relevant certificate.
