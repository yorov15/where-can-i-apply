The six Faculties/Schools -- Arts and Social Sciences, Business, Chinese Medicine, Communication, Creative Arts, and also Science and Technology-- offer 22 undergraduate programmes with over 45 majors in various disciplines. The University adopts a policy of broad-based admissions for most of its programmes. Under broad-based admissions, students can choose to study in their desired Faculty/School and enhance their overall understanding of the disciplines in the first year. After they have studied more about different subjects of the disciplines, they can then make the best choice of major in order to achieve their goals. The following Faculties adopt broad-based admissions: Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences School of Business School of Communication Faculty of Science and Technology Note: The university has not authorised any agents or agencies to manage admissions processes or to offer guaranteed or supplementary admission slots. All applications and fees must be submitted directly to Hong Kong Baptist University. Joining HKBU One of Asia's leading liberal arts universities, HKBU's reputation for academic excellence and commitment to innovation attracts students from around the globe in search of an education tailored to the demands of the 21st century. Benefitting from the strong support of the Hong Kong government and using English as medium of instruction, HKBU's research-enriched curricula, low staff-to-student ratio and close community spirit are designed to equip students with the creativity, mindset and drive needed to thrive as innovators and entrepreneurs in our fast-changing world. If you are interested in watching our recent webinars, please visit https://admissions.hkbu.edu.hk/webinar/ . Thinking in new ways can help harness the talents of people connected globally to achieve a vision that may originate anywhere. Committed to exploring the transformational power of arts and culture on society, HKBU offers a number of programmes across creative domains including visual arts, film, music, creative writing, TV, animation and screen studies enriched by a network of 50 interest clubs devoted to ignite the creativity of students. Through our interdisciplinary researches, HKBU is well positioned to apply the power of artificial intelligence, big data and cutting-edge technologies to break disciplinary boundaries and foster engagement between the sciences, arts and social sciences. Dedicated to helping students think in new ways, nurture innovations and develop as entrepreneur, HKBU tests our student's limit via opportunities ranging from entrepreneurship bootcamps, startup semester programme held with the Sutardja Center for Entrepreneurship and Technology of University of California, Berkeley, and specialised programmes and awards in fields of social sciences and visual arts. Our series of international conferences, worldwide competitions and national performances also give students the opportunity to engage with peers from around the world. Located at the intersection of East and West, our global campus, diverse faculty, extensive international network and contingent of foreign students make HKBU the gathering point for innovators, creators and entrepreneurs ready to serve the community and enhance the quality of human life. Seven new transdisciplinary second majors (TSMs) and two new second majors (SMs) will be introduced in the 2025/26 academic year. TSMs are geared for particular talent needs in Hong Kong and prepare students to pool knowledge from different disciplines to excel in multiple careers, whereas SMs prepare students to gain in-depth knowledge from another discipline to complement the first major. This approach equips students not only for their careers, but also the future development of their respective fields.
