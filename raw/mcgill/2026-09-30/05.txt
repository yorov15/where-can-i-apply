Undergraduate and non-thesis graduate students admitted to a degree/certificate/diploma program prior to Fall 2024 The Quebec Government announced that the rates for the academic year 2026-27 will increase by 3%. The tuition fee is a combination of the Quebec rate and the out of province supplement and will therefore be $324.35 per credit ($103.92 + $220.43). Students who change their degree or have a break of enrolment of 4 or more terms including the summer term (see Break in enrolment for definition) will be subject to the rate charged to newly admitted students for 2026-27. Students will maintain the grandfathered rate of tuition as long as they complete their studies within 5 years of Fall 2024. International Students Undergraduate students admitted as of Fall 2026 Guaranteed Tuition Model for International Students in a Bachelors, DMD, or MDCM degree: Undergraduate students enjoy the benefits of a guaranteed, set tuition rate for the duration of their time at McGill, eliminating the unpredictability of year-over-year increases and making it easier to plan ahead. This allows prospective students to know the total cost of tuition for their degree program before committing to McGill. The Quebec basic tuition fee in 2026-27 will be $103.92 per credit ($1,558.80 per term, which represents a 3% increase relative to 2025-2026). For international students, the fee charged is a combination of the Quebec rate, the international supplement, and 10% international supplement. In Fall 2026, the supplements for international students in second cycle research programs (Master's Thesis) will be $607.70 per credit ($9,115.50 per term), and $534.84 per credit ($8,022.60 per term) for international students in third cycle programs (PhD). Increases will be officially confirmed as soon as we receive the information from the Québec government.
