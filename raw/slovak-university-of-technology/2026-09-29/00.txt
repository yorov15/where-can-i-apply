Application is delivered electronically without a secure online signature via the university information system of STU (UIS) , which allows the applicant to verify the application's registration in UIS from the day of submission to the end of the admission process. E-application Study programmes in current admission process Admission process at STU is conforms to internal STU regulations . Admission conditions Admission conditions for each level of study at STU faculties and Institute of Management are not identical. They differ in certain details, so it's important to check and keep track of admission information on the specific faculty/Institute of Management and study level you want to apply to: Faculty of Civil Engineering Admission conditions Faculty of Mechanical Engineering Admission conditions Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology Admission conditions Faculty of Chemical and Food Technology Admission conditions Faculty of Architecture and Design Admission conditions Faculty of Materials Science and Technology Admission conditions Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies Admission conditions Institute of Management Admission conditions Fee for admission process The university requires applicants to pay a fee for the material provision of the admission process. It must be paid in advance, and proof of payment should be attached to the e-application for study. The amount of the fee is determined by the Rector's Directive valid for the academic year in which the admitted applicant is to begin their studies. Result of admission process Information about admission results will be communicated to the applicant by the relevant faculty or Institute of management that they submitted an application for studies to. Applicant with specific needs If the admission process includes an entrance exam as part of the assessment of study abilities, the applicant with specific needs will, upon request, have the form and method of the entrance exam adjusted based on an evaluation of their specific needs. The request for adjustments to the form and method of the entrance exam must be submitted by the applicant with specific needs along with the application for study. More information and request form (information only in Slovak) Applicants with education completed abroad For applicants that have completed their secondary (high school) education abroad, equivalency will be ensured for the purposes of evaluating the admission conditions to study at STU. This means that the grades awarded during studies at a foreign secondary school will be converted into the Slovak grading scale according to the grade conversion manual for foreign secondary and primary schools, which is published on the Recognition Portal of the Ministry of Education, Research, Development and Youth of the Slovak Republic. The applicant does not need to send a request for grade conversion to the Ministry of Education, Research, Development and Youth of the Slovak Republic, it will be done by the relevant part of STU to which the applicant has submitted their application for study.
