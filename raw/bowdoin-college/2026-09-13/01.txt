Learn more about the international student aid process. Required Application Material Deadlines The application itself is subject to the application deadline (listed above). Additional required materials (items such as recommendations, transcripts, etc.) that are not automatically submitted with your application are accepted after the deadline. Please submit these materials as soon as is possible. Applicants can check their Bowdoin Application Portal to view a checklist of application materials received. If you or your counselor recently sent materials, note that it can take 3-5 business days for them to appear on your checklist.
