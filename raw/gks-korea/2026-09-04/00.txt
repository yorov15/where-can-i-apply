GKS Exchange Students Program Supports the expansion of international student exchange to enhance the international competitiveness of domestic universities, while providing exchange students with opportunities to understand Korean education and culture. Scholarship Duration 4 months (1 semester) Selection Quota Approximately 500 international students (subject to budget changes) Scholarship Benefits Scholarship Benefits Monthly Allowance KRW 630,000 per month (may vary based on stay and semester end date) Round-trip Airfare Round-trip economy class airfare based on the actual cost Eligibility Requirements Undergraduate and graduate (Master's and Doctoral degree) students of foreign nationality who are enrolled in an overseas university that have agreements with a Korean university affiliated with NIIED. Students must be enrolled in a degree exchagne program (those enrolled solely in Korean language courses are excluded). Students whose overall academic performance at their home university is 80% or above. Students who have previously received scholarship from the korean government are not eligible. Dual nationals who hold Korean citizenship are not eligible. Students currently stuying in Korea are not eligible. Studnets cannot apply to more than Korean university at the same time. ※ Scholarship recipients who have completed one semester (4 months) are not eligible to apply for a scholarship extension.
