Researchers European University - EELISA Schools Applying for Bachelor's Degrees at PSL Home Education Applying for Bachelor's Degrees at PSL Share : Facebook Linked in Print Undergraduate education at PSL is highly selective and designed to help every student reach their full potential. Our admissions process prioritizes geographical, social, and cultural diversity, reflecting Université PSL's commitment to equal opportunity. Whether your interest lies in physics, applied economics, the humanities, or artistic creation, you will pursue a rigorous, multidisciplinary curriculum that opens doors to countless opportunities. One hundred percent of PSL graduates go on to top Master's programs and prestigious Grandes Écoles, both in France and worldwide.
