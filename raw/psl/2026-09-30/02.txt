Take the first step with us, and get ready to unlock new possibilities at one of the world's top universities! Step 1 | Explore study opportunities at PSL Search engine Find programs based on your specific criteria Training fields map Explore our programs across six multidisciplinary training fields Master's taught in English Discover our programs offered entirely or partially in English Aspiring to pursue a research-oriented career? Explore PSL PhD tracks : a highly selective, fully funded, 5-year Master's-PhD program taught entirely in English. Step 2 | Learn about tuition fees at PSL At PSL, you'll receive a world-class education with minimal financial burden. You can find information about tuition fees on each program's page, under the "Admissions" tab. Step 3 | Explore scholarship opportunities tailored to your study plan Explore the multiple scholarship opportunities PSL offers for both French and international students, and find the one that best supports your academic ambitions! Step 4 | Get informed about the admissions procedures Application platforms and deadlines at PSL vary depending on your degree program, nationality, and other criteria. Explore our dedicated pages below to find out when, where, and how to apply: Apply to PSL Bachelorr's programs Apply to PSL Master's programs International admissions procedures at PSL Step 5 | Discover why PSL is the ideal match for you Now that you've explored all the exciting opportunities at PSL, you might still have questions: Is PSL the right fit for me? Why study in France? Don't worry-we're here to help! Check out our guide to PSL and studying in France to discover what makes PSL exceptional and understand how higher education works in France. Chat with our student ambassadors to hear directly from them-ask any questions you have! Dive into our student blog for firsthand insights on academic and campus life, along with valuable admission tips. Explore our student stories and learn about their academic experiences and journeys at PSL Step 6 | Prepare for your journey Congratulations on your admission to PSL! It's time to prepare for your exciting journey, and we're here to support you every step of the way as you begin this path to success. The PSL Welcome Desk offers personalized assistance with all your administrative formalities, from visa applications and health insurance to other essential paperwork, both before and during your stay at PSL. The PSL Housing Service is here to help you find the perfect place to live in Paris. To enrich your experience, our Buddy Program and Tandem Program (language exchange) connect you with peers, offering a chance to immerse yourself in Parisian life and culture. Got questions about campus life, living in Paris, or why students choose PSL? Our students are here to chat and share their experiences! You might find these blog posts interesting!
