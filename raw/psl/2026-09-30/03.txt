Which undergraduate program at PSL University is right for you? No matter your field, you will receive a rigorous, interdisciplinary education that opens up endless possibilities, helps you express your talents, and shapes your future. PSL's programs are selective and tailored for high-potential students from all backgrounds, aiming to guide each one toward success. 100% of our graduates go on to top master's programs and prestigious schools, both in France and worldwide. Come discover the exciting multidisciplinary programs we offer! Université PSL is founded on the belief that science and the humanities must work together in all academic programs, as a true understanding of the world requires expertise in both fields. The College offers five study tracks, each allowing students to personalize their education and explore knowledge across disciplines. Students also benefit from unique opportunities, including: Education on key sustainable development challenges Access to nearly 13 foreign languages through the PSL Language Center A focus on student involvement and engagement Small classes and dedicated support for success Courses and masterclasses at the Collège de France Research opportunities starting in the second year of the Bachelor's degree, including the UROP program and internships in the University's research labs Cultural enrichment and a global perspective CONTACTS Academic director of the Sciences, Humanities & Society College of Université PSL : Dorothée Butigieg Admissions director at Université PSL : Claire Boitard → Applicant contact : admissions-cpes@psl.eu → PSL student & Alumni contact : scolarite-cpes@psl.eu Learn more about Dauphine-PSL Dauphine-PSL Bachelor's Degrees Choose from three programs in the first year, with the option to specialize in the second or third year: management, applied economics, social sciences, law, applied mathematics, organizational IT, AI, and organizational sciences.
