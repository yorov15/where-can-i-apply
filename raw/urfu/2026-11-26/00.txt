There might be an additional creative exam (e.g. drawing/ drafting/ composition etc.) for certain specialties. *If an applicant has a certificate of completion of a Russian language course (either from UrFU Russian Language School or any other authorized institution), it can be submitted instead of taking the language test. Required Documents Scan of the applicant's passport Translation of the applicant's passport into the Russian language (with notarial attestation) Previous education documents (school diploma / certificate or equal) with notarized translation into the Russian language. Full transcript of grades with notarized translation into the Russian language. Note: please consider that in accordance with the Russian education standards all education documents issued in other countries require legalization / apostille verification, otherwise they cannot be considered by the Admissions Office. Deadlines Application period: June 20 to August 20 Master's Degree Programs Eligibility Criteria Previous education: Completed Bachelor's / Specialist's degree program Entrance exam: Online interview with the Program Administrator Required level of language of instruction (Russian / English): B1-B2 Required Documents Scan of the applicant's passport Translation of the applicant's passport into the Russian language (with notarial attestation) Previous education documents (Bachelor's diploma or equal) with notarized translation into the Russian language. Full transcript of grades (for the whole period of studies) with notarized translation into the Russian language. Note: please consider that in accordance with the Russian education standards all education documents issued in other countries require legalization / apostille verification, otherwise they cannot be considered by the Admissions Office. Notarized translations of the documents into the Russian language are absolutely required regardless of the language of instruction of the target program. Deadlines Application period: June 20 to August 20 Preparatory Department for Foreign Students Graduates 2020 - UrFU Preparatory Dept. for Foreign Students Eligibility Criteria The Preparatory Department (Russian Language School) is open for everyone who finds interest in studying the Russian language, no restrictions in terms of the age (except that the student should be over 17) or previous education level apply. In case you want to continue the studies at the University after completion of the language course you have to make sure that your education is in accordance with the admission criteria of the program.
