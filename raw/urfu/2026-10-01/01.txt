About the Russian Scholarship Project Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project is your chance for an early admission to a tuition-free program at one of the leading universities in Russia! Who can participate: Foreign citizens; Stateless persons; Compatriots living abroad. Format of the competition: online at all stages. Essential requirements for each track: For Bachelor's track - a completed general secondary education; For Master's track - a higher education degree (at least a Bachelor's); For PhD track - a higher education degree (at least a Master's or a Specialist's); Participants of the postdoctoral track should have a PhD or a candidate/doctor of science degree. Registration period: August 20 - November 1, 2026. Stages of Open Doors Competition Stages of Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project Competition August, 20 Registration on the website https://od.globaluni.ru . August 20 - November 1 Registration and portfolio submission November 1 Deadline for registration and portfolio submission November 13 Announcement of the first stage results November 13 - December 21 The second stage: completion of tasks for bachelor's, master's, doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks of the competition November 16 The second stage registration deadline December 21 Announcement of the second stage results December 22 - February 26 The third stage (ONLY for participants of the doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks) January 10 The third stage registration deadline February 26 Announcement of the third stage results (winners of doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks) Winners & Runners-up Participants who get a sufficient number of points after 2 stages and are among 25% of the best, fall into the list of winners and runners-up of the Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project. The winners of the Open Doors have the right to tuition-free education. Runners-up of the Open Doors have the right to tuition-free education, if there are winners who have not submitted necessary documents or refused to study at the 2nd stage. Winners and r unners-up can choose a program in English or Russian available for foreign citizens in the area corresponding to the profile of the Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project.
