The scholarship is valid for any program and any education level and includes: tuition fees for the full period of study; monthly scholarship (for the full period of study, regardless of the student's academic results); on-campus accommodation. The selection of foreign applicants is carried out by Rossotrudnichestvo through its network of representative offices in different countries, as well as through Russian embassies abroad.
