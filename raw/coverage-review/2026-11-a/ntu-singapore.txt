…ts Financial aid Financial planner Tuition fees and grants As a full-time undergraduate student, you have to pay tuition and miscellaneous fees for Semesters 1 and 2 of each academic year. These fees are payable as long as you're actively enrolled and have not applied for a leave of absence. Miscellaneous fees help to meet part of the costs of providing insurance, student programmes and amenities, IT services and academic-related services that are not covered by the tuition fee. For full-time undergraduate programmes, the fees are fixed for your entire candidature under a cohort-based system. For detailed guidelines on tuition fees, please click here . To support students, Singapore's Ministry of Education provides tuition grants that cover a substantial portion of tuition fees to eligible students.
