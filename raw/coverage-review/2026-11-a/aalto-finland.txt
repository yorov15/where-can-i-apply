Application fee Applicants who are not citizens of the European Union (EU), or the European Economic Area (EEA) member states, or Switzerland must pay a €100 application fee to apply for higher education in Finland. Citizens of EU or EEA member states and Switzerland are exempt from the fee. In addition, certain types of Finnish residence permits provide an exemption from the fee. Detailed information on the application fee, exemptions and payment instructions are available on the Studyinfo page Application fee . If you have a document that exempts you from the application fee, you must upload it to your application form. Aalto University's tuition fees vary by study field. Tuition fees are: Bachelor's programmes Tuition fees for rights to study beginning on or after 1 August 2025 Field of Business €12 000 per academic year Fields of Art and Architecture €15 000 per academic year Field of Technology €12 000 per academic year Multidisciplinary programmes* €12 000 per academic year *Multidisciplinary bachelor's programme: - Bachelor's Programme in Technology, Business, and Design. Master's programmes Tuition fees for rights to study beginning on or after 1 August 2025 Field of Business €15 000 per academic year Fields of Art and Architecture €20 000 per academic year Field of Technology €17 000 per academic year Multidisciplinary programmes** €17 000 per academic year **Multidisciplinary master's programmes: - Master's Programme in Creative Sustainability, - Master's Programme in International Design Business Management, and - Master's Programme in Urban Studies and Planning. The normative duration of bachelor's degree studies is three academic years, and that of master's degree studies is two academic years. Aalto University reserves the right to change the tuition fees. International joint degree programmes may have their own tuition fees and scholarship schemes, read more on joint programmes' web pages . Other costs In addition to tuition fees, students need to finance their living expenses. For information about living costs in Finland, please see Financial matters page. Aalto University Excellence Scholarships are merit-based and are granted on a competitive basis to the highest-achieving applicants based on the programme's evaluation criteria. Programmes may decide not to grant any scholarships. Scope of scholarship Aalto University Excellence Scholarships are awarded as tuition fee waivers that cover the full tuition fee. Aalto University Excellence Scholarships do not cover living costs. Students need to cover all living expenses (at least 800 euros per month) and other study related costs from their own financial resources. Please consider your finances before submitting an application. You can read more information about living costs in Finland at: Financial matters . How to apply for a scholarship?
