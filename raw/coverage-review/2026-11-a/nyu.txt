Who is eligible to have their demonstrated need met? All students admitted as first-year undergraduate students to our New York campus and who apply for financial aid by the financial aid deadlines will have 100% of their demonstrated need met, regardless of citizenship status. That includes international applicants, as well as undocumented students. Meeting demonstrated need means NYU will cover, through institutional scholarships, federal/state grants, and expected student work, the cost of attendance-including tuition, housing, food, books, and supplies-less the amount that your family is expected to contribute as determined by the information provided on the CSS Profile. Who is eligible to have tuition covered? Students admitted as first-year undergraduate students to our New York campus who apply for financial aid by the financial aid deadlines , and who have a family income under $100,000 with typical assets, will not have to pay tuition.
