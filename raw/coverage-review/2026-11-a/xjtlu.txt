Early bird discount The Early Bird Discount (10% of the tuition fee per year) is applicable to international undergraduate students who submit all required documents through the admissions process on time. The requirements are: Submit their applications and documents by 31 March 2027. Plus, receive a conditional or unconditional offer by 31 March 2027. For conditional offer holders, providing all supporting documents by 30th June, except IB and A-level final results where details will be communicated with such students at a later stage of their applications. For those conditional offer holders whose final academic transcripts are released by their schools later, we would require the students to advise Admissions in advance. Pay their deposits correctly and successfully by the deposit deadline written on the unconditional offer letter. Failure of payment due to the mistakes from the sender will not be considered as being eligible for the early bird discount. Please note Early Bird Discount is not applicable to Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan students. Sibling discount Students, who have sibling(s) enrolled on the XJTLU degree program will receive a sibling tuition fee discount. Discounts are calculated as follows: 5% for the second sibling; 10% for the third sibling and 15% for the fourth sibling. Students should contact the Admissions Team via international@xjtlu.edu.cn by 31 May 2027. Scholarships 1) Entry Scholarship for Outstanding Academic Applicants We offer an entry scholarship of up to 50 percent of the tuition fee per year for international and Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan undergraduate students, based on academic excellence. Your indication of interest during the online programme application process and filling in the 'XJTLU Entry Scholarship' section is required to be considered for this scholarship, and you must submit key documents within the round to be qualified for a conditional offer.
