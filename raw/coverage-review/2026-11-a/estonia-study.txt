Cost of living in Estonia is usually dependent upon the student's accommodation choices, lifestyle, and spending patterns. Accommodation The currency in Estonia is the Euro (€). The monthly average living cost for students can be around 300-500 euros. Housing costs depend on the location, quality of the accommodation and on the season but the prices per month usually range from €150-350 in the dormitories to €350-750 in private flats . Most universities in Estonia have their own dormitories and all of them are ready to help international students settle into their life in Estonia.
