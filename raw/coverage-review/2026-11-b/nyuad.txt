All Other Applicants (Non-Emirati Citizens) All financial aid grants offered by NYUAD (for non-Emirati citizens) are based on financial need. To be considered, students should file the CSS Profile by the required deadline , which is prior to the admission notification dates . Applicants with divorced, separated, or unmarried parents will need to have both the custodial and noncustodial parents submit their own separate CSS Profile application online. Students who are required to submit noncustodial parent information and fail to submit an application/waiver request will make you ineligible to be considered for institutional grants while you pursue your studies at NYU Abu Dhabi. Award amounts vary depending on the student's financial need as determined by NYU's Office of Financial Aid. It is important to meet the filing deadline and to submit any additional documentation, if requested, right away for full consideration. Please do not submit any additional documentation unless specifically requested by NYU's Office of Financial Aid.
