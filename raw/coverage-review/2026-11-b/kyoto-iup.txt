To ensure a smooth transition to life in Japan, each Kyoto iUP student is allocated a room in a Kyoto University International House for the first 12 months following enrollment. This residential requirement eliminates the need for students to search for their own accommodation, facilitates seamless access to university support services, and fosters a collaborative learning community.
