A Bachelor Degree requires, at least, four years of full-time university-level study. The scope of these programs is 240 credits. The programs follow a specific curriculum with an academic load of about 26-30 hours per week. The course content is fixed in accordance with the State Educational Standard. Foreign applicants and stateless persons have several way to get the right to receive higher education: - at the expense of budgetary allocations in accordance with: 1) international treaties of the Russian Federation, 2) federal laws, 3) the quota for education of foreign citizens and stateless persons established by the Government of the Russian Federation. Budget-funded education on a general basis Budget-funded education according to the quota of Ministry of Education Budget-funded education in the framework of the Olympiads Budget-fundeteducation according to the status of Compatriot - at the expense of individuals and legal entities: in accordance with contracts for paid educational services.
