The family contribution For international students, your family contribution (the amount your family is expected to pay annually) is finalized when you enroll and will be the same each year until graduation, regardless of changes in family financial circumstances, including a younger sibling's enrollment in college, or currency fluctuations. Additional costs Annual costs for the student beyond the comprehensive fee include the cost of the annual student health insurance premium (approved health insurance is required of all students attending college in Massachusetts), travel costs, and the estimated personal and book expenses of $2,000. The financial aid package A typical financial aid package for an international student consists of need-based grant aid , a fixed-interest student loan and student employment . Due to College financial aid policies, some aid packages may not include the maximum amount of student loan or campus employment each year.
