Once enrolled, approximately 32.5% of our current students are eligible for additional merit and/or performance based scholarships and awards. Scholarship offer will not be deferred to the next academic year. Nottingham global scholarships There are two types of Nottingham global scholarships available. UNNC Provost International Excellence 100% Scholarship awards the student an equivalent of 100% tuition fee for the first year of study. UNNC Emerging Leaders 50% Scholarship awards the student an equivalent of 50% tuition fee for the first year of study. This scholarship is an entry-based scholarship which means the awardee's tuition fee for the first year will be waived or it will be refunded if the full tuition fee was already paid. Eligibility Full time international undergraduate student who have applied and received an offer from UNNC. Selection criteria These prestigious scholarships are competitive and application based. Academic grades, (eg A level/IB/High School predicted grades) will be an important part of the selection criteria. Applicants will need to submit a scholarship statement (maximum 500 words) to the application hub. The scholarship statement should include details of your extra-curricular, volunteer/work experience or how you have contributed to your community.
