Programmes taught in Czech Whereas students in programmes taught in English are charged very affordable fees, programmes taught in Czech are completely tuition free , but a serious working knowledge of Czech is required. If you are interested in studying in Czech or would like to improve your command of the language, you might consider registering in a preliminary language course one year prior to your enrolment at Masaryk University. This intensive course will help you prepare for studying one of the 300 fields of study taught in Czech.
