Q [Others] Does GIST provide scholarships for international students? A All international undergraduate students are granted GIST scholarship without any separate application process. Tuition fees are fully waived for 8 semesters, and there are monthly allowances, settlement subsidy and flight reimbursement. However, dorm fee is not included in GIST Scholarship. GIST undergraduate dormitories cost approx. 106,000 KRW / per month. And meals are available at on-campus cafeterias at a cost of 4,000 ~ 7,000 KRW. Q [Online Application] Should I pay the application fee? A We do not charge any application fee. Q [Online Application] Do I have to choose my major when I apply to GIST? A When first admitted to GIST undergraduate program, students are inducted into Division of Liberal Arts and Sciences for their first year before selecting their major.
