New first-time students President's Scholarship The President's Scholarship is open to applicants who have completed their secondary education outside the UAE and covers 100 percent of tuition and lab fees and an application fee waiver. It also provides a monthly stipend for the duration of the scholarship, on-campus housing, three round-trip tickets for awardees during their years of study, and student medical insurance. It is offered for qualifying first-time students who enroll at AUS in the fall semester. The selection process depends heavily on academic excellence and a series of other requirements that showcase students' achievements. The scholarship is renewable for students who continue to maintain the scholarship requirements. To be eligible to apply for the President's Scholarship, you must first receive an admission offer from AUS. Please consider the time required for the admission process when planning for the President's Scholarship deadline.
