The payment link is accessible exclusively through your personal account on the application portal. What is the payment deadline? The deadline is 10 days for both the application and tuition fee payments (as set in the online system). If you need more time, you may request an extension to adjust the system deadline. Are there any tuition fee waivers or scholarships available? For those seeking financial aid, we recommend applying for the Stipendium Hungaricum (SH) scholarship. I have dual citizenship (EU and non-EU). Which tuition fee applies to me? The tuition fee is determined by the passport you upload during the application process. What is the refund policy? The tuition fee refund procedure can be found here . Post-Admission & Logistics Does the university assist with the visa process? Applicants are responsible for managing and arranging their own visa applications. How long does it take to receive the Final Acceptance Letter after payment? It takes a maximum of 10 working days. Can an agent or a third party inquire on my behalf? We only provide answers to general application questions to third parties. Is there dormitory placement for admitted students? Students who have received their Final Acceptance Letter will be provided with contact information to request accommodation options. What is the cost of living in Hungary? You can find more information at the following link: https://studyinhungary.hu/living-in-hungary/menu/your-costs-of-living.html Where can I find an apartment? The university does not provide private rental services. We recommend using reputable real estate websites or verified social media groups for students. Information for Current Students Where do I register/enrol? Enrolment is handled exclusively at your respective Faculty's Registrar's Office (Kari Tanulmányi Osztály). Who is my study coordinator and where are they located? Each Faculty has its own Registrar's Office. Please check your Faculty's website for the contact details of your specific coordinator.
