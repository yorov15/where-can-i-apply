We have found that students with scores in the following ranges are best prepared to study and learn at Carleton College: TOEFL iBT: 5.5 and above iBT before 2026: 100 and above Take a mini TOEFL practice quiz for free IELTS : 7.0 and above Duolingo English Test (DET) : 130 and above Financing Your Education Your future should be guided by talent and ambition - not student loan debt. Carleton meets 100% of your demonstrated financial need, without loans. We calculate the amount each student's family can reasonably pay and then offer aid to cover the rest of Carleton's cost. There are no income caps for determining financial need or eligibility for aid. All international students must submit a Certification of Finances (CoF) with their application materials. This includes students who do not apply for aid. The CoF demonstrates how much money your family can contribute to your education and is used to prepare your immigration paperwork. You will gain access to the CoF after you submit your application. View a sample CoF to preview the questions that will be asked. The estimated cost of attendance for the 2026-2027 academic year is $104,746 . This amount includes tuition, housing, food, fees, plus estimates for books, personal expenses, travel, health insurance and expenses over breaks. You should expect this cost to increase each year between 4-5%. If you cannot cover the entire cost of all four years at Carleton, you should apply for financial aid.
