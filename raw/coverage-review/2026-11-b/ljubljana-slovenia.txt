Tuition Fee Non-EU citizens must pay a tuition fee, usually in full at enrolment for the current academic year. The fee does not include compulsory health insurance, which must be arranged before the start of studies. Tuition fee for full-time studies is waived for citizens of countries with which the Republic of Slovenia has concluded bilateral or multilateral agreements on educational cooperation or reciprocity (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, and Kosovo); for citizens with permanent residence in Slovenia who themselves of their guardians are tax residents of Slovenia; persons with international or temporary protection and its applicants; and non-EU citizens
