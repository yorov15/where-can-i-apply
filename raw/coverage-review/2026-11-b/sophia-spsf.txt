Sophia University New Student Scholarship Sophia University New Student Scholarship support students who are facing financial hardship and who have chosen Sophia University as their first choice. Awarded to Those who consider our university as their first choice and find it difficult to study here due to financial problems despite having outstanding grades. Be sure to apply for the scholarship at the same time as applying to take the entrance examinations. Scholarship Amount Recipient may receive the amount equivalent to the full, one-half or one-third of the Tuition Fee for the first year. Application Period The application period varies depending on the type of entrance examination to be taken. Generally, the application period for the scholarship is the same as the application period for the entrance examinations. However, this may not apply to some entrance examinations. Applicants who take more than one type of entrance examination must submit a separate application for a Sophia University New Student Scholarship for each entrance examination. Scholarship
