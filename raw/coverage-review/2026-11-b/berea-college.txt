Final admissions decisions will be released no later than March 31. When do accepted students receive an I-20? Accepted international students will be issued an I-20 after they have submitted the required $2,200 deposit and completed all required forms (e.g. passport bio page, medical history form). What is the cost per year? All admitted international students receive financial aid and scholarships that cover 100% of tuition, housing, food, and fees. As part of their participation in the College's Work Program, international students are encouraged to save money to contribute toward their personal expenses. The College does make summer jobs available to international students. Accepted international students are also expected to pay a $2,200 (US) deposit to confirm their enrollment. This deposit can be used by international students for certain expenses during their four years of enrollment. For students who are unable to pay all or a portion of the deposit, additional financial aid is available. When must I pay the $2,200 deposit? Students who have been offered admission to Berea will receive communication from the Office of Admissions with specific details on the payment deadline and instructions for submitting payment. The College does have some sources of financial aid to help cover this amount, depending upon the student's family financial circumstances.
