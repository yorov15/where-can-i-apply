For a standard study plan of between 46 and 74 ECTS and an ISEE of up to € 22,000, the amount is € 157.04 ; for a higher-value ISEE, the amount gradually increases up to a maximum of € 3,943.04 . In particular, for an ISEE between € 22,001 and € 30,000, the amount is calculated with a specific formula ; for an ISEE higher than € 30,000, the amount is established on the basis of fee bands . The percentage corresponding to the number of ECTS credits in the study plan is then applied to these amounts.
