All students sign the Declaration of acknowledgement of payment for studies during enrolment. All necessary information is available below. The rules of tuition fees applicable at Jagiellonian University are described in the Order no. 65 of the Rector of Jagiellonian University of 18 June 2024 . The amount of the tuition fees is determined in the Rector's order. The legal acts regulating the tuition fees, including those from previous years, are available here . Otherwise, the tuition fees for studies may be charged. Tuition fees - studies in English International students undertaking studies in foreign languages pay for each study programme. Depending on the programme, the fees amount from PLN 16,000 (Environmental Protection and Management) to EUR 15,500 (MD program in English) for the first year of studies. The fees are paid for one semester. Candidates admitted for the first year are obliged to pay the first instalment at the latest within 7 days of enrolment (document delivery). The amount of the tuition fees depends on the study programme and is available in the card of studies in the Online Application System (IRK) in the "About studies" section, as well as in the order of the Rector of the Jagiellonian University. In exceptional cases, such as a difficult financial situation, high grades, or participation in international exchange programmes, a student may apply for a fee reduction or exemption, but this procedure is not available in the first year of studies. Detailed information on the amount of the fees is available in the studies catalogue in the Online Application System, in the "About studies" section of the studies card. NAWA scholarship International students may also be eligible for an exemption from the fees for studies in English upon the grant of one of the National Agency for Academic Support (NAWA) scholarships. NAWA scholarship holders are exempt from fees for studies in accordance with the rules set out in the Director of NAWA's decision. Notice: if you declare that you apply for the NAWA scholarship during admissions, and fail to obtain it, you will be obliged to pay the fees for your studies!
