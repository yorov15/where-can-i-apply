Admission to Fee-Paying Programs Admission to fee-paying programs at Kimyo International University in Tashkent is available to applicants who meet the minimum admission requirements established by the University. Each academic year, the University determines admission quotas and tuition fees for programs offered on a fee-paying basis. Admission Scholarships for International Students Up to a 50% tuition fee waiver for the first academic year. Based on the results of document screening and an admission interview, successful candidates may receive an admission scholarship. Students who receive the scholarship may maintain it in the following academic years by demonstrating strong academic performance and meeting the University's scholarship renewal requirements. The scholarship aims to support talented and motivated international students and to encourage academic excellence throughout their studies at KIUT.
