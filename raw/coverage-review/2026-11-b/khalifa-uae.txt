SCHOLARSHIPS FOR INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS Khalifa University of Science and Technology offers the following scholarships to support qualified UAE national and international students enrolled in undergraduate programs on a full-time basis. The University reserves the right to make changes to the published scholarship benefits and conditions without prior notice. The scholarship supports UAE National and International students pursuing a Bachelor's degree at Khalifa University. Scholarship eligibility is assessed as part of the admission process and applicants are not required to submit a separate scholarship application. The number of available scholarship slots is limited. International students are evaluated and mapped to a specific scholarship Tier on the basis of academic merit and other relevant factors, in line with Khalifa University's strategic priorities, rules and regulations. Meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee a scholarship award. Scholarships for International Students Tier I Full coverage of tuition fees. Textbooks provided by the University. Tier II Partial waiver of 75% of tuition fees. Textbooks provided by the University. Tier III Partial waiver of 50% of tuition fees. Textbooks provided by the University. Tier IV Partial waiver of 25% of tuition fees. Textbooks provided by the University. Tier V Textbooks provided by the University. Please refer to the Tuition Fees webpage for information on tuition costs. The University reserves the right to make changes to the published scholarship and fees information without prior notice. SCHOLARSHIPS BY EXTERNAL SPONSORS Khalifa University partners with external sponsors to provide scholarships that go beyond financial support, incorporating research, industry connections, and practical skills to enhance students' education and career opportunities.
