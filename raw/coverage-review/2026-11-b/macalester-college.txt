at Macalester. How much financial aid does Macalester offer? Macalester is committed to meeting the full demonstrated need of every enrolling student. "Demonstrated Need" is calculated by Macalester using a student's financial aid application forms to determine how much (if anything) a student's family can pay towards their education costs. That "Expected Family Contribution" is then subtracted from the College's "Estimated Cost of Attendance" for the upcoming academic year. The "Estimated Cost of Attendance" includes costs for tuition, housing, meals, health insurance, and estimated costs for supplies. The difference between the calculated "Estimated Family Contribution" and the College's "Estimated Cost of Attendance" is a student's "Demonstrated Need." Academic factors such as test scores do not influence the calculation of demonstrated need. What does Macalester scholarships and aid cover? Can I get a 100% scholarship? Macalester is committed to meeting the full demonstrated financial need of every student we admit up to our estimated cost of attendance. There is no minimum family contribution. Rather we use the financial aid application materials to calculate a student's Expected Family Contribution (EFC) and subtract this amount from our estimated cost of attendance to determine a student's demonstrated financial need.
