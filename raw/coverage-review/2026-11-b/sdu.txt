Yes , the travel allowance is paid regardless of academic results . What are travel allowances and when are they paid? Travel allowances are payments provided to students as compensation for transportation expenses (travel tickets). These payments are granted to all students and master's degree students of higher and postgraduate education institutions studying under a state educational grant. The allowance is paid twice a year, during the winter and summer vacations, in an amount equal to four times the Monthly Calculation Index (MCI). For graduating students (Bachelor's degree - 4th year, Master's degree - 2nd year), the summer travel allowance is not paid. What determines the travel allowance amount?
