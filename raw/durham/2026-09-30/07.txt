Durham University scholarships and bursaries are either funded by the University, through partnerships with external organisations or through generous donations from alumni and friends of the University. Find out more Finance Information Durham University supports students from USA or Canada with obtaining loans from their home country. F or more information about Foreign Loans see our corresponding section. Canadian students have the ability to borrow government loans through their province and you can find more information from the Government of Canada Student Loans website . Durham University authorises enrolment verification forms on behalf of Canadian students, and you can bring your forms to the Scholarships and Student Funding Service or email us . Durham University's Institutional Code is: PUAP US citizens and eligible non-citizens can apply for William D. Ford Direct Loans, private loans through Sallie Mae or combination of both. Direct loans interest rates are lower than private loans and students should investigate all options before borrowing. Please find additional information about Federal Student Aid from studentaid.gov website. Federal School Code: G08587 OPE-ID: 008587-00 Students receiving aid from other foreign countries should, once in Durham, bring their loan and deferment forms to the Scholarships and Student Funding Service in The Palatine Centre. More Information Fee's and Funding opportunities and guidance for International Students. Fee Status and Payment You are classified as 'Home/EU' or 'Overseas' for fees purposes depending on your nationality and the country where you are 'ordinarily resident'. Living Costs Living costs are typically much lower in the North of England than they are in the South, however, your own budget will depend on your lifestyle. International Scholarships Durham University is committed to supporting the best students irrespective of financial circumstances. College and Department Scholarships There are number of scholarship and bursary opportunities from colleges and departments across the University available for International Students. Tuition fee deposits (postgraduate only) All postgraduate taught courses require international applicants to pay a deposit towards their tuition fees, once they are holding an unconditional offer at Durham. Student Aid (USA) The U.S. Department of Education's office of Federal Student Aid provides more than $120 billion in financial aid to help pay for college or career school each year.
