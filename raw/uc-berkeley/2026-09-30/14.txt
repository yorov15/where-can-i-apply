While federal and state financial aid is generally unavailable to international students, the university offers several institutional scholarships for undergraduates from around the world. __________ This website serves as a resource for scholarship opportunities specifically for undergraduate international students. These scholarships do not require students to submit a separate application- all admitted undergraduate international students are automatically considered for the scholarship programs for which they are eligible. Regents' & Chancellor's Scholarship The Regents' & Chancellor's Scholarship is UC Berkeley's most prestigious undergraduate scholarship, awarded to exceptional students who demonstrate creativity, leadership, service, and strong convictions. Scholars exemplify the university's commitment to excellence and receive numerous benefits, including a monetary award. Candidates are selected through a holistic review process from the top applicants to UC Berkeley. All students who apply, including first-year and transfer international students, are automatically considered. Robinson International Scholars Program The Robinson International Scholars Program enriches UC Berkeley's global learning environment by developing transformational leaders. Founded in 2022 by the Robinson family, this special program supports select international students who are refugees or stateless individuals from Sub-Saharan African and developing nations, as defined by the United Nations; or who have graduated from secondary school in a Sub-Saharan African or developing nation, as defined by the United Nations. Robinson International Scholars demonstrate resilience, passion, and a strong commitment to serving and giving back to their communities. The program includes a need-based scholarship for up to four years. Scholars receive the full benefits of the Regents' & Chancellor's Scholarship in addition to the Robinson International Scholarship. International Leaders Scholarship at UC Berkeley Program The International Leaders Scholarship supports talented international students who demonstrate academic excellence, leadership, and a commitment to making a meaningful impact. This need-based scholarship provides financial assistance for up to four years, empowering recipients to maximize their UC Berkeley education and drive positive change in their communities. Scholars contribute to the university's diversity and excellence, bringing valuable perspectives to campus. Scholars receive the full benefits of the Regents' & Chancellor's Scholarship in addition to funding from the International Leaders Scholarship. Priority consideration is given to students of Peruvian residence. Swaniker Scholars Program at UC Berkeley The Swaniker Scholars Program supports talented students from across the African continent who demonstrate academic excellence, character, leadership, and high potential. This initiative is part of a partnership between ALX Africa and UC Berkeley. A select number of outstanding ALX Pathway participants and African Leadership Academy (ALA) graduates are nominated for this scholarship. Swaniker Scholars receive a need-based scholarship covering up to the full cost of attendance for four years. Scholars receive the full benefits of the Regents' & Chancellor's Scholarship in addition to the Swaniker Scholarship.
