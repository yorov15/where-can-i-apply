In order to finalize your admission, you are required to submit school leaving documents, which include academic records or transcripts, for all years of secondary school attended, years 9-12. If you are attending a local school, the school leaving document(s) prove(s) that you qualify for access to a university in your home country or the country from which you are graduating secondary school. Students attending an international school (British, American, Canadian, etc.), will find the required school leaving document(s) by finding the country from which you are graduating secondary school in the list below. A country that has "N/A" under the Secondary School Exam/Certificate column, has no additional school leaving documents that are required; you will submit your academic records/transcripts as requested in other items on your CalCentral Task List. NOTE: Students must submit certified translations of all foreign college/university records that are not issued in English. We accept English-language translations provided by foreign universities or those performed by certified translators with knowledge of educational records and terminology.
