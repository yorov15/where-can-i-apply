Scholarship Scholarship Available Scholarships (Relevant Track) Coverage Eligibility Tuition Scholarship (All Tracks) Full Tuition Waiver (up to 8 semesters) All admitted Korean and international undergraduate students receive a full tuition waiver for the first semester upon enrollment. Continued tuition waiver support for up to eight regular semesters from the second semester onward is subject to meeting POSTECH's eligibility criteria, including a minimum GPA of 3.0 out of 4.3 in the previous semester. (The same criteria apply as for domestic undergraduate students.)
